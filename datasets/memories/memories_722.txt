The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Hui is a recent high school graduate feeling significant distress after not performing well on the college entrance exam. This setback has deeply affected Hui's confidence and social interactions, especially with friends discussing future plans.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Hui enjoys drawing, which serves as a creative outlet and source of peace.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Hui experiences low self-esteem and social anxiety triggered by academic challenges but hasn't been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Hui feels down and frustrated, perceiving a loss of confidence due to recent academic challenges.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Hui's tendency to internalize failure and significant impact on social connections yet notices openness to exploring thoughts and feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Hui should document situations where negative feelings are strongest, including the accompanying thoughts, for further analysis.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Hui identify and reframe negative thought patterns, leading to improved self-worth and social comfort.
Counseling Summary:
Hui expressed feelings of frustration and disconnection following a poor performance on the college entrance exam, impacting confidence and social interactions with friends. The consultant advised Hui to note situations where negative feelings and thoughts occur, aiming to explore how these are linked and assess perceptions of others' judgments. Hui agreed to the task, hoping to gain clarity and improve well-being, with plans to deepen the exploration in the following session.
