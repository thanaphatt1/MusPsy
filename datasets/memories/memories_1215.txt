The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
D is a university student who has recently faced a challenging situation during class awards, affecting their self-confidence and prompting self-doubt about their accomplishments. D values recognition for their efforts and contributions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
D enjoys reading and traveling, activities that provide relaxation and excitement. They are good at organizing events and offering support to their classmates.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
D experiences frustration and self-doubt, particularly in situations that involve comparisons with peers and lack of acknowledgment for their efforts.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] D feels down and frustrated after not receiving expected recognition for their contributions during a class awards event.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices D's strong feelings of self-doubt and their tendency to question their value but acknowledges their openness to exploring these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] D should note down moments of self-doubt and identify common triggers, focusing on replacing comparison thoughts with reflections on their strengths.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help D recognize and reinforce personal strengths, and guide them in reducing comparison thoughts through self-reflection.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored D’s frustration over unrecognized efforts and resulting self-doubt, leading to a pattern of unhealthy comparisons. Developed strategies including focusing on personal strengths, regular engagement in enjoyable activities, and open communication to boost confidence and garner acknowledgment. Concluded sessions with recognition of progress and equipped D with tools for handling future challenges independently.
