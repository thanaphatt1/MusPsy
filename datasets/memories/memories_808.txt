The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zeng is a high school student struggling with significant stress related to academic performance, particularly in physics, and is facing pressure from parents and teachers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds solace in playing the piano, using it as a way to relax and distance himself from academic stresses.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zeng experiences stress and anxiety predominantly due to high academic expectations, though there is no mention of a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zeng feels anxious and inadequate due to high expectations from parents and teachers, resulting in a sense of being overwhelmed.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Zeng's awareness of negative thought patterns and sees potential in exploring cognitive strategies to manage stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zeng should begin to document thoughts and feelings when overwhelmed to understand the patterns behind them better.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT techniques to assist Zeng in identifying and managing stress-related thoughts, emphasizing awareness and understanding of triggering situations.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zeng's stress due to academic pressures and high expectations, resulting in feelings of anxiety and inadequacy. Agreed on an exercise for Zeng to write down thoughts and emotions when overwhelmed to identify unhelpful patterns. Recognizing these patterns is seen as a crucial step towards managing stress and improving well-being.
