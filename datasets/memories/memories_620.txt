The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
B is a student feeling isolated due to their mother's frequent business trips, which leave them feeling alone and neglected, impacting their focus at school.

[Personal Characteristics]
B enjoys drawing and reading comics but has not engaged much in these activities recently.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
B experiences feelings of isolation and self-reported social challenges, without any formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] B feels isolated, struggling with loneliness and the impact of their mother's absence on their well-being.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes B's genuine openness to exploring their feelings despite their withdrawal due to perceived neglect.
[Counseling Assignments:] B is tasked with keeping a journal to express thoughts and emotions, focusing on recurring feelings of loneliness.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help B express and navigate feelings of isolation, encouraging the engagement in fulfilling activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored B's feelings of isolation and neglect due to their mother's absence and difficulty forming connections with peers. Agreed on two tasks: keeping a journal to express recurring emotions, and scheduling regular drawing sessions to manage stress and foster self-expression. Anticipated reviewing these activities in the next session to identify emotional patterns and promote empowerment.
