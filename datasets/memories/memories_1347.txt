The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoguo is a university student who feels disappointed and uncertain about their social life and future after failing to join a club they were eager about. They value being part of a community and exploring new interests.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoguo is open to exploration and has a past positive experience with joining and enjoying a book club, which highlights their potential for communal engagement when stepping out of their comfort zone.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoguo experiences feelings of self-doubt and anxiety related to missed opportunities and social integration at university, though there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoguo feels disappointed after not being accepted into a club and is uncertain about future opportunities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoguo's susceptibility to self-doubt but notes their willingness to explore new avenues.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoguo should list alternative clubs or activities at the university that interest them, considering other pathways for involvement.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Xiaoguo to explore new opportunities without self-criticism, fostering a mindset open to new and fulfilling experiences.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaoguo expressed disappointment after failing to join a club, which affected her social life and future plans. The consultant encouraged her to explore new university opportunities and develop interests, emphasizing a patient exploration approach. Xiaoguo agreed to the idea, feeling more hopeful about discovering activities that align with her passions.
