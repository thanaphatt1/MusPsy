The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student from a rural area in Anhui, struggling to adjust to the new environment, leading to feelings of overwhelm and self-doubt.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys drawing, which serves as a stress-relief activity, and aims to connect more with classmates.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety primarily related to academic and social pressures, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels overwhelmed and doubtful about his abilities since starting university and is struggling to adjust.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Wang's anxiety linked to self-expectations and social isolation but appreciates his willingness to explore solutions actively.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to keep a journal of his thoughts and feelings when tense or doubtful, providing material for future discussions.
[Session Goal:] Help Wang identify and understand thoughts linked to the overwhelmed feeling and suggest strategies to ease his transition into university life.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's difficulty in adjusting to university life, particularly his anxiety and self-doubt stemming from a new environment and lack of interaction with classmates. Discussed the importance of allowing time for adjustment and connected thoughts, feelings, and behaviors to help ease the transition. Agreed on a task for Wang to journal his thoughts and feelings when tense or doubtful, to be reviewed in the next session.
