The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaojun is a university student who experiences significant stress related to upcoming exams. He worries about meeting personal and parental expectations, often feeling anxious about academic performance.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaojun enjoys playing basketball, which helps him unwind and manage stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaojun has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder, but he experiences anxiety primarily triggered by academic stress and a tendency toward negative thought patterns.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaojun feels anxious and stressed about the upcoming exams, manifesting in physical symptoms like a faster heartbeat.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaojun's pattern of jumping to worst-case scenarios but also notes his willingness to reflect on past successes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaojun should jot down recurring anxious thoughts to identify patterns and try a structured relaxation routine when feeling anxious.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaojun manage exam anxiety by using CBT to identify thought patterns and explore relaxation techniques to ease anxiety responses.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaojun's exam-related anxiety and identified pressure as a key trigger, while recognizing his tendency to anticipate worst-case scenarios. Agreed on strategies including thought tracking, structured relaxation routines, and reflecting on past successes to manage anxiety. Scheduled to review noted thoughts and the effectiveness of relaxation techniques in the next session.
