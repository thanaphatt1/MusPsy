The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mrs. Li is a professional who recently underwent surgery, which resulted in being reassigned to a logistics department due to voice issues. Previously, she had direct interaction with patients and colleagues. She feels marginalized and undervalued in her new role.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mrs. Li experiences feelings of marginalization and reduced self-worth related to her professional transition, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Mrs. Li finds solace in gardening, which helps alleviate stress and offers a sense of achievement akin to her earlier professional roles.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mrs. Li feels frustrated and marginalized due to her reassignment at work, experiencing a loss of purpose and value.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Mrs. Li grappling with a sense of injustice regarding her role change but notes her openness to self-reflection and exploring positive contributions in her new job.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mrs. Li is encouraged to start a daily journal documenting her feelings and any positive contributions she notices, serving as a tool for emotional clarity and growth.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to assist Mrs. Li in recognizing her value in her current role and developing strategies to combat feelings of marginalization.
Counseling Summary:
Mrs. Li expressed feelings of marginalization and a loss of purpose after being moved to the logistics department due to voice issues from surgery. The consultant encouraged Mrs. Li to maintain a journal to track her emotions and identify positive contributions in her new role to reshape her perception of competence and value. Mrs. Li agreed to focus on acknowledging her achievements and intends to leverage these insights to adapt to her new chapter positively.
