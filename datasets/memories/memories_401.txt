The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a project manager experiencing stress due to project delays and communication issues within his team. He feels a heavy responsibility to align team efforts and meet deadlines, impacting his mental well-being.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys cycling as a way to relieve stress and reconnect with himself.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences anxiety provoked by workplace challenges but has not received a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels anxious and overwhelmed by the project's slow progress and team communication problems.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Zhang Wei's tendency toward self-blame, although Zhang Wei is open to examining his thoughts around communication issues.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is encouraged to document specific instances of communication breakdowns and identify recurring patterns.
[Session Goal:] Assist Zhang Wei in applying CBT strategies to recognize stress triggers and begin exploring effective communication solutions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang Wei's work-related stress, particularly around leading a challenging project and facing team communication issues. Discussed impact on personal anxiety and overwhelm. Agreed on a goal to document specific communication breakdowns to identify patterns and manage stress.
