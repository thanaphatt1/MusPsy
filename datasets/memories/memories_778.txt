The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zheng is a student struggling with stress due to academic pressures exacerbated by involvement in a school gaming competition. Zheng has noticed a significant decline in grades, which has caused tension with his mother, who is concerned about his academic performance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zheng enjoys gaming, but also has an interest in drawing, which he finds relaxing and enjoyable.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zheng experiences stress and anxiety related to academic performance and family dynamics, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zheng feels stressed and caught in a dilemma between his interests in gaming and meeting academic requirements, leading to familial tension.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zheng's difficulty in balancing gaming and academics, along with the impact of perceived parental disappointment, yet also identifies Zheng's potential for introspection.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zheng is assigned to track gaming time and related emotions, aiming to create awareness of these patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Zheng to cognitive-behavioral strategies, emphasizing the importance of time management and emotional awareness to manage stress.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zheng's struggles with gaming habits impacting academics and family relationships, focusing on negative thought patterns and stress management. Implemented strategies like setting small, manageable goals for schoolwork, creating a balanced routine, and introducing drawing as a leisure activity to improve well-being and communication with his mother. Zheng gained insight into the importance of openness and realistic thinking, developing skills to manage future challenges effectively.
