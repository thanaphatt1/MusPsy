The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li Ming is a working professional feeling overwhelmed by an increased workload, leading to a sense of disconnection from friends and social activities. He is also concerned about not keeping up with work demands.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li Ming previously enjoyed morning runs as a form of stress relief and values connections with friends for support.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li Ming experiences heightened stress and anxiety primarily triggered by work demands, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li Ming feels overwhelmed and anxious due to work demands, which has led to social withdrawal and self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Li Ming’s tendency to think negatively and his openness to exploring ways to reduce anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li Ming should try to brainstorm ways to balance work and leisure, and start documenting recurring negative thoughts to identify patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to challenge negative thought patterns and explore ways to reconnect with friends.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li Ming's feelings of overwhelm due to work pressures, leading to social withdrawal and heightened anxiety. Discussed reframing negative thoughts about competence and reconnecting with friends through small, casual meetups, as well as reintroducing stress-relieving activities like morning runs. Agreed on setting achievable goals like scheduling short runs and journaling thoughts to foster a healthier work-life balance and manage stress.
