The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Shang is a university student experiencing significant stress due to fears of failing exams and repeating a year, leading to overwhelming anxiety that impacts concentration and relaxation. Xiao Shang values academic success and is deeply concerned about achieving his academic responsibilities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing video games and drawing, which he occasionally uses for stress relief and personal expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Shang is experiencing heightened anxiety triggered by academic pressures but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Shang feels deeply anxious and is struggling with fear of academic failure, which dominates his thoughts and daily life.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiao Shang's anxiety rooted in fear of failure and recognizes his openness to trying new strategies for managing stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Shang is encouraged to create a structured schedule with dedicated short study sessions followed by gaming and drawing breaks to balance study time with relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiao Shang manage stress by identifying negative thought patterns and incorporating enjoyable activities like gaming and drawing into a balanced routine.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Shang's academic anxiety and its impact on concentration and stress levels, while identifying video games and drawing as potential stress-relievers. Agreed on a structured routine of short study sessions followed by gaming or drawing breaks to balance study and relaxation efforts. Encouraged challenging negative thoughts, celebrating small achievements, and sharing progress in future sessions.
