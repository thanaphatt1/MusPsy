The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a young adult who has recently been experiencing distress due to being insulted by peers on social media, leading to significant feelings of upset and anger.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They enjoy playing soccer, which serves as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user has not been diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder but exhibits signs of emotional overwhelm triggered by external social stressors.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels upset and angry, struggling to manage emotions and focus on studies due to ongoing social media harassment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's heightened emotional responses to negative social interactions and their openness to exploring ways to manage emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to document their emotional responses to social media interactions and try relaxation techniques like deep breathing.
[Session Goal:] Help the user identify emotional triggers and introduce basic coping strategies such as relaxation techniques to manage stress more effectively.
Counseling Summary:
The consultant guided the user through expressing the emotional impact of cyberbullying and suggested documenting specific emotions as a first step in managing anger and aggression. They agreed on practicing deep breathing and mindfulness to control reactions, setting a goal to track moments where these techniques help calm emotional states. The focus was on gradual progress, aiming to empower the user with strategies for emotional resilience.
