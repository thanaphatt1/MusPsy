The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Miles is experiencing immense stress and self-doubt after the failure of his business and separation from his wife, who left with their children. He continually struggles with the critical voice of his father in his head, undermining his self-esteem.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Miles finds tranquility in gardening, which he uses periodically to alleviate stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Miles exhibits stress and negative self-assessment linked to recent life events but hasn't been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Miles feels like a failure and is overwhelmed by negativity, with a critical paternal voice contributing to these feelings.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Miles's awareness of his internalized self-criticism and his need for affirming his strengths amidst overwhelming stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Miles should keep a journal of his thoughts and emotions to identify patterns and work on disrupting the cycle of negativity.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT techniques to discover connections between Miles's thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, and help him develop strategies to break the negativity cycle.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Miles' overwhelming stress and self-doubt following personal and professional setbacks, linking his current negativity to past critical experiences with his father. Agreed on a journaling task to track thoughts and emotions to identify patterns and facilitate progress.
