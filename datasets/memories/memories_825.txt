The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a 25-year-old, recently completed a detox program, and experiences uncertainty and anxiety about the future. They live with supportive but not very communicative parents. 

[Personal Characteristics]
The user enjoys working with their hands, like fixing things or building models, which offers them a sense of accomplishment and relief from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user struggles with anxiety and fear of potential relapse into drug use, exacerbating feelings of uncertainty about personal and professional future goals.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels uncertain and anxious about the future, particularly worried about relapsing after a detox program.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's commendable willingness to confront fears and consider new directions for their future, despite the fear of the unknown.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to note specific fears about the future and to imagine feasible scenarios and small goals to begin addressing these fears.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help the user identify and confront specific fears, promoting a focus on practical and small steps towards recovery and future planning.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's feelings of uncertainty and anxiety about the future following addiction recovery, focusing on fears and comparisons causing withdrawal and self-doubt. Agreed on cultivating self-compassion, integrating affirmations into a daily routine, setting small job application goals, and planning a supportive conversation with parents to aid in job-seeking efforts. Highlighted importance of celebrating progress to build confidence and scheduled a follow-up session to review Liu's updates and progress.
