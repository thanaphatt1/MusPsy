The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
John is an adult male who recently ended a 10-month romantic relationship, feeling confused and guilty after the breakup, which his partner attributed to feeling ignored.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
He has previously enjoyed running as a means to clear his mind and cope with stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
John exhibits patterns of negative self-reflection and self-blame, particularly involving his recent breakup, with no mention of a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] John feels overwhelmed, grappling with feelings of confusion and guilt following the abrupt end of his relationship.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes John's tendency towards self-blame and guilt but notes his willingness to explore and understand his emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] John is encouraged to keep a journal to document his thoughts and feelings surrounding the breakup.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help John process emotions related to the breakup and begin identifying patterns in his negative thinking.
Counseling Summary:
Explored John's feelings of confusion and guilt following a sudden breakup, focusing on understanding his thoughts and emotions. Agreed on a journaling exercise this week to process these reflections. Aimed at developing deeper self-awareness and emotional processing.

[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies John's realization of the impactful nature of his negative thoughts and his exposure to extreme thinking patterns.

John was guided to gradually shift perceptions, acknowledge small victories, and implement these strategies to reduce the tendency to overanalyze and achieve more peace of mind.
