The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jiang is a university student who recently experienced a strained relationship with a formerly close uncle due to a conflict over smoking and drinking. The tension affects Jiang's emotional state and academic concentration, especially after the loss of his father, which has left Jiang feeling pressured by family expectations, particularly from his mother.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing basketball, which previously served as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jiang experiences stress and feelings of sadness, primarily due to familial struggles, but there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jiang feels stressed and sad due to the conflict with his uncle, which affects his studies and mental peace.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Jiang's struggle with guilt and family expectations but notes a readiness to find resolution with his family relationships.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jiang should write a letter to his uncle or mom expressing his feelings and also plan to reintroduce basketball into his routine as a coping mechanism.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Jiang to express his feelings constructively and use basketball as a regular stress-relief activity.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Jiang's stress stemming from a strained relationship with his uncle after a disagreement about smoking and drinking. Jiang agreed to write a letter to his uncle to express his feelings and to begin playing basketball again as a way to manage stress and clear his mind. The consultant emphasized the importance of honest communication and consistency in both personal expression and recreational activities to aid emotional processing.
