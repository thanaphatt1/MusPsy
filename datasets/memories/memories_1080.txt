The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yuma is a student who has recently moved from Tokyo to Nagoya, struggling with adapting to the new environment and missing the familiarity of their old home.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yuma enjoys drawing as a means to express emotions and connect with memories, occasionally finding solace in this creative activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yuma experiences difficulty adapting to change, accompanied by feelings of nostalgia and discomfort in a new environment. No specific mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yuma feels out of place and discomforted by the new surroundings, finding the emptiness of nostalgia hard to grasp.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Yuma's openness to self-exploration through art and shares supportive family dynamics despite current adjustment struggles.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yuma will create drawings of favorite memories from Tokyo to maintain a connection with the past and share these with family to facilitate communication about feelings.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Yuma integrate old memories with new experiences, alongside fostering communication with their family through shared artistic expression.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yuma's feelings of disconnection after moving from Tokyo to Nagoya and how drawing helps retain positive memories from the previous home. Agreed on sharing artwork with parents to communicate emotions and planned a family outing to a nearby park to gather new inspirations and foster adjustment. Encouraged setting a creative goal to integrate old and new experiences through art, fostering personal growth and comfort in the new environment.
