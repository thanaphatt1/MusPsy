The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Duan is a university student experiencing stress and discomfort from conflicts with roommates, after a recent escalation from minor disagreements to a significant argument.

[Personal Characteristics]
Duan enjoys sketching as a soothing activity to help unwind from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Duan is experiencing anxiety predominantly triggered by personal relationships and roommate conflicts, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Duan feels overwhelmed and pressured, leading to a sense of discomfort and withdrawal.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Duan's avoidance of confrontation and tendency to overthink social interactions, yet recognizes a willingness to explore reflection and journaling for clarity.
[Counseling Assignments:] Duan should begin journaling about their feelings to help process them and identify troubling aspects of the situation.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Duan process their emotions, reevaluate negative assumptions, and encourage constructive expression through writing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Duan's anxiety stemming from tension with roommates after an unresolved argument and how it affects his sense of comfort and academic focus. Suggested journaling to process emotions and plan a conversation to address unresolved issues, acknowledging Duan's apprehension about initiating dialogue. Emphasized the importance of readiness and preparedness for effective communication in future sessions.
