The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He also enjoys painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures and recent misunderstandings in class, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels stressed and anxious due to a recent misunderstanding in math class that makes them feel humiliated and ashamed.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Liu's difficulty in managing perceived negative judgments from peers and the anxiety surrounding interactions but also sees the potential for Liu to open up and express their feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Liu should jot down recurring thoughts and emotions experienced during math class for further exploration in managing stress and perceptions.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Liu challenge negative assumptions about peer perceptions, identify thought patterns contributing to anxiety, and consider expressing feelings to friends for support.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's recent incident in math class that triggered feelings of anxiety and shame, affecting his class interactions and perception of friends. Agreed on an exercise for Liu to reflect on and document his thoughts and emotions during math class to identify patterns. Encouraged considering sharing feelings with friends to alleviate emotional burden while emphasizing progress made in acknowledging these feelings.
