The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a diligent student, generally maintaining above-average grades but currently struggling with anxiety about class participation, particularly fearing judgment from classmates. Wei is under pressure from teachers to participate in discussions, which has heightened these feelings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei enjoys biology and finds the workings of living organisms particularly fascinating, which can sometimes alleviate stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei experiences anxiety particularly in academic settings, demonstrated through fear of embarrassment in class, but there's no formal diagnosis beyond this observed anxiety.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels anxious and overwhelmed by the pressure to perform well in class discussions.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes that Wei feels a significant fear of judgment, which is amplified by a lack of preparation.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Wei is encouraged to identify situations where anxiety intensifies and to focus on positive classroom aspects, such as topics of genuine interest.
[Session Goal:] The goal is to help Wei recognize anxiety triggers, shift focus towards engaging in class topics of interest, and reduce fear of judgment using CBT principles.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wei's anxiety around class discussions and avoidance of peer judgment, focusing on performance pressures and the impact on peer interactions. Agreed on observing emotional responses, especially in biology classes, recognizing interests to foster engagement, and setting a small task to share biology facts with classmates to build confidence. Emphasized gradual exposure and positive focus as steps to manage anxiety and enhance participation.
