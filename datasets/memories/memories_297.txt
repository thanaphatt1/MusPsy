The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Dong is a university student who is experiencing significant stress due to academic challenges, such as the risk of expulsion from missing classes. Dong grew up in a supportive family which holds high expectations, leading to concerns about disappointing them.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Dong enjoys playing badminton, which serves as a potential stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Dong has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder but demonstrates anxiety and stress linked to academic obligations and fear of disappointing family.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Dong feels worried and stressed about the potential consequences of missing classes, with anxiety about managing workload and academic pressures.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Dong's high level of anxiety and fear of academic failure, yet notes Dong's open-mindedness to solutions and self-awareness.
[Counseling Assignments:] Dong should create a checklist for classes and assignments, plan to play badminton for stress relief, and consider contacting classmates to form a study group.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Dong break tasks into manageable steps, develop a schedule for academic responsibilities, and engage in stress-relieving activities like badminton.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Dong shared his feelings of anxiety and stress about potentially being expelled due to missing classes, emphasizing the challenges of balancing workloads and fear of falling behind. The consultant suggested breaking tasks into manageable steps and encouraged Dong to engage in stress-relieving activities such as badminton, while also planning to set reminders and form a study group for academic support. Dong agreed to focus on setting reminders and contacting professors, aiming to regain control and clarity in his academic routine.
