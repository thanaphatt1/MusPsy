The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoli is a school student who has been experiencing significant anxiety in social situations. Recently, Xiaoli felt exposed during a school photo event when peers asked for their mask removal, leading to increased feelings of vulnerability. Xiaoli values family interactions, finding comfort and acceptance when engaging with their brothers at home.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoli enjoys drawing, which provides a sense of relaxation and a creative outlet to express emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoli experiences social anxiety, particularly feeling judged by peers, though no formal mental health disorder diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoli feels anxious and vulnerable, especially in situations that require public exposure or judgment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Xiaoli's struggle with perceived judgment and identifies a resilience in seeking comfort through understanding and gradual exposure.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoli is encouraged to observe situations where they feel less anxious and to note down these instances for discussion in future sessions.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Xiaoli identify anxiety triggers, promote understanding of anxiety patterns, and encourage exploration of safe environments to build confidence.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoli's anxiety at school, focusing on her fear of judgment and sense of vulnerability without a mask. Identified safe, judgment-free environments, such as time with family, as potential models for increasing comfort in other social situations. Xiaoli agreed to observe moments of anxiety and peace, noting patterns and using positive past experiences and supportive self-talk to challenge her perceptions before the next session.
