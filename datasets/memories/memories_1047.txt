The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a motivated individual who has recently been affected by criticism from a class advisor, causing significant anxiety regarding their perception by authority figures.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys writing short stories, which serves both as a creative outlet and as a means of stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety primarily triggered by criticism and the validation of external perceptions, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels tense and worried due to perceived negative perceptions following a class presentation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's tendency to dwell on criticism and their openness to exploring constructive ways to view feedback.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is advised to keep a journal of moments when anxious thoughts occur, noting triggers and emotions to better understand thought patterns.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Wang view feedback from a constructive perspective, manage anxiety, and explore maintaining open communication with trusted individuals.
Counseling Summary:
In this counseling session, Wang expressed anxiety and unease after receiving criticism from a class advisor, leading to worries about being judged negatively. The consultant encouraged Wang to keep a journal tracking instances of these thoughts, aim to reframe criticism as an opportunity for growth, and consider discussing feelings with a trusted friend for additional support. Wang was also advised to incorporate positive affirmations into their daily routine to reinforce self-worth and manage anxiety.
