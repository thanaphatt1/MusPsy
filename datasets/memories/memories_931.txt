The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xuanxuan is a student navigating feelings of loneliness and decreased motivation following a fallout with a close friend, Xiaoqi. This conflict has contributed to a lack of connection and challenges in academic performance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xuanxuan enjoys playing basketball, which serves as an outlet for stress relief and a means to foster social connections.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xuanxuan experiences sensations of loneliness and stress primarily due to social dynamics and academic pressures, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xuanxuan feels down and isolated, noting a decrease in academic motivation since the friendship fallout with Xiaoqi.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xuanxuan's longing for reconnection and the impact of social isolation on their well-being, alongside a readiness to initiate repairing relationships.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xuanxuan is encouraged to prepare a calming note and potentially reach out to Xiaoqi to rebuild the friendship and engage in shared activities like basketball.
[Session Goal:] Support Xuanxuan in addressing feelings of isolation by using CBT techniques, such as enhancing social connections and fostering engagement in stress-relieving activities like basketball.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xuanxuan's feelings of isolation and disconnection after a fallout with a close friend, Xiaoqi, and how this has impacted her academic performance and social life. Discussed steps to reconnect, like preparing a message and suggesting shared activities like video games, and strategies to build new connections through common interests like basketball. Encouraged Xuanxuan to document positive social interactions and incorporate enjoyable activities regularly to improve her mood and by the next session, focus on progress and developments with Xiaoqi and others.
