The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a newcomer in his workplace, grappling with feelings of isolation after joining his new job. His interactions with colleagues have been limited, leading to feelings of exclusion and stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang is historically reserved, enjoys running and playing the guitar as stress-reduction activities, and seeks to build meaningful connections within his social environment.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences stress and anxiety related to workplace social dynamics and has no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels isolated and stressed at his new job, keenly aware of his exclusion from colleagues' social interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's introversion contributing to feelings of anxiety and notes the potential for self-awareness in reshaping his social experiences.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should document his thoughts and feelings during moments of exclusion at work to facilitate further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhang identify patterns in his anxiety related to workplace exclusion and initiate a shift in perspective via CBT techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's feelings of anxiety and exclusion due to being left out by colleagues at his new job, impacting his emotional state and focus. Agreed on a task for Zhang to observe and record his experiences of exclusion to gain insights for perspective shift. Emphasized the potential for these observations to be a proactive step toward altering the impact of these experiences on Zhang.
