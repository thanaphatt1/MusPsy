The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaofeng is a senior-year university student feeling significant pressure about deciding between graduate school and job hunting. Recently, the lack of responses from job applications has affected Xiaofeng's confidence and sleep.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaofeng used to enjoy drawing but had stopped due to other priorities. Xiaofeng finds certain solitude moments, especially at night, to be anxiety-inducing.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaofeng experiences anxiety and stress tied to career decisions and academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaofeng feels down and anxious, predominantly due to uncertainties surrounding career choices and the lack of feedback from job applications.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaofeng grappling with self-doubt and increased anxiety but notes an openness to using artistic expression as a coping strategy.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofeng should maintain a journal documenting thoughts and feelings and spend time drawing in the evenings to explore its impact on anxiety.
[Session Goal:] Identify negative thought patterns through journaling and reintroduce relaxing activities like drawing to reduce anxiety and stress.
Counseling Summary:
The consultant and Xiaofeng discussed the anxiety and pressure Xiaofeng is experiencing due to decisions about graduate school and job hunting, which have affected mood and daily routine. They agreed on keeping a thought journal and revisiting drawing as a hobby to help manage stress and improve self-understanding. The consultant encouraged Xiaofeng to explore these activities as a way to express feelings and unwind, with the aim of identifying and addressing anxiety triggers.

[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofeng should draft responses to negative thoughts as if advising a friend, continue drawing, and track thoughts in the journal for objectivity.

In the session, Xiaofeng explored his anxiety related to self-worth and rejection, recognizing cognitive distortions such as focusing on negatives and assuming lack of response as rejection. Emphasizing the importance of open communication, he plans to share more with loved ones to prevent misinterpretations, and he is encouraged to continue his journey of self-discovery despite challenges.
