The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jane is a university student who recently went through a breakup, struggling with feelings of loneliness and sadness. She has not been sharing her emotions with her family or peers and tends to isolate herself in her dorm room, avoiding social interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Jane used to enjoy running, drawing, and photography but has stepped back from these activities since her breakup.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jane is experiencing symptoms of depression and significant emotional distress due to the breakup but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jane feels down and alone, experiencing a deep sense of sadness and isolation following the breakup.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Jane's intense feelings of self-blame and the tendency to isolate herself, although she is open to trying new strategies for coping.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jane is encouraged to join a friend or group for an activity, like a walk, and to start noting down negative thoughts to identify thinking patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Jane manage feelings of loneliness by encouraging gradual social involvement and recognizing negative thought patterns.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, the consultant supported the user in addressing feelings of isolation and sadness following a breakup, recommending small social interactions to overcome avoidance. They encouraged the user to begin exploring negative self-perceptions by documenting recurring thoughts to identify patterns and challenge them, fostering greater self-compassion and understanding. The session emphasized gradual progress and mindfulness in managing emotions, paving the way for a more open and connected mindset.
