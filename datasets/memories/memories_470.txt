The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Limin is a student managing feelings of loneliness and tension due to drifting friendships and parental conflicts, which impacts their emotional well-being. Limin is keen on maintaining connections with others but struggles with fear of rejection.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They enjoy drawing, finding it a comforting and expressive medium amidst personal challenges.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Limin experiences heightened anxiety likely influenced by social disruptions and a challenging home environment, although no formal mental health disorder is diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Limin feels lonely and anxious as friendships drift apart and home becomes a space of conflict and tension.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Limin's sensitivity to relational changes and their potential for resilience by engaging in self-expression through drawing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Limin should jot down thoughts and emotions linked to feelings of isolation and consider reaching out to friends to re-establish social connections.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Limin identify stress triggers, encourage self-compassion, and foster reconnections with peers to mitigate feelings of loneliness.
Counseling Summary:
The session explored Limin's feelings of loneliness and anxiety rooted in drifting friendships and family conflict, highlighting the impact of social media on exclusion feelings. Limin agreed to document their thoughts and emotions, engage in therapeutic activities like drawing, and consider reaching out to friends while managing the fear of rejection. The consultant encouraged self-compassion and preparation for gradual change, emphasizing the importance of identifying positive relationships and nurturing personal interests.
