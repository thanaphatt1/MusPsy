The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a young professional encountering significant work-related stress, particularly about approaching deadlines, and feels pressure to perform well. Wei also struggles with maintaining social connections due to these stressors.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He previously enjoyed running and has supportive friends, although recently he feels disconnected from them.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei exhibits high levels of stress stemming from work pressures and self-critical thoughts, leading to anxiety, though there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels overwhelmed and pressured due to work deadlines, which is affecting both his social and personal life.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wei's willingness to manage his stress and recognize his competent work, despite recurring doubts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei should journal daily to record self-critical thoughts and assess their realism, while also listing positive accomplishments.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Wei manage stress by balancing critical thoughts with positive acknowledgments and promoting personal accomplishment recognition.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wei's work-related stress and its impact on social life, identifying self-doubt as a key contributor to anxiety. Agreed on a daily routine to challenge negative thoughts with self-affirmation and to invest time in a personal hobby or family interaction to balance stress. Encouraged mindfulness and self-advocacy for managing work pressure.
