The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a retired individual who recently visited their children and felt misunderstood about the challenges they face in daily life due to health issues. Zhao values family connections deeply and seeks recognition and support from their children. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhao enjoys gardening, which provides a sense of accomplishment and peace, despite the physical challenges associated with it.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao experiences feelings of loneliness and misunderstanding, primarily triggered by family interactions, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels down and neglected after visiting their children, perceiving a lack of understanding regarding their daily struggles.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes Zhao's feelings of being misunderstood and the emotional impact this has on them, recognizing Zhao's openness to explore these emotions further.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Zhao should note down moments of feeling misunderstood for discussion in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Zhao express their needs to their children more effectively and explore ways of improving family communication.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhao's feelings of loneliness and misunderstanding following interactions with his children, highlighting a desire for understanding and support. Encouraged Zhao to note moments of feeling misunderstood and observe instances of support, aiming to shift perspectives by recalling supportive interactions with friends and neighbors. Agreed on nurturing relationships and having open discussions with family from a positive viewpoint to enhance understanding and connection.
