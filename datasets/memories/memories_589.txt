The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaogao is a student experiencing sadness and anxiety due to pressures from school performance, particularly after poor midterm results and criticism from a teacher. Xiaogao feels a strong obligation to meet the expectations of their grandparents.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaogao enjoys drawing, which provides a sense of relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaogao experiences symptoms of stress and anxiety related to academic performance and interpersonal expectations, with no formal diagnosis currently mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaogao feels stressed and anxious after performing poorly on exams and being publicly criticized by a teacher.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaogao's heightened anxiety about disappointing their grandparents and self-critical thoughts associated with academic performance.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaogao is tasked with writing down negative thoughts related to school to identify patterns and consider engaging in regular drawing sessions for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Xiaogao manage school-related stress, explore negative thought patterns, and incorporate calming activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaogao's academic stress and fear of disappointing family, emphasizing the self-imposed pressure he faces. Assigned homework of noting negative thoughts and strategized having an open conversation with grandparents; additionally, integrated regular drawing sessions as a positive outlet for stress relief. Xiaogao agreed to these steps, creating a plan to enhance emotional management and self-expression.
