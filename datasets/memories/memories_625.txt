The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
M is a university student experiencing significant stress due to financial concerns, stemming from their family's long-term economic focus. M's parents own a restaurant, and their busy schedules have influenced M's anxiety about finances.

[Personal Characteristics]  
M enjoys reading novels as a relaxing and cost-effective hobby and has shown interest in initiating creative writing.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
M experiences financial anxiety and stress, mainly influenced by family dynamics but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] M feels stressed and tense about financial matters, often overwhelmed by managing expenses as a student.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes M's internalization of family financial stress and their tendency to exert control over expenses, but sees potential for breaking these patterns through reflection.
[Counseling Assignments:] M should observe and note moments when financial anxiety peaks to help identify specific triggers and patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help M become aware of thought patterns regarding finances and explore ways to manage these thoughts practically and constructively.
Counseling Summary:
M explored financial anxiety influenced by family concerns and university life, identifying moments when these anxieties peak and their impact on social interactions. M agreed to try setting a balanced budget allowing for leisure spending without guilt and to establish a reading routine for stress relief. The consultant encouraged M to continue using these strategies and to reach out if further support is needed, highlighting M's progress in managing challenges.
