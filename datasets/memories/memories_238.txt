The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaowei is a university student facing stress due to social isolation after a misunderstanding involving a dormitory incident and family communication. Xiaowei recounts feeling like an outsider and worries about how peers perceive him, despite trying to improve living conditions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaowei enjoys reading mystery novels, which serve as a distraction from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaowei experiences stress and anxiety partly from strained social relationships, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaowei feels stressed and isolated, concerned about being seen as an outsider by peers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaowei’s vulnerability to peer perceptions but also identifies resilience in seeking constructive solutions to stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaowei is encouraged to engage in reading as a relaxation activity and note how these moments impact stress levels.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to manage stress, focus on relaxation through enjoyable activities, and develop awareness of stress patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaowei's feelings of isolation and stress due to social misunderstandings in her dorm, prompting emotional distress. The session focused on setting a small, constructive goal of reading mystery novels to divert attention from stress and enhance relaxation, encouraging mindfulness around emotions and stress management. Xiaowei agreed to observe changes in her mood while engaging in reading, appreciating the manageable approach of small steps for emotional recovery.
