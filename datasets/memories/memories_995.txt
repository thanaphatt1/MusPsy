The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student facing considerable stress due to failing two courses and the potential risk of expulsion, compounded by family expectations. Previously enjoyed activities like painting and calligraphy have lost their appeal due to anxiety and stress.

[Personal Characteristics]
The user has a strong interest in creative activities such as painting and calligraphy, which have been utilized as stress-relief methods in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences significant stress and anxiety primarily related to academic pressures, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed and stressed, with anxiety affecting focus and interest in previously enjoyable activities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's heavy burden of expectations and self-perceived inadequacy, noting the user's initial openness to self-exploration through journaling.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to try journaling to explore and articulate emotions, with the aim of reducing stress.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help the user identify stressors and thought patterns, facilitating engagement in relaxing activities like painting and journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed the user's overwhelming stress and anxiety due to academic challenges and fear of disappointing family. Recommended journaling as a tool to explore emotions non-judgmentally and suggested reintroducing yoga and creative activities like painting for relaxation and mindfulness. Concluded with encouragement to embrace the process and find balance between stress and hope.
