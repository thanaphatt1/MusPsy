The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
H is a college student who is struggling to adjust to the challenges of university life. These challenges are compounded by perceived academic failures, which, in H’s case, have notably affected self-esteem and confidence.

[Personal Characteristics]  
H has a previously enjoyed social life but has recently become more withdrawn. Music, specifically playing the piano, serves as a form of stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
H experiences diminished self-esteem and discouragement, particularly related to academic performance, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] H feels down and discouraged after a failed exam, questioning their abilities and potential in college.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes H's struggle with self-doubt and withdrawing behaviors, yet observes an openness to self-reflection and exploring healthier thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] H should document moments of discouragement and the thoughts accompanying these feelings to identify patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Introduce H to CBT strategies to identify and challenge negative thought patterns promoting academic self-doubt.
Counseling Summary:
Explored H's feelings of discouragement and low self-esteem following a failed exam, highlighting the impact on their confidence and social withdrawal. Agreed on the importance of recognizing negative thought patterns, and encouraged H to engage in small, manageable social interactions with a trusted classmate as a strategy to combat isolation and build a sense of belonging. Emphasized the value of gradual progress and maintaining mindfulness toward their thoughts and surroundings.
