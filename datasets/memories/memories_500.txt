The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Fan is facing significant stress due to parental conflicts over educational expectations, experiencing anxiety and sleep disturbances as a result. Fan is a student who feels increasingly pressured to mediate familial tensions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Fan enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming activity but has not engaged in it much recently. Friends, especially Mia, provide a supportive network.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Fan experiences anxiety and stress resulting from familial discord and academic pressure, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Fan feels anxious and overwhelmed by parental arguments impacting focus and sleep.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Fan's internalization of familial stress and sees potential in leveraging supportive friendships.
[Counseling Assignments:] Fan is encouraged to jot down feelings during stressful moments and consider sharing thoughts with a supportive friend like Mia.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to assist Fan in managing anxiety by recording stress-related thoughts and engaging in supportive dialogues with friends.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Fan's anxiety related to parental arguments affecting sleep and focus. Agreed on strategies like journaling feelings, deep breathing exercises, reducing screen time before bed, and discussing concerns with a supportive friend, Mia. Fan committed to trying these methods, aiming for gradual improvement in managing anxiety and sleep disturbances.
