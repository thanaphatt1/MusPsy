The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaogang is a university student experiencing significant stress related to final exams and family expectations. Despite being physically away from home, Xiaogang feels the pressure of his parents’ academic expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaogang enjoys playing basketball, which serves as a potential stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaogang is encountering stress and anxiety mainly due to academic pressures and a fear of not meeting expectations, though there hasn’t been a diagnosis of a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaogang feels overwhelmed by the stress of upcoming exams and the challenge of meeting his parents' expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaogang's significant stress related to high expectations and a lack of parental support, given their physical absence, while also noticing Xiaogang’s willingness to explore managing stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaogang is tasked with reflecting on moments of confidence in his learning process each day for the upcoming week.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Xiaogang manage exam-related stress and encourage focusing on positive progress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaogang's stress related to exam pressure and parental expectations, recognizing his tendency to catastrophize and develop negative thoughts. Developed strategies focusing on mindfulness, positive reinforcement through reflecting on past successes, and engaging in enjoyable activities like basketball to alleviate stress, while encouraging open communication with his parents for better emotional support. Concluded by reinforcing realistic self-assessment and expression as key tools in managing future challenges.
