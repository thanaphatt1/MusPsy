The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Stefani is a young adolescent who is feeling stressed and upset due to changes in their family dynamics, particularly after the birth of a new baby nephew which has shifted familial attention. Stefani has previously enjoyed positive interactions at school but is currently struggling with anger and behavioral issues.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Stefani enjoys drawing and coloring, which has been identified as a potential stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Stefani's feelings of being overlooked and her subsequent behavioral changes have no noted formal diagnosis at this time but indicate stress-related challenges due to family dynamics.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Stefani is feeling upset and angry due to perceived neglect stemming from family’s focus on the new baby.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Stefani's vulnerable sense of invisibility and feels her reluctance to express emotions verbally is met with openness to cognitive exploration.
[Counseling Assignments:] Stefani should jot down feelings when feeling ignored and use drawing as a relaxation tool to express emotions creatively.
[Session Goal:] Assist Stefani in recognizing and challenging assumptions about familial neglect and enhancing emotional expression through CBT techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Stefani expressed feelings of being upset and overlooked due to her family's focus on her new baby nephew, impacting her behavior at school. The consultant encouraged Stefani to communicate her feelings to her family and suggested expressing how special she felt when receiving more attention, while also recommending drawing as a regular activity for relaxation. A plan was made for Stefani to initiate the conversation with her family during a calm weekend and to incorporate drawing into her weekly routine as a coping mechanism.
