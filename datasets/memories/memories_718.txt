The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zheng is a university student experiencing stress and anxiety, particularly due to issues in a romantic relationship with a junior at their university. Zheng is feeling insecure due to frequent arguments with their partner.

[Personal Characteristics]
Zheng enjoys reading novels, especially historical fiction, and finds it helps them relax during stressful periods.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zheng experiences stress and anxiety largely triggered by relationship challenges, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder reported.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zheng feels upset and anxious due to relationship issues with a junior, which is affecting their interactions with classmates and friends.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zheng's heightened sensitivity and insecurity in their relationship, which impacts their social interactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zheng is encouraged to note specific feelings or thoughts related to their relationship issues to identify patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Assist Zheng in recognizing thought patterns that might be contributing to their anxiety and stress, aiming to improve relationship dynamics through increased self-awareness and communication.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zheng's relationship-related anxiety and its impact on his interactions with others. Zheng agreed to note specific thoughts and feelings to identify triggers and patterns in preparation for discussing concerns with his partner. Encouraged mindful reflection and communication as steps toward resilience and understanding.
