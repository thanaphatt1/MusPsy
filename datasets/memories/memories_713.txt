The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Cui is navigating the emotional turmoil of discovering her husband's long-term affair. She feels shock and frustration as she processes this revelation, which has hugely impacted her sense of reality and trust.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Cui has expressed an interest in reading and gardening, using these activities as potential avenues for relaxation and self-discovery.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Cui is experiencing overwhelming emotions including shock, betrayal, and intense self-blame but no formal diagnosis of a mental disorder has been recorded.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Cui feels overwhelmed by shock and frustration after learning about her husband's affair.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Cui's struggle with intense emotions and the beginning of self-exploration through the suggested journaling.
[Counseling Assignments:] Cui should maintain a journal to document her emotions and triggers, enhancing her awareness regarding the relationship between her thoughts, emotions, and situations.
[Session Goal:] Help Cui start identifying emotions related to her discovery, using CBT strategies to organize thoughts and recognize emotional patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Cui expressed feelings of shock and frustration after discovering her husband's affair, prompting her to question her marriage, self-worth, and future. The consultant suggested journaling to help Cui process and better understand her emotions, identify triggers, and explore the effectiveness of her current coping mechanisms. They also discussed incorporating self-compassion and exploring the concepts of trust and restoration in future sessions to support her emotional healing journey.
