The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mr. Qi is a middle-aged individual navigating significant stress, primarily stemming from his recent divorce and anxiety about his health, despite medical reassurance. Professionally, Mr. Qi faces a demanding workload which exacerbates his anxieties. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Mr. Qi has a supportive circle of friends and enjoys activities such as playing tennis and reading, which serve as potential stress-relief outlets.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mr. Qi experiences anxiety, particularly surrounding health concerns and workplace stress, with no formal diagnosis provided at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mr. Qi feels stressed and overwhelmed by lingering health anxieties and work pressures following his divorce.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Mr. Qi's intense focus on health anxieties but also his openness to engaging in social and leisure activities to manage stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mr. Qi is to maintain a journal documenting thoughts during episodes of anxiety and engage in social activities like tennis to identify patterns and relieve stress.
[Session Goal:] Help Mr. Qi understand the role of thoughts in triggering anxiety using CBT, and encourage participation in activities for emotional relief.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mr. Qi's anxiety stemming from his divorce and work stress, particularly his fear of illness despite being medically cleared. Recommended journaling to identify triggers and patterns of stress and suggested increasing social interactions and revisiting hobbies like tennis to relieve anxiety. Agreed to review his observations and experiences in the next session to better understand and manage his stress and anxiety.
