The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaokun is a primary school student who recently experienced disappointment after not being nominated as a "three-good" student. He places significant importance on meeting his mother's expectations and finds himself worried and anxious about this. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaokun enjoys drawing, which serves as a means of relaxation and self-expression during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaokun shows signs of anxiety and self-criticism, primarily driven by perceived academic failures and family expectations, but there is no formal diagnosis provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaokun feels anxious and disappointed about academic performance and worries about not meeting expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes self-imposed pressure and a high level of self-criticism but notes creatively using drawing as a coping mechanism.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaokun is tasked with writing down recent positive achievements and setting 30 minutes aside each weekend for drawing to help counteract negative thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaokun use CBT techniques to recognize personal accomplishments, manage anxiety through positive self-reflection, and incorporate enjoyable activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
During the session, Xiaokun shared feelings of anxiety and disappointment over not being nominated as a three-good student, expressing concerns about meeting expectations and making his mother proud. The consultant encouraged Xiaokun to recognize positive achievements and engage in drawing as a form of self-care, setting aside time each week to balance stress and utilize mindfulness to manage negative thoughts. They concluded with a commitment to focus on small victories to build confidence and support Xiaokun's emotional well-being.
