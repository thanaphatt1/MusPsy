The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mengmeng is a college student experiencing significant anxiety and stress, particularly around social interactions and feelings of inadequacy compared to peers. Mengmeng values connection and is navigating the challenges of fitting into a new college environment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys sketching, which serves as a means of relaxation and self-expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mengmeng shows signs of social anxiety and self-doubt, triggered by perceived judgments from classmates, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mengmeng feels anxious and isolated due to the pressure of fitting in with peers at college.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Mengmeng's willingness to explore her anxiety and perceptions but notices the strong influence of negative self-talk on her feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mengmeng should note occurrences and feelings tied to moments of judgment at social events, aiming to identify patterns.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Mengmeng to use CBT strategies to understand and challenge negative perceptions in social situations, promoting more realistic self-assessment.
Counseling Summary:
Mengmeng's counseling session focused on navigating social anxiety and feelings of isolation by identifying thought patterns, particularly in social and academic settings, and exploring positive aspects of college life. Mengmeng agreed to engage in small tasks, such as attending a club meeting, to challenge avoidance behavior and foster connections through shared interests, while continuing to document thoughts to balance negative perceptions with positive experiences. Mindfulness and self-reflection were emphasized to aid in managing anxiety and shifting perspectives.
