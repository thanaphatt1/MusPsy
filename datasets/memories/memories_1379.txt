The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a university student dealing with anxiety and intensified hallucinations following the anniversary of his father's death. He values familial connections and experiences fear related to his mental health symptoms.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang has a supportive network that includes family members like his uncle and cousin, and he used to enjoy writing as an expressive outlet.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang experiences anxiety and hallucinations, particularly exacerbated by stress and the anniversary of his father's death. There is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels anxious, upset, and frightened due to the recurrence of hallucinations and fear of losing control.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Yang's withdrawal and hesitance to trust his perceptions but also his openness to exploring thought patterns and seeking relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang should identify and document shifts in his thinking from negative to positive and attempt to reach out to supportive figures, like his uncle or cousin, during moments of overwhelming emotion.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Yang recognize distorted thoughts and strengthen social connections to create a support network for managing anxiety and hallucinations.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yang's feelings of anxiety and inadequacy triggered by the anniversary of his father's passing and intensified by hallucinations, impacting daily life and social interactions. Discussed identifying patterns and cognitive distortions contributing to negative emotions, and encouraged recognizing small victories to shift perspective. Agreed on reaching out to supportive networks and documenting thoughts to gradually enhance emotional resilience and connection.
