Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student struggling to adapt to their new academic environment. This includes handling the academic workload and adjusting to life with new roommates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys playing the guitar, which serves as a relaxation activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences stress and anxiety due to academic and social pressures but has no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels anxious and overwhelmed by academic demands and new social interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Li's uncertainty and anxiety over adapting to new social and academic conditions but appreciates Li's openness to identifying stressors.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to maintain a daily journal, documenting moments of stress and anxiety to discover patterns.
[Session Goal:] Identify specific stress triggers and encourage reflection to help Li manage anxiety using CBT techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's difficulty adjusting to university life, focusing on academic stress and interpersonal challenges. Agreed on using a daily journal to identify and understand anxiety triggers and stress patterns. Encouraged Li to document thoughts and feelings for future sessions to develop stress management strategies.
