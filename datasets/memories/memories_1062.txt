The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoli is a healthcare professional who recently experienced a traumatic event involving a patient’s death during a resuscitation attempt. This event has triggered extreme anxiety and fear, particularly associated with wreaths, manifesting in physical symptoms like dizziness and a racing heart.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoli shares a supportive relationship with her husband and finds solace and relaxation in gardening, enjoying working with flowers which she finds therapeutic.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoli exhibits signs of acute stress and anxiety related to the traumatic event in the hospital, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder presented.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoli feels anxious and distressed due to strong associations between wreaths and a traumatic event involving a patient’s death.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoli’s acute awareness of her anxiety symptoms and her willingness to identify and explore her emotional responses.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoli should document situations where she encounters wreaths and her corresponding feelings to better understand her anxiety triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT principles to increase Xiaoli’s awareness of her trauma-linked reactions and begin reducing the power these have over her emotional state.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoli's anxiety stemming from a traumatic experience involving a patient's death, which has led to a trigger fear of wreaths. Discussed the automatic physical and emotional reactions to this trigger and initiated a task for Xiaoli to identify and document specific situations and emotional responses when encountering wreaths. Encouraged further exploration of associated thoughts and memories to aid in understanding and managing the anxiety.
