The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Marty is a high school student who recently faced suspension after being caught smoking at school. The situation has led to familial tension, especially with movement to his father's barn, causing discomfort. Marty is navigating the shift away from his previous academic achievements and the emotional impact of disappointing his mother.

[Personal Characteristics]
Marty finds solace in playing the guitar, which serves as a way to escape and relax.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Marty is experiencing significant stress and feelings of inadequacy due to recent life changes, though there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Marty feels stressed and burdened by the transition to his father's home and the resulting strain on family relationships.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Marty's struggle with maintaining previous academic standards and adapting to new living conditions but notes his resourcefulness in seeking comfort through music.
[Counseling Assignments:] Marty should identify activities that provide relief and begin reflecting on recurring thoughts, particularly those contributing to stress.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Marty to CBT techniques by focusing on identifying stress triggers and redirecting attention towards positive coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Marty’s stress stemming from being caught smoking, suspension, and subsequent move to his dad's barn, impacting his family relationships and self-view. Marty identified playing the guitar as a source of comfort and agreed to notice and write down recurring thoughts tied to his stress, while observing patterns or triggers. The consultant emphasized strategies to manage anxiety, such as integrating guitar playing for relief and recording thoughts for self-reflection.
