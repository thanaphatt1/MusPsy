The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yaya is a university student experiencing stress related to academic performance and adapting to online learning. Yaya is worried about falling behind and disappointing teachers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys drawing as it serves as a calming activity amidst academic stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yaya feels anxious, particularly triggered by school subjects such as math, but has not been diagnosed with any formal mental health condition.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yaya feels worried and stressed due to slipping grades, fearing falling behind peers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Yaya's proactive approach to addressing her academic worries and her openness to experimenting with new stress-relief techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yaya should break assignments into smaller tasks to make them manageable and schedule regular drawing sessions.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to foster stress management by leveraging structured learning, creative activities, and collaboration.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yaya's academic anxiety due to difficulties with online learning and fear of falling behind. Agreed on breaking tasks into manageable steps, incorporating relaxation through drawing, and forming a study group for math while considering reaching out to teachers for support. Yaya aims to feel more confident and reduce stress by implementing these strategies.
