The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a school-aged individual facing bullying and harassment from peers, which significantly affects their mood and self-esteem.

[Personal Characteristics]
They enjoy drawing and sometimes utilize it to alleviate stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences stress and low self-esteem triggered by bullying incidents, without indication of a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels isolated and stressed due to ongoing bullying incidents at school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's feelings of being trapped and a significant decline in self-confidence but also sees potential in shifting their focus to positive activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to document bullying incidents and their emotional responses, and incorporate drawing into their daily routine for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to manage stress, bolster self-esteem through positive self-affirmations, and leverage artistic activities for emotional relief.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling session, the consultant addressed the user's distress over bullying at school, suggesting she document these incidents to identify patterns and lessen their emotional impact. The user was encouraged to incorporate drawing into her routine for stress management and introduced to positive self-talk with daily affirmations to boost confidence and resilience. The session concluded with a focus on gradual progress and continued exploration in future discussions.
