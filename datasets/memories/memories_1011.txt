The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaozhang is a university student experiencing considerable stress and anxiety, particularly related to public speaking and peer perceptions after an incident at a student representative conference. Xiaozhang values clear communication and worries about judgment from classmates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing the piano and finds it to be a relaxing activity that provides mental relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaozhang experiences anxiety that is heightened by fears of criticism and embarrassment in public settings. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaozhang feels stressed and anxious, particularly around peers after the conference incident.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaozhang's struggle with negative self-perception and the weight of peer judgment, yet observes Xiaozhang's willingness to engage in new anxiety management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaozhang is advised to practice deep breathing and note down recurring negative thoughts to recognize patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiaozhang manage anxiety, challenge negative thoughts, and encourage engagement in calming activities like piano practice.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaozhang's anxiety stemming from a challenging experience at a student conference, particularly related to public speaking fears and judgment from peers. Agreed on strategies to manage anxiety, including practicing deep breathing and challenging negative thoughts, while also reflecting on positive experiences to promote self-awareness and confidence. Encouraged Xiaozhang to keep a journal for self-reflection and to implement these techniques consistently for progress.
