The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student feeling lost and overwhelmed at a new school due to academic pressures and language barriers. He attended this school after not performing well on entrance exams, which adds to his stress. He worries about his future and feels out of place among well-off classmates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu finds comfort and a sense of escape in drawing, which he considers therapeutic, despite feeling guilty about not spending more time studying.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu exhibits signs of anxiety related to academic challenges and intense self-doubt. However, there is no formal diagnosis mentioned regarding a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels anxious and isolated at school, struggling with confidence and future uncertainties.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Liu's struggle with adaptation but recognizes his openness to explore small positive moments.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu is encouraged to reflect on daily positive moments and to integrate drawing regularly as a form of stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Help Liu identify and appreciate small positives to gradually shift his mindset and integrate drawing for relaxation.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's feelings of being overwhelmed and isolated due to a new school environment and language barriers. Agreed on strategies to balance academic responsibilities with personal interests, like art, to foster a sense of comfort and reduce anxiety. Encouraged mindfulness and family involvement to support Liu's journey towards finding balance and positive moments in daily life.
