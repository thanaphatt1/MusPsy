The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a college student experiencing significant anxiety and stress, primarily driven by adjusting to new social environments and academic pressures. They have expressed difficulty in forming friendships and maintaining focus due to feeling overwhelmed.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They have rediscovered an interest in reading, particularly mystery novels, which serve as an effective stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user shows signs of anxiety and stress related to academic and social pressures without formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious due to difficulty in adjusting to college life and forming new friendships.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's high self-expectations and pressure from perceived social judgments, noting their openness to exploring new anxiety management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to note moments of feeling overwhelmed and the thoughts accompanying them for pattern identification.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help the user manage anxiety by identifying stress-related thoughts and promoting self-awareness in social settings.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's social anxiety related to adjusting to college life and the impact on self-esteem. Discussed identifying patterns of overwhelming thoughts and challenged negative self-beliefs by focusing on positive traits like listening and helping others. Agreed on practicing authenticity and concentrating on strengths in social situations, while continuing to record experiences for further analysis.
