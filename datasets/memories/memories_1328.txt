The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyun is a recent university graduate experiencing anxiety regarding job hunting and transitioning into the workforce. He is concerned about being deceived in the job market, stemming from trust issues developed due to challenging family dynamics during childhood.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoyun enjoys reading, particularly fiction novels, as a way to escape reality and relieve stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyun exhibits anxiety primarily associated with fears of being exploited or misled in professional settings, without any formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyun feels anxious about approaching graduation and the subsequent job search, fearing deception and exploitation in the workforce.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes Xiaoyun's pervasive distrust and anxiety related to job hunting but recognizes his openness to developing strategies for managing these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyun should write down his thoughts during stressful moments to confront and rationalize them and engage in reading as a relaxing activity.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoyun utilize CBT techniques to manage job search anxiety, identify distrust patterns, and integrate enjoyable activities into his routine for relaxation.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoyun's anxiety about entering the job market, rooted in trust issues from past experiences, and identified fears of being misled or exploited. Discussed actionable steps for reducing anxiety, including thorough research of potential employers, leveraging professional networks, and using review platforms like Glassdoor and LinkedIn; suggested writing down feelings and taking breaks as coping strategies. Xiaoyun agreed to implement these strategies and integrate enjoyable activities like reading to manage anxiety.
