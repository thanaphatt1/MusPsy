The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wen is a student who recently faced difficulties in academic performance, specifically doing poorly on finals and receiving criticism from a teacher, which has led to feelings of embarrassment and helplessness. Wen is close to their grandmother, who provides significant emotional support.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wen enjoys drawing, finding it a relaxing and enjoyable activity, and they are interested in integrating this hobby more regularly into their routine.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wen is experiencing negative self-talk and feelings of worthlessness related to academic performance, but there is no formal mental health disorder diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wen feels embarrassed and helpless due to poor exam performance and fear of disappointing important figures like their grandmother.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wen's tendency toward harsh self-criticism, heightened by external criticism, but also notes a potential openness to altering this narrative through supportive influences.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wen should practice drawing after dinner to focus on positive experiences and gradually challenge negative self-perceptions.
[Session Goal:] Help Wen recognize and question negative self-assessments, encouraging consistent self-compassion and engagement with supportive activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wen's feelings of embarrassment and helplessness following poor exam performance and public criticism, and discussed his tendency towards self-criticism. Suggested sharing these feelings with a trusted family member for support and encouraged incorporating a daily drawing routine to challenge negative thoughts and reinforce positive affirmations. Agreed on setting aside time each day for drawing to help cultivate a more positive self-perspective.
