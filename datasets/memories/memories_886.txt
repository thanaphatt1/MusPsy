The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student dealing with social discomfort and perceived judgment from peers, stemming from an incident during a class activity. Wang values harmonious relationships within the class and desires to understand and improve these interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys gardening, which provides a sense of peace and personal accomplishment.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang demonstrates anxiety related to social interactions and misunderstandings within a group setting. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels frustrated and misunderstood following a class incident where they were criticized, leading to feelings of awkwardness and lack of motivation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Wang's struggle with social dynamics and identifies their openness to exploring ways to resolve the discomfort with peers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to note moments of stress related to the class activity to better understand their emotional triggers.
[Session Goal:] Assist Wang in identifying their emotional responses and exploring methods to re-engage with class activities and peers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's feelings of frustration and misunderstanding following criticism in a class activity, with emphasis on avoiding assumptions and engaging in open communication. Supported Wang to identify automatic negative thoughts and consider discussions with class leaders to resolve tension, facilitating increased self-awareness and relationship-building. Encouraged engaging in activities like gardening for stress relief, enhancing emotional resilience, and fostering Wang's ability to manage similar situations with confidence in the future.
