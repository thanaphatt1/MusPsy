The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yi Yi is a high school student experiencing significant stress related to academic performance and pressure from parents and teachers. Yi Yi recently received disappointing exam results, which has intensified feelings of guilt and loneliness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yi Yi enjoys drawing, which helps express feelings and provides a calming effect when stressed.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yi Yi experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic expectations and feelings of inadequacy, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yi Yi feels anxious, lonely, and guilty after not meeting parental expectations with recent exam results.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yi Yi’s difficulty in recognizing small achievements and self-worth but notes an interest in self-discovery and stress management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yi Yi should begin documenting small accomplishments and positive moments daily to help build a more balanced self-view.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to aid Yi Yi in recognizing achievements alongside challenges and encourage communication about interests and stress with parents.
Counseling Summary:
Yi Yi expressed feelings of pressure and inadequacy due to not meeting parental expectations and recent poor exam results, which led to anxiety and hopelessness. The consultant encouraged Yi Yi to focus on small positive achievements, like helping a friend and receiving compliments, as a way to shift focus from negatives, and proposed integrating art, an enjoyable activity, into Yi Yi's routine for stress relief. They discussed strategies for better time management and open communication with parents about stress and hobbies, with Yi Yi agreeing to try these suggestions to improve emotional well-being and support resilience.
