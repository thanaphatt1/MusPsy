The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaojun is a student who struggles with temper issues, particularly during sports events at school. Recent incidents, like chasing a peer during soccer, have amplified his feelings of embarrassment and stress over being labeled a troublemaker. He desires to be perceived as a good friend and student.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaojun enjoys reading comic books, which he uses to relax and find solace from daily stresses.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaojun has not been formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder, yet he experiences challenges in managing emotions, particularly anger triggered by social interactions.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaojun feels upset and frustrated, particularly after an incident at school where he responded impulsively out of anger.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaojun's willingness to explore the root of his emotional reactions and sees potential in his openness to understand and articulate his feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaojun should write down instances when he feels strong emotions to identify patterns and triggers, aiming to gain better insight into his emotional responses.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiaojun recognize emotional triggers, develop greater self-awareness, and explore healthier expression of feelings.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaojun's difficulty with anger management and its impact on his social relationships, with an emphasis on understanding and coping with his emotional triggers. Encouraged Xiaojun to write down moments when these feelings arise to help identify triggers and patterns, and discussed the value of talking to friends and family for support. Agreed to review his writings in the next session to further address and manage his responses.
