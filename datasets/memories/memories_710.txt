The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xia is a university student who recently moved into dorms for the first time, experiencing stress due to new living conditions with roommates. Xia grew up with her father and sister, values her personal space, and is adjusting to the noise and lack of privacy in the dorm environment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xia enjoys walking in the park as it helps clear her mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xia is experiencing difficulty focusing due to environmental stress from dorm life but has not been diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xia feels overwhelmed and stressed due to the noise and lack of privacy in her new dorm setting.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Xia's struggle with adjusting to a shared living environment while noting Xia's willingness to explore solutions to manage stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xia should keep a journal of moments of stress in the dorm to identify problematic scenarios and include thoughts experienced during those moments.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Xia recognize and document stress triggers, aiming to improve her adjustment to dorm life through awareness and reflection.
Counseling Summary:
In this counseling session, Xia expressed feeling overwhelmed and stressed due to the noise and lack of privacy in her new dorm environment. The consultant suggested Xia keep a journal to track both stressful moments and positive experiences to gain insights into her feelings and strengths. The goal is to help Xia identify patterns in her thoughts and emotions, providing a clearer understanding of her situation and fostering resilience.
