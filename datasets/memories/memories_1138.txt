The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Li values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They also enjoy painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li recognized a pattern of self-criticism and negative assumptions, especially concerning academic performance and relationships, feeling heavily burdened by these thoughts.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li's awareness of their tendency to magnify negative scenarios and the willingness to observe and challenge these assumptions, showing openness to cognitive reframing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should actively challenge negative assumptions by seeking evidence and considering alternative explanations while finding a routine to incorporate art for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Facilitate Li's ability to identify and refute unrealistic thoughts to reduce stress, and encourage engagement in art as a method of self-expression and stress relief.

#
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's self-judgment and negative assumptions in relationships and studies, identifying unrealistic thought patterns. Agreed to challenge these thoughts, set small, manageable goals for coursework, and incorporate art as a stress-relieving activity. Planned to monitor progress and continue developing strategies for stress management.
