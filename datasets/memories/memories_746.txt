The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a university student who has recently faced academic challenges, including failing some courses, which has contributed to increased stress and self-doubt. Zhao is determined to succeed academically but fears disappointing their parents and themselves.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhao values learning and previously found enjoyment in reading novels as a relaxing pastime.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao experiences anxiety and stress primarily related to academic performance, characterized by self-imposed high standards and fear of failure.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels overwhelmed and discouraged due to academic setbacks and the pressure to meet personal and familial expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes Zhao's internal struggle with self-doubt and the perceived inability to meet academic standards but also notes Zhao's potential openness to seeking support and resources.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao is encouraged to identify potential support systems, such as reaching out to a professor for guidance and considering forming a study group with peers.
[Session Goal:] Assist Zhao in managing academic stress by exploring cognitive-behavioral strategies for support and structuring study routines effectively.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhao's academic stress and feelings of being overwhelmed due to struggles with university workload. Agreed on reaching out to a supportive professor and classmates for academic support, while also planning a study group. Encouraged Zhao to incorporate structured study routines and relaxation activities, like reading, for stress management.
