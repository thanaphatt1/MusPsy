The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Pengpeng is a young child who has recently started attending a new kindergarten. They feel nervous and stressed due to feeling like an outsider as others in the class already know each other.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Pengpeng enjoys drawing, which helps them feel happy and calm.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Pengpeng experiences anxiety and stress related to social integration in the new environment but has not been formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Pengpeng feels scared and anxious about being the "odd one out" in their new kindergarten class.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Pengpeng's fear of exclusion and the underlying desire to be included within their peer group, along with an open-mindedness towards proactive strategies for social engagement.
[Counseling Assignments:] Pengpeng should notice and reflect on any positive interactions or friendly gestures from their classmates over the upcoming week.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to assist Pengpeng in recognizing and overcoming anxious thoughts about social acceptance and encourage small steps toward social interaction.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling sessions with Pengpeng focused on addressing his anxiety about fitting into a new kindergarten class and making friends. Through the sessions, Pengpeng explored his feelings and thoughts, recognized positive interactions with peers, and identified strategies to manage his anxiety, such as engaging in drawing and initiating social interactions. By the end of the sessions, Pengpeng felt more confident in approaching classmates and using drawing as a coping mechanism.
