The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiayang is a university student who recently experienced disappointment due to not entering the university's English experimental class, coupled with anxiety over English pronunciation issues. This has affected his social interactions, as he feels teased by classmates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiayang enjoys playing basketball, which he previously found to be a positive outlet.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiayang experiences anxiety and embarrassment, primarily stemming from perceived social judgment and self-doubt concerning English proficiency, without specifying a diagnosed mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiayang feels down and anxious about his pronunciation issues and social interactions, leading to embarrassment and insecurity.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiayang’s tendency to avoid speaking due to self-doubt, as well as his willingness to explore these thoughts and beliefs.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiayang should note recurring negative thoughts related to pronunciation to disentangle factual concerns from unfounded beliefs.
[Session Goal:] Introduce positive self-talk and affirmations to build self-esteem and challenge negative thought patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiayang's self-doubt and social anxiety due to language challenges and peer teasing, affecting interactions. Agreed on tracking thoughts and using positive affirmations to build self-worth and challenge negative self-talk. This approach aims to cultivate self-compassion and improve Xiayang's confidence and social engagement.
