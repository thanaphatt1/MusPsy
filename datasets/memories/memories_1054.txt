The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student navigating the challenges of transitioning from a structured high school environment to a more autonomous college setting, which includes difficulties with study methods and the pressure to manage time effectively. Wang values academic success and desires to meet personal and institutional expectations while feeling anxious about falling behind.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys playing the piano, which has been a pastime since high school and serves as a potential stress relief when incorporated into his routine.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety linked to academic pressures, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder at this stage.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels overwhelmed and anxious due to the new demands of university life and a lack of structured study approaches.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Wang is struggling with the transition to self-directed learning and shows willingness to explore new strategies for managing academic demands.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should reflect on specific study strategies, potentially reintroducing a structured approach like a planner or timetable to regain control over his studies.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to aid Wang in managing academic stress, adapting effective study techniques, and fostering greater self-confidence in his academic abilities.
Counseling Summary:
Wang shared difficulties in adapting to university life, particularly with self-study and managing time, leading to feelings of anxiety and frustration. The consultant recommended reflecting on effective study strategies and implementing structure with planners to regain control, while suggesting Wang seek peer support and possibly join study groups. The dialogue concluded with encouragement for Wang to take small steps towards building a support system and developing a balanced study rhythm.
