The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang Mou is an office worker dealing with increased stress due to staff shortages in their department, resulting in more communication-heavy tasks which they find challenging. Wang Mou generally prefers working independently but is now required to depend more on colleagues, adding tension and stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang Mou enjoys reading, which they have not been able to indulge in recently due to work pressures, but plans to reintegrate this activity into their routine for relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang Mou experiences anxiety and frustration primarily from increased workload and communication demands; there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang Mou feels overwhelmed and anxious due to increased workload and communication demands.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notices Wang Mou's preference for independent work and the stress induced by increased reliance on colleagues, as well as the potential positive impact of engaging in hobbies like reading.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang Mou should note specific situations that increase feelings of anxiety and frustration to identify patterns, and incorporate reading for relaxation, dedicating 15 minutes each day to this activity.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Wang Mou manage stress by recognizing situations that trigger anxiety and foster a routine that includes relaxation activities such as reading.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang Mou's work-related anxiety due to increased communication demands and how it impacts his emotional well-being. Agreed on tracking specific anxiety-inducing situations and creating a flexible schedule to include leisure activities, starting with reading for 15 minutes daily. Aimed to enhance coping strategies and emotional resilience for improved stress management.
