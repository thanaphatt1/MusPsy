The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He also enjoys painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels stressed and overwhelmed due to coworkers learning about his divorce, leading to embarrassment and difficulties concentrating.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liu's heightened anxiety regarding perceptions of failure and his sensitivity to colleagues he perceives as judgmental.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu is to keep track of daily thoughts and feelings, focusing on identifying negative thinking patterns stemming from his divorce.
[Session Goal:] Help Liu use CBT to recognize negative thought patterns and develop strategies to manage perceptions of judgment and maintain wellbeing.
Counseling Summary:
Liu expressed stress due to his colleagues' awareness of his divorce, leading to anxiety and sleep disturbances. The consultant proposed tracking Liu's thoughts and feelings to address negative thinking patterns and encouraged journaling to identify triggers, aiming to challenge assumptions and develop healthier perspectives. Liu was receptive to this approach, looking forward to gaining a deeper understanding of himself and achieving better work-life balance.
