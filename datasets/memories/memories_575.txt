The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lin is a student who recently faced an emotional setback due to delaying graduation. This change has led to feelings of embarrassment and isolation from peers. Lin is struggling with uncertainty in life plans and a deep sense of stress, feeling left behind while friends move forward.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lin used to actively participate in a photography club and finds joy in taking photos, which serves as a creative outlet. Lin has a close relationship with their sister, who is supportive.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lin experiences stress, self-criticism, and loneliness stemming from academic setbacks. There is no mention of a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lin feels stressed, alone, and overwhelmed, perceiving life plans as uncertain due to the graduation delay.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Lin's feelings of embarrassment and self-isolation, noting Lin's openness to setting goals to reconnect with others.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lin is encouraged to set a small goal to reconnect with peers or engage in a joyful activity, such as considering rejoining the photography club.
[Session Goal:] Identify Lin's stress sources through CBT and explore strategies to increase social connections and personal joy.
Counseling Summary:
In this counseling session, Lin discussed feelings of loneliness and self-criticism following a delayed graduation, leading to social isolation. The counselor suggested setting small goals to re-engage with social activities, such as rejoining a university club, and encouraged Lin to reflect on positive achievements to counter negative self-talk. They agreed on starting with low-effort interactions, alongside monitoring thoughts, to gradually build confidence in reconnecting with a community.
