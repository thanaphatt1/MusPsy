[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes a cycle of stress-prompted absenteeism from work and the user’s struggle with self-confidence.

Aimed to reduce anxiety by building on the relaxation experienced at home, reinforcing the need for mindfulness and consistency in managing stress.
