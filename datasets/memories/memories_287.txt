The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a student struggling with math-related stress following negative experiences in class. He feels pressured to maintain good academic performance to meet parental expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys playing basketball and engaging with friends, which he finds stress-relieving and enjoyable.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences stress and anxiety linked to his educational performance, exacerbated by critical classroom experiences. There is no formal diagnosis yet mentioned of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels frustrated and overwhelmed by math due to previous critical encounters and mounting pressure to perform well.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wang's sensitivity to external criticism and the internal pressure to meet high expectations, as well as his openness to exploring alternative learning strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should record his feelings and thoughts during math classes, focusing on automatic negative thoughts, and identify alternative strategies to learn.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Wang recognize and challenge negative thoughts, increase self-efficacy in managing academic stress, and improve his approach to learning math.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's frustration with math due to perceived unfair comments from a teacher, which led to avoidance and negative automatic thoughts. Facilitated Wang's discovery of alternative learning resources like video tutorials, fostering increased confidence and encouraging positive engagement. Suggested incorporating joyful activities, such as basketball, to help manage stress, and reinforced the importance of challenging negative thoughts and seeking support, setting a foundation for ongoing progress.
