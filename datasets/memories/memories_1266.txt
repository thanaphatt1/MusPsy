The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lin Xiang is a high school student who recently witnessed distressing events at a market involving animals, leading to increased agitation and mistrust. He lives with his father, stepmother, and stepbrother, feeling like an outsider within his family dynamic.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lin Xiang enjoys reading, finding it a soothing escape from his worries, and is passionate about stories that offer different perspectives on life.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lin Xiang exhibits signs of anxiety and emotional distress stemming from recent experiences but has not been diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lin Xiang feels uneasy and restless, having difficulty trusting others after witnessing mistreatment at the market.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Lin Xiang's heightened emotional response and mistrust, compounded by feeling marginalized at home and in social settings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lin Xiang is encouraged to schedule daily reading sessions for relaxation and to jot down overwhelming thoughts to understand their roots better.
[Session Goal:] Help Lin Xiang establish a sense of balance through structured activities, using CBT strategies to explore and challenge overwhelming thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Lin Xiang's emotional distress following a disturbing market experience and its impact on relationships and trust. Suggested establishing a routine with reading for emotional relief and encouraged journaling for self-reflection. Lin Xiang feels more hopeful about gradual progress and acknowledges the journey towards understanding and managing emotions.
