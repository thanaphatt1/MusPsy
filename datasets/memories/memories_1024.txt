The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a college student from a rural village, stressed by academic pressures especially after failing two courses, and worried about letting down his family’s high expectations. He is the first in his village to attend college, which heightens the pressure. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys reading as a means to relax and disconnect from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao experiences anxiety and stress tied to academic performance and family expectations but has not mentioned a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels overwhelmed and anxious due to academic pressures and expectations, impacting his sleep and focus.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhao's heightened self-pressure due to familial expectations but acknowledges Zhao's willingness to address anxiety through noticing thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao should jot down anxious thoughts as they arise to identify patterns related to stress.
[Session Goal:] Educate Zhao on how thoughts influence emotions and behaviors, introduce basic CBT concepts, and explore relaxation and sleep strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhao's stress and anxiety related to familial expectations and academic failures, impacting sleep and daily functioning. Agreed to an introspective task of recording anxious thoughts to identify patterns and address them, with a future focus on improving sleep through relaxation techniques. Encouraged Zhao's resilience and dedication to managing stress with clear guidance and support.
