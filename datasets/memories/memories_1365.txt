The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhen is a woman dealing with profound grief following the passing of her son. She struggles with maintaining her marriage as her husband and she drift apart due to their loss.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She used to find peace in gardening and is considering reintroducing it as a source of relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhen experiences profound grief and overwhelming feelings, impacting her emotional state and relationship dynamics, but a formal mental health diagnosis is not mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhen feels deeply hurt and overwhelmed by the recent loss, struggling with her grief and misunderstanding within her marriage.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Zhen’s struggle with grief and the weight it places on her marital dynamics but appreciates her willingness to explore thoughts and delineate the effects of loss on her emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhen should write down her feelings and any positive memories or interactions, aiming to gain perspective on her emotional state.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Zhen explore her grief-related thoughts and cultivate tools for self-awareness and reflection to improve emotional understanding and communication within her marriage.
Counseling Summary:
Zhen is experiencing profound grief and relational strain following her son's passing and feels misunderstood by her husband. She has begun journaling to reflect on her thoughts and recognize self-critical patterns and is encouraged to focus on small positive moments and reintroduce calming activities like gardening to aid in her healing process. Zhen is gradually finding direction and plans to continue these practices to nurture both her personal grief and her relationship.
