The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaowen is a student dealing with considerable emotional distress following a recent exam failure and subsequent criticism from a teacher. This has intensified their existing low self-esteem issues and led to feelings of worthlessness and anxiety.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaowen enjoys sketching and reading novels, both of which provide temporary escapism and mood elevation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaowen experiences episodes of severe anxiety and self-doubt triggered by academic performance and high expectations from themselves and others. No formal diagnosis has been reported.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaowen feels overwhelmed by self-worth doubts and expresses fear about their future after academic criticism.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist sees Xiaowen's deep impact from external criticism and the challenge in maintaining self-esteem, yet notes Xiaowen's willingness to explore thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaowen should note instances of negative thought intrusions during the week and start reflecting on personal values versus external expectations.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaowen recognize and challenge negative self-perceptions using CBT and align personal values with self-expectations.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaowen discussed feeling overwhelmed by criticism and struggling with self-esteem, which led to anxiety and negative thoughts. The consultant encouraged Xiaowen to track these negative moments and explore personal values to reduce external pressures, while also suggesting reaching out to a trusted friend for support. They agreed that acknowledging small successes and reflections could build a stronger self-narrative and lessen emotional burdens.
