The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
A recently transferred to a new company and is a professional facing conflicts with higher management, namely a general manager and two deputy managers. A's recent promotion brought excitement which is now overshadowed by the self-doubt fueled by conflict at the workplace.

[Personal Characteristics]  
A enjoys spending quality time with their son, engaging in activities like playing soccer and reading, which serves as an emotional relief from work stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
A struggles with self-doubt and stress, amplified by workplace conflicts and the pressure to perform well in their new role. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] A feels overwhelmed and doubts their capabilities in light of managerial conflicts.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices A's potential for strong leadership, hampered by self-imposed doubts and external resistance to change.
[Counseling Assignments:] A should reflect on past successful moments and bring those examples to future sessions to identify strengths.
[Session Goal:] Assist A in identifying strengths and building confidence amid managerial challenges through cognitive behavioral techniques.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling sessions focused on addressing the user's workplace conflicts and self-doubt following a company transfer, highlighting the impact on their confidence. Discussions led to the user recognizing negative thought patterns and exploring open communication with colleagues as a means to enhance understanding and collaboration. Additionally, the user identified personal activities, such as spending time with their son, as effective stress relievers and committed to maintaining work-life balance by dedicating regular time to family activities.
