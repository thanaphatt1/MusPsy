The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background

[Personal Profile]  
Feng is a high school student experiencing tension and conflict leading to distress after lending a biology answer book to a classmate who later ignored his attempts to retrieve it.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Feng enjoys reading comic books, finds them relaxing, and is considering using music to help unwind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Feng displays signs of anxiety and frustration due to interpersonal issues, impacting concentration and enthusiasm for favorite activities.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Feng feels misunderstood, anxious, and frustrated, particularly after the conflict with his classmate over the borrowed book.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Feng's sensitivity to interpersonal interactions and eagerness to explore emotional understanding and coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Feng is encouraged to jot down feelings and associated thoughts daily and listen to music before bed for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Help Feng identify stress patterns, explore relaxation techniques, and initiate self-awareness practices.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Feng's feelings of anxiety and frustration stemming from a conflict with a classmate, which has been impacting his studies and general mood. Agreed on practicing emotional awareness by documenting feelings and thoughts, and trying relaxation techniques like listening to music before bedtime to manage anxiety. Discussed the importance of developing assertive communication skills for setting boundaries in interpersonal situations, with a plan for Feng to brainstorm ideas for future interactions.
