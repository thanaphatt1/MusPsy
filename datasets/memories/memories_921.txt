The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wen is a university student who experiences anxiety and self-doubt, particularly pertaining to social situations and fitting in with peers. She often feels inadequate and carries the weight of these feelings in her daily interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wen enjoys listening to music and journaling, both of which are calming activities that help her organize her thoughts. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
While Wen frequently struggles with anxiety and social apprehension, there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wen feels anxious and self-critical, questioning her ability to fit in with others.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wen's tendency to assume negative perspectives and highlights her awareness of these patterns as a positive first step toward change.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wen is tasked with jotting down moments when anxious thoughts are triggered to help uncover specific patterns.
[Session Goal:] To help Wen identify and challenge negative thoughts through CBT techniques, ultimately fostering self-compassion.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wen's feelings of inadequacy and self-doubt, acknowledging how they heighten anxiety in social interactions. Agreed to identify thought patterns and triggers, working towards self-compassion and an evening routine of music and journaling for emotional balance. Set a small social task of initiating conversation with a classmate to practice new skills and build confidence.
