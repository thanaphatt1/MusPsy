The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a recent graduate preparing to start a teaching career, experiencing significant anxiety related to this new life phase. Zhang often doubts his capabilities and fears that emotional struggles might impact his job performance. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds relaxation in gardening, which connects to fond memories with his grandparents and serves as a stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety largely tied to career uncertainties and a tendency towards self-doubt but has not reported a formal diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang is anxious about entering the teaching profession, uncertain about his ability to handle responsibilities effectively.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's deep-seated self-doubt concerning new challenges yet recognizes his willingness to explore these feelings for better emotional management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is tasked with identifying situations that trigger strong emotional responses, coupled with reflecting on past effective emotion management strategies.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Zhang to CBT techniques to manage anxiety, focusing on identifying emotional triggers and developing effective self-support strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang expressed anxiety about transitioning from graduation to a teaching career, struggling with self-doubts and emotional management. Throughout the sessions, they worked on identifying and challenging negative thoughts, drawing on past positive experiences, and setting personal goals to bolster confidence. Additionally, engaging in activities like gardening was introduced to alleviate stress, helping Zhang develop a toolkit for managing emotions and fostering a positive outlook on their capabilities.
