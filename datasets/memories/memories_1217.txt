The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
F is a university student currently feeling overwhelmed with challenging classes and responsibilities as a class representative, which are impacting study time and rest. F holds high standards for academic performance to avoid letting people down.

[Personal Characteristics]  
F enjoys playing basketball as a means of stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
F experiences anxiety and stress primarily due to academic and social pressures, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] F feels anxious and under pressure due to the challenging balance of academic and representative duties.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices F’s struggle with high expectations and the fear of disappointing others, yet sees F’s willingness to incorporate new stress management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] F should practice deep breathing exercises routinely and prioritize study tasks with a structured schedule, while including basketball sessions for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Assist F in managing stress using CBT techniques, focusing on task prioritization and incorporating activities that alleviate stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored F's academic and social pressures contributing to anxiety and stress, marked by negative thought patterns and physical symptoms. Developed a plan to implement deep breathing exercises for stress management, prioritize study tasks through effective scheduling, and incorporate regular basketball sessions to balance responsibilities and provide mental relief. Encouraged F to use positive affirmations to counteract negative thinking and maintain a positive mindset.
