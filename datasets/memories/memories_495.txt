The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lihua is a professional facing overwhelming stress due to a complex project at work, with deadlines looming and a high pressure to perform. Lihua has sacrificed hobbies like reading and yoga, feeling low on time and energy.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys reading and practicing yoga, activities that previously served as stress relievers.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lihua experiences significant stress and anxiety related to work pressure, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lihua feels overwhelmed and stressed by project demands and the impending deadline, leading to a neglect of personal joy and relaxation activities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Lihua's high levels of stress linked to performance pressures and observes a willingness to explore methods for reintegrating relaxation into daily life.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lihua is encouraged to engage in yoga for at least 15 minutes a day to help reduce stress and slowly reintegrate it as part of her routine.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to manage work-related stress, encourage engagement in relaxation activities, and foster a healthier work-life balance.
Counseling Summary:
Lihua faced overwhelming stress due to a demanding project at work and struggled to maintain personal activities that help her relax. Throughout the counseling sessions, she learned to identify negative thought patterns and practiced reframing them, using compassionate self-talk as she'd offer a friend, and reintegrated yoga into her routine to manage stress. By preparing to communicate her needs and planning a balanced routine, Lihua gradually built confidence and resilience to tackle future challenges independently.
