The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Bernadette is a business owner managing stress related to her work partnership and recent events where her colleague made unilateral decisions. She values collaboration and control in her business environment and wants to maintain trust and effective partnership communication.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Bernadette enjoys gardening, which acts as a soothing activity and a space where she feels a sense of control and peace.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Bernadette experiences anxiety primarily triggered by perceived loss of control at work and partnership dynamics, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Bernadette feels stressed and anxious after her partner made unilateral decisions, causing her to worry about her role and influence.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Bernadette's need for control and recognition in her partnership and notes her stress from feeling sidelined.
[Counseling Assignments:] Bernadette is encouraged to track moments of anxiety to identify specific triggers and reflect on positive past interpersonal experiences.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT strategies to help Bernadette recognize and manage anxiety triggers and enhance positive personal perspectives.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Bernadette’s stress over her business partner Daisy’s unilateral decisions, impacting her control and increasing anxiety. Agreed to track anxiety triggers for better understanding and document moments of intense feelings, aiming to improve partnership dynamics through balanced communication. Planned to discuss communication strategies with Daisy in the next session to address these issues proactively.
