The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student who initially finds the academic transition challenging. Li's self-confidence has been shaken by recent midterm exam results, prompting doubts about academic abilities and causing feelings of loneliness. Li struggles with adapting to new study habits and making friends due to different accents and cultural adjustments.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys reading novels and hiking, activities that used to offer relaxation and joy.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences self-doubt and stress due to academic pressures, alongside a sense of loneliness. While not formally diagnosed, these feelings bear similarity to adjustment and anxiety issues.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels lost and unsure, particularly after facing unexpected academic challenges, leading to self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li's openness in acknowledging the loneliness and self-doubt, suggesting a readiness to explore these feelings further.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should note moments of self-doubt or loneliness to uncover recurring themes that may contribute to these feelings.
[Session Goal:] Help Li identify and challenge critical self-thoughts, fostering a balanced view of personal abilities.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session with Li focused on addressing feelings of self-doubt and disconnection due to challenges in adapting to university life. The counselor encouraged Li to identify triggers of these emotions, shift focus towards more balanced self-perspectives, and document critical thoughts along with constructive alternatives. This approach aims to foster self-compassion and clarity, with ongoing support from the counselor.
