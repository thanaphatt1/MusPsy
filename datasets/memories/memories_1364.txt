The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ms. Wu is a professional woman who recently changed jobs, now working at a new company. During her transition, she encountered an uncomfortable situation with her male supervisor, who exhibited overly attentive behavior, causing her significant distress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ms. Wu values professional respect and has a supportive network of friends and family who provide comfort outside of her work environment.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ms. Wu experiences anxiety and discomfort primarily linked to her supervisor's behavior at work, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ms. Wu feels anxious and uneasy due to her male supervisor's unsolicited attention at her new job.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Ms. Wu’s discomfort in the work environment but observes her willingness to explore strategies to address her feelings and situation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ms. Wu is tasked with keeping a journal to document her feelings and any patterns related to her anxiety, particularly around interactions with her supervisor.
[Session Goal:] To use CBT techniques to help Ms. Wu recognize and manage anxiety stemming from workplace interactions and to explore her thoughts' accuracy, particularly self-blame.
Counseling Summary:
Ms. Wu expressed stress and anxiety due to inappropriate behavior from her male supervisor at her new job, which is impacting her emotions and ability to focus both at work and outside. The consultant acknowledged the challenge and suggested keeping a journal to identify patterns in Ms. Wu's feelings, particularly recurring self-blaming thoughts and concerns about reporting him. The consultant tasked Ms. Wu with documenting these thoughts and considering more balanced alternatives to help alleviate her anxiety.
