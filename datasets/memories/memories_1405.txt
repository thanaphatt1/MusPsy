The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
M is dealing with anxiety, often experiencing stress and panic attacks when faced with social situations, particularly involving classmates from their computer training course.

[Personal Characteristics]  
M enjoys running, which serves as a stress-relief activity, and is used to help manage anxiety.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
M experiences social anxiety, marked by avoidance of social situations and automatic negative thoughts about others' perceptions, though no formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] M feels anxious and overwhelmed, particularly in social scenarios with classmates, and experiences panic attacks in triggering situations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes M's tendency to avoid social situations and their belief in negative judgments from others, alongside a willingness to explore and understand their anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] M is tasked with jotting down instances when social anxiety arises to increase awareness and identify specific triggers.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT techniques to understand and manage social anxiety by identifying automatic thoughts and reducing avoidance behaviors.
Counseling Summary:
Explored M's social anxiety and the impact of negative assumptions about classmates' perceptions, leading to increased avoidance behaviors and insecurity. Encouraged M to challenge these assumptions through evidence-based thinking and consider sharing feelings openly with classmates, while also incorporating running as a stress-relief activity. M showed progress in managing anxiety by addressing unrealistic thoughts and using running for self-care, recognizing the importance of assessing situations realistically and continuing to build on these strategies.
