The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student experiencing heightened stress and feelings of being overwhelmed due to academic pressures and life in general. Recent conversations indicate these feelings have been compounded by the pressure to meet familial expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user expresses an interest in creative activities, including drawing, and recalls this hobby as a source of relaxation and contentment in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user shows signs of stress and anxiety, primarily linked to self-imposed pressures and expectations, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user is feeling significantly down and overwhelmed, unable to pinpoint specific triggers but recognizing a buildup of pressure from various life spheres.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user expressing feelings of isolation and an inability to manage the cumulative stress without support.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to note situations or thoughts that intensify feelings of being overwhelmed for discussion in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Identify patterns in the user's thoughts and emotions using CBT strategies to develop coping mechanisms and ease the sensation of being overwhelmed.
Counseling Summary:
The user initially struggled with feelings of overwhelm and pressure from academic expectations, particularly due to perceived parental expectations. Through counseling sessions, they began to identify and challenge negative thoughts, adopting a mindset focused on personal bests rather than perfection. Additionally, they revived a former interest in drawing as a creative outlet for stress relief, finding it beneficial in managing their emotions.
