The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student struggling with significant academic pressure, stemming primarily from having failed eight courses during the pandemic and now facing the pressure to retake exams. Zhang describes feeling unmotivated and stressed about these academic challenges. 

[Personal Characteristics]
Zhang enjoys video gaming as a pastime and is recently exploring drawing as a relaxation activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety and stress mainly due to academic performance issues, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels overwhelmed, stressed, and lacks motivation to study, largely due to academic pressures and the impact of the pandemic on study habits.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's feelings of guilt and anxiety caused by self-imposed expectations and assumptions about judgment from others.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Zhang should maintain a daily feelings journal and begin a conversation with a trusted friend, Lily, about his experiences and emotions.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to reduce academic-related stress by identifying distorted thoughts and encouraging Zhang to seek support from personal connections.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's academic stress and feelings of guilt due to failed courses, exacerbated by pandemic-induced isolation. Discussed pervasive fear of judgment from peers and proposed reaching out to a trusted friend (Lily) as a supportive step to alleviate disconnection. Encouraged Zhang to continue journaling emotions and patterns for further discussion in future sessions.
