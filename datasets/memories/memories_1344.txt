The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyu is an employee dealing with anxiety, particularly related to pandemic hygiene concerns in public and work environments. Recently, Xiaoyu experiences distress due to frequent thoughts about germs and cleanliness at work.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoyu values hygiene and health safety, demonstrating high standards for cleanliness which sometimes results in social withdrawal.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyu experiences anxiety and obsessive thoughts around cleanliness, with no formal diagnosis of a specific mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyu feels anxious and preoccupied with germs, leading to excessive handwashing and avoidance of coworker interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoyu's struggle with persistent anxiety about hygiene and the impact of these thoughts on their work interactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyu should try to limit handwashing to specific times and record thoughts related to hygiene anxiety for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiaoyu manage hygiene-related stress and identify specific thoughts contributing to anxiety, promoting realistic thinking.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoyu's germ-related anxiety and its impact on daily life, identifying habitual negative thought patterns and avoidance behaviors. Implemented strategies including limiting handwashing and shifting perspective, alongside introducing meditation, aiming to develop a balanced approach to anxiety management. Through the sessions, Xiaoyu recognized progress in awareness and coping skills, feeling more prepared to handle future challenges independently.
