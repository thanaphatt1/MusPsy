The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Young is a student experiencing exhaustion due to sleep difficulties and personal challenges, including the recent loss of a cousin and relationship conflicts. These factors have impacted their study efficiency and emotional well-being. Young values academic performance highly and seeks ways to manage stress and emotional strain effectively.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Young finds joy in painting and has supportive friends. They also enjoy walking, which serves as a helpful coping mechanism during overwhelming times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Young experiences stress and anxiety exacerbated by recent personal losses and relationship issues, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Young feels exhausted and overwhelmed, struggling with grief from a cousin's passing and anxiety related to their relationship and studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Young’s interconnected stressors and appreciates Young's willingness to explore how thoughts, emotions, and behaviors influence each other.
[Counseling Assignments:] Young should jot down moments when stress-related thoughts and feelings surface, aiming to identify specific triggers.
[Session Goal:] Begin identifying and understanding patterns in thoughts and behaviors related to stress, using CBT strategies for initial management.
Counseling Summary:
Young discussed challenges with sleep, grief from losing a cousin, and relationship stress, which are impacting their emotional well-being and academic focus. To gain insights, the consultant suggested a reflection exercise to identify triggers and patterns, while recommending small steps like reaching out to friends and engaging in familiar activities to mitigate feelings of isolation. Agreement was reached to try these strategies and revisit their effectiveness in the next session.
