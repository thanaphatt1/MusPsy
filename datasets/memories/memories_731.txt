The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family. 

[Personal Characteristics]
They also enjoy painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels anxious and isolated due to difficulties in settling into the new university environment and communicating with local roommates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Liu's feelings of invisibility and homesickness, impacting their motivation and social interactions, but also sees Liu's willingness to explore ways to break the cycle.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should try using shared interests, such as science fiction, as conversation starters with roommates and consider joining a book club for meeting like-minded individuals.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to challenge Liu's feelings of invisibility and loneliness, encouraging openness in interactions and engagement in interest-based activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's feelings of anxiety and loneliness due to challenges in adapting to a new university environment, particularly difficulties communicating with roommates. The consultant encouraged Liu to initiate conversations through shared interests, such as science fiction, and to join a campus book club to foster connections. Strategies for managing stress included setting aside time for reading and noting negative thoughts to assess their validity, with an overall focus on gradual personal and social growth.
