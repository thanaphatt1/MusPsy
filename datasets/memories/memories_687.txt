The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
M is a young adult experiencing feelings of restlessness and loneliness due to spending significant time alone at home. M is particularly affected by a lack of family presence, as their mother is often away.

[Personal Characteristics]  
M used to enjoy drawing and is interested in exploring new activities, including potentially reconnecting with art to combat feelings of isolation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
M is showing signs of anxiety and detachment linked to excessive internet use and isolation, although there is no mention of a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] M feels restless and lonely, using the internet to escape these feelings and is worried about the impact on their productivity.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes M's growing awareness of their internet habits as a coping mechanism and their openness to exploring alternative activities for fulfillment.
[Counseling Assignments:] M should observe triggers for internet use and attempt to engage in drawing as a mode of expression and connection.
[Session Goal:] Encourage M to identify triggers for internet use, explore drawing as a means to reduce loneliness, and begin recognizing the role of activities in their daily routine.
Counseling Summary:
M expressed feelings of loneliness and detachment due to extensive time spent online and identified the internet as a coping mechanism for isolation. The consultant encouraged M to explore activities like drawing and reading to find fulfillment beyond the digital world and suggested tracking online habits to understand underlying triggers. M agreed to experiment with these activities and establish a routine to create a more balanced and engaging use of free time.
