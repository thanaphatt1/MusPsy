The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student who is currently experiencing feelings of loneliness and isolation since transitioning to university. The unfamiliar climate and living environment contribute to these feelings, along with difficulty in forming new friendships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu loves reading and enjoys playing basketball, using both as outlets for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu is not diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder but is experiencing adjustment challenges and feelings of loneliness in the new university setting.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels lonely and out of place, struggling to adjust to the university environment and finding it hard to make friends.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Liu is experiencing transitions common among new university students, showing initial signs of isolation but also openness to potential social connections.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should note down thoughts and situations that induce feelings of disconnection. Additionally, consider reaching out to an old friend or joining a book club to initiate new social connections.
[Session Goal:] Help Liu manage feelings of loneliness by identifying disconnection triggers and encouraging social engagement activities, integrating Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's feelings of loneliness and difficulty adjusting to university, including unfamiliarity with surroundings and lack of social connections. Agreed on strategies to combat isolation, such as reaching out to an old friend and attending a book club, while focusing on identifying stress-related patterns. Emphasized the importance of social connections and self-care alongside academic efforts.
