The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a university student who grew up in the countryside and is adapting to life in a busy city while navigating the emotional challenges of his parents' recent divorce. He expresses feeling lost and overwhelmed as he tries to balance academic pressures and familial expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang enjoys reading and drawing, which serve as comfort activities during times of stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang exhibits signs of stress and anxiety primarily triggered by adjusting to a new environment and familial changes, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels overwhelmed with city life and the aftermath of his parents' divorce, impacting his academic confidence and sense of belonging.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yang's isolation and underlying stress-driven self-criticism amidst personal and academic pressures, including feelings of inadequacy and fear of judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is asked to record moments when he feels overwhelmed and his accompanying thoughts, aiming to identify patterns and understand these feelings better.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT techniques to help Yang manage stress, increase self-awareness regarding his thoughts, and encourage openness to discussing feelings.

---
Counseling Summary:
Yang is experiencing stress and a sense of overwhelming disconnection after moving from the countryside to a fast-paced city environment, complicated further by their parents' divorce. Their feelings of not fitting in and fear of judgment are affecting their academic performance and leading to isolation. To help Yang manage these emotions, the consultant encouraged them to identify and challenge negative thought patterns and to document specific stressful moments to gain better insight into their feelings.
