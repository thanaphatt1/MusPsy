The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Tang is a university student who recently felt overwhelmed after taking a psychological health test at school, prompting introspection about past experiences, including his father's strictness and bullying during school. He values self-improvement and is concerned about how these experiences might be influencing his current feelings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Tang enjoys reading and drawing, which provide a sense of calm and distraction from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Tang is experiencing anxiety triggered by social situations, worrying about being judged or not fitting in, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Tang feels overwhelmed and anxious after reflecting on past experiences during a psychological health test, especially when around new people or in crowded places.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Tang's heightened sensitivity to perceived judgments but notes his willingness to explore and manage these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Tang should take note of situations where feelings of anxiety arise and reflect on the thoughts accompanying these moments.
[Session Goal:] Begin identifying patterns in Tang’s social anxiety and addressing them with CBT techniques to build awareness of anxiety triggers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Tang's experiences of anxiety in social situations linked to past experiences, such as parental strictness and school bullying. Identified a pattern of self-conscious thoughts during social interactions and tasked Tang with noting and challenging unrealistic assumptions. Aimed to help Tang manage anxiety by increasing awareness of thought patterns.
