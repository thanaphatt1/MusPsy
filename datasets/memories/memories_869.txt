The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a student who has been feeling stressed and overwhelmed due to underperformance in academic classes, which has impacted their confidence levels. Zhang fears making mistakes in class and feels isolated, avoiding both class discussions and social events.

[Personal Characteristics]
Zhang finds joy and relaxation in reading novels, particularly those in the fantasy genre, which provides an escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences stress and anxiety primarily triggered by academic pressures but there are no formal diagnoses of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels stressed and overwhelmed with underperforming in class, leading to lowered confidence and avoidance of social interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's fear of making mistakes and pressure to perform well academically, along with a reluctance to engage socially due to fear of judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to begin journaling thoughts and emotions after class to identify patterns and triggers, and to focus on celebrating small academic successes.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Zhang manage stress, identify recurring negative thoughts, and encourage engagement in activities like reading for relaxation.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's academic anxiety impacting class performance and social engagement. Agreed on maintaining a journal to identify feelings and triggers, and discussed recognizing small successes and sharing concerns with peers. Encouraged reaching out to classmates for support and using positive reinforcement to boost confidence and motivation.
