The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu Hua is an adult male experiencing significant stress and unease due to recent departmental adjustments at his company, leading to worries about job security and future uncertainties.

[Personal Characteristics]
He used to enjoy jogging as a positive activity to manage stress, which he's willing to incorporate back into his routine.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu Hua experiences anxiety and stress heightened by workplace changes, with a pattern of negative thinking triggered during periods of transition or evaluation such as exams or career changes.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu Hua feels stressed and trapped in a loop of negative, worst-case scenario thoughts due to workplace changes.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Liu Hua's propensity for anxiety when facing change and his openness to exploring mindset shifts from past experiences.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu Hua should jot down specific stress-inducing thoughts and reactions, explore the connection between these thoughts and previous experiences, and consider reintroducing jogging as a stress-relief activity.
[Session Goal:] Identify thought patterns contributing to stress and introduce cognitive-behavioral strategies to manage stress, encouraging Liu Hua to recognize and modify thought patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu Hua's stress related to workplace changes, identifying a pattern of negative thinking during transitions similar to past experiences. Agreed on noting specific thoughts that trigger worry and incorporating jogging into the routine as a positive activity to manage stress and shift perspective. Encouraged observation of thought patterns and integration of physical activity to assess its impact on emotional responses.
