The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is an employee recently transferred to a new position, facing significant anxiety due to adjustments to the new environment and expectations. Li is concerned about their ability to engage effectively in the new role and comparing themselves unfavorably to others.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys reading science fiction, which serves as a form of stress relief.  

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by workplace situations, with habitual patterns of self-comparison and doubts about personal competence.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels anxious and uncertain, overwhelmed by adapting to a new role at work.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Li's anxiety is exacerbated by self-comparisons and pressure to perform, identifying Li's willingness to explore and shift thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should record situations that provoke anxiety and accompanying thoughts, also reflecting on personal achievements to balance self-doubt.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Li recognize and adjust cognitive patterns, encouraging awareness of personal achievements and strengths to reduce negative self-comparisons.
Counseling Summary:
Li is experiencing anxiety and uncertainty due to a recent job transfer, struggling with adjusting to a new team and role expectations. The session focused on identifying patterns in Li’s thoughts and feelings related to stress and comparisons with others during team meetings, emphasizing recognizing Li's own strengths and achievements to boost confidence. Li agreed to document moments of anxiety and accomplishments to facilitate a more balanced perspective and improve confidence in the new role.
