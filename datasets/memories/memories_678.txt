The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Mou is a university student who struggles with feelings of exclusion and anxiety in social settings, particularly when engaging in group activities or dorm conversations. Zhang Mou often overthinks interactions but desires to improve their social connections and well-being.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Mou enjoys reading, which serves as both a hobby and a strategy for alleviating stress and providing mental escape from anxious thoughts.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Mou primarily faces anxiety related to social exclusion and negative self-perceptions but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Mou feels anxious and excluded during group social interactions, often doubting their own worth.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang Mou's tendency towards negative self-assumptions but also notes a willingness to explore and confront these thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Mou is tasked with observing and documenting evidence related to assumptions of social exclusion and speaking to a classmate about their feelings.
[Session Goal:] To help Zhang Mou identify and challenge negative thoughts using CBT, and encourage social engagement to foster a sense of belonging.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang Mou's feelings of exclusion and anxiety during group activities, identifying negative thought patterns that exacerbate these emotions. Agreed on exercises to challenge assumptions and improve social connections, such as speaking with a classmate, alongside incorporating reading as a regular calming activity. Zhang Mou will document insights and moods as part of ongoing reflection and progress tracking.
