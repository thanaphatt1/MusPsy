The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student who experienced a sudden vision blackout during military training, leading to ongoing anxiety about health and stress management. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user finds solace in art and enjoys listening to calming music, which aids in relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user is experiencing heightened anxiety and stress, without a formal diagnosis, mainly due to a recent intense event and associated triggers.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels extremely anxious and stressed following the incident during military training.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices the user's valid concerns about health and stress, observing their openness to exploring coping mechanisms like journaling and music.
[Counseling Assignments:] Start a journal to note thoughts and feelings daily, and try using music and breathing exercises to manage anxiety spikes.
[Session Goal:] Help the user identify anxiety patterns and introduce initial stress management techniques through CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session focused on the user's anxiety and stress resulting from a frightening incident during military training. The consultant suggested journaling to help identify patterns and triggers of anxiety and introduced deep breathing exercises, specifically the 4-7-8 method, along with listening to calming music, as strategies to manage anxious moments. The user agreed to incorporate these techniques and will update their progress in the next session.
