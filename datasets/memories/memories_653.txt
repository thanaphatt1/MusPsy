The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student struggling to adjust socially, feeling isolated after starting university and facing challenges bonding with new classmates and roommates. Wang feels anxious about peer interactions, worrying about being left out.

[Personal Characteristics]
He has a keen interest in music and photography, which he wishes to share with others to form connections.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences social anxiety, particularly related to fears of rejection and exclusion, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels disconnected and anxious, struggling to fit in with classmates, impacting his focus and overall emotional state.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's perception of social rejection and low self-esteem, identifying his eagerness to find ways to connect despite his anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should attempt to engage faculty by exploring shared interests with classmates, starting a conversation about a popular TV show to foster connection.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Wang address negative self-perceptions, encourage initial social interactions, and identify new relational opportunities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's feelings of isolation and anxiety due to difficulties fitting in at university, particularly with roommates. Agreed on small steps to connect socially, such as initiating conversation on common interests (e.g., a popular TV show) and considering joining a photography club. Emphasized using old friendships for support and taking gradual steps towards building new relationships, with the ongoing practice of mindfulness to manage underlying anxiety.
