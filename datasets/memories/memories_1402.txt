The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lin Feng is a junior high school student who has been experiencing stress due to academic pressures and is worried about self-worth following recent disappointing exam results.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lin Feng enjoys drawing, which he finds to be a relaxing and enjoyable activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lin Feng struggles with anxiety and self-doubt, particularly centered around academic performance, but there is no formal diagnosis indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lin Feng feels discouraged and hopeless about the future due to academic disappointments.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Lin Feng's vulnerability to self-doubt and harsh self-comparisons, but also identifies his willingness to explore methods for overcoming discouragement.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lin Feng should document situations that trigger feelings of discouragement and the accompanying thoughts for analysis.
[Session Goal:] Help Lin Feng recognize and understand negative thought patterns, using CBT to establish healthier perspectives towards academic challenges and self-worth.
Counseling Summary:
Lin Feng struggled with self-doubt and anxiety due to academic pressures and was encouraged to identify and challenge negative thought patterns. Throughout the sessions, Lin Feng worked on recognizing strengths, setting small achievable goals, and incorporating enjoyable activities like drawing to improve well-being and self-esteem. By the end of the counseling, Lin Feng felt more confident in managing challenges using these strategies and planned to continue applying them to future situations.
