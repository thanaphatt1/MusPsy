The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaomou is a high school student preparing for the college entrance exam, experiencing significant stress and compulsive behaviors related to studying and performance pressures.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaomou used to enjoy playing basketball and finds family support important.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaomou exhibits stress and anxiety linked to the college entrance exam but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaomou feels anxious and overwhelmed by compulsive behaviors that prevent effective studying.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaomou’s difficulty with compulsive thoughts and stress, expressing an openness to managing these patterns better.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaomou is encouraged to start a journal to track compulsive thoughts and identify triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiaomou recognize triggers of compulsive behaviors and implement soothing routines.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaomou's compulsive behavior of flipping through books, which interferes with effective study and exacerbates anxiety related to an upcoming exam. Proposed keeping a journal to track triggers, incorporating a calming pre-study routine, and setting smaller, achievable study goals to manage stress and enhance focus. Agreed to review Xiaomou’s progress with these strategies in the next session.
