The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a recently divorced parent adjusting to life changes, including a daughter starting high school and living in a dorm while they have moved into their company dormitory to avoid returning home.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user has previously enjoyed reading as an activity for relaxation but hasn't engaged in it recently due to their life circumstances.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user is experiencing emotional distress, feelings of isolation, and regret, especially in relation to past family and career decisions, though no formal mental health diagnosis has been provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed by feelings of loneliness and regret, particularly in the evenings.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices the user's struggle with emotional upheaval post-divorce and isolation due to current life changes, along with openness to seeking help.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to resume reading as a routine relaxation activity and note recurring regret-related thoughts for further examination.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help the user manage loneliness and regret by identifying specific thought patterns and engaging in relaxing activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's feelings of isolation and regret following a divorce and transitioning to new living arrangements. Identified the user's cycle of regret and sadness tied to past decisions. Agreed on strategies for building new perspectives by revisiting positive intentions behind past choices and incorporating self-care, like reading, into a daily routine.
