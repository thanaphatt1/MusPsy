The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Beibei is a young child experiencing anxiety regarding their kindergarten environment, primarily fearing abandonment by their caretakers, specifically their uncle and aunt.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Beibei enjoys drawing and playing with blocks, activities which serve as comfort in unsettling environments.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Beibei is experiencing anxiety, much of which is associated with separation anxiety, typical in early childhood adjustment periods, though not formally diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Beibei feels anxious about being left at kindergarten but finds slight relief in familiar activities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Beibei's imaginative approach toward anxiety management, particularly the use of drawing and storytelling as coping methods.
[Counseling Assignments:] Beibei is encouraged to recall past experiences where worries resolved positively, focusing on engaging comforting activities at kindergarten.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Beibei to CBT strategies by building connections through enjoyable activities, encouraging the creation of new social bonds, and fostering positive thinking practices.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Beibei's anxiety about being left at kindergarten and feeling surrounded by strangers, establishing a connection between past reassurance and current fears. Encouraged Beibei to engage in comforting activities like playing with blocks as a way to manage anxiety and build familiarity at kindergarten. Discussed the potential for social interaction by inviting others to join in play, fostering a sense of community and challenging the fear of isolation.
