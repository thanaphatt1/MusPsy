The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Wang is a university student experiencing significant stress, especially regarding exams and academic performance, also facing pressure from their mother to excel academically. The pressure from recent additional classes adds to Xiao Wang's stress, making it challenging to find time for personal activities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
An interest in running and a desire to manage academic stress effectively.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Wang exhibits stress and anticipatory anxiety related to academic pressures, with no formal diagnosis noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Wang feels overwhelmed by academic pressures and family expectations, impacting concentration and energy.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiao Wang's struggle with perfectionism and sees willingness to explore and implement stress management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Wang should schedule short morning runs to manage stress levels and jot down recurring thoughts causing stress.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to identify stress-related thoughts and set a balanced routine to include relaxing activities like running.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session with Xiao Wang focused on managing stress from academic pressures and parental expectations. They discussed the impact of stress on Xiao Wang's ability to concentrate and socialize, explored the benefits of communicating feelings to his mother, and decided on strategies like writing down thoughts and incorporating running into his routine to enhance self-care and reduce anxiety. The consultant encouraged small, manageable changes to improve balance and well-being.
