The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a recent university graduate feeling overwhelmed with the pressure of securing a job as graduation approaches. They struggle with sleep and avoid communicating with potential employers due to fear of rejection and a history of unstable upbringing, which has led to self-doubt.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They find enjoyment in writing, particularly crafting short stories, which sometimes helps in processing emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety and self-doubt largely related to job search pressures, although there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed by the pressure of job searching and is experiencing sleep disturbances.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes the user’s fear of rejection and the impact of self-imposed expectations while noting their openness to trying new coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should maintain a journal to track thoughts and feelings during job applications, identifying patterns of stress and negative thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help the user identify and manage negative thoughts and increase self-confidence by exploring stress coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
The consultation addressed the user's anxiety around job searching due to fear of rejection and self-confidence issues arising from their unstable upbringing. The consultant introduced a journaling task to identify thought patterns associated with job applications and recommended mindfulness techniques, including breathing exercises, for stress management. The user agreed to incorporate these strategies, especially to improve sleep and reduce overwhelming feelings.
