The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student experiencing increased anxiety and stress, particularly regarding English speaking classes, and is concerned about making mistakes in front of peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing and appreciates nature, finding these activities to be a source of relaxation, especially during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user reports anxiety triggered by performance situations like public speaking but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious and overwhelmed by the thought of speaking in front of classmates, fearing judgment and potential mistakes.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices the user's habitual prediction of negative outcomes, which exacerbates their anxiety but recognizes the user's willingness to explore coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to write down specific thoughts that arise when preparing for or participating in class to gain insight into anxiety triggers.
[Session Goal:] Introduce the user to CBT strategies to understand the link between thoughts, emotions, and behaviors, with a focus on reframing negative predictions into constructive thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
The session focused on the user's social anxiety related to speaking in English classes, balancing their desire to connect with fear of judgment. The consultant suggested a task of writing down specific thoughts to understand patterns and encouraged reframing negative self-talk to manage anxiety better. The user planned to apply these strategies, recognizing mistakes as learning opportunities and aiming to reduce anxiety and enhance class participation.
