The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyan is a university student who feels overwhelmed by the social demands of university life. Xiaoyan describes themselves as shy and self-conscious, struggling with feelings of not belonging, and assuming that others are judging them. 

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoyan enjoys drawing and uses it as a calming activity to clear their mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyan experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by social interactions and the perception of being judged, although there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyan feels overwhelmed and stressed due to university's social demands.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoyan's struggle with social interactions and self-consciousness but also identifies openness to trying self-reflection through writing.
[Counseling Assignments:] The assignment is for Xiaoyan to jot down their thoughts and feelings each day to help process and organize them.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiaoyan identify and challenge negative assumptions and encourage the processing of feelings through writing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoyan's challenges with social anxiety in university, focusing on her discomfort in social interactions due to fear of judgment. Agreed on a strategy for Xiaoyan to write down daily thoughts and acknowledge positive interactions to manage anxiety and build realistic perspectives. Encouraged her to reflect on past positive experiences with a counselor to enhance self-understanding and articulate emotions effectively.
