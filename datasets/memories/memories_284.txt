The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jiani is a school student struggling to adjust to a new environment, with challenges in making friends and focusing on studies, which contributes to feelings of isolation and stress. She expresses a strong interest in reading and considers joining a book club.

[Personal Characteristics]
Jiani shows an interest in reading, finding solace in books and considering them a pathway for making connections.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jiani experiences feelings of isolation and stress but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jiani feels overwhelmed and isolated due to difficulties in adapting to a new school environment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Jiani's struggle with self-imposed feelings of exclusion and highlights her openness to exploring self-reflection strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jiani is encouraged to keep a journal to organize her feelings and explore thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Jiani manage feelings of isolation by identifying negative thought patterns and fostering social engagement through shared interests.
Counseling Summary:
The session focused on Jiani's feelings of isolation and stress due to difficulty adapting to a new school environment and social challenges. The consultant recommended journaling to help organize thoughts, identify negative patterns, and consider the use of affirmations and positive self-talk to counter self-doubt. Additionally, Jiani was encouraged to reach out to her sister for emotional support, with the aim of building a supportive network and tracking progress through small victories.
