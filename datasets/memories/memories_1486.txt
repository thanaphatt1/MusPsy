The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoy is a student who recently faced the challenge of being moved to a non-specialized class, triggering significant stress and anxiety due to not fitting in. Her concerns are exacerbated by her mother’s high expectations, and she reports trouble sleeping as a result of the pressure.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoy used to enjoy reading, which was a source of relaxation, but has found it difficult to engage in this hobby due to increased stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoy experiences high levels of anxiety and stress, mainly stemming from academic pressures and familial expectations. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoy feels stressed and anxious, struggling with feelings of inadequacy after being moved to a non-specialized class.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoy's difficulty balancing self-worth with performance amid high expectations but recognizes her willingness to explore change.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoy should start jotting down her daily feelings to help identify key stressors and areas for focus.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoy use CBT strategies to manage anxiety by recognizing and documenting stressors and integrating relaxing activities into her routine.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoy's adjustment struggles due to being in a non-specialized class, with emphasis on negative thought patterns affecting self-worth tied to academic performance and pressure from parental expectations. Established strategies for anxiety management, including using positive affirmations, open communication, and re-engaging with hobbies like reading as a means to relax. Concluded with Xiaoy feeling more equipped to manage stress and challenges independently.
