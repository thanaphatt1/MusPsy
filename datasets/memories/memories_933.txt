The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student experiencing high stress due to upcoming final exams and concerns over academic performance. They express anxiety about social situations, particularly fearing embarrassment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They enjoy sketching as a form of creative expression and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user demonstrates anxiety related to academic pressures and social interactions but lacks a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed by exam stress and anxious about academic performance and social interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's apprehension towards perceived judgment in social settings and notes their willingness to explore stress relief methods.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is tasked with maintaining a personal diary to track anxiety triggers and patterns.
[Session Goal:] Begin utilizing CBT techniques to help the user manage exam stress, identify anxiety triggers, and engage in stress-relief activities like journalling.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's academic stress and social anxiety, focusing on the fear of poor performance and embarrassment. Proposed using a personal diary to identify triggers and patterns of anxiety and concentration difficulties. Agreed to discuss insights from the diary in the next session to develop coping strategies.
