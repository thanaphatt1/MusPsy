[Personal Characteristics]

[Session Goal:] To assist Xiaoxiao in managing conflict-induced stress through CBT, encouraging relaxation practices like drawing to alleviate emotional tension.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoxiao's feelings of distress following an argument with her mother over phone usage, which negatively impacted her studies. Recommended engaging in a relaxing activity, specifically suggesting a return to drawing, to help manage emotions and shift focus. Set a small goal for Xiaoxiao to dedicate time to drawing weekly and agreed to discuss its effects in future sessions.
