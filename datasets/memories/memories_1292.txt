The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xie Na is a college student experiencing discomfort and anxiety due to conflicts with her roommates, affecting her sleep. She values harmony in relationships and strives to maintain it.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys reading novels as a form of relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xie Na exhibits signs of anxiety linked to interpersonal conflicts, but there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xie Na feels anxious and uncomfortable, primarily due to unresolved tension with her roommates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xie Na's tendency to blame herself for conflicts but appreciates her willingness to explore the interconnectivity of her thoughts and emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xie Na is encouraged to maintain a journal to track her thoughts and feelings when stressed about her roommates.
[Session Goal:] Identify and challenge self-blaming thoughts using CBT to foster more balanced perspectives on conflicts.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling sessions, Xie Na worked through a conflict with her roommates, initially feeling anxious and blaming herself. Through journaling, she identified her automatic negative thoughts and learned to view the situation from different perspectives, gaining insight that the argument was not solely her fault. By practicing deep breathing and scheduling time for reading, Xie Na developed strategies to manage stress, leading her to feel more prepared and confident in addressing conflicts with calm, open communication.
