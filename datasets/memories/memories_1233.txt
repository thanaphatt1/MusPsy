The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background  
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaowang is a university student who has been experiencing increased anxiety and worry following the recent passing of a classmate. This event has sparked fears related to germs and contamination, leading to obsessive hand-washing behaviors.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaowang enjoys painting and reading, using these activities as a means for relaxation and mental peace.  

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaowang experiences anxiety primarily related to contamination fears, induced by a recent traumatic event. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder disclosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaowang feels anxious and terrified of germs, prompted by the recent death of a classmate and fears regarding contamination.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes a significant link between Xiaowang's current fears and the recent traumatic event, noting the presence of anxiety-driven obsessive behaviors like excessive hand washing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaowang should note patterns and triggers related to the urge to wash hands and engage in distracting activities such as painting.
[Session Goal:] Assist Xiaowang in recognizing anxiety triggers and adopting relaxation activities to manage contamination fears using CBT frameworks.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaowang's heightened anxiety related to contamination fears following a classmate's passing and discussed the impact of compulsive behaviors like handwashing. Identified that engaging in enjoyable activities, such as painting and reading, can distract from anxious thoughts, and Xiaowang agreed to incorporate these activities into daily life. Planned to review the progress in the next session, emphasizing the importance of small steps in managing anxiety.
