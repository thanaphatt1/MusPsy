The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a youth, gender unspecified, experiencing tension primarily due to the differing levels of oversight and freedom in interactions with parents. The mother is perceived as overly concerned, while the father provides a more relaxed environment during activities such as swimming.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing and coloring, often using these activities to find peace and relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user is experiencing stress and anxiety, particularly in balancing the relationship dynamics with their parents, without a formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed by their mother's constant questioning and desires more personal space.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's insight into their need for space and relaxation, highlighting the differing dynamics with each parent.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should observe and note moments when they feel relaxed and consider keeping a journal of these experiences to understand their needs better.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT strategies to help the user recognize patterns and feelings when interacting with their parents, assisting in balancing these dynamics.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's struggle with feeling overwhelmed by their mother's constant attention versus feeling more relaxed and authentic with their father. The user identified a need for more personal space to potentially improve their relationship with their mother and agreed to observe moments of relaxation to gain insights. The consultant encouraged the user to document these observations to develop strategies for achieving a healthier familial dynamic.
