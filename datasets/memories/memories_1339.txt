The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user, who has been working in a bank, recently faced a professional setback that significantly impacted their career and self-esteem. Once competing for a senior position, they ended up losing both the opportunity and their previous managerial role, leading to feelings of disappointment and stress. Support from family, particularly their husband, provides some comfort.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user appreciates the encouragement from family and has found cooking as a relaxing hobby.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety and stress, largely driven by career setbacks and self-imposed standards, without any formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels stressed and disappointed after a professional setback, which has shaken their confidence and adjustment at work.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's vulnerability to self-blame and the social embarrassment they feel while managing professional and personal spheres.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to journal their thoughts and emotions concerning the setback to gain clarity and understanding of their emotional responses.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to address the user’s stress and promote self-compassion through awareness and re-framing of negative thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored User's loss of confidence and anxiety following a demotion at work, focusing on self-blame and anticipatory dread. Encouraged journaling to clarify emotions and identify negative thought patterns, while challenging assumptions of judgment. Agreed on homework to monitor and reinterpret negative thoughts, fostering self-awareness and balanced perspectives.
