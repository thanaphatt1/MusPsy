The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei, a professional facing anxiety, concerning recent organizational restructuring at his company, is worried about team dynamics and doubts his leadership skills.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds comfort in playing soccer, which acts as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences anxiety due to workplace changes; no formal diagnosis has been mentioned for a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels anxious and overwhelmed due to the changes and doubts about his leadership capabilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang Wei's self-doubt linked to new management procedures and his openness to exploring solutions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is encouraged to note specific situations triggering anxiety and the thoughts in those moments.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT tools to help Zhang Wei identify triggers of anxiety and develop strategies for managing leadership doubts.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang Wei expressed anxiety about his leadership due to recent organizational changes in his company, leading to stress both at work and home. The consultant suggested he track specific situations that trigger anxiety and note his thoughts during those times to gain clarity. Zhang Wei agreed to this reflective exercise to better understand and manage his concerns in preparation for the next session.
