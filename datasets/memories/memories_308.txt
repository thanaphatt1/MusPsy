The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jessica is a student who recently received a warning in school due to misbehavior, which has created stress about the possibility of expulsion if counseling is not attended. This incident has affected her relationships at school as she feels anxiety and regret over the situation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Jessica enjoys cycling, an activity which helps her clear her mind and find peace.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jessica exhibits anxiety and stress primarily attributed to social interactions at school and concerns over others’ perceptions. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jessica is feeling stressed and anxious following a misbehavior incident at school, which has led to a threat of expulsion unless she attends counseling.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Jessica’s self-critical thoughts and her perception that others view her negatively, yet identifies her willingness to change and improve relationships with her peers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jessica is encouraged to document positive interactions each day to counterbalance negative perceptions and to practice self-compassion.
[Session Goal:] Help Jessica manage her anxiety by applying CBT techniques to challenge self-critical thoughts and highlighting positive interactions to adjust her perception.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling session, Jessica discussed her anxiety and regret following a classroom incident, fearing it confirmed negative perceptions of her among peers. The consultant helped her challenge these thoughts by recalling positive interactions, setting a daily task to note such experiences, and emphasizing self-compassion when facing criticism. Jessica was encouraged to share her feelings with trusted friends, fostering hope through small, consistent steps.
