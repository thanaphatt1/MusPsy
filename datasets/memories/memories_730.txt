The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyi is a high school student experiencing stress and homesickness after leaving home for boarding school for the first time. Xiaoyi reports missing family greatly and struggling with feelings of isolation due to their reserved nature.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They have an interest in writing, especially storytelling, which used to be a calming activity back home, and enjoy listening to music familiar from their family environment.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyi exhibits signs of adjustment disorder related to the new boarding school environment, highlighting challenges in social integration and a sense of belonging.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyi feels stressed, out of place, and homesick, showing difficulty adjusting to the new boarding school environment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoyi's openness to exploring self-help techniques and expressions of creativity for emotional processing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyi should keep a journal to document thoughts and feelings before bed, incorporating storytelling elements to enhance engagement.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT techniques to help Xiaoyi manage homesickness by exploring emotional expression through writing and maintaining connections with home.
Counseling Summary:
Exploring Xiaoyi's challenges with homesickness and isolation at boarding school, the session focused on practical strategies like journaling with storytelling elements and creating a music playlist to enhance connection and emotional expression. Encouraging small steps, the consultant emphasized maintaining communication with family to bolster support and adjust strategies as needed.
