The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
A is a university student dealing with anxiety in social situations, particularly with dormmates, fearing they might say something foolish. A often second-guesses themselves.

[Personal Characteristics]  
A appreciates reading, which serves as a form of relaxation and escape.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
A displays patterns of anxiety driven by negative self-talk and social fears. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is stated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] A feels tense and anxious in social settings due to worries about being judged or saying something wrong.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices A’s tendency toward negative self-talk and second-guessing, as well as A's readiness to explore and address these thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] A should track negative thoughts as they occur to gather precise examples for discussion in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Help A recognize and challenge negative self-talk and distorted thinking patterns through CBT techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Explored A's social anxiety, focusing on tension experienced in social situations with dormmates and excessive worry about negative judgments. Identified negative self-talk contributing to anxiety and agreed to track these thoughts for further discussion. Aims to challenge unrealistic perceptions and reduce self-critical thinking.
