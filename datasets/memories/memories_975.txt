The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student experiencing significant anxiety due to returning to school, fear of low grades, and previous criticism from teachers. The user is particularly stressed by academic performance and how it might be perceived by others.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user finds drawing to be a helpful stress-relief activity and is open to incorporating it into their routine as a relaxing exercise.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety primarily triggered by academic stress and fear of failure, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis present.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious about returning to school and the potential criticism for their academic performance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's fear of criticism linked to self-worth but perceives an openness to strategies aimed at overcoming these fears.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is tasked with writing down moments of anxiety and identifying specific thoughts or situations that trigger these feelings.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help the user manage anxiety by identifying patterns and triggers, ultimately aiming to reduce stress through self-awareness and reflection.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's anxiety regarding school performance and fear of criticism, identifying how thoughts and emotions contribute to their stress. The user agreed to a task of writing down anxiety triggers to identify patterns, alongside re-engaging in drawing as a stress-relief activity. Together, they planned to gradually reframe negative thoughts from perfectionism to focus on effort and progress.
