The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Z is a young individual feeling sad and lonely due to their parents being away, affecting their concentration and academic performance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Z enjoys drawing animals and landscapes, finding comfort in activities connected to happier family times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Z exhibits signs of loneliness and sadness linked to parental absence, yet no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Z feels sad and lonely, especially missing their parents and struggling with declining grades.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Z's emotional struggle with the absence of their parents but also recognizes Z's openness to exploring creative outlets for comfort.
[Counseling Assignments:] Z is encouraged to draw or write about happy or calming memories related to their parents as a means of emotional expression.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Z manage loneliness by engaging in creative activities that evoke positive memories and emotions.
Counseling Summary:
Z expressed feelings of sadness and loneliness due to their parents' absence, which has also impacted their academic performance. They were encouraged to engage in creative activities like drawing and letter writing to channel positive memories and maintain a connection with their parents. It was suggested that sharing these activities with their grandpa and finding an interesting book to read together could help them cope during this period.
