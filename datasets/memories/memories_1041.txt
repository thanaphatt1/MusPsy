The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyu is a university student dealing with significant stress due to upcoming exams and the pressure to achieve high grades. Since attending university, Xiaoyu boards away from home, often feeling a lack of immediate parental support.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoyu has an interest in music, especially playing the piano, which serves as a relaxing activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyu experiences stress and anxiety primarily due to academic pressures, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyu feels stressed and anxious about the upcoming exams, with a constant worry about not meeting personal academic expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoyu's pressure to fulfill academic aspirations often stems from a perceived lack of support and an internal dialogue shaped by worry and stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyu is encouraged to keep a daily diary of thoughts and feelings to identify patterns of stress and anxiety triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Xiaoyu identify and understand the interplay between thoughts, emotions, and behaviors, aiming to alleviate exam-related stress.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaoyu expressed stress and anxiety about upcoming exams, exacerbated by feelings of isolation due to boarding at school. The consultant suggested keeping a diary to monitor thoughts and feelings, aiming to identify patterns and triggers, with plans to explore these insights in the next session to help Xiaoyu cope better with academic pressures.
