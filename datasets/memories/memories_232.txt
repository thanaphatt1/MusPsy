The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyu is a student passionate about math competitions who feels under pressure to meet perceived high expectations, particularly in light of recent perceived underperformance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He previously enjoyed journaling and reading mystery novels as hobbies, valuing them as forms of relaxation and self-expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyu experiences stress and self-doubt tied to performance pressure, although there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyu feels down and unmotivated due to a perceived drop in efficiency and inability to achieve expected results in math competitions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Xiaoyu's struggle with balancing personal expectations and external support, acknowledging his openness to exploring these issues.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyu is encouraged to review his daily routine, identify when feelings of lowered energy and efficiency arise, and note small achievements.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoyu identify thought patterns contributing to low motivation and encourage acknowledgment of personal achievements.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoyu's feelings of frustration and lack of motivation stemming from unmet expectations in math competitions, and how these feelings impact interactions with supportive parents and teacher. Agreed on reviewing daily routines to identify moments of lowered energy, with focus on writing thoughts to recognize patterns and victories, while fostering a positive and self-kind attitude through journaling. Encouraged maintaining a mindset that acknowledges progress and expressing support for Xiaoyu's reflective journey.
