The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
E is a university student experiencing stress, primarily due to impending exams and misunderstandings with friends, which has led to feeling overwhelmed. E values friendships deeply and hasn't mentioned a formal mental health diagnosis.

[Personal Characteristics]
E enjoys playing the piano, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
E feels constant anxiety triggered by both academic pressures and social conflicts, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] E feels overwhelmed due to upcoming exams and misunderstandings with friends.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes E’s difficulty in managing both academic and social stress, and notes E’s receptiveness to practical strategies for better communication and time management.
[Counseling Assignments:] E should break down study sessions into more manageable parts, and begin drafting thoughts to communicate with friends regarding misunderstandings.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help E manage stress by organizing tasks and fostering open communication with friends.
Counseling Summary:
Explored E's stress due to finals and conflicts with friends, leading to anxiety and difficulty concentrating. Discussed strategies like structuring study sessions and communicating feelings to friends, with a focus on creating a balanced plan for studying and resolving misunderstandings. Agreed on tasks to draft a study and communication plan, emphasizing progress over perfection.
