The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a young individual navigating self-esteem and self-perception challenges, with particular difficulty in appreciating their own worth compared to others.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys creative activities such as sketching, which provides a therapeutic escape, and values perseverance, notably in their dedication to singing practice.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li grapples with negative self-image and self-critical thoughts, experiencing feelings of inadequacy, but there is no formal mental health disorder diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li struggles with negative thoughts about their appearance and abilities, feeling ashamed and caught in a cycle of low self-esteem.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li's harsh self-criticism, which impacts their emotional well-being and social interactions, but also notes Li's openness to explore and challenge these negative thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to jot down negative thoughts and recognize triggers, aiming to gain clarity and distance from these patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Li identify and reframe critical self-assumptions, fostering a more compassionate and balanced self-view.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's self-critical thoughts and their emotional impact, identifying their influence on her social interactions and rehearsals. Agreed on journaling negative thoughts to gain perspective and awareness of triggers, with emphasis on offering self-understanding and patience. Li is encouraged to observe these patterns to discuss further in the next session.
