The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
G is a college student about to start their first internship related to their major, which is causing significant anxiety about performance and expectations. G values personal growth and learning opportunities and is concerned about managing stress effectively during this period.

[Personal Characteristics]  
G finds relaxation in playing the piano, using it to divert attention from stress and enjoy personal time. G also benefits from a supportive family environment.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
G experiences anxiety primarily due to fear of underperformance and unknown professional tasks, though no clinical diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] G is feeling anxious and insecure about their capabilities due to the impending internship.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes G’s tendency to catastrophize and struggle with self-imposed high expectations, also observing G’s openness to challenging these thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] G should jot down recurring thoughts about the internship and later evaluate them to identify patterns and alternative outcomes.
[Session Goal:] Assist G in identifying and reframing automatic negative thoughts related to the internship, reinforcing a mindset focused on growth and learning.
Counseling Summary:
Explored G's anxiety about their upcoming internship, focusing on performance fears and self-doubt. Encouraged G to track and challenge negative thoughts, explore alternative positive outcomes, and integrate relaxing activities like playing the piano for stress management. G reported progress in managing anxiety, with a commitment to using self-reflection and music therapy techniques as strategies for future challenges.
