The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student feeling unsettled after recently starting university, struggling to adjust to the noisy dorm environment and communicating with roommates. He grew up in a quieter household and values personal space and quiet time.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li has a strong interest in painting, enjoys reading, and occasionally takes walks as a way to clear his mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li is experiencing adjustment anxiety related to the transition into university life, with no formal diagnosis given.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels overwhelmed by the new demands and environment of university life.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li's difficulty in adapting to the dorm setting and his openness to exploring activities like painting for stress relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should note down moments of discomfort and triggers, exploring painting and establishing a routine for stress management.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Li cope with transition stress, identify discomfort patterns, and integrate painting and routine for stability.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's challenges adapting to university life, including noise and social difficulties with roommates. Agreed on taking up painting and establishing a daily routine for stress management, and suggested joining a club to connect with others. Assigned Li the task of noting discomfort moments and identifying peaceful activities to guide next session.
