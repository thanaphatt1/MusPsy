The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student experiencing significant stress from academic responsibilities and recent interpersonal conflicts. He has been feeling overwhelmed and is struggling with sleep due to these pressures. Recently, he had a disagreement with a friend, Zhang, which has led to tension and feelings of alienation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys playing the guitar, which he finds therapeutic and stress-relieving.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences stress and anxiety related to academic pressures and interpersonal conflicts, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels stressed and overwhelmed by academic and interpersonal pressures, leading to feelings of alienation and self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's struggle with feeling unsupported and his concerns about peer judgment, yet notes Wang's openness to exploring new coping mechanisms.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should initiate a discussion with Zhang to express his feelings and aim to resolve misunderstandings, while also beginning a routine guitar practice for stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Help Wang manage feelings of alienation by fostering open communication with Zhang and encourage engaging in a stress-relieving hobby, such as playing the guitar.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's feelings of stress and alienation stemming from a class project rejection and a subsequent falling-out with his friend Zhang. Wang agreed to initiate a conversation with Zhang to express his feelings and work towards mending their friendship, focusing on shared interests. Additionally, they discussed incorporating regular guitar playing into Wang's routine for stress relief.
