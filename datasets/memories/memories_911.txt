The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jia is a high school student dealing with feelings of loneliness and guilt after a disagreement with a friend who recently transferred schools. This unresolved conflict is impacting Jia's concentration and mood.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Jia enjoys singing, which functions as a form of self-expression and emotional release.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jia experiences anxiety and self-blame primarily due to the unresolved conflict with a friend, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jia feels down and anxious, especially due to unresolved issues with a transferred friend.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Jia's tendency to dwell on self-blame but appreciates their willingness to introspect and potentially benefit from cognitive behavioral interventions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jia is encouraged to journal thoughts and emotions regarding the unresolved conflict to identify distressing thoughts more clearly.
[Session Goal:] Use cognitive-behavioral techniques to help Jia identify and manage self-critical thoughts, promoting a healthier emotional state and perspective.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Jia's feelings of loneliness and guilt tied to unresolved conflict with a peer. Discussed the impact on concentration and daily life, and agreed to initiate journaling as a tool to identify distressing thoughts. Aim is to help manage emotions through reflective writing.
