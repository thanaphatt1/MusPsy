The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoya is a young individual who feels neglected since the birth of her sister, experiencing tension and arguments with her mother. She's dealing with feelings of being less important in the family and has expressed feeling overwhelmed and alone.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoya enjoys drawing as a form of self-expression and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoya is experiencing emotional distress due to perceived neglect from her family, leading to feelings of isolation and decreased familial bond.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoya feels overwhelmed and isolated due to changes in family dynamics since her sibling’s birth.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoya's struggle with feelings of neglect and appreciates her willingness to engage in therapeutic activities like drawing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoya should dedicate time each evening to drawing, particularly when she feels stressed, as a means of finding relief and self-expression.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Xiaoya manage her emotions, reconnect with activities she enjoys, and establish a daily routine that includes stress-relieving activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoya's feelings of isolation and conflict with her mother following her sister's birth. Encouraged Xiaoya to reconnect with her passion for drawing as a stress-relief strategy, recommending reserving specific time for this activity to potentially improve emotional well-being and reduce tension in family interactions. Agreed to monitor the impact of this approach on Xiaoya's emotions and family dynamics over the coming week.
