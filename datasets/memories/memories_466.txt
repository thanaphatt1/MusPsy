The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei, a professional in the software development field, is experiencing stress from a major project setback and strained relationship dynamics. He places a strong emphasis on professional success and maintaining harmonious personal relationships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys cycling and reading, using these activities to alleviate stress and gain mental clarity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences stress and self-doubt, particularly when faced with setbacks, but there is no record of a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei is feeling down and unmotivated due to recent challenges at work and in personal relationships.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang Wei's fluctuating self-esteem impacted by work and relationship stress, highlighting his openness to introspection for stress management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei should keep a journal documenting his feelings daily to identify recurring thoughts and emotional patterns for future discussions.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhang Wei recognize and challenge negative thought patterns through journaling and increase awareness of emotional triggers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang Wei's feelings of inadequacy and stress following a work project setback, as well as the associated impact on his personal life. Agreed on daily journaling to process emotions and identify patterns in stress and self-doubt triggers, with plans to discuss insights in the next session. Emphasized awareness and the validity of his feelings as a foundation for progress.
