The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Wang is a university student experiencing significant stress and anxiety related to academic performance, particularly after not achieving desired exam results and scholarships. Xiao Wang feels pressure from self and perceived expectations from family and peers, leading to a loss of interest in previously enjoyed activities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Wang enjoys social activities like playing badminton, which helps in stress relief and reconnecting with friends.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Wang is experiencing stress and anxiety primarily attributable to academic pressures, with recurring self-critical thoughts, though no formal diagnosis is recorded.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Wang is feeling low and pressured by not meeting personal academic expectations, affecting concentration and social interest.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiao Wang's tendency to internalize perceived expectations and compare self unfavorably to peers, yet sees openness to exploring thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Wang should write down thoughts and feelings daily, examining those that seem overly harsh or unreasonable.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to help Xiao Wang identify and challenge negative thoughts while integrating enjoyable activities like badminton for stress relief.
Counseling Summary:
Xiao Wang expressed feelings of inadequacy and pressure from not meeting personal and perceived family expectations, which affected his academic and social engagement. The consultant encouraged Xiao Wang to document his thoughts daily to challenge negative perceptions and advised incorporating activities like badminton to add joy and reduce stress. By promoting self-compassion and recalling past achievements, the consultant aimed to help Xiao Wang redefine his self-evaluation and cope better with external pressures.
