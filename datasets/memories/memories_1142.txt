The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyu is a university student dealing with stress from preparing for graduate school exams and experiencing anxiety due to perceived social distance from classmates. Xiaoyu values friendships and fears misunderstandings with peers.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoyu finds relaxation in painting, using it as a creative outlet to relieve stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyu exhibits patterns of anxiety notably related to social interactions and academic pressures, although there is no formal diagnosis of any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyu feels anxious and stressed due to changes in social dynamics with classmates and upcoming graduate school exams.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoyu’s sensitivity to social cues and a tendency toward negative assumptions about peers' attitudes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyu should take short breaks from studying and try deep breathing exercises or brief walks to manage stress levels.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiaoyu manage social anxiety and academic stress by incorporating breaks and relaxation techniques.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling sessions explored Xiaoyu's feelings of anxiety stemming from strained relationships with classmates and the pressure of graduate school exams. Through structured activities including note-taking, open communication with peers, and engaging in hobbies like painting, Xiaoyu learned to manage stress and overcome negative thought patterns. As a result, Xiaoyu gained confidence in maintaining social connections and balancing emotional well-being with academic commitments.
