The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a student experiencing stress primarily from academic pressures, especially due to a strict professor. This has led Wei to fear making mistakes in class and being judged publicly by peers. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei enjoys photography as a stress-relief activity, offering a creative outlet outside of academic challenges. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei struggles with anxiety particularly related to academic settings, although no formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei is afraid and tense due to fear of judgment from a strict professor in class.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wei's sensitivity to criticism and high self-expectations but acknowledges Wei's willingness to explore anxiety management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei should attempt to identify specific anxiety-inducing thoughts related to class participation and document them.
[Session Goal:] Help Wei manage academic stress using CBT techniques by challenging irrational fears and fostering a more balanced perspective.

---
Counseling Summary:
Wei discussed his anxiety stemming from the fear of criticism by a strict professor, which led to stress and avoidance in class participation. To address this, Wei started challenging negative predictions by focusing on effort rather than perfection and planned to gradually face fears through small contributions and advance preparation. Additionally, engaging in photography as a pastime was identified as a way to maintain emotional balance and relieve stress.
