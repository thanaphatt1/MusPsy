The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Huahua is a student who recently experienced a disagreement with a classmate, leading to feelings of unease and regret. They lost their temper during a class project, which they now reflect on as a regrettable incident.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Huahua enjoys reading and walks, which serve as relaxation activities for them.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Huahua experiences impulses and regrets in social situations, often feeling stressed and overwhelmed, yet no formal mental health diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Huahua feels uneasy and regretful about a recent argument with a classmate, impacting their interactions with peers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Huahua's tendency to ruminate on feelings of impulsivity and judgment but recognizes their openness to exploring the situation and learning from it.
[Counseling Assignments:] Huahua should reflect on calmer reactions in past situations and consider the triggers of their discomfort, writing down thoughts when they arise.
[Session Goal:] Help Huahua manage emotional reactions to stress using CBT techniques, emphasizing self-reflection to enhance awareness of thought patterns.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session with Huahua focused on managing regret and anxiety resulting from a recent conflict with a classmate. Huahua was encouraged to observe and note down challenging thoughts and feelings of being judged, while also identifying patterns related to stress triggers. As a strategy, Huahua will challenge these thoughts with evidence-based questioning and pay attention to positive interactions to bolster confidence and reduce stress.
