The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ji is a young individual who recently experienced a shift in family dynamics due to the arrival of a baby brother, causing feelings of confusion and being left out. Ji values connections with family and seeks ways to maintain a balanced relationship amidst these changes.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ji enjoys creative activities like drawing and values time spent with family, aiming to strengthen these connections.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ji feels left out and struggles with adjusting to family changes, though there is no formal diagnosis of any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ji feels confused and somewhat excluded from family life due to the attention focused on their baby brother.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Ji's openness to discussing their feelings and willingness to explore ways to reconnect with family members.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Ji should set aside time for special activities with their mom to share daily experiences, aiming to strengthen their bond.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Ji express feelings of exclusion constructively and find ways to reconnect with family.
Counseling Summary:
The session focused on Ji's feelings of confusion and exclusion due to changes in family dynamics following the arrival of a baby brother. The counselor suggested Ji set aside special time with his mother to strengthen their bond and encouraged him to reflect on moments that make him feel valued and loved within the family. They agreed to revisit conversation strategies to express Ji's feelings in the next session.
