The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zheng, an office worker whose work schedule has changed, now commutes through busy areas that induce anxiety. Zheng previously encountered someone in mourning clothes, which was distressing, leading to ongoing unease.

[Personal Characteristics]
He enjoys running, finding it clears his mind and reduces stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zheng experiences anxiety triggered by commutes and reminders of distressing events, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zheng feels unsettled and anxious due to changes in their commute and a distressing past encounter.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zheng's associations of daily events with anxiety, and a readiness to explore awareness of thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zheng is asked to note instances of anxiety during their commute and identify associated thoughts and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Help Zheng better understand the relationship between their experiences and feelings using CBT methods like thought tracking.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zheng's anxiety related to a specific unsettling encounter during their commute and its impact on their daily routine, noticing a pattern of irrational negative thoughts and heightened alertness. The consultant suggested mindfulness exercises and affirmations to manage these anxiety-inducing thoughts, with the aim of interrupting the pattern and grounding Zheng in the present. Zheng agreed to implement these strategies consistently and track any changes, aiming to gradually ease their commute-related anxieties.
