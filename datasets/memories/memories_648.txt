The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xi is experiencing emotional distress due to the recent passing of a cousin from a heart attack. This loss is affecting their ability to focus on studies and contributing to feelings of not meeting familial expectations, particularly their father's.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xi finds joy and relaxation in sketching, though they've struggled to engage in this activity following recent events.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xi experiences emotional overwhelm stemming from grief and guilt, negatively impacting daily activities, with no mention of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xi feels overwhelmed by grief and sadness, struggling with guilt over not spending more time with their cousin.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xi's significant emotional burden and openness to exploring supportive strategies to process grief.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xi should dedicate time to sketching as a creative outlet and express feelings through this medium weekly.
[Session Goal:] Support Xi in processing grief by fostering self-compassion and integrating relaxing activities such as sketching into their routine.
Counseling Summary:
Xi shared feelings of sadness and overwhelm following the passing of their cousin, contributing to struggles in academic focus and living up to paternal expectations. The consultant encouraged Xi to explore self-kindness and balanced thoughts, suggesting sketching as a therapeutic outlet for emotional expression. Xi agreed to integrate this creative activity into their weekly routine, aiming for an hour on weekends, and to approach this journey with compassion moving forward.
