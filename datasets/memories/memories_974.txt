The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Miao is a student facing various challenges related to school performance and interpersonal relationships. Miao has recently experienced disciplinary action at school, which heightened feelings of embarrassment and self-doubt.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Miao finds joy and solace in drawing, which often serves as a way to cope with stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Miao exhibits signs of anxiety related to academic performance and peer perceptions but does not have a formal diagnosis of any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Miao is feeling down and upset due to recent trouble at school, which has impacted motivation and self-esteem.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Miao's critical self-view and tendency to internalize mistakes as personal failures, yet notes a willingness to explore constructive coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Miao is encouraged to note instances of feeling overwhelmed and to practice deep breathing exercises during these times to manage stress.
[Session Goal:] Help Miao identify and challenge negative thought patterns using CBT techniques, and foster awareness of stressors and physical responses to them.
Counseling Summary:
Miao expressed feeling distressed due to a negative incident at school, which impacted her motivation and academic performance. The counselor guided her to become aware of her thoughts and physical stress responses, suggesting relaxation exercises like deep breathing, and encouraged her to recall past successes to counter negative assumptions. Miao agreed to engage in these strategies, aiming to manage her stress and anxiety better.
