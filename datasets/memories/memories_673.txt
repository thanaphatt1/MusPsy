The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student experiencing significant anxiety, especially around young males, which affects their academic focus and social interactions. Zhang tries to avoid interactions with male classmates and instructors due to these feelings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys reading novels, which they find to be a helpful escape and stress-relief activity. Zhang is also considering activities like meditation or yoga to maintain calmness.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety specifically associated with interactions involving male peers and authority figures, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious and uncomfortable around young males, leading to isolation and difficulties in focusing during classes.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's hesitancy in male-dominated settings and the impact on their academic performance but also sees willingness to explore and manage these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to journal situations that trigger anxiety, noting thoughts and feelings to identify specific triggers.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhang recognize anxiety triggers and begin to understand patterns in thought processes, using CBT to manage anxiety and improve social interactions.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's anxiety and discomfort around young males, particularly affecting focus during classes. Agreed on journaling specific anxiety-inducing situations to identify triggers, with the goal of better understanding and managing these emotions.
