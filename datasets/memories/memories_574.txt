The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student stressed about not being able to graduate due to incomplete credits and is concerned about family expectations. The user often worries about disappointing their family, who plays an important role in their life. 

[Personal Characteristics]
The user is determined and values the support of friends, often being there for them when needed.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety and stress related to academic pressures, with no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed and anxious about not completing credits and is concerned about family expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes strong family influence contributing to the user's stress and notes the user's openness to understanding and managing stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is advised to keep a journal of thoughts and feelings during stressful moments to identify thought patterns.
[Session Goal:] Help the user understand the relationship between thoughts, emotions, and behaviors, focusing on managing stress through CBT techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's stress about graduating due to incomplete credits and family expectations, revealing underlying anxiety and negative self-talk. Encouraged journaling to track thoughts and emotions, and discussed recalling past successes during negativity to shift perception of setbacks from failures to learning opportunities. Agreed to continue journaling to monitor realizations and perspective shifts, aiming to reduce stress by redefining thoughts and embracing small, positive changes.
