The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Long is a university student feeling overwhelmed by his current major and lack of connection with roommates. He expresses disinterest in his courses and a sense of loneliness in his living situation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Long enjoys painting, a hobby he has not practiced recently but is considering reigniting as a stress relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Long is experiencing low mood and questioning potential depression due to disconnection and unfulfilled academic pursuits. There is no formal diagnosis provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Long feels overwhelmed and isolated, struggling with his course interest and social connections.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Long's dissatisfaction with his current major and social isolation but also recognizes his openness to potential positive changes through hobbies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Reflect on one positive thing each day and consider resuming painting as a form of emotional expression.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Long to identify daily positives, integrate relaxing activities like painting, and explore avenues for social interaction through shared interests.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Long's feelings of overwhelm and disconnection related to university courses and distant roommates, contributing to low mood and questions about depression. Agreed on reflecting on daily positives and revisiting painting as a creative outlet to manage emotions, with the suggestion to join an arts group for social connection. Encouraged ongoing mindfulness by focusing on moments that bring joy or relaxation to gradually shift perspective.
