The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Rui is a high school student experiencing feelings of exclusion and stress at home since their dad's remarriage and the birth of a new sibling. Rui often feels left out as family dynamics shift, impacting both academic performance and social interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]
Rui enjoys skateboarding, which serves as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Rui is experiencing anxiety and feelings of isolation due to changes in family dynamics, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Rui feels overwhelmed and disconnected from the family since their dad's remarriage and the arrival of a new sibling.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist gathers that Rui feels marginalized within the family; however, there's an inclination towards openness and suggests potential in expressing feelings to family members.
[Counseling Assignments:] Rui should identify specific situations that intensify feelings of exclusion and keep a record of these occurrences for discussion.
[Session Goal:] Explore CBT strategies to help Rui manage feelings of alienation and to initiate gradual, open communication with family members.
Counseling Summary:
Rui has been feeling excluded and overwhelmed due to changes in family dynamics following his father's remarriage and the birth of a new sibling. The consultant encouraged Rui to explore specific situations that amplify these feelings and suggested starting small initiatives, like a family movie night, to foster reconnection. Rui was also advised to dedicate regular time to skateboarding for stress management and to keep notes on when he feels connected or disconnected from his family, to facilitate further discussion in future sessions.
