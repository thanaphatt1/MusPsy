The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Miss Zhang is a devoted mother overwhelmed by the stress of raising her daughter and ongoing scrutiny from social services. She feels anxious and judged, aware of previous notable struggles, and remains committed to progressing for her daughter's sake.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Miss Zhang used to enjoy reading, a hobby she wants to reincorporate into her routine for relaxation and escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Miss Zhang experiences anxiety under external pressure, particularly from social services evaluations of her parenting. She is keen on countering these feelings by reaffirming her competencies as a parent.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Miss Zhang feels overwhelmed and anxious, fearing constant judgment on her parenting abilities from social services.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Miss Zhang’s significant stress from perceived judgment but notices her openness to internalize and manage these thoughts non-defensively.
[Counseling Assignments:] Begin by dedicating five minutes each day to reading for relaxation and document recurring thoughts that highlight stress triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Miss Zhang reinterpret stress-inducing evaluations and facilitate relaxation through engaging in routine activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
The consultation focused on Miss Zhang's feelings of anxiety and judgment due to interactions with social services, exploring how these thoughts impact her emotions and linger after meetings. The consultant encouraged Miss Zhang to share her feelings with a support group or trusted individuals to lessen her burden and suggested incorporating daily reading as a small, manageable self-care practice to help distract and alleviate stress. Miss Zhang agreed to start with five minutes of reading per day and to take note of her thought patterns to foster self-awareness and healthier thinking.
