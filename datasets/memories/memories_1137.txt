The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a young man who enjoys drift boarding. Recently, he experienced emotional turmoil in his relationships, feeling neglected by his long-distance boyfriend and being surprised by a friend's new relationship status. These incidents have left Wang confused and seeking clarity on his emotions and relationships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang has a keen interest in reading and practicing calligraphy, which he finds relaxing.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences emotional distress and confusion primarily due to relational dynamics. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels upset and confused due to unexpected developments in his relationships, leaving him unsure about his feelings and desires.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's struggle with mixed emotions and recognizes his openness to introspection and uncovering genuine feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to note down his feelings about both relationships, reflecting honestly to guide future discussions.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Wang gain clarity on his genuine feelings and explore relational desires, fostering open and honest self-reflection.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's emotional turmoil stemming from an unexpected relationship revelation and the complexities of her long-distance relationship. Agreed on engaging in self-reflection about her feelings and relationship needs as a task to gain clarity. Emphasized the importance of introspection to guide future decisions and alleviate confusion.
