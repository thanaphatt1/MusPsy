The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
E is a university student experiencing emotional distress after a recent relationship ended due to disapproval from their partner's parents. This has affected E's self-esteem and focus on studies. E values friendships and academic performance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
E enjoys hiking, reading, and music as hobbies and finds these activities soothing. E also has supportive and understanding friendships that provide emotional support.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
E experiences low self-esteem and negative self-worth thoughts triggered by relationship issues and social comparisons, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] E feels down and confused, grappling with feelings of rejection and low self-worth after a relationship's end.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes E's sincere effort to cope and openness to exploring emotional patterns and triggers within the context of recent personal experiences.
[Counseling Assignments:] E should keep a daily journal to document thoughts and feelings, aiming to spot emotional patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Help E understand the connection between thoughts and emotions and develop initial strategies for emotional awareness and self-care through cognitive reflection.
Counseling Summary:
E is experiencing feelings of rejection and low self-esteem following a relationship breakup that was influenced by external disapproval, leading to negative thinking patterns of inadequacy and withdrawal. Through sessions with the consultant, E is encouraged to keep a journal, identify triggers, challenge automatic negative thoughts, and build positive connections and a routine that incorporates uplifting activities such as hiking. By planning to engage more with friends and cultivating self-awareness and resilience, E aims to transform emotional responses and reinforce self-worth.
