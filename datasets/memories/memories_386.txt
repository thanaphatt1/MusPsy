The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mr. Li is a professional experiencing significant stress after a failure in a major work project, leading to pressure from superiors. He is concerned about how this might impact his self-confidence and position at work.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing basketball, which provides a mental release and helps him stay active.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mr. Li experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by professional pressures, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mr. Li feels nervous and overwhelmed due to recent project failure and the pressure from colleagues and superiors.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Mr. Li's self-doubt and anticipation of negative judgments but notes a willingness to reflect on his thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mr. Li should document stress-related feelings throughout the week and try to integrate short walks as a stress-relief activity.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Mr. Li manage stress triggers, identify specific negative thought patterns, and encourage engagement in relaxing activities like walking.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mr. Li's stress and anxiety related to a recent work project failure and pressure from superiors, leading to self-doubt and fear of further mistakes. Agreed on tracking stressful feelings and incorporating short walks for stress relief. Emphasized the importance of small, consistent changes for positive outcomes.
