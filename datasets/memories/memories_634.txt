The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
A is a university graduate feeling overwhelmed by career decisions, with an offer from a shipping company that seems uncertain due to a lack of family guidance in this field.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
A experiences anxiety linked to career indecision and uncertainty about future paths but does not have a formal diagnosis.

[Personal Characteristics]  
A has a strong interest in stability, growth opportunities, logistics, and problem-solving.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] A feels overwhelmed by career options and the upcoming transition after graduation, leading to anxiety about making the wrong choice.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices A's hesitancy to make decisions and an anxiety stemming from self-imposed doubts, but also sees hope in A's ability to recognize personal values.
[Counseling Assignments:] A is tasked with writing down which aspects of the job align with personal values and goals to enhance clarity.
[Session Goal:] Help A identify values and personal goals while managing the stress associated with fear of making incorrect career decisions.
Counseling Summary:
The sessions explored A's career-related anxiety due to uncertainty about accepting a job offer that didn't align with their passions. A was guided to identify personal values, challenge extreme thoughts, and explore alternative career paths, ultimately showing interest in logistics and project management. Through these sessions, A gained a more balanced decision-making approach and planned to network and seek opportunities aligned with their interests, feeling more confident in handling future career decisions.
