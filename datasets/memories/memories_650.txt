The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background

[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He also enjoys painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels anxious about interactions with roommates due to his assumptions, leading to stress in social settings.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Liu's recognition of assumption-based thoughts contributing to stress and his openness to addressing them with evidence-based thinking.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should challenge assumptions by seeking evidence in social interactions and joining study groups more frequently.
[Session Goal:] Help Liu identify and question automatic negative thoughts, fostering a more evidence-based approach to social interactions.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Liu reflected on how keeping a journal revealed patterns of anxiety stemming from assumptions about others' perceptions. The consultant encouraged Liu to challenge these assumptions by seeking evidence of positive interactions with roommates and engaging more actively in study groups to build connections. Liu set goals to attend more study sessions and track thoughts to reduce anxiety about social interactions, aiming for more meaningful connections while cautiously optimistic about the progress.
