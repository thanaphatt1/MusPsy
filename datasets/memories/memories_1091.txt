The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhuang Jia is a young individual who fabricated a story about being robbed to gain attention, reflecting feelings of neglect from busy parents. He now grapples with anxiety and guilt due to the situation escalating. 

[Personal Characteristics]
He enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming and therapeutic activity for him.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhuang Jia experiences anxiety and guilt stemming from social and school pressures involving his fabricated story, yet he shows no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhuang Jia feels anxious and guilty about the impact of his choices at school, fearing judgment from peers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhuang Jia's heightened worry about peer judgment but also his openness to explore these emotions and consider acknowledging his actions to build trust.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhuang Jia should attempt to use drawing as a relaxation activity regularly and journal recurring thoughts to better understand his emotional responses.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to assist Zhuang Jia in managing anxiety and guilt from social situations, promoting insights through structured self-reflection and creativity.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhuang Jia's feelings of stress and guilt after fabricating a story for attention due to feeling neglected by busy parents. Discussed the fear of judgment at school and strategized ways to manage anxiety, including talking to a trusted teacher, engaging in drawing as emotional relief, and journaling to process emotions objectively. Agreed to continue exploring these methods and sharing progress in future sessions.
