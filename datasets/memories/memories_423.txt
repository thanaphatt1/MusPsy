The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a professional working on a team project that has been delayed, leading to significant stress. The challenges at work are compromising Wei's work-life balance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei has interests in running, which is used as a stress relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei experiences anxiety and stress triggered by work-related pressures, with a tendency towards self-blame during team projects.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels stressed and overwhelmed due to project delays and collaboration issues at work.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wei's struggle with maintaining balance between professional responsibilities and personal life, along with pronounced self-imposed pressure.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei is to track moments of anxiety and the accompanying thoughts, identifying patterns to discuss in future sessions.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Wei manage stress, identify stress-inducing thoughts, and promote relaxation activities such as running.
Counseling Summary:
The session with Wei focused on identifying stress and anxiety patterns related to work challenges, particularly regarding negative self-talk and feelings of sole responsibility. Wei agreed to track anxious moments and accompanying thoughts, practice differentiating assumptions from facts, and incorporate mindfulness techniques like deep breathing to better manage pressure and acknowledge shared responsibilities in team settings. The approach emphasizes gradual insight and mindfulness practice to enhance stress management and improve work-life balance.
