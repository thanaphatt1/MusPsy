The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a university student encountering stress primarily due to interpersonal conflicts within his dorm environment and class, with the most recent incident involving a disagreement over a group project. Wei is worried about the distance this has created between him and his dorm mates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei enjoys jogging, which serves as a means to relieve stress, and expresses concerns about social interactions impacting his emotional well-being.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei experiences anxiety tied to perceived judgments from peers and stress due to unresolved interpersonal conflicts, though no formal mental health disorder diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei is feeling stressed and anxious about social isolation resulting from a disagreement with a classmate over workload distribution in a group project.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wei's internalization of the conflict and his openness to understanding his emotional reactions as a pathway to resolution.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei is encouraged to observe and jot down emotions and triggers related to the conflict, focusing on moments of support and tension in interactions.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Wei clarify his emotional responses to the situation, facilitate social harmony, and build positive interaction pathways.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wei's conflict with classmates causing stress and isolation, focusing on identifying emotional triggers and thought patterns contributing to anxiety. Agreed on observing both negative reactions and supportive dorm interactions to understand underlying dynamics. Planned to analyze recurring negative thoughts and assumptions about judgment in future sessions to aid emotional clarity and anxiety reduction.
