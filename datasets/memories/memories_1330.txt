The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaozhang is a student dealing with stress and anxiety, particularly in classroom settings after a recent negative experience in an English class. Xiaozhang is concerned about being judged or embarrassed in academic situations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaozhang enjoys reading novels, which serves as a form of relaxation and escape.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaozhang experiences anxiety, mostly triggered by academic pressures and social interactions, but there's no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaozhang feels overwhelmed and scared of attending classes due to fear of being embarrassed or judged.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Xiaozhang’s anxiety is linked to self-perceived judgment and embarrassment in social or academic settings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaozhang is encouraged to journal about feelings after each class to identify anxiety triggers and explore relaxation methods like focusing on breathing.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to identify thought patterns triggering anxiety and introduce relaxation techniques as coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaozhang shared experiencing significant anxiety in class after feeling embarrassed during an English class incident, which has affected his willingness to attend classes. The consultant encouraged Xiaozhang to try journaling about his feelings after each class to identify patterns and explore mindfulness techniques, such as focused breathing, to manage anxiety. Xiaozhang agreed to incorporate these strategies and the consultant expressed support and plans to review progress in the next session.
