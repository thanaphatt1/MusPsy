The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaowu is a student facing stress, particularly after not achieving the "Three Good Student" title, which has led to pressure from both school and their family. Xiaowu values meeting expectations and is concerned about disappointing their parents.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaowu enjoys basketball and music, which serve as outlets for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaowu experiences significant stress and anxiety, primarily due to academic and familial expectations, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaowu feels anxious and lost after not receiving an academic title, questioning their self-worth and fearing to disappoint their parents.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaowu's susceptibility to external expectations, which exacerbates self-doubt, but also observes Xiaowu's openness to new coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaowu is tasked with documenting situations of pressure and associated thoughts to identify triggers and recurring themes.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Xiaowu identify and manage stress-related thoughts while fostering self-appreciation over self-criticism.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaowu's stress and anxiety related to high academic and parental expectations, leading to self-doubt and comparison with peers. Agreed on noting down daily pressures, thoughts, and positive achievements to better understand their emotional impact and shift focus from self-criticism to self-appreciation. Reflecting on positive aspects aims to encourage a balanced perspective and reduce stress over time.
