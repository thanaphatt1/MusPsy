The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Qi is a medical professional experiencing significant stress due to failing a medical licensing exam, affecting financial bonuses and career-related self-worth. Qi expressed anxiety about their career path and a fear of letting their family down, coupled with physical fatigue affecting work performance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Qi enjoys reading mystery novels and has an interest in writing, viewing these as potential outlets for relaxation and creative expression. Additionally, Qi finds fulfillment in cooking, using it as a way to unwind from professional stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Qi exhibits anxiety and stress primarily attributable to career setbacks and internal self-criticism, with a lack of a formal diagnosis of a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Qi feels anxious and overwhelmed, plagued by self-doubt after not passing their exam, which magnifies professional insecurities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Qi's tendency to amplify negative outcomes, yet observes Qi’s willingness to engage in introspection and constructive activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Contrive to jot down thoughts and emotions in a journal, particularly insights about anxiety triggers and the positive actions taken, providing clarity and perspective.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Qi recognize and dismantle negative thought patterns, boosting confidence in handling stress by focusing on strengths and achievements.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Qi's anxiety and stress following a failed medical licensing exam, impacting work performance and self-doubt about career choice. Qi agreed to journal daily thoughts and emotions to identify patterns and to focus on acknowledging positive experiences, aiming to manage stress and improve self-perception over time. Encouragement was given for incorporating these reflections into a consistent practice to build resilience and shift perspective positively.
