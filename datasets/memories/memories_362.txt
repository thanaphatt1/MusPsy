The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang San is a professional dealing with substantial pressure at work, particularly related to managing a critical company project. Zhang San frequently worries about potential errors, concerns about being judged by colleagues, and strives for maintaining high performance standards.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing chess, which serves as a means to relieve stress and enjoys the strategic elements the game offers.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang San is experiencing anxiety and stress related to job pressure, focusing strongly on decision-making scenarios and the fear of negative outcomes.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang San feels anxious and overwhelmed by the responsibility of successfully managing an important project at work.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang San's high self-imposed standards and susceptibility to self-doubt and comparison with others.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang San should note past work successes whenever doubts arise, and consider discussing pressures with supportive colleagues for diverse perspectives.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Zhang San reframe expectations as flexible guidelines, encourage self-reflection on past successes, and support using hobbies like chess for stress relief.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang San's anxiety and self-doubt amid work pressures, emphasizing his tendency to set high standards and imagine worst-case scenarios. Encouraged him to view expectations as flexible guidelines, reflect on past successes, and seek support from colleagues, alongside maintaining personal hobbies like chess for stress relief. Zhang San agreed to these actionable strategies to reframe his internal dialogue and foster emotional balance.
