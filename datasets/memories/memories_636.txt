The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
B is a prospective graduate facing significant stress due to familial tension regarding career choices, particularly interested in pursuing a maritime career against their parents' wishes. The conflict has led to emotional turmoil as B balances personal aspirations with familial expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
B has a passion for exploration and learning about maritime activities, finding solace in running as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
B exhibits stress and anxiety primarily triggered by family disputes and career uncertainties, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] B feels conflicted and stressed due to tension with their parents over career decisions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes B's internal conflict between following personal passion and fulfilling parental expectations, noting B's genuine engagement and reflection on values.
[Counseling Assignments:] B is encouraged to reflect on the core reasons behind their career choice and consider how it aligns with personal values.
[Session Goal:] Explore B's career decision-making process through CBT, focusing on aligning personal values with career aspirations, while preparing to communicate effectively with their parents.
Counseling Summary:
B expressed inner conflict and stress stemming from disagreements with parents about career choices; the counselor helped B explore underlying values and beliefs, maintain open communication, and manage stress through physical activities like running. B developed constructive strategies to address parental concerns and gained clarity on career aspirations while incorporating stress-relief activities into a routine. Throughout the sessions, B built confidence in communication and learned to navigate future challenges with openness and self-care.
