The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mr. Wang, a middle-aged man, experienced a stressful event where he accidentally collided with a little girl in a park, leading to feelings of guilt and anxiety. He is committed to understanding and addressing these emotions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys gardening, which serves as a therapeutic activity and helps him feel connected to nature. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mr. Wang is dealing with anxiety and guilt related to the park incident, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mr. Wang feels significant stress and guilt due to a recent accidental interaction in public, leading to ongoing anxiety about how others perceive him.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Mr. Wang's tendency to assume blame and overestimate negative perceptions from others, along with his willingness to address these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mr. Wang should write down thoughts related to the incident to explore and understand the feelings of guilt and anxiety.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to identify and challenge negative thoughts, replacing them with balanced perspectives, while encouraging engagement in relaxing activities like gardening.
Counseling Summary:
Mr. Wang expressed feelings of guilt and anxiety after accidentally colliding with a girl in the park, fearing negative judgment from others. In counseling, he agreed to write a letter to the girl's family to express regret, clear up misunderstandings, and offer help, as well as to engage in gardening as a stress-relief activity. Through the sessions, Mr. Wang learned to acknowledge his feelings, avoid negative assumptions, and consider open communication as key strategies for future challenges.
