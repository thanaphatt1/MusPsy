The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student dealing with anxiety and feelings of neglect, primarily related to academic performance. After receiving exam scores below their usual standards, Li feels overlooked by teachers and is anxious about the consequences on interpersonal relationships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys calligraphy and finds solace in engaging in the activity as a stress-relief method.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li is experiencing anxiety influenced by perceived inadequacies and fears of being ignored, without a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels anxious and worthless due to poor exam results and perceived neglect from teachers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Li's hesitancy to voice their struggles to others but recognizes their openness to identifying moments of feeling disregarded.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is tasked with noting instances where they felt ignored and reflecting on moments of support from family.
[Session Goal:] Help Li develop perspective and emotional resilience strategies using CBT, focusing on recognizing supportive interactions and noted feelings of neglect.
Counseling Summary:
Li shared feelings of low self-esteem and anxiety stemming from poor exam scores and perceived neglect by teachers, leading to social withdrawal. The consultant encouraged Li to jot down moments of feeling overlooked and balance them with supportive interactions from family to develop a balanced perspective, while also reflecting on past achievements to boost confidence. Strategies for gradual improvement included journaling contrasting experiences and regular self-reassurance to reinforce self-worth and resilience.
