The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student who feels pressures related to social interactions and relationships. Most recently, he has been experiencing feelings of loneliness and isolation, especially as many of his peers have entered into relationships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing the guitar, which serves as a hobby and a source of emotional expression and relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety around social situations, coupled with a fear of rejection, although there is no formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels lonely and inadequate, largely due to unsuccessful attempts at forming romantic relationships and comparing himself to peers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's struggle with self-doubt and fear of rejection, highlighting Wang's openness to exploring these feelings and considering behavioral changes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should observe and document his feelings related to rejection and note any recurring thoughts, in addition to engaging in activities that he enjoys, such as playing the guitar.
[Session Goal:] Assist Wang in identifying and managing self-doubt through CBT by utilizing thought records and engaging in fulfilling activities that enhance self-worth.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's feelings of loneliness and inadequacy stemming from repeated rejections and the pressure of social expectations. Agreed on strategies such as observing social interactions before engaging, practicing guitar for emotional self-care, and journaling thoughts and small victories to address self-doubt and alleviate social anxiety. Emphasized gradual exposure to social settings and the importance of patience and self-compassion in Wang's journey towards building connections and managing anxiety.
