The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei recently transitioned to a new job at a larger company, which has been a significant source of stress and uncertainty regarding his adaptability and ability to meet expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys playing the guitar and has shown interest in sports but has yet to engage actively in related activities due to time constraints.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei's experiences anxiety and self-doubt primarily triggered by work-related challenges and team culture adjustment. There is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels stressed and uncertain about adapting to the demands and culture of his new job.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang Wei's challenges with self-doubt and recognition of successes, yet notes readiness to engage in new coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei should begin journaling positive work moments and seek opportunities to join team activities to foster deeper connections.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Zhang Wei manage self-doubt, recognize achievements, and encourage engagement in team-building activities.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session with Zhang Wei focused on managing stress and self-doubt related to his new job. Zhang Wei was encouraged to reflect on positive work moments and contributions to build confidence and start a journal for these instances. The session also addressed challenges in fitting into the team, suggesting joining colleagues in shared interests, like sports, to foster connections, and reclaiming personal hobbies, like playing the guitar, to balance work stress and enhance well-being.
