The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Little T is a student experiencing confusion and feeling down, particularly with difficulties around a school science project. Little T often feels less competent compared to peers, and lacks direct support since a parent is frequently busy.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Little T reports feelings of anxiety and stress in group settings, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Little T has an interest in drawing, and finds it a relaxing activity, which can be utilized as part of stress management.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Little T feels overwhelmed and inadequate concerning a group science project and uncertain about their role within it.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Little T's self-doubt and the challenge of not having parental support due to busy schedules but also recognizes Little T's willingness to take small actionable steps for improvement.
[Counseling Assignments:] Little T should focus on breaking the project into manageable parts and practice initiating conversations with group members to improve collaboration.
[Session Goal:] Help Little T reduce project-related anxiety through task breakdowns and foster open communication with group members for better teamwork.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Little T's feelings of confusion and anxiety surrounding a school science project, focusing on challenges with understanding the topic and group dynamics. Agreed on practical steps, including focusing on one aspect of the project at a time and practicing conversations with group members to ease communication-related anxiety. Encouraged Little T to recall positive interactions with the group and promised to continue supporting their progress in future sessions.
