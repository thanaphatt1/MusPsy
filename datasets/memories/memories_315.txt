The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is experiencing significant stress due to demanding work projects, feeling anxious about his abilities despite recent professional success. He values his family's time but struggles to balance work and personal life.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys running and sketching, finding them effective ways to relieve stress and express creativity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei exhibits self-doubt and anxiety linked to work pressures, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels stressed and anxious, questioning his professional capabilities despite recent successes.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Zhang Wei's tendency to internalize stress and self-doubt, noting his openness to developing coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei should note when self-doubt arises, share work pressures with a trusted colleague, and plan family activities to enhance work-life balance.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Zhang Wei manage work-related stress and self-doubt, facilitating a healthier balance between professional and personal life.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang Wei discussed feeling stress and self-doubt following a successful but demanding work project, and the consultant guided him to recognize the link between these emotions and his professional achievements. They agreed on two small tasks: keeping a log of self-doubt triggers, and initiating a supportive conversation with a trusted colleague. To alleviate stress and improve work-life balance, Zhang Wei was encouraged to reintroduce family activities, such as weekend ice cream outings, to enhance personal connections and reduce anxiety.
