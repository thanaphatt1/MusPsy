The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Li is a child who recently changed schools, feeling overwhelmed and lost in the new environment. Xiao Li finds it challenging to connect with classmates and feels anxious about fitting in. 

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiao Li enjoys drawing, which serves as both a hobby and a potential avenue for social connection.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Li expresses feelings of anxiety and social integration challenges, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Li feels overwhelmed and unsure about how to connect with new classmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiao Li's hesitance to integrate and acknowledges their openness to exploring ways to connect with peers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Li is tasked with observing classmates during breaks to identify potential activities to join.
[Session Goal:] Facilitate Xiao Li's integration into the new school environment by identifying and exploring social opportunities through CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Li's feelings of being overwhelmed and lost in a new school environment, including social challenges like shyness and feeling disconnected during break times. Agreed on a small observational task for Xiao Li to watch classmates' activities during breaks to identify potential ways to connect, emphasizing starting small and recognizing others may also experience similar feelings. Encouraged patience and incremental steps towards building confidence in adjusting to the new setting.
