The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yueyue is a young child who has recently returned home from kindergarten due to frequent illnesses and anxiety about being away from home. Yueyue struggles with feelings of missing out on social activities but feels relieved to be back in a familiar environment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yueyue enjoys drawing as a way to distract from stress and is developing a routine that includes art as a coping mechanism.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yueyue experiences anxiety related to separation from home and concerns about health, without a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yueyue feels anxious about potential illnesses and missing social interactions, but also relieved to be home.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yueyue's sensitivity to environmental changes and the apparent benefit derived from familiar routines like drawing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yueyue should keep a simple journal to track emotions and engage in daily drawing activities to manage stress.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Yueyue identify stress triggers and develop a supportive routine to enhance emotional stability.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yueyue's anxiety surrounding kindergarten and her relief at being home, balanced with concerns about missing out on social activities. Identified morning anxiety triggers and agreed on incorporating enjoyable activities and engaging her mom for support as coping strategies. Planned to observe thought patterns and emotional responses, with the goal of enhancing her sense of well-being and easing social interactions.
