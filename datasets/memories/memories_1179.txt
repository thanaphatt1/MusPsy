The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wu is a university student undergoing the challenging transition to campus life. He feels lost and financially stressed due to adjusting to the new environment and his spending habits. Wu reports feeling anxious and embarrassed about these issues.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wu enjoys reading and drawing, both of which help him relax and unwind during stressful times. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wu experiences anxiety rooted in adjustment challenges to university life, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wu feels anxious and overwhelmed by the transition to university life, particularly with navigation and financial management.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wu's openness to exploring his anxiety triggers and recognizing patterns in his stress response.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wu should document specific moments of stress, along with associated thoughts, to better understand stress triggers.
[Session Goal:] Identify anxious thoughts connected to university adaptation and consider practical steps for stress management.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wu's challenges in adapting to university life, focusing on anxiety from navigating campus and financial stress. Identified negative thought patterns and established strategies, like mapping expenses and integrating enjoyable activities like reading to manage stress. Wu embraced practical steps for self-compassion and empowerment, feeling more confident in managing future challenges independently.
