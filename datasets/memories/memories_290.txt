The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
L is a university student adapting to new academic stress and strained family dynamics, particularly with their father after the birth of a younger sibling. L values academic achievement and seeks understanding in familial relationships.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
L enjoys painting as a means of relaxation and self-expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
L is experiencing feelings of overwhelm and stress, largely due to adapting to university life and familial expectations, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] L feels overwhelmed by adjusting to university life and ongoing family tension, particularly with their father.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes L's sensitivity towards familial expectations and their openness to finding coping mechanisms like journaling and painting.
[Counseling Assignments:] L is encouraged to journal their thoughts and emotions, noting interactions with their father to identify patterns.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT techniques to help L manage stress by identifying thought patterns and incorporating stress-relief activities such as painting.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed L's feelings of overwhelm due to university adjustment and family tension, particularly with their father. Recommended journaling to identify patterns in family dynamics and encouraged painting for stress relief as part of their routine. Agreed to continue exploring these themes in future sessions.
