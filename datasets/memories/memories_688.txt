The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yi is dealing with stress from recent family challenges, particularly due to their mother's absence to care for an ill grandmother and financial struggles. Yi also faces duties related to community service while juggling feelings of isolation as their father works night shifts.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yi used to enjoy online gaming as an escape but acknowledges a need for more balance, as well as having an interest in drawing landscapes for relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yi experiences stress and feelings of isolation connected to family and financial pressures, with self-reported tendencies toward gaming addiction. There is no mention of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yi feels overwhelmed by recent family and financial responsibilities, creating a sense of isolation and stress.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Yi's awareness of their coping mechanisms becoming problematic and their willingness to explore alternatives such as expressing thoughts and engaging in hobbies like drawing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yi is tasked with noting down overwhelming thoughts and feelings to identify patterns and facilitate clearer understanding.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Yi identify and manage stress-related thoughts, encourage engaging in hobbies such as drawing, and develop new strategies for stress relief.
Counseling Summary:
Yi is feeling overwhelmed by stress due to family obligations and financial concerns, alongside feeling isolated and developing an addiction to online games. The consultant encourages Yi to acknowledge these feelings through mindfulness by recording thoughts and emotions and suggests revisiting hobbies like drawing for self-care. Yi is open to these strategies and willing to try new approaches to manage stress, with a plan to allocate daily time for drawing and noting emotions.
