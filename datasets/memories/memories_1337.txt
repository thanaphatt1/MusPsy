The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu X is a university student who has recently experienced a breakup and failed a major course, leading to the necessity of retaking an exam. These events have contributed to feelings of being overwhelmed and self-doubt.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu X enjoys reading novels and finds comfort in taking walks.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu X experiences stress and self-doubt, particularly heightened by recent personal and academic setbacks, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu X feels overwhelmed and lacks self-confidence due to a recent breakup and academic failure.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liu X's struggle with low self-esteem and withdrawal from social supports after recent personal challenges.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu X is encouraged to find small moments of self-kindness, such as taking walks or engaging in enjoyable activities.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Liu X manage stress and begin rebuilding self-esteem through positive activities and self-reflection.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu X's emotional struggles stemming from a recent breakup and academic failure, addressing self-esteem and coping mechanisms. Developed strategies such as journaling negative thoughts to identify unhelpful patterns and encouraged self-kindness through activities like walking and reading. Concluded with a focus on reaching out for support and applying insights to future challenges, empowering Liu X to manage stress more effectively.
