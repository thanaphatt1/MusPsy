The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Gao is a university student experiencing anxiety and loneliness, especially after struggling with class discussions. Gao feels classmates may be excluding and laughing at them due to this perceived failure.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Gao enjoys writing in a journal, which serves as an emotional outlet and calming activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Gao encounters anxiety and insecurity primarily associated with social interactions and academic performance, though there is no formal diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Gao feels anxious and insecure, particularly about being judged and excluded by classmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Gao's tendency to magnify social fears and notes the ongoing impact of social anxiety on participation in class.
[Counseling Assignments:] Gao should note down thoughts during anxious moments to identify patterns and potential distortions.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Gao identify and challenge cognitive distortions and reduce anxiety related to social performances.

---
Counseling Summary:
Gao experienced social anxiety and insecurity, fearing judgment from classmates after a challenging class discussion. Through counseling, Gao worked on recognizing and challenging negative thought patterns, engaged in positive activities like journaling, and reached out to classmates for feedback, which helped in gaining reassurance and new perspectives. As a result, Gao developed effective strategies such as questioning negative thoughts, seeking feedback from trusted individuals, and maintaining supportive activities to better manage anxiety and social interactions.
