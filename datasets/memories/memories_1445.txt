The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoning is a student who recently witnessed a traumatic event involving a classmate, which has triggered past traumatic memories. This has led to heightened anxiety and feelings of helplessness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoning enjoys playing the guitar and finds it soothing and helpful in managing stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoning experiences significant anxiety, particularly triggered by past trauma and current stressors, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoning feels anxious and unsettled due to witnessing a traumatic event, which revives past traumatic memories and fears.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoning's heightened anxiety linked to trauma-related memories and an openness to explore and understand these emotional responses.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoning is encouraged to jot down thoughts and feelings when recalling traumatic events to better understand them.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Xiaoning identify and manage anxiety-inducing thoughts, focusing on distinguishing past trauma from present reality.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoning's trauma-related anxiety and negative thinking patterns stemming from witnessing a distressing event. Engaged in cognitive restructuring by documenting fears versus reality and encouraged anxiety-relieving activities like regular guitar practice. Concluded with Xiaoning developing skills to challenge irrational fears, emphasize reality checks, and incorporate enjoyable activities to cope with stress.
