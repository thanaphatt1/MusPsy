The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
C is a university student struggling with social anxiety triggered by financial insecurities when interacting with economically more advantaged classmates. C often feels left out at social gatherings due to affordability concerns.

[Personal Characteristics]  
C enjoys listening to music and reading as means of stress relief and personal enjoyment.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
C experiences anxiety mainly in social settings due to perceived economic disparities, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] C feels stressed, self-conscious, anxious, and isolated due to perceived financial disparities and the sense of not fitting in with classmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes that C is aware of feeling anxious and isolated and is open to exploring coping mechanisms to manage these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] C is encouraged to jot down thoughts and feelings related to these stressful situations for later exploration.
[Session Goal:] To help C understand and identify stress-related patterns, utilizing CBT strategies for managing and reframing negative thoughts about belonging and self-worth.
Counseling Summary:
Explored C's feelings of stress and self-consciousness stemming from economic differences impacting social interactions. Agreed on tasks to increase self-awareness by noting thoughts and emotions, alongside integrating enjoyable activities like music and reading for stress relief. Emphasized the importance of understanding emotional patterns, with a proactive approach for reframing perceptions and fostering well-being.
