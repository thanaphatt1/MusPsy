The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
B is a student who has recently encountered frustration and dissatisfaction due to chaotic event organization at school. He values effective communication and is concerned about being heard and valued in group settings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
B enjoys discussing social issues and current events, which serves as a passionate outlet for him.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
B demonstrates anxiety and irritation in response to situations where he feels unheard or dismissed. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] B feels frustrated and overwhelmed due to disorganized event planning and perceiving a lack of acknowledgment from others.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes B’s tendency towards negative conclusions about others’ perceptions and recognizes his proactive approach to emotion management.
[Counseling Assignments:] B should jot down observations about his emotions concerning situations where he feels unheard or undervalued.
[Session Goal:] Explore B’s emotions in relation to his thoughts and behaviors, aiding in managing these situations through CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored B's frustration over feeling unheard and disorganized in a school event, which heightened their anxiety and fear of judgment when expressing opinions. B identified negative assumptions about being dismissed, and the consultant suggested trying to express input in low-stakes situations to build confidence and reduce anxiety. B agreed to track positive interactions and emotions to reinforce confidence, with plans to review progress in future sessions.
