The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yu is a university student with pressure around academic performance and difficulty focusing in class, which affects confidence. Recently, exams did not go as planned, leading to increased stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yu enjoys drawing, a hobby that has been set aside due to academic demands, and feels hesitant about social interactions due to fears of underperforming.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yu experiences stress and fatigue potentially exacerbated by negative self-talk and anxiety over grades but has yet to receive formal diagnosis for a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yu feels pressured about grades and is frustrated by falling asleep in class, impacting self-confidence.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes Yu's fatigue and high stress related to academic pressure but identifies Yu's willingness to explore relaxation and self-expression techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yu should keep a journal to identify patterns related to stress and fatigue and explore calming pre-sleep routines such as listening to music.
[Session Goal:] Using CBT strategies to help Yu manage stress, improve sleep patterns, and reintroduce enjoyable activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yu's stress and fatigue stemming from academic pressure, impacting confidence and social interactions. Suggested journaling stress triggers and adopting relaxation techniques, such as music and drawing, to manage anxiety and enhance study focus. Encouraged gradual social engagement through study sessions, emphasizing small steps and personal pace for comfort.
