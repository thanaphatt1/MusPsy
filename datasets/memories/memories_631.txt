The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoke is a school-aged student facing recent disciplinary trouble due to a fight at school, specifically involving a classmate named Xiaoya over a toy. Xiaoke is concerned about the consequences and their parents' reaction.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoke enjoys drawing, which serves as a creative outlet and stress reliever.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoke struggles with feelings of anger and worry, often stemming from peer interactions such as teasing, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoke feels angry and worried, mainly about the consequences of a school altercation and potential parental dissatisfaction.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoke's difficulties with emotion regulation and impulses but also recognizes an openness to discussing feelings and thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoke should note specific situations at school that trigger anger and stress, creating a record to better understand these incidents.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT techniques to help Xiaoke understand and manage anger, identify stress triggers, and introduce constructive coping strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoke's recent altercation at school and the triggers for aggressive reactions, focusing on managing anger and negative thoughts. Encouraged Xiaoke to practice deep breathing and thought observation as tools for handling stress, particularly in situations involving teasing. Agreed on using role-play to rehearse calm responses and emphasized the importance of consistent practice in building resilience.
