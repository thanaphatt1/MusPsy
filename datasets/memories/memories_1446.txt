The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ziyou is a university student navigating stress related to academic performance and social interactions, having faced tension arising from classmates' remarks about their study habits. Ziyou values dedication to studies deeply but seeks to balance this with maintaining friendships. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ziyou enjoys reading novels, which provides an escape and stress relief. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ziyou experiences anxiety and self-consciousness influenced by peers' perceptions and academic pressures, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ziyou feels stressed and self-conscious following an argument with classmates about their academic focus.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notices Ziyou’s sensitivity to perceived judgments and their impact on social interactions but also sees a willingness to explore solutions and engage in enjoyable activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ziyou is encouraged to identify moments when peers’ opinions affect them most and engage deeply with preferred subjects like history to reduce stress.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Ziyou manage stress by understanding triggers, encouraging engagement in appealing activities, and improving interaction with classmates.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ziyou's feelings of withdrawal and self-consciousness following a conflict with classmates over academic dedication. Agreed on the plan to focus on subjects Ziyou enjoys to alleviate pressure from others' opinions and to track thought patterns to better understand triggers. Encouraged engaging deeply with interests and journaling experiences for insights to discuss in the next session.
