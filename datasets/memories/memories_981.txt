The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
H is a student experiencing significant academic and social pressure shortly after starting a new school year. H reports feeling overwhelmed and frustrated, particularly due to academic demands and social misunderstandings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
H has a strong interest in art and finds drawing to be a stress-relieving hobby.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
H exhibits signs of anxiety and stress, particularly related to perceived social judgment and academic difficulties, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] H feels overwhelmed by academic pressures and believes they are not liked by peers and teachers, leading to self-esteem issues.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that H frequently feels misunderstood and isolated, often jumping to negative conclusions about their social interactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] H is tasked with noting down instances that trigger these overwhelming feelings, including the emotional and thought patterns associated with them.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help H identify and counteract negative thoughts, encouraging expression through art as a means to process emotions.
Counseling Summary:
H is experiencing overwhelming pressure from academic and social interactions, leading to feelings of irritability, loneliness, and inadequacy. The conversation focused on recognizing H's enjoyment of drawing as a potential therapeutic outlet and developing structured art sessions to alleviate stress and emotional challenges. H agreed to track emotional patterns and explore recurring themes in doodles to better understand triggers and manage their anxiety.
