The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ma is a student who feels disconnected and uninterested in school. Recent poor exam results and teacher feedback have prompted this introspection, though there is no significant pressure from home.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ma enjoys spending time with friends and tends to do so at the cost of academic focus, leading to feelings of frustration and boredom with school subjects.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ma encounters disinterest and a lack of motivation regarding schoolwork, although no formal diagnosis has been made.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ma feels disengaged and uninterested in school, questioning its relevance and often feeling bored.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Ma's lack of connection to school and sees a pattern of amusement-seeking behavior with friends, rather than focusing on academic responsibilities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ma is asked to keep a journal documenting any thoughts or feelings related to school to better explore these perceptions in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Identify Ma's thoughts and perceptions about school to better understand and manage feelings of disinterest and disengagement.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ma's feelings of disinterest and disconnection from school, influenced by a lack of external pressure and feedback from teachers. Recognized how this behavior impacts her classroom attitude, leading to avoidance and disengagement. Agreed on trying journaling as a tool to track thoughts and feelings about school for further exploration in future sessions.
