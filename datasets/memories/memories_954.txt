The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is applying for a university abroad and is overwhelmed by the preparation involved. He is stressed and mentally exhausted, feeling uncertain about meeting personal academic standards.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys reading and finds comfort in returning to old favorite books, though this has been limited due to application pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences anxiety and stress linked to self-doubt and application pressures. There is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels stressed and mentally sluggish, with anxiety due to application pressures and self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li's tendency to overthink and self-doubt, but also sees a willingness to explore and identify stress patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should journal thoughts and feelings for a week to identify stress triggers and patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Li identify specific stress-related thoughts and patterns while encouraging the expression of thoughts through journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's overwhelming feelings and anxiety related to applying to university abroad, highlighting how they affect day-to-day life. Agreed on starting a journaling practice to identify thought patterns and emotional triggers, which may help manage stress and reframe negative thoughts. Emphasized patience and self-kindness in the gradual process of gaining self-awareness and balanced thinking.
