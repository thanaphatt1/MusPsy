The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student with aspirations in psychology, influenced significantly by their father to pursue finance studies, which they find unfulfilling. They experience conflict between their passion for psychology and familial expectations, which causes significant emotional strain.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user is interested in psychology and enjoys being part of communities that share this interest, like clubs or forums, as it helps them feel connected and supported.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user faces stress and anxiety stemming from a struggle to align personal aspirations with imposed academic paths. There is no confirmed diagnosis of a psychological disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user is frustrated and overwhelmed due to recent academic choices misaligned with their interests and feels disheartened by unmet personal expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes a strong internal conflict between the user's passion and familial expectations, which contribute to their feelings of being trapped and anxious.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should journal about their passion for psychology and reflect on the emotions related to their academic situation to gain clarity on their inner struggles.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to help the user explore and manage the disparity between their aspirations and obligations, focusing on reducing stress through reflective and insightful practices.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling sessions focused on the user's struggle between their passion for psychology and the pressure of pursuing a finance major, which was influenced by their father. Through discussions, journaling, and participation in a psychology club, the user gained insights into managing negative thought patterns, connecting with their passion, and balancing interests, ultimately leading to increased motivation and a more hopeful outlook on their academic and personal journey. Future strategies include maintaining open communication with family and integrating meaningful activities aligned with their interests.
