The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jing is a high school student dealing with familial stress from parental conflicts and feelings of isolation at school. Jing tends to feel anxious and overwhelmed without anyone to confide in, exacerbating the stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Jing enjoys drawing, which previously served as a relaxation outlet but has felt unmotivated to engage in recently.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jing experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by familial discord and isolation at school, with no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jing feels anxious, overwhelmed, and isolated due to a tense home environment and a lack of social connections at school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Jing's feelings of being overwhelmed by a lack of control over their environment and an openness to explore emotional outlets.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jing should pay attention to their feelings, write them down when they arise, and try drawing as a way to express emotions.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Jing to become aware of emotional patterns and explore artistic expression as a way to manage stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Jing's feelings of anxiety and isolation stemming from parental conflict and lack of social connections at school, with a focus on understanding and documenting emotional triggers. Agreed on using drawing as a small, manageable creative outlet for emotional expression and stress relief. Emphasized the importance of taking mindful, incremental steps in building coping strategies and self-awareness.
