The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaolin is a university student who recently resigned from a class party secretary role due to feeling overwhelmed, leading to misunderstandings with classmates. She feels isolated and misunderstood in her interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]
She enjoys reading science fiction novels, which serves as a relaxation and escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaolin experiences feelings of anxiety and self-doubt, primarily triggered by her perceived social misunderstandings and the pressure of responsibilities, although there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaolin feels overwhelmed and misunderstood following her resignation from a student role, leading to feelings of isolation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaolin's struggle with perceptions of judgment from her peers, yet notes her self-awareness of these assumptions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaolin is encouraged to jot down recurring stress-related thoughts and practice reminding herself of situations that contradict those fears.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiaolin challenge and modify negative assumptions and foster self-compassion in perceived social judgments.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaolin struggled with feeling overwhelmed and misunderstood after resigning from a demanding role, fearing judgment from classmates despite lacking direct evidence. Together with the consultant, she identified the impact of negative assumptions on her emotions and agreed to reflectively track these thoughts and incorporate mindfulness and enjoyable activities, such as reading, into her routine to reduce anxiety and foster self-awareness.
