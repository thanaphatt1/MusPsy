The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a student who struggles with test anxiety, leading to physical symptoms like headaches and nausea. He has a strong desire to meet high academic standards and fulfill parental expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys sketching, which provides a sense of relaxation and mental escape from stressors.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences significant anxiety around exams, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels overwhelmed and disappointed after not performing well in his recent midterm exams due to heightened anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang's tendency to focus on perceived failures, although showing openness to strategies for managing anxiety more effectively.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to note moments of anxiety and the associated thoughts, and to jot down past successes to serve as positive reminders before exams.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT techniques to identify specific patterns of stress-inducing thoughts and implement positive reinforcement through reminders of past achievements.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's heightened test anxiety and sense of self-pressure, notably in math, potentially exacerbated by parental expectations. Agreed on tracking anxiety patterns and focusing on past successes to build confidence. Encouraged open communication with parents about stress to create understanding and a supportive environment.
