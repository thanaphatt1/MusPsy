The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Lei is a university student navigating the challenges of transitioning to university life, feeling particularly stressed and disconnected from academics and social interactions. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Lei previously enjoyed running, which was a stress-relief activity, and is considering joining clubs or groups to feel less isolated.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Lei experiences stress and anxiety related to academic and social pressures, without a formal diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Lei feels overwhelmed and anxious about the fast pace of university life and social expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiao Lei’s struggle with adapting to university demands but also observes Xiao Lei's openness to trying methods that have previously brought relief, like running.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Lei should attempt a short run over the weekend and start exploring campus clubs that align with personal interests.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to aid Xiao Lei in managing stress through physical activity and to encourage social engagement to reduce feelings of isolation.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Lei's feelings of stress and disconnection due to university's academic and social pressures. Agreed on incorporating running on weekends for stress relief and considering joining interest-based clubs to reduce isolation. Discussed reframing perfectionist expectations to focus on effort and learning as means to alleviate anxiety.
