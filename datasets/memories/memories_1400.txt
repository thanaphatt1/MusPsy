The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student experiencing significant stress, especially related to academic responsibilities and self-expectations. Li is concerned about how classmates view them, which influences their self-worth and confidence.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys reading fiction, particularly fantasy novels, as a means of relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li reports frequent feelings of anxiety and self-doubt, largely stemming from perceived academic inadequacies and social evaluation, though there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li often experiences feelings of inadequacy and self-imposed pressure, believing that they are not good enough for university.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li’s tendency towards self-blame and avoidance due to anxiety but sees potential in Li's willingness to challenge these thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should focus on engaging in reading as a relaxing activity and reflect on any recurring thoughts for further exploration in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Help Li manage stress by encouraging the recognition and reframing of self-critical thoughts, and to develop coping mechanisms through engaging in positive activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's self-critical thoughts and their impact on stress and avoidance behaviors. Discussed reframing thoughts during stressful situations and set a goal to practice this approach at a study group. Agreed on monitoring recurring thoughts for further reflection in the next session, emphasizing progress over perfection.
