The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a university student experiencing difficulty adjusting to a new environment, particularly in making friends due to a history of moving frequently during their childhood. Zhao enjoys video games and feels uneasy starting conversations but is open to improvement.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhao has an interest in video games, which they see as potential conversation starters.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao experiences mild social anxiety, largely stemming from concerns about others’ perceptions and fears of making poor impressions, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels anxious about building relationships, influenced by a transient childhood.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhao's willingness to improve and acknowledges their adaptability and resilience from past experiences.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao should start a conversation with a classmate related to shared interests and reflect on how it went to understand their social interactions better.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhao build confidence in social settings by focusing on shared interests and using CBT to challenge negative thoughts about making poor impressions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhao's challenges with making friends due to frequent relocations during childhood, resulting in difficulty forming long-term connections. Discussed using Zhao's adaptability as a strength in social interactions, suggested engaging classmates by asking questions about shared interests, such as video games. Agreed on a practical task to initiate conversations and reflect on these experiences to identify patterns and areas for improvement.
