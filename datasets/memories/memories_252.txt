The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaopan is a student who recently experienced the loss of their grandpa, with whom they were very close, having been raised by him until middle school. This event occurred during the National Day holiday, impacting Xiaopan’s emotional state and making school life challenging.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaopan enjoys reading and drawing, activities they have turned to for comfort and relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaopan shows signs of grief and sadness, feeling overwhelmed and having difficulty focusing at school due to the recent loss but exhibits no formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaopan feels sad and lost due to the recent passing of their grandpa, impacting concentration and feelings of isolation at school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaopan's deep emotional connection with their grandpa and notes their isolation and difficulty in coping with the grief amidst academic pressures.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaopan is encouraged to reflect on positive memories with their grandpa to find comfort and motivation, writing them down as they arise.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaopan begin processing their grief, incorporate comforting memories into daily life, and explore healthy coping mechanisms using CBT techniques to manage their emotions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaopan's grief following the loss of his grandfather and its impact on his concentration and feelings of isolation at school. Encouraged Xiaopan to reflect on comforting memories and the lessons learned from his grandfather to help navigate daily pressures and emotional healing. Suggested Xiaopan jot down these memories and values to provide encouragement and incorporate them into his life, especially during overwhelming moments.
