The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a university student experiencing anxiety, especially with public speaking situations, often linked to past bullying experiences. Zhao values acceptance and fears negative judgment from others.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys writing and reading novels, activities that provide relaxation and serve as an emotional outlet.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao exhibits symptoms of anxiety, particularly tied to social interactions and public speaking, stemming from earlier life experiences.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels anxious and stressed about speaking in front of people, triggered by past bullying.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhao's fear of negative judgment and avoidance tendencies but recognizes Zhao's willingness to explore these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao is tasked with writing down feelings when anxiety surfaces to identify and explore patterns in thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Zhao recognize and alter negative thought patterns, gradually building confidence in speaking situations.
Counseling Summary:
Zhao faced significant anxiety related to public speaking, linked to past bullying experiences, leading to avoidance of social interactions. Throughout counseling, Zhao engaged in journaling to identify negative thought patterns, shared feelings with a supportive roommate, and developed affirmations to counteract negative self-perceptions. Zhao also planned to integrate enjoyable activities like writing and reading into daily life, which helped increase confidence and manage stress.
