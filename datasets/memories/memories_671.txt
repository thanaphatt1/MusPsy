The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a new university student facing difficulty adjusting to campus life, particularly with fitting in and dealing with lingering stress from high school exams. He values connections and seeks a sense of belonging while managing these transitions. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys listening to music and painting, which he finds soothing and expressive, and has interests in potentially exploring art clubs.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang is experiencing adjustment difficulties with symptoms of anxiety related to social integration in university but has not been formally diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels overwhelmed by the transitions into university life, which has impacted his sleep and energy levels, leading to feelings of loneliness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's willingness to explore coping mechanisms and his openness to incorporating soothing activities like music and painting into his routine.
[Counseling Assignments:] Pursue moments of relaxation during the week, noting peaceful times and integrating them into daily life; consider allocating time for music to establish stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Facilitate Wang's adaptation to university life using CBT strategies by identifying stress moments and encouraging self-soothing activities like music and art.
Counseling Summary:
Wang's transition to university has been challenging due to loneliness and residual stress from high school exams, affecting his sleep and mood. The consultant suggested focusing on moments of peace and engaging in music-related activities as a way to manage these feelings and meet new people. Over several sessions, Wang explored negative thought patterns and was encouraged to adopt more balanced perspectives, integrate creative hobbies into his routine, and connect with fellow students through campus events, ultimately feeling more equipped to handle future challenges independently.
