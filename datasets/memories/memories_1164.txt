The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a student experiencing significant stress due to self-doubt and lack of confidence, particularly regarding public speaking and school activities. Recent events like a negative experience during a school singing competition have exacerbated these feelings, causing Li to fear public exposure and judgment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys writing and has found comfort in journaling, which helps in processing and expressing thoughts and emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li struggles with anxiety, particularly in public settings, driven by thoughts of inadequacy, yet no formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels stressed and emotional, battling diminished confidence, especially in public speaking situations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li grappling with self-imposed high expectations and fear of judgment from peers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is to note down triggering situations and thoughts, aiming to identify patterns causing anxiety to address them in future sessions.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT techniques to help Li identify and manage anxiety triggers, fostering self-awareness and more balanced thinking during public interactions.
Counseling Summary:
Li explored his confidence issues stemming from a past public speaking embarrassment and its impact on his anxiety, especially during school activities. He agreed to reflect on and jot down moments when these feelings are particularly strong to identify triggers, while considering small, manageable steps like breaking tasks down and using relaxation techniques as potential solutions. Encouraged to share his feelings with a trusted friend and visualize past successes for reassurance, Li is optimistic about these new approaches and the progress they can foster.
