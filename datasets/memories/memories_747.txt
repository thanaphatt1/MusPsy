The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaotian is a university student struggling with anxiety stemming from interpersonal conflicts, particularly following a recent argument in their dormitory. Xiaotian is worried about harmony and relationships with roommates, which affects their focus on studies and sleep patterns.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaotian enjoys listening to music and reading fantasy novels, both of which serve as relaxation activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaotian experiences anxiety and stress primarily due to interpersonal conflicts and self-blame, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaotian feels anxious and tense due to a recent argument with roommates, leading to trouble sleeping and concentrating.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaotian's heightened emotional response to conflict and their willingness to explore strategies for emotional relief through music and reading.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Xiaotian is encouraged to jot down thoughts and feelings related to the argument and explore listening to music as a way to manage stress.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT techniques to help Xiaotian manage anxiety by identifying stress-related thoughts and encouraging relaxing activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaotian's emotional distress resulting from a dormitory argument and its impact on daily life, including difficulties with focus and sleep. Discussed strategies for stress relief, such as journaling, music, and reading, and prepared for future interactions with roommates by considering empathetic communication. Encouraged Xiaotian to pursue personal hobbies for stress management and to reflect on expressing feelings clearly to reduce tension.
