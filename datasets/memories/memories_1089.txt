The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Hao is a student dealing with anxiety and intrusive thoughts, experiencing recent confusion over certain repetitive behaviors both at school and home. Hao particularly values the support of his family, feeling comfortable confiding in his grandmother.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys drawing landscapes and listening to music, which serve as calming activities for him during moments of stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Hao experiences anxiety characterized by compulsive tendencies but has not received a formal diagnosis for an obsessive-compulsive disorder or other mental health conditions.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Hao feels anxious and confused by his urges to perform certain rituals, worried that not following through might lead to negative outcomes.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Hao's openness to engaging with therapeutic techniques to understand his worries but also senses a hesitation in sharing these concerns with others beyond the therapy space.
[Counseling Assignments:] Hao is encouraged to note when these compulsive urges occur and how he responds in order to identify patterns and triggers for these behaviors.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to assist Hao in identifying triggers for his compulsions, foster openness in communication with trusted family members, and incorporate relaxing activities like drawing into his routine.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Hao's anxiety connected to compulsive behaviors both in class and at home, and discussed how these actions might be linked to underlying worries. Agreed that Hao would track occurrences of these urges and consider discussing them with a supportive family member while integrating drawing as a calming daily activity. Encouraged Hao to view these steps as progress toward managing anxiety and reaffirmed ongoing support.
