The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student experiencing significant anxiety and overwhelm due to the transition from a rural area to a big city and coping with academic pressures. Wang is homesick and feels disconnected from family and familiar environments.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys painting, which serves as a potential means of stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety, homesickness, and stress primarily triggered by environmental changes and academic pressures, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels anxious and overwhelmed, intensely experiencing loneliness and academic pressure.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wang's tension and physical symptoms of stress but also sees willingness to engage in reflective practices like journaling.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should maintain a journal to record thoughts and feelings, paying particular attention to stressful situations and exploring stress-reducing activities such as music.
[Session Goal:] Help Wang manage stress and emotional overwhelm using CBT techniques, such as cognitive reframing and enhancing engagement with positive activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's anxiety due to loneliness and academic pressures with the transition to university in a big city. Wang agreed to keep a journal to track emotions and triggers, set goals for coping strategies like music, and committed to trying comforting activities like cooking familiar meals. Emphasized gradual positive changes through small actions and fostering self-compassion.
