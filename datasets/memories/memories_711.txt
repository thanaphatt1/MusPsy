The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang, a university student, recently diagnosed with prostatitis, is struggling with intense anxiety and stress, particularly focusing on long-term health implications. He feels isolated due to distant family relationships, contributing to emotional distress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys playing the guitar, and finds it provides moments of relief from his worries and anxiety.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences heightened anxiety and stress due to health concerns, but lacks a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels overwhelmed by his health diagnosis, contributing to a general sense of anxiety and stress impacting his studies and overall emotional state.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's feelings of isolation and his lack of familial support, alongside an openness to using journaling as a therapeutic tool.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to start a journal to record thoughts and emotions, enhancing awareness and clarity around his anxieties.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to aid Zhang in managing anxiety, fostering self-compassion, and exploring the influence of structured self-reflection through journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed Zhang's anxiety related to his prostatitis diagnosis and its impact on his mood and ability to focus on studies. Introduced journaling to explore thoughts and emotions and encouraged incorporating guitar playing into his routine as a form of emotional relief. Agreed on tracking any changes in mood and stress through these activities for discussion in the next session.
