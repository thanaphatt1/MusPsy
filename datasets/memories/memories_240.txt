The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoke is a student who recently transferred to a new class due to a switch in focus from arts to science, feeling the challenge of not having connected with classmates yet and expressing anxiety about fitting in.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoke enjoys reading fiction, which serves as a relaxation tool after stressful days.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoke experiences feelings of anxiety and loneliness triggered by social dynamics but has not been diagnosed with any specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoke feels overwhelmed and a bit lonely after transitioning to a new class, struggling to connect with classmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoke's sensitivity to feelings of exclusion and a tendency to avoid social interactions due to fear of rejection.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoke should note both positive and negative interactions with classmates to gain better insight into their social experiences.
[Session Goal:] Using CBT, aim to help Xiaoke manage social anxiety by identifying and altering negative assumptions about peer interactions.
Counseling Summary:
The dialogue explored Xiaoke's feelings of loneliness and anxiety after transferring to a new class and struggling to connect with classmates. They worked on shifting negative assumptions, initiating small social interactions, and balancing social efforts with personal relaxation activities like reading, which helped Xiaoke build confidence and a more positive mindset. By the end of the sessions, Xiaoke felt more open to socializing, recognized the importance of challenging negative thoughts, and planned to apply these skills to future challenges.
