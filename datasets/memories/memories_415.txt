The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a dedicated professional in a demanding field, currently facing significant stress due to pressure from work projects. He highly values career advancement and is concerned about meeting expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys jogging, which he finds helpful for clearing his mind and managing stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences stress and anxiety mostly driven by self-doubt and perceived high expectations at work, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels overwhelmed by the pressure at work, leading to anxiety and self-doubt regarding his capabilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang Wei's tendency to internalize stress and doubts, and notes his openness to exploring these feelings and triggers further.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei should note specific thoughts and situations at work that trigger anxiety, focusing on performance reviews and feedback moments.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Zhang Wei recognize anxiety triggers and validate accomplishments, promoting a focus on growth rather than self-criticism.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang Wei's anxiety related to career self-worth and managing stress from work pressure. Discussed identifying specific thoughts and situations at work triggering these feelings and agreed on documenting feedback moments and successes to gain balanced perspectives and reinforce strengths. Encouraged use of self-kindness and gradual understanding to support personal and professional growth.
