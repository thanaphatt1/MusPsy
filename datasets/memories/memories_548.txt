The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoma has recently taken a break from school due to feeling overwhelmed and questioning the value of education, particularly struggling with self-comparisons and concerns about academic performance affecting arts class prospects. 

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoma values art, which serves as a calming outlet and personal expression tool amid academic pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoma is experiencing stress and low motivation primarily due to academic-related self-doubt, with no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoma feels overwhelmed and frustrated, uncertain about the direction and purpose of schooling.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoma's reliance on self-comparison and a struggle with motivation, alongside an openness to reflecting on these thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoma should identify specific moments of low motivation and journal them to focus on discernible triggers in future sessions.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Xiaoma identify and address demotivating thoughts, encouraging personal introspection into specific stress triggers.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaoma expressed feelings of overwhelm and frustration, stemming from comparisons with peers and doubts about the importance of school, which affect motivation and self-worth. The counselor suggested tracking moments of low motivation to identify triggers and patterns, aiming to address these in the next session. This approach allows Xiaoma to reflect on thoughts and behaviors to foster understanding and develop strategies for managing these challenges.
