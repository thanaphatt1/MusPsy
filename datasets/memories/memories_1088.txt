The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xinyi is a college freshman dealing with the stress of high expectations and the recent loss of a close friend. Xinyi expresses feelings of being overwhelmed despite academic achievements and feels stuck in a fog of sadness, originating from these pressures and the emotional weight of grief.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xinyi has a passion for drawing, which was previously used as a relaxing and comforting activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xinyi experiences symptoms of grief and stress, triggered by both the pressure of expectations from family and school, as well as the loss of a friend. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xinyi feels downhearted and tense, overwhelmed by sadness from the loss of a friend and stress from academic pressures.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes the emotional weight Xinyi carries due to high expectations and grief but also identifies openness to processing emotions through journaling and drawing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xinyi is encouraged to journal nightly to release emotional weight and begin drawing again as an escape and form of reflection.
[Session Goal:] Help Xinyi process grief and stress by incorporating activities like journaling and drawing, fostering a balance between emotional processing and self-care.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xinyi's emotional struggles, including feelings of overwhelm and sadness following a friend's loss amid high expectations. Agreed on helpful strategies including journaling and drawing to process emotions and find joy, while encouraging self-compassion. Scheduled a follow-up to discuss her progress with these activities for emotional processing and balance.
