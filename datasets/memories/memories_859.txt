The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaolu is a university student experiencing overwhelming stress due to balancing studies, preparing for the TOEFL, uncertainty about a career path, and the thought of studying abroad without family support. Coming from a modest background, there is significant pressure to excel academically.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaolu enjoys reading novels, particularly fantasy, which serves as a stress-relief activity. Cooking is also a creative interest.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaolu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures and thoughts of studying abroad, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaolu feels lost due to difficulty balancing studies, test preparation, and uncertainties about their future without family support.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaolu's feelings of being overwhelmed and helpless, yet there is also a willingness to explore ways to approach these challenges positively.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaolu is encouraged to write down main concerns and any positive outcomes hoped to achieve, aiming to clarify priorities and find a way forward.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaolu manage stress through structured reflection, using CBT strategies to identify specific concerns and encouraging writing exercises for clarity.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaolu's feelings of overwhelm regarding balancing studies, preparing for the TOEFL, and uncertainty about her career path, while also considering the challenges of studying abroad without family support. Discussed the impact of academic pressures and the lack of financial support on her sense of helplessness. Agreed on a written exercise to identify main concerns and desired outcomes to help clarify priorities and provide a sense of direction.
