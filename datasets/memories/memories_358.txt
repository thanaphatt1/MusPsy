The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang San is an individual who recently lost their job, causing significant stress and confusion about their future. This situation has also put pressure on their family, which adds to Zhang San's feelings of being lost.

[Personal Characteristics]
Zhang San enjoys creative activities and has found fulfillment in past work involving creativity and project management.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang San is experiencing stress and self-doubt primarily due to recent job loss, but no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang San feels stressed and confused, struggling with a sense of direction after losing a job.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang San's openness to exploring career goals and past work experiences, showing an initial willingness to reflect on strengths.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang San should write down career goals and note recurring negative thoughts, focusing on identifying triggers.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Zhang San identify career interests aligned with strengths, reduce negative self-talk, and explore strategies for stress management.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang San's stress and confusion following job loss and its effect on personal relationships and self-esteem. Agreed on tasks to outline career goals, track negative thoughts, and recognize past strengths to build self-awareness and address anxiety. Focused on aligning career choices with personal interests, strengths, and preferred work environments.
