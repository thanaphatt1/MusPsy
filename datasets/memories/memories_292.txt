The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoli is a university student feeling uncomfortable and aversive towards their new school environment. They're introverted and have been struggling to adjust to new schedules, people, and teaching styles.

[Personal Characteristics]
Introverted, tends to keep feelings to themselves and avoids reaching out.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
No formal diagnosis, but Xiaoli experiences discomfort and anxiety related to environmental changes.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoli feels out of place and overwhelmed by the new environment and changes at the university.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoli’s resistance towards change and a tendency to internalize discomfort without expressing or addressing it.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoli should note specific moments or thoughts when they feel uncomfortable to identify patterns.
[Session Goal:] Identify specific triggers of discomfort and begin the process of observation and documentation of thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoli's discomfort with the new school environment and her tendency to internalize her feelings due to introversion. Discussed the overwhelming nature of change and suggested a journaling task to identify specific moments and thoughts of discomfort. Planned to use the collected insights to help Xiaoli adjust more comfortably to her new surroundings in future sessions.
