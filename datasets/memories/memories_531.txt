The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoming is a university student experiencing significant academic pressure and financial concerns, having recently missed out on a scholarship. He supports his family financially through part-time jobs and is deeply concerned about letting them down.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds enjoyment and stress relief in playing basketball, which helps clear his mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoming experiences stress and self-doubt primarily fueled by academic setbacks, without an official diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoming feels stressed and disappointed about not meeting academic expectations, coupled with financial pressure on his family.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoming's strong sense of responsibility and self-doubt but recognizes his openness to implementing self-compassionate strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoming should reflect on how he would support a friend in a similar situation and identify recurring self-critical thoughts for discussion.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to alleviate stress and self-criticism by encouraging Xiaoming to balance self-evaluation and set achievable goals.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoming's feelings of disappointment and self-judgment after missing a scholarship, compounded by family financial pressure. The session focused on identifying and challenging negative self-perceptions, recognizing supportive relationships, and setting smaller, achievable goals to improve confidence. Practicing self-compassion and involving family in discussing these goals were agreed upon as steps toward emotional and academic improvement.
