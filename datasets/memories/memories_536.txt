The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
L is navigating the challenges of the job market and is struggling with stress and anxiety, especially during interviews. They express concerns about performance, often feeling overwhelmed by the prospect of being judged harshly.

[Personal Characteristics]
L loves reading fiction, particularly science fiction and fantasy, which serves as an escape and stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
L displays anxiety primarily manifesting through negative self-talk and stress around high-pressure situations like job interviews.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] L feels intensely stressed and anxious during job interviews, struggling with negative thoughts and self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices L's tendency to default to negative assumptions and perceive interviews as high-stakes, yet sees willingness to explore anxiety management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] L should write down thoughts and feelings during interviews to identify patterns in stress responses, and actively challenge negative thoughts with evidence-based balanced alternatives.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help L recognize and modify negative thought patterns and promote self-reassurance in stressful situations.
Counseling Summary:
Explored L's anxiety about job interviews, identifying negative thought patterns and their impact on performance. L worked on reframing negative thoughts, integrating relaxation techniques, and engaging in hobbies for stress management, leading to improved confidence and composure. Implemented structured interview preparation and relaxation routines, reflecting on experiences positively, and embracing learning opportunities for ongoing growth.
