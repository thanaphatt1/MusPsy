The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a student experiencing significant stress and confusion, primarily due to feedback from teachers about a lack of attention in class and pressure from their mother about grades. Li feels the dual pressure of school expectations and strict parental standards regarding academic performance. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys drawing, which serves as a source of happiness and as a potential stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences stress and anxiety mainly from academic pressures and familial expectations without a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels stressed and confused, believing they are not meeting their teachers’ and mother’s expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li's significant burden from external expectations but notes Li’s willingness to reflect on stress-related triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should keep a journal to note distractions and stress triggers to uncover patterns.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT to help Li identify stress triggers, reflect on thought patterns, and explore how drawing could alleviate stress.
Counseling Summary:
Li struggled with stress from school and home pressures, initially feeling overwhelmed by criticism and fear of disappointing others. Through journaling exercises and discovering positive self-talk, Li learned to recognize extreme thoughts and adopt affirmations about imperfection, which, paired with drawing as a stress-reliever, improved mood and resilience. Li plans to maintain these strategies and explore open communication with teachers and family to ensure continued progress in managing stress.
