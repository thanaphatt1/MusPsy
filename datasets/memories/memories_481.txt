The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang San is an adult experiencing intense anxiety and feelings of being lost, compounded by recent homelessness concerns after losing their place to live. Having faced unemployment and struggling with substance use in the past, Zhang San battles self-blame for these situations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang San previously enjoyed playing music, which used to be a source of peace and relaxation. They also value connecting with supportive individuals like family and friends.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang San is dealing with heightened anxiety, pervasive negative thoughts stemming from life instability, but has not been formally diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang San feels anxious and overwhelmed by the recent loss of housing, coupled with self-blame regarding past substance use.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Zhang San's tendency to internalize guilt and sees potential in exploring cognitive patterns and emotional connections.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang San is asked to maintain a daily journal to document thoughts and feelings, reflecting on clarity and perspective in these emotions.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Zhang San identify negative thought patterns, gain insight into emotional impacts, and initiate conversations with supportive individuals in their life.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang San shared feelings of anxiety and shame about his current housing crisis and past substance use issues. The consultant encouraged him to start journaling his thoughts and feelings to gain clarity and suggested reaching out to his cousin for support, emphasizing gradual progress through small, manageable steps. Zhang San agreed to try these strategies and send a casual message to his cousin to open communication.
