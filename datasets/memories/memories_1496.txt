The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Little W is a student grappling with anxiety stemming from perceived threats when commuting home from school. Recently, her feelings of being followed and scared have intensified.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She finds calmness in painting, which serves as both a hobby and a potential anxiety relief method.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Little W encounters anxiety primarily linked to perceived external threats; there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Little W feels anxious and frightened, particularly worried about being followed during her walks home from school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Little W's heightened vigilance and the uncertainty about her surroundings, alongside her willingness to take steps toward feeling more secure.
[Counseling Assignments:] Little W is encouraged to write down thoughts and feelings experienced during her walks home to better identify triggers and patterns.
[Session Goal:] Explore Little W's feelings of insecurity during her walks and introduce initial strategies to enhance her sense of safety.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed Little W's anxiety about feeling followed during walks home and its impact on her concentration at school. Agreed on a task to record thoughts during walks to identify triggers and considered strategies like walking with a friend, engaging family in conversations, and incorporating painting as a calming routine. Emphasized being gentle with herself and using these strategies for anxiety management, with plans to review progress in the next session.
