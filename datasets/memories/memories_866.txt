The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a college student, currently in their second year, who is experiencing significant anxiety about their future career prospects, influenced by observing the struggles faced by seniors in securing jobs. Zhang has an interest in business and marketing, yet feels uncertain about making the right career choices.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang has shown an enjoyment in organizing events and working collaboratively, offering a sense of accomplishment when things go smoothly.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang is experiencing anxiety primarily focused on future career prospects, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious about future career prospects, finding it difficult to concentrate on coursework due to worries about not doing enough for future job security.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang's heightened anxiety due to uncertainty about the future, yet appreciates Zhang's willingness to explore and write down career-related thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is tasked to write down specific career goals or interests and observe situations that intensify feelings of anxiety for further discussion.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhang balance worries with actionable steps toward career goals by identifying manageable actions that can reduce anxiety levels.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's anxiety about his future career prospects and the impact of observing seniors struggling with job searches. Agreed on a task for Zhang to write down career goals and interests to help manage anxiety and provide clarity. Encouraged mindfulness about emotional fluctuations and situational triggers as a way to gain insight.
