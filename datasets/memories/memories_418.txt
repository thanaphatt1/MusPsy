The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ryder is a survivor of significant traumatic experiences, grappling with ongoing sadness linked to memories of those lost, particularly during memorial gatherings. Ryder often finds these memories overwhelming and struggles with feelings of guilt. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ryder enjoys woodworking, which serves as a potential outlet for emotional expression and distraction from distressing thoughts.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ryder shows signs of survivor's guilt and persistent sadness, although no formal diagnosis of a specific mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ryder is experiencing deep sadness and feels engulfed by unresolved memories and emotions stemming from past traumatic experiences.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Ryder's looping negative thoughts and the weight of survivor's guilt, but also sees a willingness to explore new strategies for relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ryder should begin journaling to externalize and process recurring emotions, and aim to connect with friends to diminish isolation.
[Session Goal:] Facilitate Ryder's emotion management by implementing CBT techniques to process trauma memories and encourage rebuilding of social connections.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ryder's struggle with reliving painful memories and feeling stuck in sadness after a survivor gathering, identifying specific triggers and recognizing existing support systems. Agreed on trying journaling as a gentle way to process emotions and setting a small goal to connect with friends, aiming to alleviate feelings of isolation and sadness. Reinforced the importance of small steps and resilience in managing emotions, encouraging continued open-mindedness and adjustment in strategies.
