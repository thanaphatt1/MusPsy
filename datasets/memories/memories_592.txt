The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
A is a high school student who has recently been a victim of a phone scam that led to involving a friend unintentionally. This has resulted in feelings of guilt and stress, also causing reluctance to return to school.

[Personal Characteristics]  
A enjoys drawing, which serves as an escape and a means to clear their mind during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
A experiences significant guilt and anxiety following the scam incident, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] A feels overwhelmed and isolated, dealing with recurring guilty thoughts over the phone scam incident.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes A's struggle with self-critical thoughts but also recognizes A's willingness to engage in activities like drawing and seek healthier perspectives.
[Counseling Assignments:] A should try engaging in drawing and record recurring negative thoughts to identify patterns and gain clearer insight.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to address patterns in negative thoughts, encourage self-compassion, and promote engagement in relaxing activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored A's feelings of guilt and stress following a phone scam incident, leading to social withdrawal. Encouraged self-compassion and suggested resuming stress-relief activities like walks and music, along with reaching out to trusted individuals for support. Agreed on scheduling specific activities and writing down negative thoughts to identify patterns and challenge them.
