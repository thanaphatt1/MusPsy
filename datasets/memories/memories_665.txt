The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student confronting academic pressure alongside additional stressors like personal safety concerns following a roommate's recent phone theft incident. Zhang is deeply focused on academic success and feels anxious about failing to meet expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They enjoy taking walks around campus to clear their mind and find solace in both reflective activities and social interactions with peers.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety heightened by specific triggers such as academic stress and recent events, with no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels overwhelmed by academic demands and increased anxiety following a theft incident affecting personal feelings of safety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's heightened anxiety and tendency to feel on edge due to multiple pressures, along with a willingness to address these through awareness and relaxation techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should note situations eliciting anxiety to better understand triggers, aiding future discussions on managing stress.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to assist Zhang in identifying anxiety triggers and introducing relaxation practices to cope with stressors.
Counseling Summary:
In this counseling session, Zhang discussed feeling overwhelmed by academic pressure and heightened anxiety after a theft incident. The consultant suggested Zhang note situations that trigger anxiety to address them more effectively in future sessions and recommended incorporating relaxation techniques into his study routine for better focus, while also exploring practical steps to enhance safety and control. Zhang agreed to these tasks, feeling more hopeful about understanding and managing his anxiety.
