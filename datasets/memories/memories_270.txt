The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoling is a high school student navigating significant stress primarily due to upcoming mid-term exams and memorization-heavy hospitality courses. Having transitioned from elementary and junior high with previously good grades, Xiaoling faces unexpected academic pressure in her current environment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys painting as a hobby, which provides a calming effect during stressful phases.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoling is experiencing anxiety and stress attributable to academic pressures without a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoling feels overwhelmed and stressed due to the volume of content required for upcoming exams, impacting social interactions and leading to irritability and isolation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoling’s struggle with adapting to increasing academic demands but notes her openness to exploring ways to manage stress and anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] Try noting down moments of peak stress and anxiety to better understand and tackle those patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Xiaoling manage academic stress by identifying stress triggers and promoting enjoyable, relaxing activities like painting.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoling's academic stress and anxiety (transitioning from junior high to high school pressures). Developed strategies like breaking tasks into manageable parts, focusing on past successes, and engaging in painting as a stress-relief hobby. Xiaoling agreed to incorporate these approaches and maintain a supportive social circle to manage emotions effectively.
