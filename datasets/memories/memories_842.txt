The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Huahua is preparing for postgraduate exams and feels shifted attention from family after the birth of a younger sister. She values academic validation and seeks parental support in these pursuits.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Huahua enjoys photography, using it as a soothing activity to navigate stress and gain new perspectives.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Huahua does not have a formal mental health diagnosis but experiences feelings of being undervalued and overlooked, contributing to anxiety and frustration regarding family support.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Huahua feels overwhelmed and undervalued, especially as her parents' focus shifts to her younger sister.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Huahua’s desire for parental support and recognition, and her struggle with feelings of neglect amid family changes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Huahua is tasked with considering what specific support she needs from her parents and potentially sharing her thoughts with a trusted friend.
[Session Goal:] Help Huahua articulate her needs effectively to her parents and find coping mechanisms, such as photography, to manage emotional distress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Huahua's feelings of being overwhelmed and undervalued due to perceived parental neglect following her younger sister's birth, impacting her motivation for postgraduate exams. Discussed strategies to communicate her needs to her parents and considered involving friends for support, alongside journaling and photography as coping mechanisms. Agreed on small tasks to articulate desired parental support, engage in photography, and track emotional shifts to improve resilience and perspective.
