The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
XM is a student who has been feeling increasingly anxious and down, troubled particularly by academic stress and an obsessive focus on a blinking issue with their eyes. XM is concerned about grades slipping and the lack of control over their mental focus, especially in quiet environments.

[Personal Characteristics]  
XM has a past interest in creative activities like doodling, and is open to re-engaging with such hobbies as a means of stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
XM experiences anxiety related to academic pressures and involuntary blinking focus, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] XM feels frustrated and out of control, with anxiety heightened by their focus on the blinking concern and academic stress.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes XM's significant distress linked to self-critical and obsessive thoughts, coupled with a willingness to explore the origins and remedies for their anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] XM is encouraged to track when the blinking concern becomes overwhelming and explore how it affects them, while trying doodling or another creative activity for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Assist XM in understanding the connection between their thoughts, feelings, and behaviors using CBT strategies, including acknowledging intrusive thoughts and engaging in relaxing activities to reduce their impact.
Counseling Summary:
Explored XM's anxiety relating to an obsessive focus on a blinking issue and its impact on daily life, particularly during quiet times when internal thoughts become more apparent. Discussed the potential benefits of acknowledging rather than avoiding intrusive thoughts and introduced coping strategies, like redirecting attention to the present moment and engaging in relaxing activities such as doodling and deep breathing. XM agreed to practice these strategies, aiming to reduce the power of the intrusive thoughts over them.
