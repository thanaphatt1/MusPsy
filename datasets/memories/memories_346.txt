The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is in his mid-twenties and recently experienced the emotional upheaval of a breakup, which has left him feeling isolated and uncertain about his social connections. He is seeking to regain confidence and find emotional stability.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei enjoys running, which provides him with a sense of freedom and grounding.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei is dealing with anxiety and self-doubt stemming from a recent relationship breakup, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels anxious and lost following the breakup with his girlfriend, leading to a diminished support system and increased self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wei's vulnerability due to his recent loss but also notes his willingness to engage in self-reflective practices like journaling.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei is encouraged to start journaling to capture both distressing emotions and uplifting moments to gain insight into what fosters his confidence.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Wei identify and build upon personal strengths while managing feelings of isolation through structured reflection and physical activity like running.
Counseling Summary:
Wei is dealing with heightened anxiety and uncertainty following a recent breakup, feeling the loss of a crucial support system and questioning his social connections and self-confidence. In this session, he agreed to journaling to capture both challenging emotions and any uplifting moments, alongside reflection on activities, like running, that provide enjoyment and grounding. This process aims to increase emotional resilience and self-awareness, with the consultant supporting future exploration of evolving insights.
