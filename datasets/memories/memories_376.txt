The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liming is a student who has recently been receiving positive feedback from teachers and classmates, particularly in a project. Liming feels anxious about meeting high expectations and worries about the possibility of disappointing those who believe in them.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liming enjoys reading and playing chess, which serve as relaxing and unwinding activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liming experiences anxiety and stress related to maintaining performance levels, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liming feels both motivated by positive feedback and stressed due to the expectations it brings.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liming's awareness of self-imposed pressure and their ability to rationally challenge unfair expectations.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liming should set aside Sunday afternoons for reading and consider discussing their concerns with a supportive family member.
[Session Goal:] Help Liming manage expectations using CBT techniques, like thought awareness and stress-reduction activities.
Counseling Summary:
During the session, Liming shared anxiety about meeting academic expectations and explored how this pressure affects day-to-day feelings, with recognition of disproportionate thoughts about failure. Liming was encouraged to share concerns with supportive family members and engage in relaxing activities like reading and chess, establishing a routine for these hobbies. The session concluded with Liming focusing on strengths, communication with a trusted person, and regular involvement in enjoyable activities to handle future challenges.
