The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyu is a student who is feeling overwhelmed by difficult roommate relationships and concerns about her sister's depression. She is dedicated to resolving misunderstandings and wants to support her sister but feels unsure about the best approach.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoyu enjoys reading as a way to de-stress and occasionally finds peace in yoga and other activities that connect her socially and physically.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyu experiences anxiety primarily due to personal relationships and family health concerns, without mention of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyu feels overwhelmed, anxious, and isolated due to strained relationships and worries about her sister's depression.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoyu's efforts to maintain control in difficult situations and recognizes her willingness to improve her routine to manage stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyu should create a structured study routine with dedicated reading time as a relaxation activity and draft an email to her roommates to clear misunderstandings.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoyu manage anxiety through CBT by organizing her schedule and encouraging open communication to resolve interpersonal tensions.
Counseling Summary:
In this counseling session, the consultant addressed Xiaoyu's feelings of overwhelm and anxiety related to difficult relationships with roommates and concerns about her sister's depression. They collaboratively established an action plan involving setting a structured study routine, incorporating reading as relaxation and drafting an email to reconcile with roommates, while also exploring sources of personal support to manage worries about Xiaoyu's sister. The consultant emphasized the importance of small steps and consistent routines to help Xiaoyu gain a sense of control and emotional relief.
