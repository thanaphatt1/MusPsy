The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lin Feng is a middle school student feeling significant stress about upcoming exams and their academic performance. A recent poor performance on midterm exams has increased anxiety about studies and the future. Lin Feng values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lin Feng enjoys playing basketball and reading novels, which are sometimes used as stress-relief activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lin Feng experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lin Feng feels overwhelmed and trapped after poor midterm results, leading to uncertainty and hopelessness about their academic future.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Lin Feng's inclination towards extreme self-critical thoughts about academic performance but appreciates their openness to recognizing and addressing these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lin Feng should try journaling thoughts whenever feeling overwhelmed to identify recurrent stress triggers and patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Lin Feng manage stress, identify thought-emotion connections, and encourage engaging in balancing activities like drawing and listening to music.
Counseling Summary:
Lin Feng expressed feeling overwhelmed and anxious after performing poorly in midterm exams, leading to a loss of confidence and increased stress about the future. The counselor suggested identifying the connection between thoughts, emotions, and behaviors by journaling to uncover patterns and encouraged integrating stress-relieving activities and setting small, manageable goals to improve study habits and mental well-being. Lin Feng agreed to try these strategies, aiming for progress in managing anxiety and not allowing setbacks to define their future.
