The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Z is an athlete, preparing intensely for the upcoming World Military Games, with this event being one of the last before retirement. The pressure to perform well is immense, causing insomnia and increasing stress levels.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Z enjoys painting, which has been used as a stress-relief activity when time allows.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Z is experiencing stress and anxiety due to the high stakes of the upcoming competition, but no formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Z feels significant stress due to the intensive training and the pressure of performing well in the upcoming competition.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Z's awareness of self-imposed pressures and the insight into how thoughts influence feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Z is advised to keep a journal of thoughts and feelings to uncover patterns and to consider engaging in painting as a form of stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Z identify negative thought patterns related to stress and explore relaxing activities like painting.
Counseling Summary:
Z is experiencing stress and insomnia due to the pressure of performing well in an upcoming competition, as it is one of their last before retirement. The consultant helped Z recognize the impact of negative thought patterns like catastrophizing and suggested journaling and cognitive restructuring to challenge these thoughts, alongside stress management techniques such as meditation, light exercise, and engaging in hobbies like painting. Z agreed to maintain a journal, practice thought evaluation, and dedicate weekly time to painting as part of their stress-management routine.
