The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoqiang is a university student who is struggling to adapt to the new academic environment after experiencing a significant drop in grades from high school. He is dealing with feelings of being overwhelmed and unmotivated due to the lack of structured schedules he was accustomed to.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoqiang enjoys playing the guitar, which he finds helps him relax and take his mind off his stresses.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoqiang is experiencing stress and anxiety due to the transition from high school to university, particularly finding it challenging to self-manage and adapt to a less structured academic environment without any formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoqiang feels overwhelmed and lost due to the lack of structure in university compared to high school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoqiang's difficulty with adjusting to self-management in a university setting while noticing his willingness to explore strategies for better organization.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoqiang should try creating a simple daily routine to help organize his tasks more effectively.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoqiang understand how his perceptions affect his emotions using CBT strategies and encourage the establishment of a daily routine to regain a sense of structure.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaoqiang confronted feelings of overwhelm due to adjusting to university's lack of structure, which affected his motivation and performance. Through the counseling session, Xiaoqiang was encouraged to create a daily routine, recognize negative thoughts, track achievements, and embrace past successes to foster a balanced perspective. He also planned to incorporate guitar playing for self-care to manage stress and boost morale, with the aim of fostering a mindset of resilience and gradual progress.
