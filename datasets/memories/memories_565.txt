The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Roberto is a high school student facing challenges in classroom participation, particularly with difficulty sitting still and concerns about being singled out. Roberto feels upset when attention is drawn to this behavior, impacting his desire to engage in school activities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Roberto enjoys drawing, which he uses as a relaxing and creative outlet to manage stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Roberto experiences anxiety related to classroom interactions and peer perceptions, though there is no formal diagnosis indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Roberto feels upset and embarrassed in class due to being singled out for restlessness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Roberto's lowered confidence in classroom settings and a willingness to try exercises for managing restlessness.
[Counseling Assignments:] Roberto should practice sitting quietly at home for a few minutes daily to help manage restlessness in class.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT techniques to help Roberto improve classroom engagement and manage feelings of embarrassment and worry.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Roberto's feelings of being singled out in class and how it impacts his participation and confidence. Worked on strategies to manage restlessness and anxiety, such as practicing sitting quietly, focusing on personal strengths, and using deep breathing and visualization techniques. Agreed on scheduling regular time for drawing to complement relaxation practices and enhance emotional well-being.
