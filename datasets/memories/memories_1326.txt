The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a sophomore at a military academy, feeling anxious since returning to school due to academic performance concerns. Wang's grades from last semester are a source of distress, affecting his focus and sleep. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang values physical fitness and maintains strong friendships, particularly with his friends from Shaanxi.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety primarily triggered by academic pressures, but there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels anxious and overwhelmed due to concerns about last semester's grades, impacting his focus and sleep.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's struggle with self-doubt and anxiety but notes his willingness to explore thought patterns and improve his situation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is tasked with noticing specific moments of anxiety and recording his thoughts during those times for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Wang recognize thought patterns contributing to his anxiety and develop effective coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
In counseling sessions with Wang, we explored his anxiety concerning academic performance and self-doubt. Wang identified negative thought patterns and was encouraged to reflect on his strengths and achievements, leading to a more optimistic outlook. By implementing structured study plans and engaging in enjoyable activities, Wang found a balanced approach to managing stress and felt confident in his ability to handle challenges independently.
