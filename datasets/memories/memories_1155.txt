The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Fang is a university student experiencing significant emotional distress due to a recent breakup. The breakup has had a considerable impact on her academic focus and has caused her to feel detached from her family, particularly from her grandmother, with whom she was previously close.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Fang enjoys painting and used it as a relaxation activity, though she has stopped since the breakup. She also expresses a close bond with her grandmother.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Fang is going through emotional turmoil primarily triggered by the breakup. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Fang feels lost and devastated after her boyfriend's unexpected breakup.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Fang's sense of abandonment and isolation but also her willingness to explore ways to manage her emotional distress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Fang is encouraged to jot down moments of emotional distress and accompanying thoughts, and to start re-engaging in sketching.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Fang manage emotional distress by identifying thought patterns, and encourage re-engagement in creative activities like sketching.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Fang's emotional distress following a breakup, recognizing its impact on various aspects of life, including disengagement from hobbies and family connections. Established tasks to help Fang identify distress patterns and gradually reengage in creative hobbies and communications with loved ones, fostering emotional relief and connection. Encouraged small, manageable steps towards rebuilding supportive relationships and integrating enjoyable activities, aiming for consistent attention to emotional and social needs.
