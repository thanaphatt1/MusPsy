The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a teacher feeling uncertain about their abilities due to some negative feedback from students. They value teaching and wish to foster a positive environment in their classroom.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys reading mystery novels, which they find to be a mental escape and a way to manage stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety and self-doubt triggered by negative feedback, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed and anxious due to negative feedback, leading to self-doubt and avoidance behaviors.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's heightened sensitivity to criticism but also their openness to exploring reflection techniques and focusing on positive experiences.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to start a reflection journal to document both positive and challenging teaching experiences for perspective.
[Session Goal:] Help the user identify and counter negative thoughts by focusing on positive feedback and experiences to build resilience.
Counseling Summary:
The dialogue explored the user's feelings of self-doubt due to negative feedback from students, which triggered past insecurities and anxiety. The consultant encouraged the user to engage in structured reflection exercises to differentiate between constructive criticism and unwarranted negativity, helping build resilience and gain a balanced perspective. They agreed on trying weekly reflections complemented by focusing on positive feedback to boost the user's confidence in their teaching abilities.
