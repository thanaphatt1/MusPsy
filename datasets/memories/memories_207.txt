The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He also enjoys painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels stressed and overwhelmed due to approaching exams and the pressure to succeed.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Liu's struggle with self-imposed high standards and pressure from perceived family expectations but also identifies Liu's openness to exploring anxiety management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Liu should try painting as a relaxation activity and jot down recurring stress-related thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Liu manage stress, identify specific stress-related thoughts, and encourage engaging in relaxing activities like painting.
Counseling Summary:
The session with Liu addressed stress and the pressure of upcoming exams, compounded by worries about disappointing family. Together, Liu and the consultant agreed on dedicating time to painting as a stress relief task and considered communicating with family about his concerns. Liu was encouraged to jot down recurring stress-related thoughts for further exploration in future sessions.
