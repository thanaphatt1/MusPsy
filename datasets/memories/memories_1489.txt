The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyuan is a student who feels significant stress tied to academic projects and meeting familial expectations. Xiaoyuan expresses a commitment to trying their best and desires understanding over perfection from their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoyuan enjoys drawing and finds it a therapeutic activity during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyuan experiences anxiety primarily related to a need for perfection and fear of disappointing family, with no formal diagnosis indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyuan feels tense and overwhelmed due to the pressures of perfectionism and academic demands.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoyuan's struggle with perfectionism and the anxiety stemming from familial expectations, and sees potential in Xiaoyuan's willingness to explore constructive stress management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyuan is encouraged to take regular short breaks during study sessions and consider simple relaxation activities, such as stretching or deep breathing.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Xiaoyuan manage stress by integrating structured breaks and relaxation techniques in their routine.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session focused on Xiaoyuan's stress and anxiety related to high expectations and perfectionism during a challenging school project, affecting her sleep and interactions with others. They discussed the importance of incorporating regular breaks to alleviate stress and improve productivity, with Xiaoyuan agreeing to start a habit of taking short, planned breaks to manage her feelings constructively. They concluded with Xiaoyuan planning to implement this strategy after school, looking to add variety with enjoyable activities during breaks, and agreed on reviewing the approach in the next session.
