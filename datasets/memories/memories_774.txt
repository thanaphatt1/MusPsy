The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
User is a technical school student experiencing significant anxiety, primarily centered around upcoming midterm exams. They are in their first year and feel immense pressure to perform well, fearing the possibility of disappointing their family who have always supported them.

[Personal Characteristics]
User enjoys activities like reading novels and painting, which they find refreshing and helpful for stress relief when they find the time.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
User exhibits anxiety patterns characterized by catastrophic thinking, fearing failure and disappointment which amplify stress levels. No formal mental health diagnosis reported.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] User feels extremely anxious and nervous due to the looming midterm exams and pressure to succeed.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes the user’s struggle with high expectations and fear of disappointment, yet observes their willingness to explore stress management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] User should write down their anxious thoughts when they arise to help identify recurring stress patterns and consider discussing these feelings with family to gain perspective.
[Session Goal:] Encourage user to identify and challenge stress-inducing thoughts using CBT strategies and consider sharing anxieties with a support network for reassurance.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's anxiety about first-year midterm exams, emphasizing the pressure of family expectations and the tendency to catastrophize outcomes. Suggested writing down anxious thoughts to identify patterns and encouraged the user to discuss these feelings with family for support and reassurance. Highlighted the importance of recognizing assumption-based fears and reframing them into balanced thoughts, with a focus on progress over perfection.
