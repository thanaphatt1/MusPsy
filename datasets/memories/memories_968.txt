The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaozhe is a student struggling with pressure related to school performance, especially since a freezing incident during a chemistry class. Xiaozhe feels a heavy expectation from a strict family, which contributes to their anxiety.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaozhe used to enjoy drawing, which offers a sense of calm and focus away from daily stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaozhe experiences anxiety, majorly linked to academic performance and social interactions in school, but there's no formal diagnosis provided for any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaozhe feels overwhelmed and anxious, fearing failure and disappointing family expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Xiaozhe as heavily burdened by fears and pressures, but recognizes their willingness to address these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaozhe is tasked to record thoughts and feelings related to school or home situations to identify specific stress points for later examination.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to aid Xiaozhe in recognizing and addressing anxiety triggers, particularly focusing on thoughts during school and at home.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaozhe's overwhelming feelings of inadequacy at school, particularly after a panic incident in class, and how pressure from home exacerbates her anxiety. Through sessions, we identified unhelpful thinking patterns, discussed the value of opening up to teachers and friends for support, and encouraged engaging in a joyful activity like drawing to manage stress. Xiaozhe plans to challenge negative assumptions, reach out for help, and use mindfulness strategies learned to improve her coping mechanisms.
