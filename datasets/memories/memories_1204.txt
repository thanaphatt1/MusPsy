The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
User is a university student experiencing anxiety largely due to social interactions and approaching graduation. They have expressed worry about their ability to connect with others, especially with the opposite sex, feeling their social skills are limited.

[Personal Characteristics]  
User enjoys reading and playing basketball, seeing these activities as potential ways to improve social bonds.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
User is dealing with social anxiety, characterized by self-doubt and fear of judgment during interpersonal interactions. There is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] User feels anxious and worried, particularly about interacting with new people.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies the user’s hesitation in initiating social interactions and their self-imposed barrier due to fear of judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] User is encouraged to initiate a casual interaction with someone they don’t often talk to, aiming to ease their social worries gradually.
[Session Goal:] Begin utilizing CBT techniques to help the user manage social anxiety and gradually build confidence in initiating conversations.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed User's social anxiety around making new friends, particularly with girls, due to fear of judgment and lack of experience. User agreed to attempt a small social interaction to challenge avoidance, supported by self-reassuring techniques to manage anxiety. They will reflect on this experience in the next session to identify patterns and adjust strategies.
