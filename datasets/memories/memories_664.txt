The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student anxious about graduating and a prior involvement with a group discussing exam cheating, worried it might affect future academic opportunities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing the piano which he finds calming and is considering photography as a new hobby.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences significant anxiety, particularly revolving around academic pressures and potential past actions affecting his future, but lacks a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels constant anxiety and fear about past actions potentially ruining his academic future, impacting focus and daily life.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang’s anxiety is exacerbated by worst-case scenario thinking, yet notes his willingness to explore anxiety patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should jot down moments of heightened anxiety to identify patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to foster awareness of anxiety triggers and manage thoughts related to past academic concerns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's anxiety tied to past involvement with a cheating group and its impact on his academic future. Implemented strategies like identifying thought patterns, engaging in present-focused actions, maintaining hobbies like playing piano, and seeking support from trusted figures, which helped alleviate stress. Wang gained confidence in managing his feelings through awareness and practical approaches discussed with the consultant.
