The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Qin is a university student dealing with substantial anxiety and stress related to academic performance and parental expectations. Qin has recently been indulging in gaming and watching videos excessively, which has affected their grades negatively.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Qin enjoys gaming, watching videos, and has rekindled an interest in drawing as a form of relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Qin experiences anxiety linked to academic pressures and family expectations, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Qin feels overwhelmed and anxious, experiencing guilt due to excessive gaming and video watching which detracts from their studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Qin's difficulty in managing study-related stress and notices their reliance on gaming and videos as coping mechanisms.
[Counseling Assignments:] Qin is encouraged to track moments of escape and pleasure related to gaming and videos to identify triggers, initiating the process of managing these patterns.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Qin pinpoint specific triggers for gaming and video consumption and explore healthier coping strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Qin's use of gaming and videos as an escape from school-related stress and parental pressure, highlighting the link between avoidance behavior and distressing thoughts. Identified patterns of emotional triggers (e.g., tough school days, parental conflicts) and their impact on emotions and actions. Agreed on keeping a record of thoughts and feelings as a step toward understanding and managing stress responses.
