The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student dealing with increased stress due to exams and family expectations. Recently, Liu's relationship with a roommate has deteriorated, causing further emotional distress. Liu values academic success and feels pressure not to disappoint the family, having grown up in a rural setting.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu is passionate about painting, using it as an occasional outlet for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences stress and anxiety triggered by academic and personal challenges but no formal diagnosis exists for a mental health disorder at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels stressed and anxious, exacerbated by a deteriorated relationship with a roommate, affecting their studies and general well-being.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liu's tendency to internalize blame for the relationship issues but notes Liu's potential openness to discussing stress with trusted individuals, such as family.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Liu should aim to talk with their older sister about the roommate situation to seek support and track moments of heightened anxiety for further analysis.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Liu manage stress by identifying patterns in their thoughts and encourage reaching out to supportive family members as part of their coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
Liu shared that stress from a worsening relationship with a roommate is impacting his sleep and concentration, leading to dizziness and anxiety. To address his avoidance and isolation, the consultant encouraged Liu to reach out to his supportive sister to discuss his challenges and consider how to express his need for support. The counselor also suggested Liu observe and record moments of intensified feelings to identify patterns and plan for further reflection in their next session.
