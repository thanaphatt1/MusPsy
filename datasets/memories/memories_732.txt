The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mumu is expressing significant anxiety, particularly related to public speaking, following an incident that deeply affected their confidence, causing them to feel dizzy and embarrassed. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Mumu finds solace in writing, which provides a mental calmness and a means to articulate their feelings. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mumu experiences anxiety primarily triggered by public speaking and performance situations, although no formal diagnosis of an anxiety disorder has been indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mumu feels intensely anxious and fearful when considering public speaking, worrying about potential embarrassment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Mumu's cycle of fear linked to specific triggers, yet notes Mumu's openness to self-exploration and strategies for managing anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mumu is encouraged to note moments when anxiety arises, including any accompanying thoughts, and to practice speaking in low-pressure settings.
[Session Goal:] Help Mumu identify and understand the anxiety triggers, encouraging practices in safe environments to slowly build confidence in public speaking.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mumu's anxiety related to public speaking and the impact of a past incident on current fears. Proposed tracking anxiety-inducing moments and practicing speaking in a safe setting, like family dinners, to gradually build confidence. Emphasized process-focused progress and encouraged small, manageable steps for overcoming fear.
