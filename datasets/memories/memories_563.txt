The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Matt is currently experiencing significant life changes, including losing his job and facing challenges with his start-up. These events have made him feel overwhelmed and uncertain about the future. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He has an interest in films, especially thrillers, which he finds engaging and distracting during difficult times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Matt experiences anxiety and feelings of hopelessness, mainly triggered by recent professional setbacks without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Matt feels overwhelmed and lost due to recent job loss and start-up struggles.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Matt's openness to understanding his feelings and willingness to explore new perspectives on his challenges.
[Counseling Assignments:] Matt should document thoughts and emotions tied to specific events to identify patterns and gain clarity.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Matt observe his thoughts, identify recurring themes, and begin reshaping his perspective on personal setbacks.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Matt's feelings of overwhelm and hopelessness due to job loss and struggles with his start-up. Encouraged Matt to journal thoughts and emotions to identify patterns and gain insights, promoting a perspective shift without judgment. Agreed to use these reflections in future sessions to devise small actionable steps for positive change.
