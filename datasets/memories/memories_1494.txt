The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Shishi is a student stressed by challenges in her personal relationships, particularly with her mother, and academic pressures. She feels overwhelmed by expectations and has begun self-harming as a way to cope with these intense emotions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Shishi has an interest in dance, finding enjoyment and a sense of relief in watching dance videos as an escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Shishi experiences anxiety, negative self-perception, and overwhelming stress, but there is no formal diagnosis mentioned in the conversations.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Shishi feels stressed, overwhelmed, and alone after a conflict with her mother about her academic performance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Shishi's struggle with overwhelming emotions and her use of self-harm as a coping mechanism, but also sees a willingness to attempt behavioral strategies for support.
[Counseling Assignments:] Shishi is tasked with writing down her thoughts and feelings, especially when she feels the urge to harm herself, to explore these emotions further.
[Session Goal:] Assist Shishi in becoming more aware of her emotional triggers, starting by identifying and understanding her thought patterns and finding healthier coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
The counselor explored Shishi's feelings of inadequacy and self-criticism following conflicts with her mother about her grades, which led to self-harming behaviors. They worked together to identify negative thought patterns and introduced the practice of writing down thoughts and feelings as a means to externalize and challenge these perceptions. They agreed on beginning a journal to recognize small daily victories, aiming to gradually shift Shishi's self-view towards a more positive and realistic one while offering continual support and understanding in the process.
