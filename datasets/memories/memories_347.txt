The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoming is a university student who experiences anxiety in classroom settings, especially during public speaking or when receiving attention. Recent events include grappling with classroom interactions and perceived judgment from peers. Xiaoming expresses a desire to better handle these stressors through structured support.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoming enjoys drawing as a means of relaxation and values creative expression as a way to manage stress. Xiaoming also has a growing appreciation for open communication with peers.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoming exhibits signs of anxiety related to social situations in academic environments, particularly fear of negative evaluation by peers. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoming frequently feels anxious and self-conscious in class, worried about making mistakes and being perceived negatively by classmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notices Xiaoming’s tendency to amplify fears without substantial evidence and recognizes Xiaoming’s initiative to challenge these unproductive thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoming is encouraged to be more aware of automatic thoughts during anxiety-inducing situations, write them down, and reassess them for reasonableness. Trying calming techniques like deep breathing and visualization is recommended as well.
[Session Goal:] Assist Xiaoming in identifying and challenging irrational beliefs through CBT, fostering self-awareness and coping strategies to manage anxiety in class, and promoting constructive dialogue with peers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoming's classroom anxiety, tackling fear of judgment and misconception of peers' reactions. Encouraged increasing awareness of unsubstantiated negative thoughts, and incorporating calming techniques such as deep breathing and visualization during anxious moments. Suggested reassurance and sharing experiences with trusted peers to foster understanding and resilience.
