The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a university student who recently moved to a new city for school, feeling lonely and anxious without any friends. Yang struggles with introversion, making it challenging to initiate conversations and fit in socially.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang enjoys drawing, using it as a means to relax and reduce anxiety.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang experiences anxiety and social insecurities primarily triggered by unfamiliar social settings but has not mentioned any formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels lonely and anxious due to difficulties in making friends and adjusting to the new environment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yang's reluctance to socialize due to not feeling outgoing but notes a willingness to track anxious moments for deeper understanding.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang should note incidents of heightened anxiety and associated thoughts to better understand and manage these feelings.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to assist Yang in identifying anxious thoughts and begin implementing small steps towards social interactions to reduce feelings of isolation.
Counseling Summary:
Yang struggled with social anxiety upon entering a new school environment, feeling lonely and hesitant to engage with classmates. Through sessions, Yang worked on identifying and challenging negative assumptions about social interactions, practiced small gestures and initiating conversations to build confidence, and incorporated drawing as a stress-relief hobby. By the end of the sessions, Yang felt more aware of the impact of his thoughts on emotions and equipped with strategies to manage social challenges and maintain progress.
