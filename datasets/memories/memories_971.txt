The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xufeng is a university student experiencing significant stress, particularly due to academic pressures, strained relations with a roommate, and criticism from teachers. A recent altercation with the roommate has resulted in disciplinary action, further exacerbating stress levels.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xufeng enjoys drawing, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xufeng exhibits anxiety and stress, primarily triggered by interpersonal conflicts and academic pressures, though there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xufeng feels overwhelmed, worthless, and is contemplating quitting school due to high stress and a recent conflict with a roommate.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xufeng's struggle with shame and worthlessness stemming from recent conflicts and academic pressures, identifying an openess to exploring ways to manage stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xufeng should note down thoughts that occur during moments of stress and try drawing to provide moments of relaxation and self-expression.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Xufeng manage stress, recognize underlying thoughts related to worthlessness, and explore relaxation through drawing.
Counseling Summary:
In this counseling session, Xufeng expressed feelings of stress, worthlessness, and difficulty in concentrating due to a recent conflict with his roommate and criticism from teachers. The consultant encouraged Xufeng to document his thoughts to better understand his emotions, consider initiating a calm conversation with his roommate to address misunderstandings, and suggested using drawing as a stress-management technique by setting aside regular time for it. They agreed to discuss the progress in the next session.
