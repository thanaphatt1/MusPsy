The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yuehui is a vocational student facing significant stress due to upcoming exams, worried about not passing and failing to earn a diploma. Recently, his grades have declined, leading to anxiety about his future career in cooking. Yuehui has been escaping into online games, which has affected his focus on theoretical studies.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys cooking, which represents both his field of study and a source of passion, providing moments of stress relief when not tied to academic pressure.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yuehui experiences anxiety primarily triggered by academic pressures, characterized by a decline in motivation and interest in theoretical subjects.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yuehui feels stressed and anxious about vocational school exams, worried about slipping grades and unsure of his abilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yuehui's escape into gaming as a coping mechanism and appreciates his openness to discovering strategies for stress management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yuehui should reflect daily on what is most important about his studies and career goals, aiming to clarify his motivations.
[Session Goal:] Help Yuehui reduce stress by identifying core motivations and gradually reconnecting with his passions through CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling sessions, Yuehui explored the impact of stress and negative thinking on his vocational exam preparation and motivation. Through identifying thought patterns and focusing on strengths, he shifted towards a more positive mindset, utilized visualization techniques to reduce anxiety, and reconnected with his passion for cooking as a therapeutic outlet. With supportive strategies in place, Yuehui feels more confident in managing future challenges and balancing his studies with enjoyable activities.
