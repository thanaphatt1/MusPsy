The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaokong is a university student experiencing stress and feelings of isolation due to avoiding social gatherings with classmates. This has led to a perceived exclusion from the peer group. He is an introspective individual who appreciates personal space for recharging.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaokong plays the guitar as a form of relaxation and enjoys attending concerts, suggesting an interest in music as a soothing and expressive outlet.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaokong's anxiety and stress appear triggered by social interactions and perceptions of being excluded. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaokong feels isolated and anxious due to avoiding social gatherings and perceives exclusion from classmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaokong’s sensitivity to social dynamics and his tendency to assume negative intentions from others' actions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaokong should write down thoughts about classmates' interactions to identify recurring feelings and evaluate their impact.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiaokong recognize cognitive distortions related to social interactions and promote balanced thinking by exploring direct communication with classmates.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaokong's feelings of exclusion and anxiety related to avoiding social gatherings with classmates, and its impact on their social relationships. Discussed strategies such as writing thoughts down for reflection, and the potential benefits of communicating openly with a friendly classmate about their social discomforts, with support from family to build confidence. Planned a conversation with a classmate as a small step towards better interaction, with a follow-up session to assess progress.
