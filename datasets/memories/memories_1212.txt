The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student who has been experiencing disappointment and stress due to receiving a third-place scholarship, downgrading from a previous second place, which has impacted their self-esteem and perceived capability.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys creative writing as a hobby and finds it a satisfying escape from daily stressors.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences stress and anxiety primarily driven by academic expectations and self-imposed high standards, without an official diagnosis of a mental disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels disappointed and stressed about receiving a third-place scholarship.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notices the user has set high expectations for themselves and experiences self-doubt despite their achievements.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to note recurring thoughts when they’re feeling stressed or inadequate.
[Session Goal:] Help the user identify negative thought patterns and introduce them to more balanced, helpful perspectives using CBT techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's feelings of disappointment and stress related to receiving a third-place scholarship, which led to self-doubt and anxiety about achieving academic goals. Worked on identifying negative thought patterns and challenging exaggerated assumptions, ultimately encouraging a balanced perspective and integrating stress-relief activities such as creative writing. Through regular sessions, the user gained awareness of their thoughts and developed strategies, like reframing overwhelming tasks, resulting in reduced stress and an optimistic outlook on managing future challenges.
