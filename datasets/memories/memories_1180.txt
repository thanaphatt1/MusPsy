The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Sun is a university student struggling to adapt to a demanding academic environment, experiencing stress and self-doubt as a result of diminishing grades.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Sun used to enjoy drawing, finding it a peaceful activity that they have not prioritized since starting university. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Sun is experiencing stress and anxiety related to academic pressures, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Sun feels stressed and overwhelmed due to the university workload and difficulty understanding course materials.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Sun's tendency to compare themselves negatively to peers, leading to increased self-doubt and stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Sun should identify moments of personal achievement and experiment with setting aside time for drawing over the weekend to introduce relaxation.
[Session Goal:] To help Sun recognize and appreciate their achievements, reduce stress through engaging in preferred relaxing activities, and shift focus from negative comparisons with peers.
Counseling Summary:
Sun is experiencing high stress and anxiety due to university workload and adapting to a new environment, impacting sleep and focus. Counselor suggested self-reflection, recognizing small achievements, reframing comparisons with classmates, and integrating rest and hobbies like drawing into Sun's routine for stress management. Sun agreed to try tracking achievements and dedicating time to personal interests, aiming to improve emotional balance and study effectiveness.
