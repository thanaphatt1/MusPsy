The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a professional in a competitive work environment who recently experienced a setback by missing out on a promotion, which significantly impacted his confidence and caused stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys jogging as a way to clear his mind and manage stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences stress and anxiety related to work pressures and feelings of inadequacy post-promotion.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels anxious and uncertain about his capabilities after not receiving a promotion.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Zhang Wei’s self-doubt and the need to focus on personal achievements to build confidence.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is instructed to write down positive feedback and achievements from his workplace to counter self-doubt.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Zhang Wei identify and challenge negative thought patterns, enabling him to regain confidence by recognizing personal achievements.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang Wei expressed stress and anxiety following a missed work promotion, which led to self-doubt and worry about perceptions from colleagues. Through sessions, he engaged in tasks like recording positive feedback, challenging negative thoughts, and starting routines like jogging to manage stress and confidence. He plans to focus on self-improvement and coping strategies, including enrolling in courses and maintaining physical activity.
