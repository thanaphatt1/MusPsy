The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Song is contemplating applying to grad school and experiences anxiety about competition and maintaining motivation. Song struggles with concentration, often feeling overwhelmed by future pressures.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds running to be a beneficial stress-reliever, helping to clear his mind and increase his motivation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Song experiences anxiety related to academic pressures but hasn't mentioned a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Song feels anxious and unmotivated, with a significant concern about keeping up with the competition for grad school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Song's difficulty in maintaining focus, partly due to future anxieties, yet appreciates Song's introspection and willingness to discuss these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Song is advised to track daily activities and feelings to identify patterns affecting focus and motivation.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to improve Song's focus and motivation by identifying thought patterns and addressing future-related anxieties.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Song's anxiety about grad school, focusing on feelings of inadequacy and comparison to others. Encouraged Song to keep track of both negative thoughts and personal achievements to gain a balanced perspective and improve confidence. Introduced a dual approach of noting thoughts and accomplishments to challenge self-doubt, with plans to review these insights in the next session.
