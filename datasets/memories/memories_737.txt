The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Duan is a university student nearing graduation, feeling stressed and uncertain about future directions, as they choose between pursuing a PhD for academic opportunities or a government job close to family for stability.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Duan has a passion for painting, which serves as a calming and restful activity during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Duan experiences anxiety and stress related to career decisions and self-imposed high standards, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Duan feels stressed and overwhelmed by the pressure of choosing between a PhD and a government job.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Duan's internal pressure and urgency to make the "right" choice, with some relief when considering personal timelines.
[Counseling Assignments:] Duan should write a list of pros and cons for each career path and explore feelings of urgency and pressure.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to explore decision-making pressures, compare personal timelines, and engage in relaxing activities such as painting.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Duan's stress and anxiety about future career choices between pursuing a PhD or a government job, focusing on the pressure of urgency and comparison with peers. Agreed on creating a pros and cons list to clarify options, opening up more with family about these feelings, and reintroducing painting as a calming activity. Acknowledged the importance of accepting personal timelines and embracing uncertainty with creativity and patience.
