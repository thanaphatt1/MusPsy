The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Miaomiao is coping with her parents' recent divorce, living with her busy father, which leaves her feeling isolated and struggling with her emotions. She has expressed feelings of loneliness and has contemplated self-harm.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She is open to exploring new ways to manage her emotions and is encouraged by positive social interactions, indicating a proactive approach to her well-being.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Miaomiao is experiencing feelings of depression and anxiety due to familial changes and isolation; no formal diagnosis of a mental disorder has been detailed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Miaomiao feels lonely and distressed, primarily due to familial issues and a lack of communication with her father.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Miaomiao's openness to reflection and desire to explore her feelings further, indicating a willingness to engage in therapeutic exercises.
[Counseling Assignments:] Miaomiao was advised to keep a daily journal of thoughts and feelings, focusing on identifying patterns and noting small positives in her life.
[Session Goal:] Assist Miaomiao in recognizing and managing negative thoughts by employing CBT strategies, thereby improving her emotional well-being.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Miaomiao's feelings of isolation and anxiety following her parents' divorce and communication issues with her busy father, which have led to mood and sleep disturbances. Agreed to implement a daily journaling practice to identify emotional triggers and patterns, along with recognizing small positive aspects to balance negative feelings. Emphasized the importance of gradual progress and offered continuous support.
