The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student navigating significant stress related to academic pursuits and familial expectations, compounded by interpersonal tensions, primarily with a romantic partner. Liu occasionally assists in the family business, adding to their stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu enjoys playing the piano, an activity they haven't indulged in much since entering university, yet acknowledge its potential as a stress-relief method.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress linked to relationship pressures and self-imposed expectations, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels stressed and uncertain about their university relationships, particularly with their boyfriend, and struggles with a sense of loneliness and feeling overwhelmed.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Liu's high self-imposed standards, particularly in balancing academic, familial, and relationship responsibilities, and highlights Liu's openness to exploring ways to manage these stressors.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu is tasked with maintaining a journal to document instances that significantly impact their mood or relationships.
[Session Goal:] Begin identifying stress triggers and enhance Liu's awareness of underlying thoughts contributing to stress using CBT-based journaling exercises.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's relationship stress and anxiety triggers at university, focusing on open communication and emotional sharing with her boyfriend; agreed on journaling and identifying negative thought patterns to better understand and manage triggers. Liu learned to apply patience, organize her schedule for leisure activities like piano playing, and maintain open communication as key strategies for managing future challenges.
