The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yuma is a high school student who struggles with overwhelming noise in school settings, making it difficult to concentrate and maintain comfort.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys drawing, which provides a sense of calm and acts as a stress reliever.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yuma is not formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder, but he experiences significant discomfort in noisy environments.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yuma feels overwhelmed and unable to cope with the noise at school, leading to distress.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Yuma’s inclination to escape loud situations and recognizes his potential receptiveness to coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yuma should identify specific quiet spaces at school and assess any variation in distress levels related to noise.
[Session Goal:] Help Yuma manage distress by implementing practical strategies, such as identifying calmer environments and using coping techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yuma's distress and strategies in noisy environments, identifying earplugs and drawing as effective coping mechanisms. Encouraged Yuma to communicate noise concerns to a teacher and maintain calming activities like drawing. Progress acknowledged, with a focus on utilizing calm spaces and seeking support when needed.
