The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhenzhen is a university student experiencing significant anxiety related to social interactions with classmates, particularly fearing judgment during group work. She is eager to connect but feels inhibited by her shyness. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys drawing, using it as a tool for relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhenzhen experiences social anxiety, primarily triggered by fears of being judged negatively by peers. There is no confirmed diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhenzhen feels anxious and worried about negative perceptions from her classmates, particularly during group work.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Zhenzhen's tendency to anticipate negative judgment and sees her readiness to explore these anxiety patterns and implement change.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhenzhen should note situations and thoughts that trigger anxiety during social interactions, particularly in group settings.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Zhenzhen identify and challenge anxiety-provoking thoughts, fostering more positive social engagement.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhenzhen's social anxiety, focusing on fear of judgment versus desire for connection, especially in school group settings and class discussions. Encouraged Zhenzhen to observe and challenge automatic negative thoughts, and proposed a small social task to ask questions or offer comments to classmates as a way to test assumptions. Emphasized awareness of thoughts and feelings for building clearer perceptions and reducing anxiety.
