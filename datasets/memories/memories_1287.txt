The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Luomou is a recent school leaver who feels overwhelmed and lonely at home, missing the social structure and interactions that school previously provided. Though Luomou acknowledges having a supportive family, he struggles with self-esteem issues heightened by comparing himself to others online.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys reading, particularly finding comfort and insights from "The Little Prince," and sometimes writes in a journal to manage his thoughts.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Luomou experiences anxiety and self-esteem issues, largely driven by comparisons with others and perceived lack of achievement, yet there is no formal mental health diagnosis specified.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Luomou feels anxious and unsure about his place since leaving school, often questioning his worth compared to others.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Luomou's feelings of isolation despite having a supportive environment, noting his difficulty in reaching out to others.
[Counseling Assignments:] Luomou should keep a journal of his daily feelings and thoughts, focusing on identifying patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Help Luomou explore the relationship between his thoughts and emotions, aiming to uncover and challenge recurring negative comparisons.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Luomou's feelings of overwhelm and loneliness due to being away from school, discussing how these affect self-esteem and trigger anxiety. Agreed on tasks of journaling daily feelings to identify patterns, focusing on positive interactions with family, and challenging social media comparisons. Encouraged recognition and appreciation of positive personal achievements to build awareness and counterbalance negative focus.
