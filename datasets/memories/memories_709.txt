The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a university student experiencing stress related to financial constraints, particularly in social situations with roommates. Yang prioritizes managing finances due to their family's economic situation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang enjoys reading fantasy novels, which provide an escape and relaxation from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang shows signs of stress and anxiety but hasn't been diagnosed with any specific mental health disorder. The anxiety stems mainly from financial pressures and social dynamics.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels isolated and awkward in the dorm due to a financial disagreement with a roommate.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Yang's struggle with financial pressures and the impact of social expectations on their well-being.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is encouraged to reflect on moments of financial stress to discuss ways to manage these feelings in future sessions.
[Session Goal:] Help Yang navigate social dynamics and financial stress using CBT techniques, focusing on reflecting on stress triggers and exploring methods of stress management.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yang's conflict about a disagreement with roommates regarding financial boundaries, resulting in feelings of isolation and anxiety. Discussed patterns of negative thinking and considered perspectives to ease distress, concluding with encouragement to communicate openly with the roommate to reduce tensions. Yang plans to address the situation by explaining her financial constraints while aiming to restore positive relationships.
