The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student feeling stressed and anxious, especially in social settings and interactions with girls, an issue ongoing for the past three years.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing basketball, which provides a momentary escape from anxious thoughts.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences significant social anxiety, impacting his ability to engage in social situations and form connections, though no formal mental health disorder diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels anxious and isolated, worrying about being judged and doubting his social abilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Liu's struggle with social anxiety, exacerbated by self-critical thoughts and anticipatory worry about social encounters.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu is tasked with keeping a journal of moments when he feels anxious, focusing on thoughts that arise during these situations for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT strategies to help Liu identify and challenge automatic negative thoughts, aiming to reduce anxiety and promote engagement in social activities.
Counseling Summary:
Liu explored how social anxiety affected interactions, especially with peers, revealing struggles with negative self-perception and fear of judgment. Through a series of counseling sessions, Liu practiced strategies like identifying and challenging negative thoughts, offering personal advice for managing anxiety, considering alternative interpretations to anxious situations, and engaging in activities like basketball for emotional relief. Setting small goals, such as attending a basketball club meeting, helped Liu develop skills to manage anxieties more confidently.
