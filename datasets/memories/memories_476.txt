The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liming is a student actively engaged in school projects, notably the WOWW project, where expectations and perfectionism contribute significantly to their stress. Liming feels pressured to not only meet personal goals but also to fulfill family pride.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liming enjoys drawing, which serves as a meaningful way to relieve stress and connect with positive memories.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liming experiences stress and anxiety driven by high self-imposed standards and external expectations, though no formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liming feels overwhelmed and pressured, especially in academic settings, due to self-imposed expectations and fear of letting others down.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liming's struggle with perfectionism and dedication to meeting high expectations.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liming should document moments of pressure and positivity during the WOWW project to better understand stress triggers and reinforce positive reflections.
[Session Goal:] Help Liming recognize and manage the pressures of perfectionism by focusing on positive feedback and specific moments of achieved expectations.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liming's struggle with self-imposed pressure and anxiety stemming from school expectations and parental approval. Agreed to track moments of pressure and positive feedback to identify triggers and reinforce a balanced perspective. Liming committed to reflecting on these experiences to manage anxiety and shift focus.
