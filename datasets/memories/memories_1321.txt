The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yu is an employee in an office setting who recently experienced heightened stress following a work incident. The event, which involved a mistake causing a meeting delay, has led to increased anxiety and mood swings. Yu is concerned about performance and judgment from their supervisor. 

[Personal Characteristics]
Yu has expressed an interest in reading, a hobby that was previously enjoyed but hasn’t been pursued much lately.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yu mainly deals with stress and anxiety related to work performance and perceived expectations but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yu feels stressed and anxious, especially after a recent work mistake which impacted their confidence.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Yu’s anxiety seems connected to self-critical thoughts and misconceptions about others' perceptions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yu should note any specific thoughts during anxious moments to identify patterns in their thinking.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT methods to help Yu explore the relationship between thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, and to reduce anxiety by identifying and altering negative thought patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yu's anxiety stemming from a work-related incident, focusing on the impact of negative thought patterns and irrational assumptions about competency and judgment from a supervisor. Through a series of tasks, Yu worked on recording, questioning, and reframing these thoughts, leading to increased self-awareness and the realization that open communication and realistic thinking can alleviate stress. Discussed integrating relaxation practices like reading to support ongoing emotional management and agreed on strategies for maintaining these positive changes in the future.
