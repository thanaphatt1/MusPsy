The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Sheng is a college student facing anxiety about expressing romantic interest in a fellow student due to past rejection experiences and concerns related to a physical disability.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing the guitar, which serves as a therapeutic outlet and helps him relax.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Sheng experiences anxiety and self-doubt, primarily linked to past rejection and insecurity about his limp, though no formal mental health diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Sheng feels anxious and hesitant regarding expressing his feelings due to fear of rejection and insecurity about his disability.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Sheng's high level of anxiety rooted in self-doubt and concern about perceived limitations.
[Counseling Assignments:] Sheng should document moments of heightened anxiety and make time for guitar playing to ease his mind and reflect on his emotions.
[Session Goal:] Help Sheng identify anxiety triggers, increase self-awareness, and incorporate relaxation techniques such as playing the guitar.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Sheng's anxiety about confessing feelings to a girl due to past rejection and his limp, which is causing withdrawal and difficulty focusing. Agreed on an exercise to track anxious moments and use guitar playing as a therapeutic activity to relax and reflect. Plan to discuss these reflections in the next session to better understand anxiety triggers and develop management strategies.
