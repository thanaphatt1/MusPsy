The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaozhang is a student who has been feeling detached and unmotivated about school, particularly after not meeting exam expectations. They find solace in video games, which have become a frequent activity.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaozhang has an interest and skill in understanding games and strategies, making them a comforting outlet.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaozhang experiences feelings of anxiety and disappointment related to academic performance and coping through gaming escapism.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaozhang feels down, disconnected, and anxious about school, opting to escape these feelings through video games.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaozhang’s reliance on gaming as a coping mechanism for stress but also sees potential in Xiaozhang’s willingness to explore positive traits.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaozhang should write down their thoughts about school and gaming daily, aiming to gain new insights into their motivations and feelings.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Xiaozhang manage school-related stress by recognizing their positive traits and exploring healthier stress-management techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaozhang shared feelings of disconnection and stress, particularly regarding school and excessive gaming as an escape. He agreed to engage in daily reflective writing about his thoughts on school and gaming to gain new insights and explore positive aspects of his skills in gaming as they might apply to other areas of life. The conversation emphasized acknowledging supportive feedback from friends and focusing on small, positive steps to gradually change his perspective and manage stress more effectively.
