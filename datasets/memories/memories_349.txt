The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li Ming is a project manager dealing with stress and self-blame related to team performance and work responsibilities. Recently, Li Ming has been struggling with feeling blamed by colleagues, despite no direct evidence of such blame.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds solace in running, which helps to clear his mind and relax during overwhelming periods.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li Ming is grappling with stress and negative self-perception, primarily rooted in work-related situations. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li Ming feels anxious and isolated, frequently blaming himself for his team's setbacks.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies a pattern of self-blame and negative focus, noting Li Ming's willingness to explore cognitive distortions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li Ming should observe moments of defensiveness and isolation, noting situations and patterns for further discussion.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT techniques to help Li Ming recognize and challenge negative self-talk and blame.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li Ming's self-blame and negative focus regarding team performance, leading to anxiety and isolation. Agreed to observe moments of defensiveness and isolation as a task for the week to better understand his feelings. Emphasized ongoing support and collaboration in managing these challenges.
