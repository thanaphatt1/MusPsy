The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
J is a student who struggles with anxiety and obsessive thoughts, especially around women, and experiences physical symptoms such as headaches and stomach pain. Despite seeing a psychiatrist, J has resisted hospitalization in the hope that therapy can provide relief.

[Personal Characteristics]  
J enjoys playing the guitar, which serves as a source of relaxation and enjoyment.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
J exhibits symptoms of anxiety and obsessive thoughts, particularly in social settings involving women. There is no definitive diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] J feels low and anxious in social situations with female classmates, accompanied by physical discomfort.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices J's awareness of their anxiety triggers and their willingness to understand these patterns better through therapy.
[Counseling Assignments:] J should document instances when anxiety arises, focusing on thoughts and triggers, and consider journaling or using a phone to note these occurrences.
[Session Goal:] Help J identify anxiety triggers and encourage externalizing their thoughts through writing to gain new perspectives.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, J expressed anxiety and physical symptoms when around female classmates, which have roots in long-standing anxiety and obsessive thoughts. They discussed identifying triggers and externalizing these thoughts through journaling, and J planned to gradually open up to a friend about their experiences. The consultant encouraged J to set small social interaction goals, focus on observing and documenting thoughts and feelings, and be gentle with themselves in the process of managing social anxiety.
