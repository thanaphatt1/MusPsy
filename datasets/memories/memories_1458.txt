The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoli is a university student experiencing stress, particularly related to self-study, peer interactions, and academic performance expectations. Xiaoli is concerned about moments of breathlessness that occur during stressful situations and worries about potentially failing to meet family expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoli enjoys reading and taking walks in the park, using these activities as forms of relaxation and mental escape from academic pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoli experiences anxiety-related symptoms, primarily manifested as stress-related breathlessness. No formal mental health disorder diagnosis has been indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoli feels worried about episodes of breathlessness occurring during periods of focused study and social interaction.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoli’s anxiety around academic performance and peer interactions, along with an openness to exploring the triggers of breathlessness.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoli should keep track of episodes of breathlessness and note down any accompanying thoughts or situations, alongside regular walking as a stress-relief activity.
[Session Goal:] Explore the connections between thoughts, emotions, and physical sensations using CBT strategies, and help Xiaoli identify patterns and triggers for stress-induced breathlessness.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoli's experience of breathlessness during stressful situations, focusing on the connection between thoughts, emotions, and physical sensations. Agreed on tracking instances of breathlessness to identify patterns and suggested regular walks as a relaxation strategy. Plan to review observations in the next session to further understand and manage the issue.
