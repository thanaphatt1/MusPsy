The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Q is a university student facing stress from exams and pressure to work harder since the new academic year began in August.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Q enjoys drawing as a way to relax.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Q experiences stress and anxiety associated with feelings of inadequacy and self-doubt, triggered by comparisons with classmates.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Q feels stressed and overwhelmed by self-doubt after seeing others focused on their studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes Xiao Q's tendency to compare themselves to others, which fuels stress, and acknowledges Xiao Q's efficient study approach.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Q is tasked with jotting down instances of self-doubting thoughts for discussion in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Xiao Q identify and challenge self-doubt and manage stress effectively.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Q's feelings of stress and self-doubt related to academic pressure, highlighting the impact of social comparisons on anxiety. Focused on identifying and documenting instances of negative self-talk and encouraged challenging these thoughts by assessing their accuracy. Agreed to continue observing thought patterns to facilitate more objective self-evaluation in the upcoming session.
