The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
G is an adult navigating through feelings of insecurity and self-worth, particularly in the context of personal relationships. They often compare themselves to their peers who are in relationships and feel left out as a result. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
G values creativity, with a particular interest in art, though they have not engaged with it much recently. They also have a sensitive nature, which can make them more prone to self-questioning and introspection.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
G experiences insecurity and self-worth issues influenced by social situations, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] G feels down and experiences feelings of exclusion and self-doubt due to observing happiness in the relationships of those around them.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes G's introspective nature and the impact of social comparisons on their self-esteem, while also recognizing G's openness to exploring creative outlets for emotional relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] G is encouraged to note down triggers and feelings associated with their sense of inadequacy, particularly in social contexts.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help G identify and challenge negative thoughts about self-worth and promote engagement in creative activities like art for emotional balance.
Counseling Summary:
Explored G's feelings of isolation and introspection regarding relationships, acknowledging the complexity of being single and its impact on self-worth. Agreed on using creativity as a therapeutic outlet by setting aside weekly time for art to help manage emotions and self-reflection. Encouraged G to track emotional triggers in social settings to understand and address underlying thoughts for personal insight.
