The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a college student struggling to adjust to university life and the disappointment of failing a recent civil service exam. This has led to feelings of solitude and concerns about disappointing their parents.

[Personal Characteristics]
Yang enjoys drawing, which previously served as a source of relaxation and may become a helpful outlet again.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang experiences feelings of inadequacy and stress, primarily due to perceived parental expectations and academic setbacks, without any formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels miserable and defeated, avoiding social interactions and feeling pressured to meet expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yang's withdrawal from social life and struggle with negative self-perceptions, but also notes Yang's willingness to engage in identifying positive activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is asked to note down moments of happiness and moments of distress to better understand his emotional triggers and joys.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Yang identify positive experiences, and manage negative assumptions, encouraging engagement in rewarding activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yang's feelings of inadequacy and social isolation following college disappointment and a civil service exam failure, focusing on parental perception concerns. Agreed to track instances of overwhelming feelings and negative thoughts to better understand emotional patterns. Encouraged Yang to note moments that might bring happiness to identify personal interests and manage stress.
