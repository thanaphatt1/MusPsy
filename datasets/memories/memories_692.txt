The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student navigating significant changes, including the transition from high school to university and a recent relationship breakup. Liu feels overwhelmed by these adjustments and seeks to find a sense of belonging in this new phase.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys hobbies such as photography, which can offer creative outlets for expressing and coping with emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences feelings of anxiety and isolation triggered by academic and personal life changes, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels anxious and overwhelmed by the challenges of adjusting to college life and a recent breakup.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Liu's struggle with feelings of isolation and stress but sees potential for growth through reflection and community engagement.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should identify moments of feeling overwhelmed and the thoughts accompanying them, aiming to replace negative thoughts with more balanced perspectives.
[Session Goal:] Help Liu recognize patterns of stress through CBT strategies and foster engagement in supportive activities to build confidence and reduce isolation.
Counseling Summary:
In the session, Liu expressed feelings of anxiety and sadness associated with transitioning to college and recent relationship changes. The counselor encouraged Liu to track overwhelming moments, reframe negative self-talk with reminders of past achievements, and consider joining social activities for support. Liu agreed to challenge feelings of isolation and work on developing resilience through mindful awareness and balanced thinking.
