The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Dalis is a young adult working at a logistics company. He recently experienced a painful breakup, leading to feelings of regret and emotional turmoil. Dalis expressed being overwhelmed by emotions during the breakup, impacting his daily life and ability to focus at work.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He values honesty and connecting with trusted friends for support and reassurance. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Dalis experiences significant emotional distress due to personal relationship issues, characterized by self-criticism and recurrent negative thought patterns without a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Dalis feels ashamed and angry with himself following a heated breakup, leading to persistent negative emotions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Dalis's tendency to dwell on negative self-evaluation but also his willingness to explore patterns in his thoughts to improve his emotional state.
[Counseling Assignments:] Dalis should take note of moments when negative feelings intensify and reflect on the surrounding circumstances to identify patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Dalis become aware of negative thought patterns and begin exploring ways to manage emotional distress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Dalis's emotional response following a heated breakup, focusing on feelings of regret and the impact on daily functioning. Agreed to identify triggers by noting moments when intense emotions occur, aiming for deeper understanding. Continues exploring behavioral patterns to alleviate emotional distress.
