The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao H is a university student dealing with feelings of anger and isolation after being avoided by classmates due to cleanliness issues. Xiao H is quite concerned about being misunderstood and is working on changing habits.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao H enjoys playing soccer, which offers a sense of purpose and belonging.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao H is struggling with feelings of anger, isolation, and negative self-preoccupation but does not have a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao H feels angry, betrayed, and isolated, believing classmates are unjustly avoiding him due to perceived bad habits.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiao H's tendency to assume negative judgments from others and his openness to finding a resolution by discussing feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao H is encouraged to keep a journal of emotions and contact his grandparents for support and perspective on the situation.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to help Xiao H manage feelings of anger and isolation by encouraging open communication and re-engaging with supportive activities like soccer.
Counseling Summary:
Xiao H is experiencing feelings of anger and isolation due to misunderstandings with classmates over cleanliness issues, leading to negative assumptions and social disconnect. To address these emotions and improve his situation, he plans to talk to his supportive grandparents about his feelings and journal entries for guidance and then consider communicating openly with classmates about his efforts to change his habits. Additionally, Xiao H aims to alleviate stress by engaging in soccer, which may also offer opportunities for new connections.
