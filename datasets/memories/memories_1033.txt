The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaojie is an individual dealing with emotional distress due to past familial issues, specifically related to their father's absence and its impact on trust. Xiaojie has been exploring personal relationships and emotional responses towards family dynamics.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaojie enjoys reading, notably mystery novels by authors like Agatha Christie, which provides a temporary escapism from emotional stressors. Xiaojie also values supportive interactions with close friends and family, especially with their mother.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaojie experiences recurrent negative thoughts and emotions linked to familial relationships, primarily driven by perceived paternal abandonment. There is no formal diagnosis specified.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaojie feels conflicted about emotions regarding their father and is working on processing these feelings while seeking relief in understanding and acceptance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaojie's sincere effort to understand and possibly mend familial relationships, showing resilience in exploring emotional challenges with openness.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaojie should create a designated reading space for relaxation and engage in journaling to organize thoughts and emotions, working towards expressing these to their father when ready.
[Session Goal:] Support Xiaojie in recognizing and challenging negative thought patterns and fostering constructive communication with their father as part of emotional healing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaojie's distress related to her father's absence, identifying patterns of negative thought loops affecting emotions; agreed on journaling to process complex feelings and creating a reading space for comfort. Emphasized small steps like tracking emotional spikes, leveraging hobbies for relief, and seeking social connection to ease overwhelm. Promoted reflection as part of healing, highlighting the support in nurturing positive progress.
