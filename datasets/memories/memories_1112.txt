The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoche is a student who has experienced increasing anxiety over the past two years, primarily surrounding thoughts of contamination and cleanliness. These intrusive thoughts have intensified recently, impacting Xiaoche's studies due to the compulsion to repeatedly wash hands. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoche enjoys singing, an activity that provides some relief from anxiety amidst a hectic schedule.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoche shows signs of anxiety, particularly obsessive-compulsive tendencies related to contamination fears, yet there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoche feels anxious and overwhelmed, unable to break the cycle of washing hands due to a persistent fear of dirtiness in public places.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoche's deep-seated anxiety and compulsive behavior but also identifies a potential for relief through engaging in singing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoche is encouraged to establish a regular singing routine and jot down thoughts that feel overwhelming, assessing their rationality.
[Session Goal:] Begin utilizing CBT strategies to help Xiaoche manage contamination fears by integrating enjoyable activities and reflecting on the rationality of anxious thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaoche shared feelings of heightened anxiety and compulsive handwashing due to perceived contamination, affecting academic performance and concentration. The counselor suggested establishing a regular singing routine as a comforting activity and proposed using written reminders to challenge irrational cleanliness fears. Xiaoche agreed to try these strategies to reduce anxiety and achieve a more balanced day.
