The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
H is an international student dealing with financial stress after not securing a full tuition waiver, which has compelled them to gamble in hopes of solving monetary issues. Despite financial hardships, H is motivated to address these challenges proactively.

[Personal Characteristics]
H enjoys biking, which was a stress-relief activity in the past, and is keen on rebuilding a balanced life through healthy habits.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
H experiences stress and anxiety largely driven by financial pressures and has developed gambling habits, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] H feels overwhelmed and trapped by financial challenges and a resulting cycle of gambling to cope with anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices H's awareness of the negative impact of gambling on their financial situation and a willingness to explore alternative solutions.
[Counseling Assignments:] H is instructed to note gambling triggers and financial stressors to aid in identifying patterns and is encouraged to begin creating a personal budget.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help H break the cycle of gambling, recognize financial stress triggers, and begin adopting healthier coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
During the session, H shared financial stress due to not receiving a full tuition waiver and increased anxiety from gambling, a coping mechanism that exacerbates his issues. Together, they discussed tracking gambling urges and triggers, developing a budget, exploring part-time work, and engaging with a support system, while also planning to address emotional stressors such as loneliness and homesickness. The session emphasized small practical steps and understanding emotions to foster positive changes.
