The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao M is a college student struggling to adapt to college life and feeling detached from her major, leading to sleep disturbances. She experiences homesickness, misses her family, especially with her father's job change and mother's caregiving responsibilities for her grandmother.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao M has an interest in painting and music, which she enjoyed before attending college.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao M is grappling with feelings of anxiety and disconnection due to the challenges of adapting to a new environment and academic life, with no formal diagnosis of a mental disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao M feels overwhelmed, anxious, and struggles with a sense of belonging, impacting her daily interactions and sleep.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiao M's sense of isolation and reluctance to engage, but also her willingness to explore her feelings and take steps toward understanding them.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao M should keep a journal to record moments of anxiety and disconnection, alongside thoughts and reflections for review in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiao M become aware of her anxiety patterns and encourage positive social interactions through small steps, employing CBT to reframe negative thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao M's feelings of overwhelm and disconnection due to disinterest in their major and homesickness, which are affecting their sleep and social interactions. Agreed on maintaining a journal to gain insights into anxiety triggers and encouraged small positive social engagements to foster connections. Suggested focusing on one day at a time and acknowledging small positive interactions to gradually improve their adjustment to college life.

[Session Goal:] Assist Xiao M in recognizing and challenging cognitive distortions, promoting engagement in interest-based activities to foster a sense of belonging.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Xiao M shared feelings of not fitting in and disliking their major, with a tendency to assume everyone else is effortlessly happy. The consultant encouraged challenging these automatic negative thoughts and suggested joining an art club to reconnect with an activity they enjoy, promoting both social connection and personal fulfillment. Xiao M agreed to continue journaling about their experiences, both positive and challenging, and to explore new opportunities, which can offer a more balanced perspective and make the college experience more manageable.
