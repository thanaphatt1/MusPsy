The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student struggling with the pressures from academic studies and strict school rules. Additionally, they face challenges with video game addiction as a coping mechanism. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys solving puzzles and engaging with social issues, which they find relaxing and fulfilling.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences stress and anxiety, exacerbated by academic workload and dissatisfaction due to video game overuse, resulting in feelings of guilt and isolation. No formal mental health disorder diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed by academic pressures and struggles with guilt related to excessive gaming and its impact on studies and relationships.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's acknowledgment of their video game addiction as an escape from academic stress and perceives a willingness to explore healthier coping mechanisms.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to keep a journal of thoughts and feelings when gaming urges arise, to better understand and manage these impulses.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to address gaming as an anxiety coping mechanism, help the user identify triggers, and encourage personal growth focus over comparisons with peers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored student's overwhelm from academic pressure and gaming addiction, identifying gaming as an escape from anxiety and academic self-doubt. Suggested journaling thoughts when gaming, focusing on personal achievements to reduce self-comparison and build self-confidence, and incorporating mindfulness to create new coping strategies. Agreed to discuss time management further in the next session.
