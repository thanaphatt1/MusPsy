The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Amei is a university literature student feeling overwhelmed with uncertainty about her studies and career path, especially due to her parents' lack of understanding of higher education experiences. She values her interest in literature but is struggling to find purpose and direction.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Despite feeling lost, Amei finds solace in reading novels and has a deep appreciation for literature.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Amei experiences stress and uncertainty about her future, which impacts her motivation and engagement with her studies, lacking a formal diagnosis of anxiety or related disorders.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Amei feels overwhelmed and directionless, struggling to enjoy past comforts like reading novels.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Amei's deep engagement with literature and her openness to exploring mindfulness techniques to regain joy in reading.
[Counseling Assignments:] Amei is encouraged to experiment with mindfulness reading techniques and keep a reflection journal to track insights and interests.
[Session Goal:] Begin incorporating CBT techniques by reconnecting Amei with her love for reading and facilitating self-reflection to identify personal interests and potential career directions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Amei's feelings of overwhelm and uncertainty about her studies and future, focusing on her disconnection from literature as a former source of refuge. Agreed on practicing mindful reading and maintaining a reflection journal to identify interests, manage stress, and rediscover enjoyment in literature, offering new perspectives for personal and career growth.
