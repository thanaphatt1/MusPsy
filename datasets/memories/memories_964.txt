The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a student experiencing significant stress related to academic performance and family expectations, particularly from his mother. Li feels anxious about disappointing his mother and is exploring ways to cope with these feelings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys drawing, which serves as a source of relaxation and emotional expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences stress and worry stemming from academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels stressed and anxious due to academic pressures and his mother's reactions to his performance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Li's recurring focus on not meeting expectations and his openness to exploring methods to manage stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should note down recurring stress-related thoughts and integrate drawing into his daily routine as a relaxation tool.
[Session Goal:] Help Li identify and challenge stress-inducing thoughts using CBT strategies and encourage engaging in relaxation activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's feelings of stress and self-doubt stemming from academic performance and pressure from her mother. Discussed the impact of negative thoughts and proposed strategies to address them, including noting down recurring thoughts and incorporating drawing as a calming daily habit. Encouraged Li to recognize thoughts as separate from truths to reduce stress, with plans to further examine these patterns in their next session.
