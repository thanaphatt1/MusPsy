The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Barna is a young adult struggling with a significant fear of hospitals, causing anxiety about seeking necessary medical treatment. Barna has a history of occasional positive interactions with healthcare providers, which are overshadowed by the negative anticipation of hospital settings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Barna finds solace in reading and meditation, using these activities as tools for managing stress and anxiety.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Barna experiences anxiety primarily linked to hospital environments, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Barna feels anxious and trapped, recognizing the necessity of medical visits yet struggling to overcome fear.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Barna's willingness to reflect on past positive healthcare experiences and openness to exploring anxiety management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Barna is encouraged to reflect on prior positive healthcare experiences and note recurring thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Barna identify anxiety triggers and adopt a more balanced view of hospital-related experiences.
Counseling Summary:
The session with Barna focused on exploring and understanding Barna's fear of hospitals which hinders their ability to seek medical help when needed. Together, they identified small, manageable tasks, such as reflecting on positive healthcare experiences and practicing thought shifts, to help reduce anxiety associated with hospitals. Additionally, Barna committed to integrating mindfulness and relaxing activities into their routine to create a more structured and serene day, aiming for gradual progress in managing their anxiety.
