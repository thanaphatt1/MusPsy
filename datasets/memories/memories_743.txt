The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Youli is a college student feeling regretful about their major choice, experiencing anxiety and sleep difficulties, alongside concerns about their future path in academia.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Youli enjoys reading novels, especially fantasy genres, which offer a form of relaxation and escapism.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Youli experiences anxiety and stress related to academic choices yet shows no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Youli feels stuck and regretful about their major choice, leading to anxiety and difficulty focusing on studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Youli's tendency to overthink and second-guess their decisions yet notes Youli's openness to exploring change and anxiety management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Youli should jot down thoughts and feelings about their major choice to identify troubling patterns and gain insights.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Youli explore thoughts related to their major choice, aiming to break the cycle of overthinking and find clarity.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Youli's regret and anxiety regarding college major choice, discussing how these emotions impact her life and contribute to overthinking. Agreed on a task for Youli to note thoughts and feelings related to her major to identify patterns and address overthinking. Focus continues on recognizing and gently altering thought patterns to empower change.
