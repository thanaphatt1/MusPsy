The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
F is a second-year university student feeling anxious about job prospects and often compares their progress to roommates, which leads to feelings of isolation and inadequacy.

[Personal Characteristics]
F enjoys playing the guitar and finds it helpful as a relaxation activity. They have an interest in meditation and reading for mental clarity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
F experiences anxiety primarily triggered by future job prospects and social comparisons, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] F feels anxious and isolated, struggling with comparisons to roommates regarding future planning.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes F’s tendency towards negative self-comparisons and acknowledges their willingness to explore thoughts leading to anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] F should note recurring comparison thoughts and their validity, using guitar playing as a stress-relieving activity.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help F identify and challenge comparison-based anxieties and encourage engagement in enjoyable activities like playing guitar.
Counseling Summary:
Explored F's anxiety related to future job prospects and feelings of inadequacy due to comparisons with roommates. Agreed on tasks to both observe comparison thoughts for evidence and incorporate guitar-playing to manage stress, aiming to challenge assumptions and improve emotional well-being.
