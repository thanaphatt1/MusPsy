The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoqing is a high school student who has recently moved into the same city as their parents, having grown up with grandparents until fourth grade. They feel pressure from their mother regarding academic performance, especially in math and science, and experience a sense of being overshadowed by a sibling.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoqing enjoys art, specifically drawing, as a significant form of stress relief and self-expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoqing experiences stress and self-imposed pressure related to academic expectations and familial comparisons but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoqing feels stressed and overwhelmed due to academic pressures and perceived familial expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoqing's struggle with feelings of inadequacy and comparison to their sibling but notes an interest in exploring art as a coping mechanism.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoqing is encouraged to incorporate daily art practice, such as sketching before bed, and to start a journal to explore and clarify their feelings.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiaoqing manage stress, understand stress-related thoughts, and improve communication about their needs and interests.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoqing's academic stress and familial pressure, emphasizing the role of art as a calming outlet. Agreed on incorporating daily sketching and journaling to manage stress and gain clarity. Discussed strategies for better communication with her mother to foster understanding and support.
