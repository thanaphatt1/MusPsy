The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student dealing with stress and anxiety, particularly concerning academic performance and comparisons with peers. They have expressed a proficiency in math, which serves as a confidence booster, while struggles in other subjects trigger anxiety. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user finds enjoyment in reading mystery novels, which provides a means of relaxation and escapism from academic pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences academic-related anxiety but demonstrates moments of focus and self-awareness, especially when engaging in math.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels stressed and anxious due to classroom pressure and perceived inability to keep up with peers in non-math subjects.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes the user's cycle of self-doubt and anxiety but also notes their recognition of math as a personal strength and a source of confidence.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should identify moments in and out of academic settings where anxiety lessens and record recurring stress-related thoughts, focusing on recognizing and reinforcing strengths.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help the user manage stress, identify anxiety triggers, and encourage engagement in activities that enhance relaxation and confidence.
Counseling Summary:
Explored User's academic-related anxiety and the cycle of overthinking stemming from self-doubt in non-math subjects. Agreed on identifying focused moments in academics and documenting strengths, particularly in math, to manage self-doubt, alongside scheduling regular reading for stress relief. Encouraged self-compassionate thinking and tracking these practices for further discussion in the next session.
