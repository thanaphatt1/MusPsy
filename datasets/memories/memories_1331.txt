The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is an employee who has recently experienced a distressing incident at work involving a perceived electrical shock. Since then, they've been dealing with heightened anxiety and difficulty sleeping, and they avoid equipment related to electricity.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user values calmness and is open to learning new stress-relief techniques, although they have not yet fully incorporated such practices into their routine.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user exhibits anxiety, specifically related to electrical equipment after the incident at work, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious and restless, primarily driven by a recent event at work that triggered a fear-related response.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's initial fear and hesitation but appreciates their willingness to try new anxiety management techniques like mindful breathing.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to practice mindful breathing for a few minutes daily and review provided materials to develop stress-relief techniques.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to guide the user in managing anxiety stemming from the incident and initiate mindful awareness practices to reduce panic feelings.
Counseling Summary:
The session addressed the user's heightened anxiety following a scare involving electricity, leading to avoidance and sleep disturbances. The consultant introduced mindfulness exercises, focusing on brief mindful breathing and grounding techniques to gradually alleviate anxiety. The user agreed to try these strategies with provided guidance and also to maintain a journal to observe changes, with plans for follow-up to discuss progress.
