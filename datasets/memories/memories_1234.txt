The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a professional experiencing work-related anxiety, particularly during presentations and evaluations. Recently, he struggled with stress due to an important project review. Zhang tends to ruminate on mistakes and fears judgment from colleagues.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He has an interest in playing the guitar, which serves as a potential stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang exhibits signs of anxiety linked to performance situations, characterized by self-doubt and fear of negative evaluation. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels stressed and anxious about work presentations, replaying them and blaming himself for perceived shortcomings.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's focus on self-blame and perceived judgment from others but notes a willingness to explore and adjust his thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should note instances of self-blaming thoughts and the emotions they evoke for a better understanding.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Zhang identify the connections between thoughts, feelings, and behaviors and reduce self-blame.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's work-related anxiety and self-critical thinking patterns. Encouraged challenging negative thoughts and balancing them with positive feedback, practicing presentations, and engaging in relaxing activities like guitar playing. Developed practical strategies to boost confidence and manage stress effectively, leaving Zhang more aware and equipped to handle future challenges.
