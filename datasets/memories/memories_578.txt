The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family. Recently, Liu has been confronted with emotions tied to their late girlfriend, triggered by a resemblance seen in a new acquaintance at their research institute.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He also enjoys painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels overwhelmed and confused, struggling with emotions tied to memories of a past relationship.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist acknowledges Liu's emotional turmoil but appreciates Liu's willingness to explore grounding techniques to refocus their attention.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should set daily short-term goals for thesis work and try journaling to separate emotions from academic tasks.
[Session Goal:] Help Liu manage intrusive emotions by employing CBT strategies, such as breaking tasks into specific short-term goals and using reminders to stay focused.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's emotional turmoil caused by a new colleague's resemblance to his late girlfriend, which has been affecting his focus on his thesis. Agreed to manage these emotions by setting small, achievable task goals and using visual reminders, coupled with nightly journaling to process feelings. Liu plans to utilize support from his sister and embrace mindfulness techniques for better emotional regulation.
