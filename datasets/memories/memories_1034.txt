The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is dealing with overwhelming feelings and anxiety after an incident involving factory workers. He often feels out of control due to increased guilt and fear about the future.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang values family support and believes in open communication. He also appreciates reading and writing as ways to unwind and process emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang exhibits symptoms of anxiety, notably linked to a recent distressing event, but there's no confirmation of a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels overwhelmed and anxious after the incident with the factory workers, resulting in trouble sleeping and emotional distress.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Yang's openness in expressing emotions and his determination to explore anxiety triggers through self-observation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang should document stressful thoughts and consider journaling in a calm space to better understand anxiety patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Yang identify stress triggers, and encourage relaxation practices like journaling or meditation.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yang's feelings of overwhelm and anxiety related to an incident, impacting sleep and daily life. Developed strategies for emotional management, like journaling, relaxation techniques before bed, and fostering supportive connections, with a focus on self-kindness during challenging times. Agreed to reconvene for further progress evaluation.
