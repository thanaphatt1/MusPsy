The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Y is a high school student dealing with stress and pressure primarily from academic expectations set by their father. Xiao Y experiences feelings of isolation due to limited communication within the family. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Y finds solace in drawing and music, using them as means of relaxation and self-expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Y exhibits stress and anxiety triggered by academic pressures and family expectations, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Y feels overwhelmed and upset by mounting academic pressures and avoids discussing these feelings with others.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiao Y's tendency to bottle emotions and identify academic pressure from perceived expectations but notes engagement with expressive strategies like journaling for clarity.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Y is encouraged to maintain a journal to record thoughts and emotions, providing insight into recurring stress patterns.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Xiao Y express and understand emotions through journaling, aiming to alleviate bottled stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Y's feelings of being overwhelmed and isolated due to academic pressure and lack of communication with their father. Agreed on starting a journaling practice to express and manage emotions. Emphasized the importance of taking gradual steps towards emotional clarity.
