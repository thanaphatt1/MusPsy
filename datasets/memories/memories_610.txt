The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Z is a student experiencing frustration primarily due to academic challenges and parental pressure about grades. Z enjoys activities such as sports and video games, which provide a sense of competency.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Z is enthusiastic about drawing, particularly characters from video games and cartoons, which serves as a relaxing hobby.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Z experiences stress and feeling of inadequacy related to school pressures but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Z feels overwhelmed by school-related stress and a sense of inadequacy due to not meeting academic expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes Z's low self-esteem linked to academic performance but identifies a positive outlook towards exploring interests like drawing and sports.
[Counseling Assignments:] Z should document recurring stressful thoughts and emotions, and explore engaging in drawing and discuss with teachers about including creative projects in school.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Z identify specific stressors, manage feelings of inadequacy, and integrate personal interests into the school routine.
Counseling Summary:
In this counseling session, Z expressed feeling overwhelmed and frustrated by academic pressures and parental expectations, leading to self-critical thoughts and a desire to avoid school. The consultant encouraged Z to document these thoughts and emotions and explore interests in school subjects, such as science, and creative outlets like drawing, to enhance engagement and motivation. Together, they set goals for Z to discuss incorporating drawing into school projects, pay more attention to science classes, and explore joining a soccer team, aiming for a balanced routine of responsibilities and personal interests.
