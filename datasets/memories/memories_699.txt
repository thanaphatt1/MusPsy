The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Chengyang is a university student feeling considerable stress, particularly related to financial concerns and family expectations. Having recently experienced added pressure following a family visit marked by his eldest sister's childbirth, he's increasingly worried about being perceived as a financial burden to his parents, who work tirelessly in construction and farming.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Chengyang has a strong inclination towards drawing, using it as a method of relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Chengyang exhibits anxiety and guilt exacerbated by familial and financial pressures, without a formal diagnosis of a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Chengyang feels overwhelmed by familial financial struggles and pressured by self-imposed expectations to contribute.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Chengyang's tendency towards negative self-assumptions and guilt but notes his willingness to explore these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Chengyang should initiate communication with his family to express his feelings and engage in drawing to relieve stress.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Chengyang identify and challenge negative thoughts, encourage expression of feelings, and promote engaging in stress-reducing activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Chengyang explored feelings of stress and guilt related to his family's financial situation, along with assumptions of disappointment from his family that contributed to his anxiety. He considered talking to his family to express his feelings and clear up misunderstandings, and recognized drawing as a beneficial activity for relaxation. By becoming more aware of his negative thought patterns, Chengyang aimed to view situations realistically and communicate openly, thus equipping himself with strategies to manage similar challenges in the future.
