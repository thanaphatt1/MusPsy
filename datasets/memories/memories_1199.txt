The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
F is a university student navigating the transition from high school, struggling with a heavy workload and the pressure of upcoming exams. F is particularly concerned about their performance in computer science courses, noting the enthusiasm of peers and feeling the need to keep up.

[Personal Characteristics]  
F finds jogging helpful for stress relief and seeks to incorporate more enjoyable activities into their routine.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
F experiences anxiety primarily from academic pressures, especially when facing challenging coursework and study expectations, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] F feels overwhelmed with academic pressures and experiences frequent anxiety, particularly around exam preparation.
[Counselor Observations:] The counselor observes F's acute awareness of academic pressures and acknowledges F's willingness to explore stress management techniques through activities like jogging.
[Counseling Assignments:] F is encouraged to start a journal documenting thoughts and feelings regarding academic pressures and identify specific times and triggers of anxiety.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help F become more aware of stress triggers and incorporate constructive stress management techniques like regular breaks into their routine.
Counseling Summary:
Explored F's academic stress and anxiety related to transitioning to university, particularly the pressure of performing well in computer science courses. Introduced journaling to track and understand stressors, and suggested incorporating breaks and seeking help from peers to improve well-being. F plans to set specific study times with breaks and engage in collaborative study sessions, feeling more optimistic about these changes.
