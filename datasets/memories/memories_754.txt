The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lin Xia is a young adult experiencing emotional turmoil, predominantly due to an idol she admires announcing a relationship. This announcement has significantly affected her emotional well-being and focus on studies.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She used to enjoy drawing and watching anime, using these activities as a form of stress relief. Spending time with her friend Mei tends to ease her emotional distress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lin Xia displays signs of emotional distress and decreased interest in previous hobbies, triggered by external events related to her idol, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lin Xia feels emotionally overwhelmed and disconnected, struggling with a loss of interest in activities she once enjoyed.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist acknowledges Lin Xia’s deep emotional investment in external figures and recognizes her potential in reconnecting with creative outlets to regain interest and joy.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lin Xia should jot down her emotional states over time, alongside experimenting with re-engaging in drawing and anime to explore potential positive impacts.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Lin Xia regain interest in enjoyable activities and identify triggers of emotional distress for better management.
Counseling Summary:
The session focused on Lin Xia's emotional struggles following her idol’s relationship announcement, which led to a loss of interest in previous enjoyments and affected her studies. The consultant suggested tracking her emotions and gently reintroducing creative activities like drawing and anime into her routine as a means to rediscover joy. Lin Xia agreed to try these activities, noting her experiences in a journal to better understand what impacts her mood.
