The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhong is a university student struggling with the transition to university life, feeling homesick and missing family support. Zhong has considered dropping out but is encouraged by family to continue with studies.

[Personal Characteristics]
He enjoys drawing and writing, which provide relaxation and focus, and has recently developed an interest in literature, joining a literature club.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhong experiences heightened anxiety due to unfamiliar settings and self-doubt about fitting in, but there’s no mention of a formal anxiety disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhong feels overwhelmed by the new university environment and experiences significant homesickness and anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhong's intense feelings of loneliness and anxiety yet identifies a willingness to explore coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhong should try rekindling the hobby of drawing to help manage emotions and note down recurring fears to assess their realism.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhong understand emotional reactions and employ CBT to identify and challenge negative thought patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhong's adjustment challenges and anxiety stemming from being away from home and university pressures. Zhong recognized negative thought patterns amplifying anxiety, affecting concentration and sleep. Agreed to exercises for emotional management and realistic assessment of thoughts.
