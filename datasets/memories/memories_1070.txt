The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a young adult who has recently experienced disappointment from not getting into college, which has led to feelings of stress and being undervalued, especially in comparison to their sister, who receives more attention from their parents.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys drawing, which serves as a creative outlet for their emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li exhibits feelings of stress and low self-worth, which are exacerbated by family dynamics and recent academic setbacks. No formal mental health disorder diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels stressed, undervalued, and struggles with comparisons to their sister.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li's vivid upset over unmet expectations and their perceived neglect within the family dynamic.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should document any situations or thoughts that intensify feelings of being undervalued or left out.
[Session Goal:] Identify specific emotions and thoughts that contribute to feelings of inadequacy; encourage Li to express these thoughts creatively, perhaps through drawing.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling sessions, Li expressed feelings of stress and inadequacy due to not getting into college and feeling overshadowed by her sister's achievements, causing emotions of anger and sadness. To tackle these issues, the counselor suggested Li reflect on triggering thoughts and emotions, assess their realism, and reconnect with her passion for drawing to provide emotional relief and foster a positive environment for change. Additionally, Li was encouraged to maintain awareness of her emotions' intensity and consider reaching out to supportive family members, aiming to cultivate self-understanding and improve well-being progressively.
