The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
K is a university student struggling with feelings of isolation and sadness, exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic and a deteriorating relationship with a best friend. K reports difficulties sleeping and concentrating on studies following a distressing incident.

[Personal Characteristics]  
K used to enjoy writing stories, which served as a self-expression outlet, and has an interest in sketching.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
K experiences depressive symptoms and social isolation, largely triggered by strained friendships and prolonged isolation, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] K feels down and alone, expressing frustration with a sense of disconnect and misunderstanding from others.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes K's struggles with loneliness and loss of connection but recognizes K's openness to finding constructive emotions through journaling and potentially rekindling interests.
[Counseling Assignments:] K is encouraged to maintain a journal capturing both negative and positive thoughts and consider writing short stories or diary entries as a means of self-expression.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help K manage feelings of isolation, identify patterns in thoughts and emotions, and reintroduce creative outlets for self-expression.
Counseling Summary:
Explored K's feelings of disconnection and isolation due to changes in friendships and pandemic challenges, leading to emotional distress and difficulty focusing. Agreed on maintaining a journal of thoughts and engaging in writing activities to manage emotions and gain clarity. Encouraged K to recognize positive moments and use creative expression to support emotional well-being.
