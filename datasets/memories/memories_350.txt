The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a professional who recently started a new job, grappling with adaptation challenges and feeling overwhelmed by the new work environment and task management. Zhang Wei is navigating these transitions while feeling anxious and self-doubtful.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys playing video games and socializing, which helps alleviate stress when integrated into a weekly routine.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences work-related anxiety and a recurring cycle of self-doubt but has not been diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels anxious and overwhelmed in adapting to a new job, questioning their abilities and feeling stuck in a loop of self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Zhang Wei's struggle with anxiety driven by the new work environment but notes the user's willingness to explore past successes for potential coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Journal thoughts and emotions during the week, and reflect on past situations managed effectively to rediscover coping strategies.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to identify and manage stress and build confidence by recognizing past achievements and gradually shifting thinking patterns.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang Wei's anxiety and self-doubt related to adapting to a new job, emphasizing the importance of managing overwhelming feelings. Encouraged journaling as a tool to identify thought patterns and revisit previously successful coping strategies, like setting small goals. Agreed to discuss insights from journaling in the next session to facilitate healthier thinking patterns.
