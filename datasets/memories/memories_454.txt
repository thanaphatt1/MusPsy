The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is an adult who recently lost their home due to unemployment and is currently living on the streets. This situation has been compounded by a strained relationship with a social worker, adding to their feelings of helplessness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei occasionally recalls past achievements, which provides them with temporary encouragement and a reminder of their capabilities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei experiences overwhelming anxiety and feelings of helplessness, particularly concerning their current homelessness and relationship dynamics. No formal mental health disorder is diagnosed yet.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels overwhelmed and anxious due to their precarious living situation and loss of stable housing.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes that Wei struggles with negative self-attribution and feels powerless but shows willingness to explore and address these thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei is tasked with keeping a journal of daily thoughts and emotions to identify recurring negative thought patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Wei to CBT strategies to manage overwhelming emotions, identify and challenge negative thought patterns, and initiate thought reevaluation.
Counseling Summary:
Wei discussed his challenges with homelessness and anxiety after losing his job and housing, expressing feelings of overwhelm and self-blame. The consultant guided Wei to identify negative thought patterns and encouraged keeping a journal of thoughts and emotions, including past achievements, to challenge these negative thoughts with evidence and reality. They focused on gradually improving Wei's mental well-being by questioning unrealistic thoughts, aiming to build self-efficacy and emotional relief through reflection and mindful awareness.
