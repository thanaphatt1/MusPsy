The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Kirti is a high school student experiencing difficulty with peer interactions and school pressures, particularly stemming from misunderstandings and perceived judgment in class. Kirti is sensitive to criticism and desires positive social connections.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys drawing, which she uses to calm her mind and refocus on positive aspects of life.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Kirti is dealing with stress and anxiety associated with school dynamics, characterized by embarrassment and anger due to peer and authoritative misunderstanding, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Kirti feels upset and embarrassed due to criticism from classmates and a teacher's impatience.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Kirti's tendency to internalize negative experiences and sees potential in her willingness to use self-reflection for better emotional understanding.
[Counseling Assignments:] Kirti should spend a few minutes daily writing her feelings and thoughts to better understand and manage her emotions.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT strategies to aid Kirti in identifying thoughts triggered by embarrassment, encouraging self-expression through writing for emotional clarity.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Kirti's school stress and emotional responses to negative experiences with teachers and classmates. Agreed on a journaling task to promote emotional processing and identify triggers, incorporating both positive and negative experiences for a balanced perspective. Emphasized the importance of self-awareness and open communication for emotional management.
