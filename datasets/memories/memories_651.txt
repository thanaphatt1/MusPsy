The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student experiencing anxiety and difficulty sleeping due to rumors about a neighboring dorm being haunted and influenced by a recent scary movie. The user is seeking ways to manage anxious thoughts that disrupt sleep.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming activity, and finds solace in listening to soothing instrumental music.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety specifically triggered by fear-inducing stories and movies, leading to sleep disturbances. There is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious and has trouble sleeping due to distressing thoughts about ghosts and scary movies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's willingness to explore anxiety management techniques and the potential benefit of incorporating relaxing activities into their routine.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should write down distressing nighttime thoughts and consider sketching and listening to calming music as part of their bedtime routine.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help the user manage anxiety, focus on eliminating distressing thoughts, and engage in relaxing activities like drawing and music before bed.
Counseling Summary:
Explored user’s nighttime anxiety linked to a rumored haunting and a scary movie. Recommended keeping a thought journal to understand anxious thoughts better and suggested incorporating calming activities like drawing and listening to soothing music into the bedtime routine. Agreed to review the impact of these practices in the next session to manage anxiety and improve sleep.
