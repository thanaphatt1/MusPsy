The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaohao is a student dealing with frustration and a sense of misunderstanding from a recent change in school seating arrangements, leading to discomfort and a feeling of isolation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He has a keen interest in drawing, which serves as a therapeutic outlet for stress and self-expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaohao experiences feelings of anxiety and frustration, particularly triggered by changes in his routine and perceived isolation, though there's no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaohao feels frustrated and misunderstood, particularly due to unfair seating arrangements at school making him uncomfortable.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaohao's struggle with the imposed seating change but notes his openness to exploring ways to manage these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaohao should explore reaching out to classmates to identify shared interests and plan regular drawing sessions to alleviate stress.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Xiaohao to shift negative perceptions through CBT techniques, explore new social interactions, and establish a creative routine to manage stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaohao's feelings of frustration and isolation due to classroom seating arrangements, Discussed how negative self-talk might be exacerbating his distress, and encouraged him to bridge the gap with male classmates by finding common interests. Suggested communicating feelings to the teacher for a more comfortable learning environment and incorporating drawing into his routine for stress relief and emotional expression.
