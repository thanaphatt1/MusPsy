The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is an individual experiencing emotional distress following a recent breakup that occurred three months ago. Zhang often feels lonely and overwhelmed, frequently withdrawn from social interactions. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang used to enjoy drawing and is considering reigniting this creative interest. Additionally, Zhang has shown an interest in reading as a potential activity for relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety and feelings of sadness linked to negative thoughts about being left alone, without any formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels emotionally overwhelmed and lonely due to the aftermath of a breakup, struggling with mood instability.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang’s openness to exploring emotions and recognizing their influence on daily experience, alongside a willingness to engage in therapeutic exercises.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to keep a journal documenting daily thoughts and emotions, noting both challenges and moments of joy.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Zhang to CBT techniques to manage negative thought patterns and encourage incorporating creative outlets such as drawing to support emotional well-being.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang’s feelings of loneliness and emotional turmoil following a breakup, focusing on the impact on daily life, including mood instability and social withdrawal. Agreed on journaling and drawing as therapeutic tasks to identify and balance negative thoughts with moments of joy, aiming to foster emotional insight and gradual healing. Encouraged openness and creativity as paths to emotional growth, establishing a foundation for future sessions.
