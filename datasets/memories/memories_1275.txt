The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jacob is a school-aged child who recently experienced stress related to a school trip to a farm. He is concerned that his behavior, such as avoiding interaction and exhibiting fear of animals, might worry his teachers and family. Jacob enjoys drawing, which he favors over writing.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Jacob likes playing at home and feels more comfortable in familiar environments. He values creative activities like drawing and building with blocks to explore his feelings and reduce anxiety.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jacob experiences anxiety when faced with new situations or environments, notably around social interactions and new activities, but no formal mental health diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jacob felt anxious and uneasy during a school trip, leading to withdrawal from group activities and avoidance of interaction with animals.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist sees Jacob's reluctance to engage in new environments and noticed his anxiety around participating in new activities while encouraging self-expression through art.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jacob is encouraged to draw his experiences and feelings regarding the trip and reflect on those drawings to visualize positive changes in similar future scenarios.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies by using drawing as a tool for Jacob to express and rethink his experiences, fostering comfort and confidence in new situations.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Jacob's discomfort with social interactions and fear during a school trip, highlighting differences in his comfort levels around animals and new environments. Proposed using drawing as a creative outlet to reflect on his experience and visualize more positive scenarios, encouraging empowerment and perspective shift. Jacob agreed to try drawing as a therapeutic exercise to manage these feelings and expressed openness to discussing his progress in future sessions.
