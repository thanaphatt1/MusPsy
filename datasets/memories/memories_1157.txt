The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a university student experiencing stress and anxiety following a major transition at school. Recent issues include adapting to a new major, student ID complications, credit transfers, and new living arrangements, leaving Yang feeling overwhelmed and isolated.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang enjoys reading, especially inspirational stories, as a means of escaping stress and rejuvenating.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang exhibits stress and anxiety related to academic changes and compares themselves unfavorably to peers, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang is feeling stressed and anxious, noting challenges with adapting to a new academic environment and feeling isolated in their dormitory.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yang's difficulty with self-comparison and the negative impact on their perception of ability but notes openness to addressing these challenges.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang should reflect on their thoughts and feelings regarding the transition to gain insight into anxiety triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to aid Yang in managing stress, understanding anxiety-driven thoughts, and fostering self-compassion.
Counseling Summary:
Yang has been experiencing stress and anxiety following a major transition, feeling overwhelmed by academic and social challenges. Together with the consultant, Yang worked on identifying and reframing negative thought patterns, using positive self-talk, and engaging in enjoyable activities like reading to manage anxiety. Through these sessions, Yang learned to practice patience, self-compassion, and to reach out for support, leaving more equipped to handle future challenges.
