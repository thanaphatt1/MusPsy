The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mr. Zhao is dealing with the aftermath of a stroke, which has significantly impacted his independence and daily life. He feels frustrated and helpless as he can no longer perform tasks as he used to, which affects his quality of life and makes him feel burdensome to his family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He used to enjoy painting as it was a relaxing activity, though he has not engaged in it recently.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mr. Zhao is experiencing feelings of frustration, helplessness, and discouragement due to his current limitations post-stroke. There is no mention of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mr. Zhao feels frustrated and helpless, struggling with a loss of independence and a perceived burden on his family.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Mr. Zhao's deep sense of frustration and loss, yet notes appreciation for structured guidance and his openness to reflection.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mr. Zhao should reflect on positive memories and achievements from the past and consider reconnecting them to his current capabilities.
[Session Goal:] Help Mr. Zhao recognize and affirm his inherent strengths, reintroduce engaging activities like painting, and focus on process over outcome.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mr. Zhao's frustrations and sense of helplessness following a stroke, focusing on its impact on his independence and emotions. Agreed on revisiting past achievements and resuming painting as therapeutic activities to enhance mood and rediscover strengths. Emphasized mindful engagement in the process to alleviate pressure and encouraged structured reflection for future discussions.
