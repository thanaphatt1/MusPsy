The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
F is a third-year university student experiencing significant stress due to academic workload and independence challenges, particularly as they are living away from home. F is concerned about managing responsibilities without appearing overly dependent on friends.

[Personal Characteristics]  
F enjoys reading novels, especially mystery ones, which serve as a way to unwind and momentarily escape stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
F experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures and self-doubt in their abilities to handle tasks independently.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] F feels overwhelmed by the demands of university life and struggles with self-reliance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes F’s determination to tackle challenges independently and recognizes their openness to exploring and applying self-help strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] F is encouraged to note instances of independent successes and how these moments made them feel, fostering a sense of capability.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT techniques to help F recognize achievements, manage stress through effective planning, and build confidence through self-awareness.
Counseling Summary:
Explored F's feelings of overwhelm from academic pressures and reliance on friends for support while encouraging independence. Agreed to focus on planning and recognizing individual successes by breaking tasks into manageable steps, aiming to build confidence and find balance between independence and seeking help. Suggested consistent planner use with rewards to maintain the habit, fostering a sense of control over workload.
