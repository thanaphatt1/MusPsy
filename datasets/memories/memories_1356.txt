The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a high school student struggling with anxiety triggered by a recent school event. He feels compelled to wash his hands constantly, impacting both his studies and social interactions. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys playing basketball and finds it relaxing. He also listens to music and occasionally draws to ease his mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang exhibits behaviors related to anxiety and obsessive-compulsive tendencies, especially concerning cleanliness and germs, though no formal diagnosis has been made.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious and compelled to wash his hands frequently after a school event heightened his concerns about cleanliness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's heightened anxiety linked to perceived germ threats and also identifies his awareness of exacerbated thoughts despite the logical recognition that not all surfaces pose risks.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should observe and record situations that trigger his hand-washing impulse, assessing the perceptions of dirtiness or contamination.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT strategies to identify and challenge thoughts triggering compulsions, reducing anxiety and developing more balanced thinking.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's obsessive handwashing linked to anxiety and exaggerated fears about cleanliness, leading to social withdrawal and embarrassment. Over repeated sessions, Zhang identified triggers and patterns in his thoughts, realizing the anxiety often exaggerated risks; he practiced questioning these thoughts and shared his experience with a friend for reinforcement. Engaging in enjoyable activities like basketball and maintaining open communication about his feelings further helped in managing stress and boosting confidence.
