The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Benny is a professional experiencing significant anxiety and panic attacks, particularly in confined spaces like subways. A recent panic incident on public transport led to being late for work, heightening stress and self-consciousness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Benny has a passion for photography, especially capturing nature scenes like sunsets and landscapes, which serves as a calming and creative outlet.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Benny experiences symptoms of anxiety and panic attacks triggered by claustrophobia, particularly in public transportation, but does not have a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Benny feels anxious and trapped in confined spaces, leading to panic attacks, such as racing heart and a sense of losing control.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Benny's acute awareness of his anxiety triggers and a willingness to explore techniques for gaining control, despite the distress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Benny is tasked with jotting down thoughts and situations triggering anxiety and attempting deep breathing as a calming technique when symptoms arise.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Benny recognize and manage anxiety symptoms, starting with cognitive restructuring and breathing exercises for panic situations.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Benny's experiences of anxiety in closed spaces, particularly the subway, focusing on heightened physical responses and catastrophic thinking. Benny agreed to note triggers and practice slow breathing and visualization techniques to manage anxiety, with support from his friend Jamie. The session concluded with an array of strategies for Benny to implement, emphasizing progress and reassurance in facing anxiety challenges.
