The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaox is a student feeling stressed and disappointed due to recently losing a class committee election, which has affected their self-esteem and feeling of recognition, especially after previously holding leadership roles in high school. Xiaox values leadership and is concerned about others not appreciating their potential.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaox has shown an interest in engaging in leadership roles and discussions, aiming to assert their strengths more openly.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaox primarily struggles with frustration and feelings of being undervalued, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaox feels stressed and disappointed about losing a class committee election, feeling overlooked and undervalued.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaox's strong desire for recognition and leadership but also their initial openness to exploring new perspectives on their experience.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaox should write down thoughts and feelings about the election to better understand emotional responses and identify any thought patterns.
[Session Goal:] Begin exploring CBT strategies to recognize and cope with negative emotions and develop techniques for managing feelings of disappointment.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaox initially felt stressed and disappointed after losing a class committee election, believing it was due to pre-election campaigning by others. Through sessions focused on recognizing and challenging negative thoughts, Xiaox learned to communicate strengths more confidently and joined the debate club, boosting self-assurance and assertiveness. Xiaox plans to apply these strategies by avoiding assumptions and embracing proactive communication in future challenges.
