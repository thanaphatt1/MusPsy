The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a project manager facing significant stress due to project delays and team issues at work. Wei values effective leadership and worries about managing team stress and project outcomes efficiently.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei enjoys jogging, which serves as a stress-relief activity and provides clarity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei experiences anxiety and stress primarily due to work-related pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels anxious and overwhelmed by the responsibility of the delayed project and team stress.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Wei's tendency to assume full responsibility for setbacks, coupled with a readiness to explore alternative perspectives.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei is encouraged to note recurring stress-related thoughts for further discussion and exploration of alternate perspectives.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to identify and challenge stress-inducing thought patterns and encourage balanced leadership perspectives.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wei's anxiety and frustration related to project delays and team dynamics at work, acknowledging the pressure of feeling solely responsible for the issues. Identified self-critical thought patterns and agreed on the task of noting recurring thoughts during stress for further discussion of their impact and exploring alternative perspectives.
