The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is experiencing anxiety and a lack of confidence related to recent weight gain and binge eating at night, which disrupts their daily life and studies.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing as a hobby, which provides a distraction and joy. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user displays symptoms of stress eating and low self-esteem, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious and overwhelmed by changes in their weight and eating habits.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user’s distress over their lack of control regarding eating but also notes their willingness to gain insight into their habits.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should start a journal noting feelings and thoughts related to eating habits and self-image and try replacing nighttime snacking with listening to music or reading.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help the user understand the triggers for binge eating, emphasizing small, manageable change steps.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's eating habits and self-image concerns related to nighttime binge eating and weight gain. Suggested maintaining a journal to identify emotional and mental triggers and proposed replacing nighttime snacking with reading or listening to music as a small weekly goal. Emphasized the importance of self-awareness and manageable changes to promote emotional well-being and self-confidence.
