The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaofang is a young individual who has recently encountered embarrassing situations involving anxiety around boys, particularly stemming from eye contact with a boy they like. This experience has heightened anxieties in social settings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaofang appreciates reading novels, a pastime that provides relaxation and distraction from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaofang is experiencing social anxiety triggered by a fear of judgment and perception by peers, although there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaofang feels uneasy and embarrassed when interacting with boys, fearing negative judgment and scrutiny.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist sees Xiaofang's heightened self-awareness and anticipatory anxiety but notes the user's willingness to explore and analyze these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofang should spend time reflecting on social encounters and note down thoughts and feelings to identify anxiety triggers.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaofang understand social anxiety patterns and employ CBT strategies to ease discomfort by identifying and evaluating anxious thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaofang's social anxiety rooted in fear of negative judgment, particularly after interactions with a boy she likes. Agreed on a reflective task to document thoughts and feelings during such encounters to help identify triggers and patterns. Encouraged continued introspection as a step toward managing anxiety.
