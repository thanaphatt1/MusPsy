The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student feeling tense and anxious due to a heavy academic workload, particularly in math and physics. Wang is striving to manage these demands and is worried about falling behind.

[Personal Characteristics]
Wang enjoys playing the guitar, which serves as a source of relaxation and confidence.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences heightened anxiety and stress mainly due to academic pressures, with no mention of an official diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels tense and anxious with an overwhelming sense of workload pressure and insufficient sleep.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's recognition of anxiety due to academic demands and the intrusive nature of these thoughts on daily life.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to jot down thoughts and feelings whenever they become overwhelming, focusing on identifying any triggers related to math or physics.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Wang recognize and manage stress-related thoughts, while promoting self-awareness for potential cognitive restructuring.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's academic stress and anxiety related to coursework, impacting his emotional and physical well-being. Suggested keeping a written log of thoughts and feelings to identify triggers and gain insight into stress patterns, and agreed to review the findings in upcoming sessions. Emphasized focusing on progress rather than perfection for developing healthier coping strategies.
