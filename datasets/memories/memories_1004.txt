The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
L is a student experiencing considerable stress, mainly due to academic pressures and conflicts with their mother, who has high expectations. L manifests a pattern of procrastination fueled by anxiety regarding school and family interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
L finds solace in reading and drawing, which provide an escape from daily stressors and offer a momentary respite.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
L exhibits symptoms of stress and anxiety primarily linked to their academic environment and family dynamics, with no formal diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] L feels overwhelmed by school deadlines and family pressure, leading to a cycle of stress and procrastination.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes L's difficulty in managing expectations both academically and at home but notes L's willingness to address and organize these stressors actively.
[Counseling Assignments:] L is tasked with organizing their study schedule and journaling thoughts and feelings to gain clarity and control over their stressors.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to assist L in identifying stressors, enhancing task management skills, and encouraging self-recognition and stress-relief practices.
Counseling Summary:
Explored L's feelings of being overwhelmed due to school pressure and family expectations, leading to procrastination and stress. Agreed on setting a small, achievable goal of organizing a study schedule to help manage deadlines and demonstrate initiative to their mom, while also incorporating mindfulness and journaling to reduce stress and self-doubt. Encouraged self-recognition of small accomplishments to build confidence and improve mood.
