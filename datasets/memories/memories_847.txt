The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a student who is repeating a year at school and is struggling with issues related to body image, particularly self-consciousness stemming from teasing by classmates. This has affected his academic performance and general mood. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He appears to be conscientious about his academic performance and seeks ways to improve it while valuing family and supportive relationships, such as with his sister.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety and self-esteem issues related to body image and social judgments, but there is no formal diagnosis provided of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels upset and self-conscious, primarily due to teasing from classmates about his body, impacting his mood and studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's vulnerability to negative social perceptions and his willingness to work through these feelings, recognizing mealtimes as particular triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should record instances of negative self-thoughts related to these criticisms, along with rational responses that challenge them.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT to assist Wang in identifying and questioning cognitive distortions around body image, and developing healthier self-perceptions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's feelings of self-consciousness and anxiety stemming from classmates' teasing about his body, affecting his focus on studies and causing meal-related stress. Agreed to practice cognitive restructuring by noting negative thoughts and writing rational responses, and identified meal times as a challenging trigger. Encouraged the use of this constructive method to develop a more balanced perspective and acknowledged the importance of consistent, small steps towards positive change.
