The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mr. Che, a middle-aged man, was diagnosed with diabetes a few months ago, which has led to significant anxiety about potential complications, particularly the fear of amputation. He lives with a close-knit family who encourages him to maintain his health.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys gardening, which provides a sense of relaxation and connection with nature.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mr. Che is experiencing severe anxiety linked to his recent diabetes diagnosis, mainly triggered by fears of negative health outcomes. He hasn't been diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mr. Che feels overwhelmed by anxiety, particularly the possibility of severe complications from diabetes.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Mr. Che's strong focus on worst-case scenarios and his struggle to manage fear-driven thoughts but also sees his openness to trying new coping methods.
[Counseling Assignments:] Begin a daily journal to track thoughts and focus on recording instances when expectations don't result in negative outcomes.
[Session Goal:] Help Mr. Che recognize and challenge catastrophic thinking patterns using CBT to nurture more balanced thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mr. Che's anxiety about diabetes, particularly his fear of severe complications and tendency to focus on worst-case scenarios. Encouraged him to shift this perspective by recognizing past positive outcomes, the support from friends and family, and setting small, constructive health goals. Suggested Mr. Che use mindfulness and journaling to manage his anxiety and track any changes in his thoughts or emotions.
