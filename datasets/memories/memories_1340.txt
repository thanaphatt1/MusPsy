The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student experiencing social anxiety exacerbated by a nail-biting habit, leading to embarrassment and increased stress. They seek assistance in breaking this habit to decrease anxiety and enhance their social interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user faces heightened social anxiety related to nail-biting and stress, but there is no indication of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels self-conscious and embarrassed due to their nail-biting habit being noticed by classmates, increasing social anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's focus on others' perceptions and their openness to exploring habit-breaking strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should schedule drawing sessions to replace nail-biting and record recurring self-critical thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to understand and break the habit cycle, reduce anxiety, and increase engagement in calming activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's social anxiety linked to nail-biting and fear of judgment, acknowledging the resulting stress cycle. The user expressed a desire to stop the nail-biting habit, and the counselor suggested using drawing as a distraction during stressful moments. They agreed on implementing drawing and journaling recurring thoughts as strategies to manage anxiety and break the habit.
