The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a recent university student experiencing difficulty adjusting to dorm life, particularly struggling to get along with roommates due to differing habits. This transition has been quite stressful for Yang, creating a significant amount of anxiety.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang enjoys jogging, which clears his mind and offers peaceful time, and has considered returning to drawing, an activity previously found soothing.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang is dealing with anxiety largely triggered by the transition to university life and interpersonal conflicts with roommates, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels overwhelmed by the new university environment and interpersonal conflicts with roommates, leading to a sense of anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yang's anxiety stemming from environmental changes and differing expectations, as well as a willingness to explore strategies for improvement.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is encouraged to note specific situations that exacerbate his discomfort and stress levels throughout the week for detailed discussion in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Yang identify stress-inducing scenarios, challenge negative thoughts, and develop healthier coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yang's difficulty adjusting to university life, focusing on roommate conflicts driven by differing habits and resultant stress and anxiety. Yang was guided to identify and challenge negative thought patterns and was encouraged to communicate directly with roommates to address issues, while incorporating mindfulness and regular physical activity for stress management. Through the sessions, Yang developed skills and strategies to approach conflicts and manage overwhelming feelings more effectively.
