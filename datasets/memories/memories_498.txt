The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li Ming is a young professional experiencing significant stress due to a recent project failure at work, leading to self-doubt and feelings of inadequacy. Li Ming values career achievement deeply and is concerned about future job performance and social isolation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys jogging, which he uses intermittently as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li Ming experiences anxiety triggered by work-related performance issues; while there is no formal diagnosis, his self-critical thoughts indicate a stress reaction affecting his social engagement and confidence.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li Ming feels anxious and isolated following the project's failure, leading to doubts about his capabilities and avoidance of social interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li Ming's struggle with self-doubt and hesitation in socializing, while also noting his willingness to explore how these thoughts connect to his emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li Ming is encouraged to engage in jogging regularly and to challenge negative thoughts through reflection and possible conversations with colleagues for reassurance.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Li Ming identify and challenge self-doubt thoughts and encourage active engagement in stress-relieving activities like jogging.
Counseling Summary:
Li Ming expressed feelings of self-doubt and social withdrawal following a project failure at work, exacerbated by assumptions of colleagues' judgments. The consultant suggested having a constructive conversation with a manager for reassurance and feedback, engaging in regular jogging for stress relief, and setting boundaries between work and personal life. Li Ming plans to implement these strategies to improve self-confidence and overall well-being before the next session.
