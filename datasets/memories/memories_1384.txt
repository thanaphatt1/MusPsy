The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student experiencing stress due to dropping test scores and criticism from both their teacher and parents. They feel discouraged transitioning from primary to middle school and struggle to comprehend lessons.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing and sketching, which helps them relax and find calm.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user is stressed and lacks motivation, primarily due to academic challenges, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels stressed and unmotivated, doubting their ability to keep up academically.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes the user's discouragement but also their previous enjoyment of science and willingness to find motivation.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should spend 15 minutes daily on a subject they enjoy, like science, and start a journal to track negative thoughts and their evidence.
[Session Goal:] Help the user regain motivation by engaging in enjoyable academic activities and identifying negative thoughts through CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's academic stress and negative self-perceptions following the transition to middle school, identifying the impact on motivation and stress. Developed strategies including engaging with a favorite subject, journaling negative thoughts, and communicating struggles with a teacher. Encouraged stress-relief through drawing, resulting in improved confidence to ask for help and balancing school pressures with hobbies.
