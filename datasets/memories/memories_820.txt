The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]
Liu enjoys reading novels as a means to relax and escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels lost and overwhelmed due to the pressure of preparing for exams and familial expectations, impacting their self-esteem and motivation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Liu's feelings of inadequacy and comparison with others, but also Liu's openness to addressing and exploring anxiety triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu is encouraged to document moments of anxiety related to exams, identifying triggers and emotional responses for further understanding.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT techniques to help Liu identify anxiety triggers and manage stress by tracking anxious moments and responses.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's feelings of inadequacy and anxiety stemming from pressure and comparisons with others, particularly their brother. Developed strategies to manage negative thought patterns, encouraging self-compassion, focusing on personal achievements, and incorporating stress-relieving activities like reading. The user reported increased awareness of their thought processes and expressed commitment to applying learned techniques to future challenges.
