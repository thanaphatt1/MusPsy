The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student who recently experienced a breakup, which has intensified feelings of insecurity and self-doubt. Liu has expressed concern about personal worth and ability to maintain relationships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu is interested in puzzles and reading, which have been activities of enjoyment and potential stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu exhibits signs of anxiety and low self-esteem, particularly centered around relationship failures, but has yet to receive a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels not good enough and attributes personal failure to the breakup, leading to insecurity and sadness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist noticed Liu's propensity for overthinking and withdrawal, coupled with a recognition of unrealistic thinking patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu is encouraged to journal any emerging negative thoughts and to explore their accuracy and impact gently.
[Session Goal:] Develop strategies for Liu to identify and challenge negative thought patterns and to use distraction techniques to prevent overthinking.
Counseling Summary:
Liu shared negative self-perceptions stemming from a recent breakup, which have intensified feelings of insecurity and self-doubt. They agreed to identify triggers of these thoughts and use journaling as a strategy to process them without judgment. Liu committed to challenging these thoughts by actively recognizing them and engaging in comforting activities during vulnerable moments.
