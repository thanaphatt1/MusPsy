The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He also enjoys painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels apprehensive about being judged by new classmates due to their past, leading to assumptions of non-acceptance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's capacity for acknowledging resilience and determination in overcoming past challenges, particularly their health issues.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to reflect on personal strengths and achievements, creating daily reminders to shift focus onto positive attributes.
[Session Goal:] Assist the user in changing negative narratives by focusing on strengths and fostering confidence in social interactions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's tendency to dwell on past experiences, contributing to feelings of shame and anxiety around new classmates. Encouraged the user to focus on personal strengths and achievements, such as returning to school after health issues, to boost confidence and challenge assumptions about judgment from peers. Suggested practical strategies like daily affirmations and joining campus activities, such as a music club, to facilitate more positive interactions and deepen connections.
