The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Cai is a high school student who has been experiencing emotional difficulties, particularly involving interpersonal conflicts and academic stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Cai enjoys drawing and playing video games as a means of relaxation and self-expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Cai has demonstrated impulsive behavior and emotional challenges, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Cai feels upset and guilty after an argument and physical fight with a classmate, realizing difficulties in controlling emotions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiao Cai struggling with impulsive emotional reactions but notes Xiao Cai’s willingness to explore ways to manage these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Cai is tasked with jotting down feelings and triggers leading to impulsive actions and trying deep breathing exercises.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiao Cai identify triggers, manage impulsive responses, and integrate calming techniques like deep breathing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Cai's impulse control issues and resulting emotions following a conflict with a classmate. Agreed on strategies for reflection, such as noting down feelings before impulsive actions and practicing deep breathing during moments of anger to manage emotions better. Emphasized taking gradual steps toward building new habits and looking forward to discussing progress in the next session.
