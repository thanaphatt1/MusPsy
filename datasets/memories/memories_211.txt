The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liang is a high school student aiming for admission to a prestigious university like Tsinghua or Peking University. Recently, Liang has been feeling overwhelmed and discouraged due to lower-than-expected exam scores, which has led to self-doubt regarding future aspirations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liang enjoys playing chess, which serves as a mental distraction from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liang experiences significant stress and anxiety triggered by academic performance pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liang feels overwhelmed and trapped in a bubble of stress and disappointment due to self-imposed high expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liang's tendency to set exceedingly high standards for themselves and the resultant isolation, but also notes willingness to explore the underlying thoughts and feelings contributing to this stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liang should write down recurring thoughts that appear during study sessions to reflect on them before the next consultation.
[Session Goal:] Assist Liang in identifying and understanding the negative thought patterns that exacerbate stress, using CBT techniques to explore alternative thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liang's experiences of academic pressure and negative thinking patterns due to high expectations. Encouraged Liang to challenge automatic negative thoughts by adopting a more compassionate perspective and incorporating enjoyable activities, like chess, to manage stress. Suggested open communication with supportive figures and emphasized balancing study with small breaks to improve mood and motivation.
