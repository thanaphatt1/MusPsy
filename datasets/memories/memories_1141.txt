The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Song is a university student dealing with significant anxiety, especially related to interacting with male peers, which is causing overwhelming stress. Song finds the university environment challenging and has considered dropping out due to these pressures.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Song enjoys reading and has shown interest in joining book clubs or study groups related to this activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Song experiences anxiety, particularly in social situations involving unfamiliar individuals. There is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Song feels anxious and overwhelmed by the university environment and the pressure of social interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Song's high anxiety levels related to social interactions and notes Song's willingness to explore strategies for managing these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Song should document situations that trigger anxiety and the feelings associated with them for further analysis.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Song identify specific anxiety-provoking thoughts and encourage reflection to manage social anxiety effectively.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Song's anxiety around interacting with guys in the university setting and its emotional impact on daily life. Song will note specific anxiety-inducing situations and associated feelings to gain insights into underlying thoughts. Agreed on preparing conversation starters as a small task to reduce anxiety in social interactions.
