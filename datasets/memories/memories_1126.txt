The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student experiencing challenges with self-doubt and social interactions, specifically struggling with self-expression in academic settings and among unfamiliar people. They have supportive friends who encourage them, and they have begun recognizing the value of their contributions in past experiences.

[Personal Characteristics]
The user values supportive friendships and has expressed an interest in literature and film, particularly those with themes of growth and resilience, which they find inspiring and relatable.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user is dealing with self-doubt and anxiety related to social interactions and public speaking, with no formal diagnosis of a mental disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels down and struggles with self-doubt, finding it hard to speak up in front of others, especially strangers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's struggle with negative self-perception but notes a willingness to explore strategies to counterbalance this.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should note situations that trigger self-doubt, focusing on thoughts and feelings during these moments, to recognize any patterns.
[Session Goal:] Identify and challenge negative thoughts surrounding social interactions using CBT techniques to build confidence over time.
Counseling Summary:
Explored User's challenges with self-doubt and social anxiety, particularly in unfamiliar social settings, affecting class participation and interaction. Agreed on tasks to identify triggers and note positive experiences to counterbalance negative thoughts, encouraging activation of support from friends. User feels slightly nervous but optimistic about structured steps towards managing anxiety and self-doubt.
