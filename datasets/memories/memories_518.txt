The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyun is a high school student feeling significant pressure due to an impending entrance exam while studying in a boarding school away from family. Xiaoyun values family connections deeply and is concerned about maintaining academic performance to avoid disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]
She enjoys drawing, which used to serve as a relaxing activity but was temporarily neglected due to a demanding study schedule.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyun experiences anxiety and stress triggered by academic pressures and homesickness, although there is no indication of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyun feels anxious and stressed about the upcoming high school entrance exam and the pressure of academic demands.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoyun’s anxiety primarily arises from the perception of never having enough study time and missing family while in boarding school, yet sees her willingness to explore stress management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyun should journal her feelings each evening to identify specific stress triggers and consider reintroducing drawing for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Xiaoyun recognize stress triggers, engage in relaxing activities, and enhance coping through positive journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoyun's stress related to high school entrance exam pressures, exacerbated by study demands and homesickness. Agreed on implementing evening journaling and identifying triggers, alongside rediscovering drawing as a relaxing hobby, to help manage stress levels. Emphasized communication with family and acknowledging positive moments to balance feelings.
