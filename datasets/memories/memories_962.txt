The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a student who recently felt upset and overlooked due to a missed school parent meeting that her mom couldn't attend because of work. She values being able to share her achievements with her mother.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys drawing, which helps her manage emotions and serves as a calming activity amidst stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences feelings of neglect or insignificance during school events related to family participation but does not have a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels neglected and insignificant, particularly after her mother's absence at a school meeting.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li's sensitivity to family involvement and identifies her openness to finding ways to manage these feelings through creative outlets like drawing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to dedicate daily time to drawing and consider initiating a conversation with her mom about her feelings.
[Session Goal:] Help Li process feelings of neglect by using CBT strategies to embrace creative outlets and prepare to express her emotions to her mom.
Counseling Summary:
Li expressed feelings of sadness due to her mother's absence at a school event, leading to her feeling overlooked and questioning her importance in her mother's life. Together, we explored how these 'gray' feelings manifest and decided on daily drawing as a creative outlet to manage them. We also planned a thoughtful approach for Li to communicate her feelings to her mother, including practicing the conversation, reflecting on potential outcomes, and reminding herself of her mother's love and commitment.
