The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
XiaoQ is a senior year student, struggling with stress due to the competitive environment in their dorm where roommates study extensively. XiaoQ is dedicated and has excelled academically but is concerned about keeping up with peers. They have a desire to make their family proud and aim to excel in their future career.

[Personal Characteristics]  
XiaoQ enjoys playing basketball and reading, especially fiction, and finds these activities relaxing. They have supportive friends and value family encouragement.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
XiaoQ experiences stress and anxiety, primarily from comparing themselves to peers. No formal diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] XiaoQ feels stressed and anxious about not studying as much as their roommates, despite past academic success.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that XiaoQ is overwhelmed by peer comparisons but shows openness to exploring self-awareness and stress management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] XiaoQ should try reading fiction before bed as a relaxation technique and begin meditating in the mornings to start the day positively.
[Session Goal:] Encourage XiaoQ to incorporate relaxation activities into their routine while building awareness of their accomplishments and reducing pressure from peer comparisons.
Counseling Summary:
Explored XiaoQ's stress related to academic pressures and comparison with roommates. Suggested incorporating reading and meditation into daily routines to alleviate nighttime anxiety and daytime stress, while encouraging reflection on past achievements to reinforce self-worth. Established small goals with personal rewards to manage stress more effectively and promote balance.
