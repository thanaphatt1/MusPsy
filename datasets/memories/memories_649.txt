The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaozeng is a student experiencing significant stress, primarily from the high academic expectations set by his strict father and well-meaning yet non-guiding mother. He struggles to balance pleasing his family and managing his stress.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaozeng enjoys drawing, which he finds to be a valuable stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaozeng experiences anxiety linked to academic pressures, though no formal mental health disorder diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaozeng feels overwhelmed, particularly by the pressure of meeting his parents' high expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaozeng's attempts to manage internalized pressure and encourages identifying thought patterns leading to stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaozeng should devote an hour each weekend to drawing and start a thought journal to track recurring stressors.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaozeng identify stress-inducing thoughts and promote the use of drawing as an outlet to alleviate pressure.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaozeng's overwhelming academic pressures and parental expectations contributing to school avoidance and anxiety, particularly in math class. Encouraged perspective shift towards progress over perfection, and proposed two tasks: initiating a conversation with his dad to foster understanding, and dedicating time to drawing for stress relief; suggested journaling for emotional clarity.
