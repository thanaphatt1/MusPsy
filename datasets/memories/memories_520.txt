The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student who recently faced disappointment after not achieving the class monitor position, leading to awkward interactions with the current monitor. Li is navigating feelings of embarrassment and self-disappointment while aiming to contribute positively to the class.

[Personal Characteristics]
Li enjoys drawing, which serves as a method of relaxation and emotional expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences feelings of disappointment and perceived social tension but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels down and embarrassed after not being chosen as the class monitor, hindering class participation and motivation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist sees Li's difficulty in dealing with feelings of self-disappointment and senses an underlying willingness to understand and manage these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should write daily about negative and positive thoughts to gain clarity about current emotions and begin structuring a conversation with the class monitor.
[Session Goal:] Integrate CBT techniques to help Li identify and challenge negative thoughts and encourage positive communication with peers.
Counseling Summary:
Li expressed feelings of disappointment and awkwardness related to not becoming class monitor, affecting motivation and class participation. The consultant suggested Li document thoughts and emotions, including positive aspects, to identify patterns and trigger points, as well as consider writing a letter to address misunderstandings directly with the class monitor. This process aims to cultivate clarity and balance, with support provided throughout to ease emotional discomfort.
