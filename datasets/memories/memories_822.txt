The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ahong is a student experiencing heightened anxiety, particularly related to exam preparation and social interactions, leading to increased social anxiety, such as avoiding eye contact.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ahong has enjoyed reading and spending time with friends, though these activities have decreased due to current anxiety levels.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ahong demonstrates symptoms of social anxiety and stress related to academic pressure, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ahong feels anxious and stressed, primarily avoiding social interactions and finding it challenging to maintain eye contact due to fear of judgment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Ahong's willingness to explore thoughts and anxiety triggers, recognizing the isolating impact of social avoidance.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ahong is tasked with noting thoughts that arise during anxious moments about eye contact to better understand underlying fears.
[Session Goal:] Explore Ahong's anxiety related to social interactions and develop initial strategies to understand and manage these feelings.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ahong's social anxiety related to eye contact and avoidance of social situations, leading to stress and isolation. Ahong agreed to track thoughts associated with this anxiety and was encouraged to dedicate time to relaxing activities, like reading, to help manage stress and reduce exam-related anxiety. The consultant highlighted the importance of balancing study with relaxation and gradual progress toward understanding and managing anxiety.
