The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Z is a high school student dealing with increased isolation and anxiety due to a worsening stutter since moving into a new class. Z is concerned about how classmates perceive them and fears being excluded.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Z enjoys writing stories, which provides an expressive outlet free from the constraints of speech difficulties.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Z experiences anxiety primarily related to social situations at school, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Z feels anxious and ashamed about their stutter, leading to isolation and fear of judgment from peers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Z's anxiety is reinforced by self-critical thoughts, yet Z demonstrates a willingness to explore more positive interactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Z should jot down recurring negative thoughts about social interactions and also identify potential classmates who seem approachable.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Z manage social anxiety by challenging negative assumptions and encouraging gentle social engagement.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Z's social anxiety, particularly their fear of judgment due to stuttering, and its effect on their school interactions and emotional state. Agreed on trying to initiate structured social interactions during lunch or study breaks as a manageable goal, alongside journaling to reflect on thoughts and perceptions. Encouraged Z to challenge negative assumptions through proactive social experiences to reduce isolation and increase confidence.
