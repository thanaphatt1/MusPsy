The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaohong is a student who experiences feelings of loneliness and isolation at school, as they haven't yet found friends they can trust. Xiaohong expresses a desire for companionship and to partake in school activities that bring joy.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaohong enjoys drawing, particularly during art class, which provides a sense of calmness and serves as a refuge from feelings of loneliness.  

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaohong has not been diagnosed with any formal mental health disorder but expresses sadness and anxiety primarily due to social isolation at school.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaohong feels lonely and desires to connect with others at school, particularly during breaks.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Xiaohong's sincere wish to engage with peers and notices their potential to find solace in art as a bridge for connection.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaohong is encouraged to reflect on and identify any positive aspects of school life and consider using breaks to engage in art activities or share drawings.
[Session Goal:] Assist Xiaohong in exploring ways to connect with classmates through shared interests like drawing and use art as a tool to express themselves and reduce loneliness.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaohong's feelings of loneliness at school and identified art class as a therapeutic outlet. Encouraged Xiaohong to use art as a bridge for connection by considering sharing drawings with classmates, and discussed trying a low-pressure approach to start conversations. Agreed on reflecting on these interactions and emotions until the next session to better understand and manage feelings of loneliness.
