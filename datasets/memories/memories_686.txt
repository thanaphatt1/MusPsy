The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Z is dealing with feelings of anxiety and low mood, largely due to being out of school and lacking clear goals. He reports tension in his relationship with his father compounding his emotional distress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Z enjoys playing basketball and has shown a willingness to attempt activities that might lift his mood. He values family connections, having supportive moments with his brother.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Z experiences significant anxiety and depressive symptoms, mainly attributed to familial tensions and lack of structure in his daily life.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Z feels low and anxious, perceiving himself as stuck without direction.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist sees underlying feelings of lost direction and isolation, but also notes Z's openness to exploring strategies for clarity.
[Counseling Assignments:] Z is encouraged to jot down thoughts and feelings as they arise and revisit enjoyable activities like walking or reading.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Z gain insight into his thoughts, manage feelings of directionlessness, and engage in mood-lifting activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Z's feelings of isolation and restlessness due to school absence and family tension, particularly with their father. Agreed on the small task of jotting down thoughts and trying a short walk for reflection and clarity. Continued to encourage finding positive, refreshing activities to manage anxiety and promote well-being.
