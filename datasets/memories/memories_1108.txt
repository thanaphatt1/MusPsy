The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaosong is a middle school student currently on a break and staying at their grandparents' house. Feelings of being overwhelmed began in middle school, leading to a break from regular activities, with a sense of being stuck and demotivated.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaosong used to enjoy playing chess with their grandfather, though motivation has waned recently. They find their grandmother a supportive figure and are comfortable sharing with her.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaosong exhibits signs of depression, including a lack of motivation and negative thinking, but has not been formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaosong feels down, unmotivated, and trapped in their current state, struggling with a foggy mindset.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaosong's openness to discussing feelings despite the heaviness they carry and identifies a willingness to track emotional changes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaosong is encouraged to keep a daily journal to record feelings and important moments to identify patterns and changes.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Xiaosong to CBT strategies to help track thought patterns and alleviate feelings of being stuck.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling sessions focused on Xiaosong's struggles with depression and negative thinking patterns. Through journaling, Xiaosong identified recurring thoughts of failure and disappointment, and with the counselor's guidance, planned to engage more with supportive family members and revisit enjoyable activities like playing chess with their grandfather. Xiaosong expressed increased self-awareness and a commitment to utilize supportive relationships and hobbies as strategies to manage future difficulties.
