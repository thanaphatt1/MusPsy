The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
E is a university student feeling overwhelmed with social interactions and attempting to make new friends in their department and clubs. They experience anxiety and the pressure to fit in, often feeling left out due to self-imposed perceptions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
E has interests in reading and drawing, activities they find relaxing but have recently neglected due to social and academic pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
E experiences social anxiety aggravated by negative self-assumptions about fitting in and forming connections. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] E feels anxious and somewhat isolated, struggling with joining conversations and fearing they appear disinterested despite a strong desire to connect.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives E's challenges with social engagement stemming from negative assumptions about themselves in social settings but notices E's openness to exploring and understanding these patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] E should observe their thoughts and feelings during social interactions and take note of both positive and challenging moments.
[Session Goal:] Assist E in recognizing patterns in social interactions and begin addressing assumptions that contribute to anxiety, using CBT strategies for reframing thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored E's difficulty in forming new social connections, balancing a desire to connect with a fear of judgment. Discussed becoming more aware of thoughts and feelings during interactions, and recognizing patterns or assumptions that may not be true. Agreed on observing both positive and negative social interactions as a step towards more engaging social experiences, with plans to discuss further in future sessions.
