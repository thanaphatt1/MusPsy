The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is grappling with significant academic decisions and feels torn between practical considerations and personal passion. They receive mixed messages from family and friends regarding these choices, which leads to confusion.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys reading fantasy novels as a form of relaxation and can recall past strategies for dealing with decision-making dilemmas.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
No specific mental health diagnosis is evident, though the user experiences anxiety and stress around academic decision-making.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed by conflicting priorities between practicality and passion in their academic decisions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's openness to exploring values and the past success of experiential decision-making strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is tasked with reflecting on their educational and career values and considering small-scale experiments with their academic options.
[Session Goal:] Help the user recognize the balance between personal fulfillment and practicality using CBT strategies, facilitating exploration of academic options.
Counseling Summary:
The dialogue focused on the user's struggle between practical academic decisions and personal passions, causing confusion and fear of making the wrong choices. The consultant encouraged the user to reflect on their values, consider experimenting with options, and gather more information to make the decision less overwhelming. Both agreed on using journaling as a method to organize thoughts and aid in decision-making, with continued support promised for the user's exploration process.
