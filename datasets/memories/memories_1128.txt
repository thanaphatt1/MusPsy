The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a college student experiencing significant stress, particularly regarding upcoming exams. They feel overwhelmed by the fear of underperformance and the implications it may have on their academic future.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys listening to classical and jazz music, which serves as a source of relaxation. They have a history of utilizing walks as a means to alleviate stress during high-pressure times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety primarily triggered by academic pressures, with identified patterns of extreme thinking but no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious and unable to focus due to stress about exams and potential failure.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's openness to understanding their anxiety and identifying underlying thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is tasked with noting situations where stress appears and the corresponding thoughts, alongside integrating short music sessions as calming breaks during study.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help the user recognize the connection between thoughts and feelings, and encourage practical relaxation techniques like music to ease stress.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's study-related anxiety and identified distorted thoughts contributing to stress, agreeing to work on recognizing and challenging these thoughts. Introduced relaxation techniques like music and walks to help manage anxiety, with the user feeling confident about incorporating these strategies into their routine. Encouraged consistency with the new routine and aimed to observe its impact on mood and productivity.
