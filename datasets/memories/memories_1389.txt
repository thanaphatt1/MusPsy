The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhu is facing significant anxiety in their English class after a negative experience with a teacher's criticism, which has led to a fear of embarrassment. This fear persists and affects their willingness to participate. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhu enjoys drawing as a form of creative expression and relaxation, and finds reading, particularly mystery novels, as a source of enjoyment and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhu experiences anxiety primarily triggered by academic pressure and fear of embarrassment, with no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhu feels scared and stressed about attending English class due to fear of embarrassment and criticism.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhu's significant anxiety towards making mistakes and facing judgment in class, yet observes Zhu's willingness to track these feelings and thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhu is encouraged to track feelings of fear or stress that arise in similar situations to discuss in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Zhu identify anxiety triggers, actively track negative thoughts, and initiate stress-reducing activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling sessions focused on addressing Zhu's anxiety and fear of judgment in her English class after a negative experience with her teacher. The consultant encouraged Zhu to challenge negative thoughts, practice mindfulness, and engage in activities she enjoys, like drawing, to manage stress. Zhu reflected on these strategies, affirmed her progress, and planned to use these insights to navigate future challenges.
