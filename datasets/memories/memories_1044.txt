The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Rou is a student who has been feeling significant academic pressure, particularly from expectations set by their parents. This pressure has led Rou to feel unworthy and incapable when failing to meet these expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Rou enjoys reading and walking in the park, which serve as stress-relief activities. Rou's friends regard them as a good listener and often seek their advice.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Rou experiences stress and anxiety primarily triggered by academic performance and parental expectations. There is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Rou feels stressed and anxious, overwhelmed by negative thoughts about self-worth due to academic pressures.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Rou's struggle with self-doubt and recognizes Rou's openness to exploring ways to manage these negative thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Rou is encouraged to record positive affirmations and achievements in a journal to balance negative thoughts and enhance self-awareness.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Rou challenge negative thoughts, nurture self-compassion, and strengthen positive self-perceptions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Rou's academic-related self-worth issues and the pressure from parental expectations, which contributed to anxiety and avoidance behaviors. Highlighted the importance of recognizing both achievements and positive affirmations from friends to balance self-perception. Agreed on maintaining a journal of accomplishments and affirmations to foster self-appreciation and resilience.
