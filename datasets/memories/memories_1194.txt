The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student experiencing difficulty adjusting to dorm life and tension in relationships with their mother and roommates. The user feels overwhelmed by transitioning to independent living and fluctuating expectations from loved ones.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user has a deep interest in reading novels and finds it to be a stress-relieving activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user is experiencing anxiety due to interpersonal conflicts and self-imposed pressure but does not have a formal diagnosis of any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious and burdened by relational disputes with their mother and roommates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's tendency towards self-blame and difficulty in maintaining relationships but notes their openness to exploring communication strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should attempt a conversation with their roommates to express concerns and find common ground and set aside dedicated time for reading as a relaxation method.
[Session Goal:] Help the user navigate relationship tensions through improved communication and encourage engagement in stress-relieving activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
The counselor helped the user address conflicts with roommates and family, encouraging them to communicate openly to alleviate tension. The user plans to initiate a respectful conversation with roommates and introduce regular reading time into their routine for stress relief. Both approaches aim to enhance the user’s sense of stability and well-being while navigating social interactions.
