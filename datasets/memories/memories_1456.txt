The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Little Orange is a young child who has recently been feeling sad and ignored after the arrival of a baby sister. They feel less important due to the shift in family attention.

[Personal Characteristics]
Little Orange enjoys drawing, which acts as a therapeutic release for emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Little Orange exhibits feelings of sadness and reduced interest in school activities, potentially stemming from perceived neglect and family changes. No formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Little Orange feels sad and ignored since their baby sister came home, leading to a sense of being less important.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Little Orange's struggle with feeling less valued but also recognizes their openness to trying activities that could improve their mood.
[Counseling Assignments:] Little Orange is encouraged to re-engage with enjoyable activities from before the sister's arrival, like playing board games, as a way to improve mood and record thoughts detailing times they feel ignored.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Little Orange identify and challenge negative thoughts about their importance and foster engagement in activities that previously brought joy.
Counseling Summary:
Little Orange expressed feelings of sadness and neglect due to the focus on a new sibling, impacting interactions and interest in activities. The session focused on identifying and challenging negative assumptions about self-worth, encouraging emotional awareness through writing triggers and listing positive qualities to enhance self-esteem. It concluded with collaborative strategies to track progress and reinforce the idea of intrinsic worth beyond immediate attention.
