The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Alex is a sophomore college student experiencing significant stress related to academic pressure, particularly midterms. This pressure affects Alex's eating habits negatively as well. Alex is worried about not meeting family expectations and has been feeling overwhelmed by the increasing intensity of coursework.

[Personal Characteristics]
Alex used to enjoy sketching, which is now being considered as a stress-relieving activity to help balance the academic pressure.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Alex is experiencing stress and anxiety linked to academic performance and family expectations, but there has been no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Alex feels overwhelmed due to the approaching midterms, leading to negative changes in eating habits and constant fatigue.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Alex's awareness of the stress cycle affecting eating habits and recognizes Alex's openness to establishing better coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Alex should maintain a diary of eating habits and stress levels as well as note recurring negative thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Alex recognize stress-induced thought patterns and promote healthier eating habits.
Counseling Summary:
The session focused on the user's stress from intense coursework affecting their eating habits and leading to anxious thoughts about academic performance. The consultant suggested keeping a diary to track eating patterns and stress levels while encouraging the user to note and challenge negative thoughts. The user agreed to try these steps to help manage their anxiety and stress more effectively.
