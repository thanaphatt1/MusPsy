The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lan is a university student struggling with social anxiety and the challenges of adapting to university life. She feels overwhelmed, particularly in social settings, and desires to make friends but often finds herself unable to start conversations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lan enjoys reading novels as a form of escape and relaxation, which aids in calming her mind amidst stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lan experiences social anxiety primarily when engaging with classmates and perceives a negative judgment from others, although there isn’t a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lan feels overwhelmed and anxious about adapting to university life, particularly in social contexts.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Lan's difficulty initiating conversations and a tendency toward self-criticism following social interactions, along with an openness to understanding her feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lan should jot down her feelings before and after social interactions to identify patterns and note positive aspects of these exchanges.
[Session Goal:] Begin utilizing CBT strategies to help Lan understand and manage social anxiety by focusing on initiating and reflecting on social interactions.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Lan's difficulty with initiating social interactions at university due to anxiety and fear of self-criticism. Agreed on maintaining a journal to track and reflect on emotions before and after social engagements, focusing on positive aspects to boost confidence. Encouraged frequent practice of conversing on familiar topics to ease anxiety and build a more positive self-dialogue.
