The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Y is a student who feels lonely and stressed due to a past incident in seventh grade that affected their relationships with classmates. Their concern about being judged persists, impacting daily interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Y enjoys reading books, finding solace and relaxation in immersing themselves in stories.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Y experiences social anxiety and isolation, characterized by hyper-awareness and avoidance in group settings, without a formal diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Y feels anxious and lonely, fearing judgment from classmates and recalling past social tensions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Y's tendency to internalize past negative experiences, and recognizes Y's willingness to reframe anxious thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Y should try to read regularly each evening to relax and begin challenging automatic thoughts of being judged.
[Session Goal:] Help Y manage social anxiety by reframing perceptions of judgment and incorporating enjoyable activities like reading to foster relaxation.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Y's lingering anxiety from a past incident that affects relationships and perceptions of judgment by peers; discussed strategies for reframing thoughts and acknowledged the potential for misinterpretation. Agreed to practice thought reframing and integrate enjoyable activities, like reading, to support well-being and encourage gradual steps toward overcoming social anxiety.
