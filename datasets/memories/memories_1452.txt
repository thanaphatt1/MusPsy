The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background

[Personal Profile]  
Xiaomi is a student navigating through stress, mainly related to academic performance and familial expectations, following an argument with their mother about returning to their hometown for studies.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaomi enjoys drawing, which serves as a stress-relief activity, and occasionally takes walks and listens to music to unwind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaomi experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures and familial expectations, without any formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaomi feels tense and shaky due to dropping grades and concerns about disappointing parents.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes Xiaomi's struggle with academic pressure and familial expectations, but also sees openness to recognizing stress patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaomi should keep track of thoughts and emotions, especially those contributing to stress, until the next session.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiaomi identify stress-inducing thought patterns and increase self-awareness regarding anxiety triggers.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaomi struggled with stress linked to academic pressure and assumptions about her mother's reactions, leading to anxiety and decreased focus on schoolwork. Through counseling, she learned to identify and challenge negative thinking patterns and communicated her worries to her mother, resulting in positive interactions and relief. Incorporating mindful activities like drawing into her routine, Xiaomi gained self-awareness and strategies for managing stress, feeling more equipped to handle future challenges independently.
