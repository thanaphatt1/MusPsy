The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mou is a university student who has been feeling significantly stressed, particularly with the pressure of upcoming exams and managing studies. Mou has expressed difficulties in concentrating and worries about academic performance, leading to anxiety and withdrawing from social interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Mou enjoys drawing and sketching, activities that have been a source of relaxation in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mou experiences anxiety and stress primarily due to academic pressures without an indication of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mou feels stressed and overwhelmed by the demands of studying and exams, struggling with concentration and tiredness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Mou's high levels of stress related to self-expectations and academic pressure, along with an openness to explore stress management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Mou should allocate time for short walks or listening to music each day and start journaling stress-related thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to assist Mou in identifying stress triggers and encourage regular breaks and enjoyable activities to balance academic demands.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mou's academic stress and identified its impact on concentration, emotions, and social interactions. Encouraged integrating daily relaxation activities, breaking study sessions into smaller tasks, and engaging in supportive conversations to manage stress. Agreed on maintaining a thought journal to observe self-talk and gain new insights before the next session.
