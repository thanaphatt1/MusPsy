The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mrs. Zhang is a mother currently experiencing stress due to losing custody of her children and the pressure to regain custody through therapy. She lost her job last year and is struggling with financial instability, relying on social welfare for support.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys cooking and has shown interest in gardening and playing the guitar, indicating a potential for therapeutic activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mrs. Zhang is experiencing high levels of anxiety and feelings of overwhelm, particularly related to her thoughts of being separated from her children and financial stress. No formal mental health disorder diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mrs. Zhang feels overwhelmed and stressed due to the pressure of regaining custody of her children and financial instability.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Mrs. Zhang's anxiety and the weight of her responsibilities, highlighting the need for support and understanding.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mrs. Zhang is encouraged to keep a daily journal of her emotions and identify triggers, as well as practice taking daily walks to manage stress.
[Session Goal:] Help Mrs. Zhang manage her anxiety through CBT by recognizing and documenting stress triggers and establishing a daily physical routine.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mrs. Zhang's feelings of overwhelm and anxiety stemming from unemployment and custody challenges, recognizing emotional triggers like missing her children and financial stress. Encouraged her to keep a daily journal to track emotions and advised her to consider joining a support group while setting a personal goal of daily walks to manage stress. Emphasized the importance of small, positive steps to improve her emotional well-being and the potential benefits of seeking a support network.
