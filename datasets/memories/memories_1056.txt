The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a student facing challenges balancing academic responsibilities and new habits developed from playing online games introduced by classmates during the National Day holiday. Zhao values academic success and is concerned about recent grade drops and parental reactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
In addition to gaming, Zhao fondly remembers enjoying activities like soccer and drawing, which serve as potential stress-relief outlets.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao is experiencing stress and anxiety due to school performance and family expectations, with no formal diagnosis provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels overwhelmed by gaming affecting his studies and fears disappointing his parents.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes Zhao's conflict between the enjoyment of gaming and the anxiety about academic performance but notes an openness to exploring solutions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao is tasked with journaling gaming habits and emotions to understand their impact on study patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Zhao identify the effects of gaming on stress and introduce constructive habits to improve academic focus and emotional balance.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhao's struggle with online gaming impacting his studies and family relationships, and addressed feelings of disappointment and frustration. Implemented journaling to track gaming habits and fostered open communication with parents, alongside reintroducing soccer as a positive outlet for stress relief. Encouraged Zhao to confront negative assumptions, embrace supportive strategies, and recognize the importance of discussing concerns openly.
