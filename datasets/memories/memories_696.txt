The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student who has recently faced anxiety during a club promotion event at school, particularly concerned about public scrutiny and making mistakes.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing the guitar, an activity that provides relaxation and focus.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang shows signs of anxiety, particularly in social settings where he feels observed or judged. There is no formal diagnosis mentioned in the consultations.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels embarrassed and frustrated with himself due to the anxiety experienced during public speaking.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wang's tendency to overthink and assume others are critical of him but observes his willingness to identify and explore anxiety triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to jot down thoughts and feelings during anxious moments to identify patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Assist Wang in recognizing and challenging unrealistic thoughts and managing anxious reactions using CBT techniques.
Counseling Summary:
Wang struggles with anxiety, particularly related to public speaking, where he fears judgment and making mistakes. The consultant helped Wang identify cognitive patterns of overthinking and unwarranted assumptions that worsen his anxiety and suggested grounding exercises like deep breathing and challenging negative thoughts as proactive strategies. Wang agreed to track his experiences and practice these techniques to better manage his anxiety and build confidence in social situations.
