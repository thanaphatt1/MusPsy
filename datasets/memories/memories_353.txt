The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is an adult dealing with the recent death of their father, which has led to a tumultuous array of emotions, including sadness and guilt for feeling a sense of relief due to the struggle their father faced with illness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei finds cycling and painting as activities that offer relaxation and a mental escape from emotional stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei is experiencing a mixture of grief, guilt, and mild signs of anxiety, yet there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels overwhelmed by grief and guilt, navigating the loss of their father while searching for personal understanding.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wei's struggle to balance emotional relief with guilt but notes Wei's openness toward expressive outlets like journaling and painting.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei should start journaling recurring thoughts and consider creating a photo album as a tribute to their father.
[Session Goal:] Aid Wei in processing their grief through expression and tributes, encouraging self-exploration of complex emotions via CBT.
Counseling Summary:
Wei's counseling session focused on processing emotions following his father's passing, including feelings of sadness, freedom, and guilt. He was encouraged to journal about recurring feelings and consider a photo project or painting as creative outlets to honor his father's memory and find comfort. The consultant emphasized sharing burdens with family and exploring positive activities like painting to help cope with grief.
