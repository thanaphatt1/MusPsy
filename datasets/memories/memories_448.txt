Client Profile:

[Personal Profile]  
Lihua is an adult navigating health challenges that impact everyday activities, seeking to adjust career and personal goals amid current circumstances.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lihua finds joy in writing as a therapeutic activity and enjoys relaxing through gardening and taking short walks.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lihua experiences anxiety related to career and health limitations, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lihua feels anxious about balancing personal limitations with career aspirations, striving for realistic yet hopeful goals.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Lihua's proactive exploration of career options and appreciation for creative and collaborative aspects of work.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lihua is encouraged to identify enjoyable career aspects and reach out to others who have made similar transitions for insights.
[Session Goal:] Assist Lihua in aligning career goals with personal limitations, exploring adaptable and fulfilling roles within current health constraints.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Lihua's struggle with balancing career goals and health limitations, emphasizing the importance of aligning personal expectations with realistic strategies. Encouraged Lihua to focus on career aspects that bring joy and to consider remote opportunities for flexibility, while also engaging in stress-reducing activities like gardening and walking. Recommended reaching out to others for insights, maintaining self-care through consistent routines, and journaling to track progress and reflections.
