The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
E is a university student experiencing stress and loneliness, primarily due to strained relationships with roommates. E values harmonious living and feels isolated due to recent disagreements with roommates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
E enjoys reading novels, which serves as a relaxation activity, and appreciates calmness, often finding solace in nature walks.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
E experiences anxiety triggered by relationship conflicts with roommates, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] E feels stressed and lonely due to the strained relationship with roommates, resulting in anxiety and a sense of defeat.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices E's anxiety related to assumptions about roommates' perceptions and the struggle with the desire for conflict resolution.
[Counseling Assignments:] E should note thoughts and feelings experienced when around roommates to identify patterns or triggers.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help E recognize and challenge assumptions, manage anxiety, and improve interaction with roommates.
Counseling Summary:
In counseling, E addressed feelings of anxiety and isolation resulting from strained roommate relationships, recognizing a tendency to assume the worst without evidence. E worked on challenging negative assumptions and prepared for open communication with roommates to alleviate tension. E also found solace in personal hobbies like reading, established strategies for managing future challenges, and gained confidence in handling interpersonal conflicts.
