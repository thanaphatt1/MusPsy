The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoming is a kindergarten student who enjoys school and wants to maintain good behavior, specifically being quiet in class. He feels nervous because he wants to excel and be recognized positively by his teacher and peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoming likes drawing and playing games with his friends, using these activities as a way to connect with others and manage his feelings.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoming experiences anxiety mainly related to social acceptance and performance in classroom settings, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoming is feeling a bit anxious about maintaining good behavior, particularly being quiet during class discussions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoming’s eagerness to be acknowledged for good behavior and his openness to understanding the balance between speaking and listening in class.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoming should practice noticing when he wants to talk and waiting for appropriate moments in class to speak.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoming develop patience and timing in social interactions using CBT strategies to reduce anxiety and promote confidence in class.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Xiaoming expressed feelings of nervousness and anxiety about maintaining good behavior in kindergarten, especially during quiet times in class. Through discussion, Xiaoming was guided to balance the desire to talk with friends by practicing patience and waiting for appropriate moments to speak, as well as using happy memories and creative activities like drawing to manage anxious thoughts. The session concluded with an encouragement for Xiaoming to support friends by sharing these strategies, reinforcing his own skills in the process.
