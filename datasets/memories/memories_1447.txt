The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaopeng is a university student who feels anxious about social interactions, especially in academic settings. Recently, he experienced stress and anxiety during a group discussion, fearing judgment from peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing basketball, which serves as a mental escape and relaxation technique.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaopeng shows signs of social anxiety and self-doubt but has not been formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaopeng feels stressed and anxious about socializing in new environments, such as university, and experiences guilt over perceived failures to participate.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaopeng's self-imposed pressure and fear of judgment yet notes his willingness to explore small social interactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaopeng is encouraged to take small steps, such as greeting classmates or practicing basketball, and maintain a journal to track thoughts and progress.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Xiaopeng reduce social anxiety, develop confidence in social interactions, and identify negative thoughts for adjustment.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaopeng's social anxiety and self-doubt at university, focusing on feelings of guilt and fear of judgment during group interactions. Developed a plan involving small steps, like starting conversations with classmates and using hobbies such as basketball for stress relief and gradual social engagement. Encouraged journaling to track progress and reinforce positivity, fostering self-awareness and confidence in social situations.
