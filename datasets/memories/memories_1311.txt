The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lee is a university student experiencing stress, particularly from family dynamics and academic pressures. Recently, Lee has been facing frequent arguments with their mother, affecting both mood and studies. Lee has a deep appreciation for personal interests like reading and sketching, which are used as stress relief.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lee enjoys reading and sketching, both of which provide a means of relaxation and mental escape.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lee is currently experiencing heightened stress and frustration due to family expectations and academic pressures. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lee feels overwhelmed by frequent arguments with their mom, leading to stress that affects mood and studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Lee's frustration stemming from perceived expectations and acknowledges Lee's willingness to explore calming activities such as reading.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lee should document interactions with their mom, noting triggers and emotions, and incorporate reading regularly for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Lee identify stress triggers and enhance emotional regulation through structured relaxation activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Lee's feelings about family tensions affecting mood and academics, suggesting keeping a record of interactions and feelings for clarity. Encouraged using calming activities like reading and sketching, opening up to trusted friends, and improving communication during calm moments with Lee's mom to manage stress. Envisioned ongoing self-awareness and engagement in activities Lee enjoys for balanced emotional health.
