The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Z is a young adult dealing with parental pressure and seeking independence. Recently, Z faced issues with their mother after being found at an internet café late at night, causing tension and frustration.

[Personal Characteristics]
Z enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming practice during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Z experiences stress and frustration primarily due to constant parental criticism and a lack of personal space. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Z feels angry and upset due to perceived criticism from their mother, struggling with constant pressure and feeling misunderstood.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Z's tendency to react defensively, driven by frustration from repeated communication breakdowns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Z is encouraged to list thoughts and emotions arising during interactions with their mother and practice relaxation techniques, like drawing, before engaging in conversations.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Z manage stress and improve communication by breaking defensive cycles and exploring calm engagement with their mother.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Z's frustration and communication challenges with their mom, and recognized a cycle of misunderstanding. Identified actively listening and different engagement methods, alongside relaxation techniques like drawing, as potential strategies for improving interactions. Agreed on the task of observing and recording thoughts and emotions during these exchanges to better understand and transform communication patterns.
