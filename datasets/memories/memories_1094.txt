The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaolin is navigating challenges with social relationships after a falling out with a friend named Xiaoqing. She is currently a student and often experiences self-doubt about friendships, feeling skeptical of their authenticity due to recent events.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaolin enjoys playing the guitar, which serves as a therapeutic activity for her.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaolin experiences doubts and anxiety concerning social interactions, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaolin feels down and frustrated due to a falling out with her friend Xiaoqing, which has impacted her view on friendships.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaolin's withdrawal from other classmates and her willingness to attempt identifying positive social interactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaolin should observe and jot down positive interactions or support from others, big or small, for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaolin begin to explore and challenge negative perceptions about friendships by using cognitive behavioral strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaolin's feelings about a recent conflict with a friend, which has impacted her trust in friendships, leading to increased withdrawal. Agreed on a small task of observing and noting positive social interactions to challenge negative beliefs and discussed the possibility of reaching out to a supportive cousin for perspective. Emphasized the therapeutic benefit of journaling these observations and the importance of gradually challenging negative thoughts about friendships.
