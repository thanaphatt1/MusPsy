The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a professional feeling overwhelmed due to the demanding nature of his work combined with a desire to spend quality time with his family. He is navigating work-life balance challenges and trying to manage expectations at home.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds relaxation and enjoyment in spending time with his son and wife, including playing board games. This family time acts as a respite from work pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences stress and guilt primarily driven by work demands and the pressure to meet expectations, although there's no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei is experiencing significant stress, feeling overstretched between professional obligations and family responsibilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist sees Zhang Wei's struggle with guilt over not spending enough time with family but observes his willingness to explore balancing strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is tasked with noting situations where he encounters strong emotions at work and reflecting on the thoughts accompanying these moments.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT to help Zhang Wei identify the thoughts contributing to stress and explore new ways to achieve a better balance between work and family life.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang Wei explored feelings of stress and guilt related to work and family responsibilities, recognizing patterns of negative thinking and catastrophizing. Through journaling and reflecting on thoughts, he learned to shift his perspective by focusing on his strengths and achievements, and committed to scheduling regular family time to balance work and enhance well-being. The session concluded with Zhang Wei feeling more confident in managing stress, using objective self-reflection and intentional family interactions as ongoing strategies.
