The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
L is a university student struggling with significant stress, particularly around academic workloads and familial expectations. They face procrastination issues exacerbated by stress and interactions with their mother.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
L enjoys jogging, which they’ve used as a stress-relief activity in the past and are considering taking up again.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
L experiences stress and procrastination, primarily triggered by academic pressures and the desire for perfection, but there is no explicit formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] L feels overwhelmed by procrastination and stress, particularly before exams and assignments, compounded by tension with their mother regarding studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes L's difficulties with procrastination and perfection-driven anxiety, along with the impact on familial relationships, showing willingness to explore methods to improve these issues.
[Counseling Assignments:] L should track moments when the urge to procrastinate arises, noting specific triggers and thoughts, particularly around feelings of overwhelm and interactions with their mother.
[Session Goal:] To apply CBT strategies in understanding procrastination triggers and initiating tasks imperfectly, while fostering communication with their mother to alleviate stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored L's procrastination linked to overwhelming feelings and perfectionism, particularly triggered by stress from assignments and tension with their mom. Agreed on implementing a strategy to begin tasks imperfectly and document any self-defeating thoughts to identify patterns and manage procrastination. Encouraged L to communicate new strategies to their mom to alleviate pressure and improve mutual understanding.
