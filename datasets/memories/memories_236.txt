The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Hong is a student who has been struggling with feelings of being overwhelmed and uninterested in studies after transitioning back to in-person classes. They are worried about falling behind, especially after not engaging much during online classes. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Hong enjoys video games and drawing, which are potential outlets for relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Hong experiences anxiety related to academic performance but has not been formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Hong feels overwhelmed with catching up in studies and is anxious about future academic performance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Hong's tendency to expect the worst outcomes and acknowledges Hong's openness to exploring different coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Hong should note instances of stress during study sessions and the associated thoughts, to better understand and challenge negative patterns.
[Session Goal:] Help Hong identify negative thinking patterns using CBT techniques and encourage observation of how these thoughts affect their stress levels.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Hong's distress related to falling behind with studies due to the transition back to in-person classes and identified negative thought patterns contributing to his anxiety and lack of focus. Agreed on tasks to challenge pessimistic thinking, such as noting positive outcomes and discussing study techniques with peers, along with incorporating leisure activities like video gaming for stress relief. The session focused on shifting thinking patterns towards balanced perspectives and fostering peer connections to reduce feelings of isolation.
