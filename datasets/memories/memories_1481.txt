The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaow has been experiencing significant interpersonal conflicts, particularly with their mother concerning schoolwork, and with a classmate over a misunderstanding. Xiaow seems to struggle with expressing feelings and tends to internalize conflicts. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaow enjoys reading fiction, particularly fantasy novels, as a way to relax and gain different perspectives on personal situations.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaow encounters recurring thoughts of inadequacy and misunderstanding in interpersonal relationships, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaow feels overwhelmed and misunderstood due to recent arguments with their mother and a conflict with a classmate.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaow's tendency to default to negative self-assumptions and identify a need for improved communication skills.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaow is encouraged to write down moments when they feel misunderstood and what they ideally would say in those situations.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiaow recognize and challenge negative thought patterns and enhance communication skills.
Counseling Summary:
In the session, Xiaow expressed feeling overwhelmed and frustrated due to conflicts with his mom and deskmate, stemming from misunderstandings and self-critical thoughts. The consultant guided Xiaow to examine the negative beliefs about himself, encouraged him to recognize his strengths, and suggested improving communication by expressing feelings clearly to prevent misunderstandings. As a homework exercise, Xiaow was asked to write down situations where he felt misunderstood and think about how he could express his feelings constructively.
