The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Juanzi is a mother concerned about her daughter's ADHD diagnosis and how it affects her future. She has noticed a tendency towards negative thinking, especially in relation to her daughter, which sometimes causes tension in her marriage.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Juanzi enjoys cooking and finds it a relaxing activity that helps her connect with her family.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Juanzi experiences stress and anxiety related to her daughter's future and her role as a parent. There is no mention of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Juanzi feels stressed and worried about her daughter's ADHD and her ability to succeed socially and academically.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Juanzi's tendency towards negative thinking and her self-doubt as a parent, yet notes a willingness to explore positive reframing of her thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Juanzi should note down small victories related to her daughter and engage in a cooking activity with her daughter to foster bonding and create positive moments.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Juanzi recognize and reframe negative thoughts, encouraging her to focus on efforts rather than perfection in parenting.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Juanzi's negative thought patterns and how they impact her relationships and stress levels, particularly concerning her daughter's ADHD diagnosis. Agreed to focus on recognizing small victories, such as a successful school project, and incorporating joint activities like cooking to foster positive interactions. Introduced reframing techniques to shift parenting views from perfection to effort, and planned to address overthinking in future sessions.
