Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student overwhelmed by the stress of upcoming exams and family expectations. They express a strong desire not to let their supportive family down. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing as a form of relaxation but struggles to find time for it due to study commitments.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety and stress largely due to perceived familial expectations, but there is no indication of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels stressed and anxious, particularly due to upcoming exams and the fear of disappointing their family.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's tendency to internalize stress and the potential benefit of sharing these feelings with friends or family for relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to document moments of pressure and their triggers while attempting to share these concerns with peers for support.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to identify stress triggers, encourage positive communication, and incorporate relaxation activities like drawing into daily routines.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's academic stress linked to parental expectations, emphasizing the importance of documentation to identify patterns and triggers. Suggested sharing feelings with friends as a step toward alleviating pressure, and encouraged incorporating relaxing activities like walking into their routine. Agreed to follow up on the effectiveness of these strategies in the next session.
