The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a new university student who is struggling to adapt to the fast-paced teaching style compared to high school. Wang finds it challenging to manage time effectively amidst academic pressure, leading to stress and anxiety, especially when assignments and deadlines pile up. Wang has a keen interest in reading novels.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences significant stress and anxiety related primarily to academic pressures. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Besides academics, Wang enjoys reading novels, which serves as a form of relaxation and stress relief.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels overwhelmed by the transition to university life, experiencing continuous stress due to demanding coursework and time management challenges.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Wang's struggle with managing study routines and high academic expectations, but also notes Wang's openness to trying strategies for stress reduction.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should observe daily routines to identify stress points and start making small changes, such as scheduling breaks and integrating novel reading into downtime.
[Session Goal:] Help Wang establish a structured routine using CBT strategies to reduce academic stress and encourage positive stress-coping habits like regular breaks and personal interests.
Counseling Summary:
Wang is experiencing stress and anxiety due to the fast-paced environment of university compared to high school, struggling with time management and its impact on his sleep and concentration. The counselor suggested Wang observe his daily routine to identify stress points and integrate scheduled breaks with leisure activities, like reading novels, to manage stress and improve focus. Wang and the counselor discussed recognizing accomplishments to boost confidence and committed to reviewing progress in the next session, focusing on gradual improvement.
