The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a single mother dealing with stress related to a custody battle for her children and unemployment, which compounds her anxiety and feelings of overwhelm.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She has a love for gardening, which she finds relaxing and fulfilling.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang is experiencing high levels of stress and anxiety due to her current life circumstances but does not mention a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels stressed and overwhelmed by the pressure of regaining custody of her children and her current unemployment situation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang’s distress stemming from perceived judgment and her responsibilities, yet recognizes her willingness to explore coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should jot down significant thoughts and feelings that arise, particularly during stressful moments, to aid in gaining clarity and control.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to Zhang to help her understand the connection between her thoughts, emotions, and behaviors, and begin to feel more in control.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ms. Zhang's anxiety regarding custody and her struggles as a single mom without a job; focused on the connection between her thoughts and emotions. Agreed on journaling her thoughts and feelings, particularly those arising at night, to help manage stress and gain control. Encouraged consistent practice, ensuring support and guidance for her positive growth journey.
