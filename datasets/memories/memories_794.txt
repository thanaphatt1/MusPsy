The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Tang is a young adult currently undergoing treatment at a rehabilitation center in Beijing for drug addiction. Recently, he has been experiencing significant stress from trying to overcome his addiction, which affects his sleep and leads to frequent arguments with his parents, especially his mother who is staying with him. Tang mentions feeling determined to beat the addiction despite self-doubt.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Tang values open communication and is working on improving his relationship dynamics with his parents. He has previously enjoyed running, which he is considering reintroducing as a therapeutic activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Tang experiences stress and anxiety largely due to parental pressure and self-expectations in the context of addiction recovery. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Tang feels overwhelmed by the pressure of recovery and struggles with the expectations he perceives from his parents.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Tang’s resilience in facing these challenges and his willingness to engage in conversations about his experiences, indicating a proactive approach to recovery.
[Counseling Assignments:] Tang is encouraged to keep a daily journal to identify triggers and stress-related thoughts, aiding in better understanding his reactions.
[Session Goal:] To assist Tang in recognizing and breaking negative thought cycles while fostering open communication with his parents to alleviate stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Tang's struggle with addiction and the added stress from parental dynamics, focusing on self-doubt and anxiety linked to perceived expectations. Discussed the importance of open communication with parents to alleviate misunderstandings and enhance mutual support, agreeing to use a calm approach and share therapeutic techniques. Tang committed to keeping a journal to help identify stress triggers and better prepare for conversations, fostering a more supportive relationship with parents and aiding recovery.
