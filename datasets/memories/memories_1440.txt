The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Chen is a professional experiencing emotional instability and self-doubt after losing his position as a team leader, which has further impacted his self-esteem. He values being a positive role model for his daughter and is focused on managing his emotional state to provide support to her.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys spending quality time with his daughter, particularly engaging in activities like board games and nature walks.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Chen experiences anxiety and mood swings, particularly worsened by professional challenges, yet there is no formal diagnosis provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Chen feels down and rejected due to losing his leadership role, struggling with self-worth and insecurity.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Chen's desire for self-improvement and his commitment to being a supportive parent, noting his openness to developing anxiety management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Chen is encouraged to keep a daily journal to identify thoughts and emotional patterns and integrate regular joyful activities with his daughter.
[Session Goal:] Assist Chen in managing anxiety by identifying emotional triggers and encouraging activities that strengthen his relationship with his daughter.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Chen shared feelings of insecurity and doubt after losing a team leader position due to anxiety and mood swings. The plan includes maintaining a daily journal to identify thought patterns and triggers, engaging in positive activities with his daughter to balance stress, and incorporating deep breathing for anxiety management. This approach aims to build a supportive structure, with a focus on observing and addressing heightened anxiety during work meetings and difficult mornings.
