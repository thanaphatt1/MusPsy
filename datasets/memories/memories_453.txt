The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei recently experienced the loss of their father, which has caused significant emotional distress and uncertainty about the future. Zhang Wei is concerned about supporting the family and finding new direction in life without their father's guidance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei values family connections deeply and feels pressure to uphold responsibilities and supportive dynamics traditionally led by their father.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei is currently experiencing grief and associated emotional challenges but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels overwhelmed by emotions following the father's passing and uncertain about their role in family dynamics.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Zhang Wei as overwhelmed yet open to exploring coping mechanisms and acknowledges their self-imposed sense of responsibility for the family.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is encouraged to write down emotions and thoughts, focusing on starting a process of externalizing and managing grief.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT strategies to help Zhang Wei process grief by recognizing and managing overwhelming emotions, while identifying supportive future actions.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang Wei is experiencing emotional distress and a sense of being overwhelmed following the recent passing of his father, leading to feelings of being lost and uncertain about the future. The counselor and Zhang Wei discussed how expressing emotions might create a support system, suggesting he share his thoughts with family members or friends and noted the importance of self-compassion. For the upcoming week, Zhang Wei agreed to write down his thoughts and feelings, consider sharing with a trusted person, and practice small acts of kindness towards himself.
