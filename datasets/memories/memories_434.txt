The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li Ming is a professional facing significant stress from work, particularly due to project deadlines and perceived lack of teamwork from colleagues. Li Ming experiences feelings of being overwhelmed and struggles to unwind at home.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys reading, which previously served as a relaxation activity, and has considered reintroducing it into his routine for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li Ming experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by work-related pressures, with no formal mention of a mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li Ming feels stressed and overwhelmed due to work pressures and deadlines, leading to sleepless nights and a lack of focus.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Li Ming's struggle with team dynamics and tendency toward negative assumptions, but also recognizes Li Ming's openness to explore stress management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li Ming should try to reintroduce reading as a relaxation activity and jot down moments when feelings of being unsupported arise, reflecting on them for alternative interpretations.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Li Ming manage stress, challenge negative assumptions, and engage in relaxation activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li Ming's work-related stress caused by project deadline pressures and perceived lack of colleague collaboration, leading to feelings of overwhelm and withdrawal at work. Together with the consultant, Li Ming identified strategies to reintroduce relaxation through reading and to challenge perceptions by engaging with colleagues on smaller tasks, aiming to foster better communication and teamwork. The session concluded with tasks to reflect on work interactions and personal well-being, promoting an open-minded approach to colleagues' behaviors.
