The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaocheng is a student dealing with recent conflicts at school involving destructive incidents, leading to feelings of distress and fear of negative judgment from peers and family. He is seeking ways to manage anger triggered by perceived disrespect.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaocheng enjoys drawing, which serves as a therapeutic activity to calm his mind during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaocheng exhibits signs of anger and anxiety related to interpersonal conflicts, yet no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaocheng feels upset and worried due to a recent altercation involving breaking a classmate’s toy.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaocheng’s tendency to react strongly to perceived slights, yet also sees potential in his openness to learning coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaocheng is encouraged to reflect on triggers of anger and experiment with drawing as a relaxation activity when overwhelmed.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT strategies to help Xiaocheng identify anger triggers, explore alternative reactions, and incorporate drawing as a relaxing, expressive outlet.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaocheng expressed feelings of upset and worry linked to a school incident involving anger management. The consultant encouraged Xiaocheng to explore anger triggers through reflection and suggested drawing as a calming activity, while setting a goal to observe coping strategies before the next session. Xiaocheng agreed to try these approaches, feeling supported and hopeful for improvement.
