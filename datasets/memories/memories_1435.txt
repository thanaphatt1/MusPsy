The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Daisy is dealing with significant work-related pressure, resulting in overwhelming emotions and stress. Daisy seeks to better manage daily stressors and emotional responses.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She finds solace in creative activities like drawing and designing, which serve as an emotional outlet.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Daisy experiences stress and emotional overwhelm largely influenced by work pressures. Despite the intensity, no formal diagnosis has been made.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Daisy feels overwhelmed and emotionally exhausted, showing heightened sensitivity to everyday situations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Daisy's awareness of heightened emotions and willingness to observe and understand her reactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Daisy should observe and note down moments of overwhelming emotions, focusing on identifying specific triggers.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Daisy assess and manage emotional triggers, encouraging self-awareness and reflection on stress responses.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Daisy's overwhelm and heightened irritability due to pressures at work and personal encounters, identifying patterns of disproportionate reactions and underlying beliefs. Agreed on tasks for Daisy to track moments of anger and identify triggers to challenge beliefs and develop coping strategies, including setting boundaries in social interactions. Emphasized patience and persistence in this journey and provided support for gradual change and self-reflection until the next session.
