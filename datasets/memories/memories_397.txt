The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a professional engineer in his mid-30s facing significant stress following a recent project failure at his workplace. He feels uncertain about his career future and experiences self-doubt and anxiety. The stress has also affected his relationship with his wife, causing frequent arguments.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei enjoys jogging, which he finds relaxing but has not engaged in often lately.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei is experiencing anxiety and self-doubt triggered by a significant professional setback, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels stressed and overwhelmed due to the failed work project, leading to self-doubt and increased anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wei's tendency to internalize failures and the impact this has on his self-worth and interpersonal relationships.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei is encouraged to reflect on specific thoughts that arise when feeling anxious or frustrated and to note any positive feedback he might overlook.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Wei manage stress, identify and challenge negative thoughts, and encourage reflective thinking to build a balanced perspective.
Counseling Summary:
Wei expressed feelings of stress and self-doubt following a failed work project, which affected his relationship with his wife. The consultant guided Wei in identifying thought patterns linked to these emotions and encouraged him to consider setbacks as opportunities for growth, aiming to improve his interactions at work and at home. Wei agreed to reflect on his thoughts and overlooked positive feedback while gradually participating more openly in professional settings.
