The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xaolong is a student facing challenges with restlessness and impulsivity in class, which sometimes results in conflict with teachers and classmates. He finds enjoyment and calmness through drawing, which serves as a relaxation and stress-relief activity. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xaolong is a creative individual who enjoys drawing, often using it as a means to unwind after school.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xaolong exhibits signs of restlessness and impulsivity, particularly in academic settings. There is no formal mention of a mental health diagnosis at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xaolong feels bad about not being able to sit still and impacting the classroom environment negatively.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xaolong’s struggle with controlling impulses but sees potential in his openness to explore creative methods to manage this.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xaolong is encouraged to track feelings of restlessness in class and explore drawing-related activities that could help manage these impulses.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Xaolong connect thoughts, feelings, and actions, focusing on drawing as a calming influence during periods of restlessness.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xaolong's challenges with impulses in class causing social strain, focusing on connecting thoughts, feelings, and actions. Agreed on tracking thoughts before impulses and integrating calming activities like drawing to improve classroom engagement. Encouraged Xaolong to apply visualization techniques during lessons and revisit the approach in the next session for insights and further adjustments.
