The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
L is a university student experiencing significant stress and feelings of isolation due to the pandemic and the transition back to online classes. L struggles to maintain a routine and feels disconnected from peers and family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
L enjoys reading fiction and has expressed interest in reconnecting with friends and family through online interactions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
L reports anxiety and stress related to academic workload and social isolation but has not been formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] L feels anxious and overwhelmed by the transition to online classes and the isolation resulting from pandemic measures.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes L's willingness to explore routine-building strategies and interpersonal connections as a means to manage stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] L is encouraged to set a regular sleep schedule and engage in morning walks, as well as reach out to a friend for a video call.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help L establish routine, enhance connections with peers, and manage anxiety-inducing thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored L's feelings of anxiety and isolation due to pandemic-related changes in university life, focusing on their struggle with routine and connection. Agreed on steps to establish a daily routine, like setting a consistent sleep schedule and incorporating morning walks, along with planning personal interactions, such as a video call with a friend to nurture social connections. Encouraged L to note and critically assess recurring negative thoughts about their studies, aiming to manage them more effectively.
