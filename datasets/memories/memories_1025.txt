The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a college student navigating significant life changes, including adjusting to college life and dealing with family dynamics after their parents' divorce. Currently living with his father and his father's new partner, Zhao expresses difficulty in building new friendships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhao enjoys painting, finding it a relaxing escape and a means of connection with others who share similar interests.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao experiences anxiety related to social situations and fitting in with peers, driven by past family dynamics, but no formal mental health disorder diagnosis is indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels overwhelmed by the transition to college and struggles to connect with others, viewing himself negatively due to his family situation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhao's difficulty with self-esteem and perception of others, noting a tendency towards negative assumptions about social interactions and relationships.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao is encouraged to jot down situations that trigger negative self-perceptions and thoughts for further exploration in subsequent sessions.
[Session Goal:] Assist Zhao in identifying negative automatic thoughts, challenge these perceptions, and build coping strategies for better social integration at college.
Counseling Summary:
Zhao struggled with anxiety stemming from family changes and adjusting to college, notably fearing judgment in social settings. Through counseling, Zhao learned to challenge automatic negative thoughts by engaging in activities of personal interest, initiating social interactions, and reflecting on these experiences, which reduced anxiety and improved confidence. Zhao plans to continue using these strategies to maintain social connections and emotional well-being.
