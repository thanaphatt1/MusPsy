The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Bao is a police officer experiencing significant stress and anxiety at work due to a lack of training and a feeling of inadequacy compared to colleagues. Bao often encounters situations that are challenging due to insufficient training.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Bao enjoys playing the Dombra, which serves as a creative outlet and source of relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Bao experiences anxiety and stress primarily due to job-related pressures but has not received a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Bao feels stressed and inadequate at work due to insufficient training and a lack of confidence in handling new situations.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notices Bao's recognition of specific triggers for anxiety and acknowledges Bao's openness to exploring new learning approaches.
[Counseling Assignments:] Bao should note down the thoughts that occur during stressful situations for analysis and consider reaching out to colleagues for support and guidance.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Bao challenge negative thoughts, explore potential learning opportunities, and encourage asking for help from trusted colleagues.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Bao's work-related stress and anxiety related to lack of training in his role as a police officer, which impacts his self-confidence and feelings of inadequacy. Encouraged Bao to challenge negative thoughts by seeking guidance from trusted colleagues and leveraging his strengths in other areas like music. Suggested reflective practices to manage his anxiety and incrementally build resilience and confidence at work.
