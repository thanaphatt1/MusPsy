The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a professional encountering substantial anxiety related to face-to-face client interactions at work. These interactions leave Zhang feeling pressured and scrutinized, which impacts his self-confidence beyond the workplace.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang plays basketball, which serves as a significant stress-relief activity, though he currently plays only on weekends.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety primarily triggered by the fear of negative judgment in professional settings. This has not been linked to a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious and uncomfortable when interacting with clients due to the fear of negative judgment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang's struggle with overanalyzing situations and assuming negative judgments from others, yet recognizes Zhang's willingness to explore cognitive-based strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Zhang is encouraged to observe recurring thoughts during stressful interactions and note any patterns, while reminding themselves that professional competence is not defined by single interactions.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Zhang reduce anxiety by identifying and challenging negative assumptions and encouraging a more reality-based perspective.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's work-related anxiety stemming from fear of judgment and negative assumptions, affecting his confidence and interaction quality. Discussed recognizing and challenging irrational thoughts to reduce anxiety, and agreed on a self-observation task to identify recurring thought patterns. Encouraged maintaining perspective on professional competence and mindfulness in managing emotions.
