The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Dina is a student dealing with feelings of guilt and anxiety, particularly about communication lapses with her mother. She values maintaining trust and open communication within her family relationships. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Dina enjoys drawing and listening to music, which serve as relaxing and unwinding activities for her.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Dina experiences anxiety and guilt primarily related to her relationships but has not been diagnosed with a formal mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Dina feels anxious and guilty after failing to inform her mother about her whereabouts, which caused her mother concern.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Dina's tendency to be hard on herself and assume responsibility for accidental situations, yet acknowledges her willingness to adopt preventive strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Dina should set reminders to text her mom and reflect on how these small adjustments can ease anxieties in the future.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT techniques to help Dina lessen anxiety by creating practical solutions like reminders for responsibility and communication assurance.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling session, Dina shared feelings of anxiety and guilt stemming from a recent incident where she failed to text her mom, leading to her mom's worry. Together with the consultant, Dina developed practical steps, including setting phone reminders and writing down anxious thoughts, to manage her anxiety and prevent similar situations in the future. For relaxation, Dina plans to incorporate calming bedtime routines, such as listening to music, to help mitigate nighttime worries and better manage her emotions.
