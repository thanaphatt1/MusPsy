The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Ai is a student who has been experiencing difficulty focusing and managing emotions since the new school term began. She feels overwhelmed, particularly in group activities, and is concerned about not meeting expectations or being judged by classmates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys building with blocks and drawing, using these activities to find comfort and express creativity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
While there is no formal diagnosis, Xiao Ai struggles with feelings of inadequacy and distraction, especially in social and school environments.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Ai feels overwhelmed and agitated, particularly in settings where she perceives judgment from others.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Xiao Ai’s negative thought patterns and her assumption of others' judgment as contributors to her emotional distress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Ai is encouraged to observe and note patterns or thoughts that arise before and after losing focus or during emotional episodes.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiao Ai identify triggers for her emotional fluctuations and work on acknowledging and challenging those negative perceptions with CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Ai's struggles with focus and emotional regulation at school, identifying negative self-perceptions and comparison as contributing factors. Encouraged mindful observation of patterns, open dialogue with peers, and engagement in enjoyable, focus-enhancing activities like building blocks. Xiao Ai acknowledged progress through these strategies, feeling less alone, more confident, and equipped to manage future challenges.
