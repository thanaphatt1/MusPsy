The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is an employee overwhelmed by increased work pressure and deadlines, which trigger memories of a past negative therapy experience. Li is striving to feel more confident and secure at work.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li finds joy in nature, particularly enjoys going for walks in the park as a stress-reliever, and values feedback as a means of reassurance and personal growth.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences anxiety related to professional performance and the fear of disappointing others, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li expresses feeling overwhelmed by work pressures and recurring memories of past uncomfortable experiences.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li's tendency to let past experiences amplify current work anxiety, coupled with a difficulty in focusing due to stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should observe and jot down any negative thoughts that arise during work to better understand and address them.
[Session Goal:] Assist Li in using CBT strategies to identify and challenge work-related stressors, while encouraging the integration of stress-relief activities.
Counseling Summary:
Li faced overwhelming work pressure and anxiety exacerbated by memories of a past negative therapy experience. Through sessions, Li recognized and challenged negative thought patterns, focused on factual feedback rather than assumptions, and decided to communicate openly with their manager for clearer work feedback. Additionally, Li committed to a routine of stress-relieving walks, which collectively helped build confidence and manage stress effectively.

[Session Goal:] Encourage Li to build self-awareness through journaling fears and confirmations, focusing on positive evidence while reducing assumption-driven stress.
