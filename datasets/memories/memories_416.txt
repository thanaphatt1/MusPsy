The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a professional working long hours and experiencing stress related to work-life balance. He has a son and expresses feelings of guilt for not spending enough time with his family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys playing the guitar, which he considers a stress-relief activity, and values his family's opinion and presence.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei is experiencing stress and guilt due to long work hours but has not been diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels stressed and exhausted due to work commitments and guilty for not being with his family as much as he would like.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang Wei's pronounced feelings of guilt and stress, yet they observe his willingness to explore ways to manage these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei should attempt to find brief moments to connect with his family and reflect on these interactions, particularly focusing on their emotional impact.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Zhang Wei to CBT techniques for managing stress and guilt, and encourage better work-life balance through quality family interaction.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session explored Zhang Wei's stress, exhaustion, and guilt from prioritizing work over family, suggesting he focus on the quality of family interactions to alleviate guilt. Zhang Wei identified unrealistic thoughts contributing to his feelings of inadequacy and planned to integrate guitar playing into his routine as stress relief. The consultant reinforced the value of balancing work, family, and personal hobbies to enhance overall well-being and supported Zhang Wei's small, achievable goals to maintain momentum and manage reluctance.
