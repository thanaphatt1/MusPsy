The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student who has recently transitioned to a new environment at school, feeling lonely and out of place, mainly due to shyness and concerns about appearance and height. Zhang finds difficulty connecting with roommates and classmates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys drawing, which was previously an escape in the past, and appreciates reading fiction.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety and loneliness primarily triggered by social interactions and self-image concerns. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious and disconnected, struggling with shyness and self-image concerns.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's openness to exploring feelings and willingness to engage in exercises for gradual social interaction.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to note recurring thoughts of anxiety and isolation while reintegrating drawing into the weekly schedule.
[Session Goal:] The goal is to help Zhang challenge negative thoughts through CBT strategies and facilitate gradual social connections by engaging in relaxing activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's struggles with social anxiety and self-image, focusing on his feelings of loneliness and difficulty connecting with others in a new school environment. Agreed on exercises to challenge negative thoughts and a plan to gradually open up to roommates, along with scheduling time for drawing as a form of self-expression and stress relief. Encouraged maintaining patience and kindness to himself while working through these personal challenges.
