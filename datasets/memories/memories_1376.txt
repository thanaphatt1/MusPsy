The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Chen is a university student struggling with managing his time, particularly balancing between gaming and academic responsibilities. He feels gaming is an escape from academic pressures.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys basketball and has mentioned interests in creative activities like drawing and playing music.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Chen experiences anxiety linked with self-imposed high standards and fears of academic failure, but there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Chen feels a lack of control over his gaming habits and academic duties, resulting in frustration and self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Chen's use of gaming as a coping mechanism and his insight into self-critical thoughts as barriers to motivation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Chen is encouraged to set a daily 30-minute study session and document negative thoughts, replacing them with realistic alternatives.
[Session Goal:] Assist Chen in using CBT strategies to modify his thought patterns and regain control over his study habits through small, achievable goals.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Chen's reliance on gaming as a coping mechanism for anxiety related to academic performance, identifying negative thought patterns that discourage studying. Agreed on setting a 30-minute daily study goal and encouraged reframing negative thoughts to foster a more positive mindset. Emphasized small achievements and realistic alternatives to help Chen regain a sense of control and motivation.
