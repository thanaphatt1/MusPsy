The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a young adult dealing with the emotional fallout of a relationship betrayal. Li discovered an online boyfriend's unfaithfulness, leading to distress and self-blame.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys painting, which provides a calming outlet for emotional expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences significant emotional turmoil stemming from betrayal, leading to depressive thoughts and self-blame. There are no formal diagnoses mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels emotionally overwhelmed, expressing depression and self-harm thoughts following the betrayal.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li’s intense self-blame and sees the need for supportive strategies to address emotional distress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to practice deep breathing during overwhelming emotions as a first step toward managing anxiety and sadness.
[Session Goal:] Help Li identify emotional triggers and introduce basic coping strategies such as deep breathing to alleviate immediate emotional distress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's experience with betrayal in an online relationship, focusing on self-blame and overwhelming emotions leading to depression and anxiety. Introduced coping strategies including deep breathing and journaling to recognize negative thought patterns, alongside encouragement to practice self-compassion. Agreed to continue building resilience and developing strategies for challenging these thoughts in future sessions.
