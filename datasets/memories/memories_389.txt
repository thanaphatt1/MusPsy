The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a professional dealing with anxiety largely related to recent changes at work, including organizational restructuring and the addition of a new leader. These developments have led to communication issues within their team, causing Zhang Wei to feel concerned about their leadership capabilities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys reading novels as a form of relaxation and reflection, often finding solace in these moments.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences anxiety primarily triggered by workplace changes and self-doubt about their leadership abilities, without mention of a formal diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels anxious and uncertain about leading the team due to recent organizational changes and concerns about their responsibility in communication breakdowns.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang Wei's tendency to second-guess their leadership capabilities and assume fault in circumstances beyond their control, alongside a willingness to explore alternative approaches.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei should explore involving team members in addressing communication challenges and consider regular reflective reading time to unwind.
[Session Goal:] The aim is to help Zhang Wei manage anxiety by encouraging collaboration and open dialogue within the team, while developing a personal routine for stress relief.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang Wei discussed his anxiety stemming from recent organizational changes at work, feeling responsible for communication issues within his team. Through counseling, he recognized the value of collaborative problem-solving and decided to involve his team in addressing these issues, while also setting aside personal time for relaxing activities like reading to manage stress. He plans to manage future challenges by sharing responsibility and unwinding through hobbies.
