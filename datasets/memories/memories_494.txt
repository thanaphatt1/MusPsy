The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Eve is a mother recently discharged from a 28-day hospital treatment, adjusting to a new home while regaining custody of her son. She is focused on creating a stable and loving environment for her child.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys cooking and reading, using these activities as sources of joy and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Eve shows signs of anxiety related to past hospitalization and current responsibilities, with no formal diagnosis of a long-term mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Eve feels a mix of relief and anxiety, adjusting to new circumstances while eager to provide stability for her son.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Eve's resilience and her thoughtful approach toward establishing a routine that fosters stability.
[Counseling Assignments:] Eve is encouraged to list activities that provide stability and happiness, focusing on routine creation and positive experiences with her son.
[Session Goal:] Assist Eve in building a balanced routine that includes activities fostering stability, and manage anxiety related to her new environment and responsibilities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Eve's emotional experience following her transition from hospital treatment to a new home and regaining custody of her son, focusing on balancing relief and anxiety. Recognized the importance of routines and positive activities, like cooking, in providing stability and grounding, and agreed on setting daily goals to incorporate more positive experiences into her life. Encouraged tracking these moments and identifying triggers for negative thoughts as part of managing anxiety.
