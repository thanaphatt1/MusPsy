The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is recently married and experiencing stress in her relationship due to her husband's dominant personality, particularly affecting their intimate life. She desires to feel respected and valued within her marriage.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang enjoys gardening, finding peace and happiness in nurturing her plants, and also values spending time with friends for comfort and belonging.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang faces feelings of neglect and insignificance within her relationship but has no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels stressed and disheartened due to her husband's dominant behavior, which makes her uncomfortable and diminishes her sense of being heard and respected.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Yang's discomfort with perceived neglect in her relationship and identifies her willingness to explore expressing her feelings more openly.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is encouraged to observe moments when she feels disrespected and note what triggers these feelings to better understand them.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Yang recognize and understand her emotional reactions in her relationship, encouraging open communication as a means to promote mutual respect.
Counseling Summary:
Yang expressed stress and discomfort in her marriage due to her husband's dominant personality, particularly feeling disrespected in intimate moments and during disagreements. The session focused on helping Yang become more observant of triggering situations and encouraged a calm, non-blaming approach to discussing feelings with her husband. The consultant also suggested integrating personal activities like drawing and reading for stress relief, with the aim of fostering resilience and understanding patterns in her emotional responses.
