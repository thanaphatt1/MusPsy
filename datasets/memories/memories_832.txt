The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a young adult dealing with familial conflicts and feelings of isolation, stemming from a recent incident where her mother confiscated her phone. Li has expressed feelings of hopelessness and has previously considered drastic actions when facing overwhelming emotions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys drawing and reading fantasy novels as methods of relaxation and escape.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences emotional distress related to family dynamics and school pressures, manifesting in feelings of inadequacy and being misunderstood. No formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed but recurrent negative thoughts suggest potential anxiety or depressive symptoms.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels a sense of hopelessness and disappointment due to conflict with her mother, leading to isolation and distress.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Li's struggle with feelings of being misunderstood and inadequate, noting her openness to exploring feelings and attempting new coping methods.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to write down thoughts and feelings during overwhelming moments as a means to gain perspective and address recurring negative patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to identify and challenge distressing thoughts, fostering healthier emotional responses and communication.
Counseling Summary:
The session focused on Li's feelings of isolation and pressure following a conflict with their mother, which led to thoughts of hopelessness. The consultant encouraged Li to write down overwhelming thoughts to gain perspective and challenged them to reflect on self-imposed assumptions contributing to their stress. Li agreed to open up to trusted friends, engage in drawing for relaxation, and explore these thoughts creatively while gradually expressing themselves more in social settings.
