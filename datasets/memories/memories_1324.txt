The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student experiencing considerable stress due to impending exams and academic expectations. They value academic success highly and fear underperformance.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety mainly linked to academic pressures, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing, which serves as a potential stress-relief activity.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels stressed and overwhelmed by the approaching finals, struggling to concentrate and pulling away from social interactions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes the user's tendency towards procrastination when overwhelmed, accompanied by self-critical thoughts during such times.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should write down stress-inducing thoughts and situations, and incorporate short breaks with calming activities like listening to music or taking walks.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT strategies to help the user identify stress-related thoughts, initiate self-kindness practices, and encourage taking relaxing breaks to improve focus and reduce anxiety.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's exam-related stress and its impact on focus and social interactions, noting the challenge of high self-expectations. Encouraged documenting stress triggers and introduced self-kindness practices, including taking breaks with activities like walking and listening to music, to build resilience against stress. Agreed on observing how these practices affect stress and productivity for discussion in the next session.
