The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaohua is a high school student feeling significant stress, particularly due to the upcoming college entrance exams and the strain in relationships with roommates and parents.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaohua enjoys gardening, which is starting to be used as a method of relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaohua experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, particularly related to exams, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaohua feels stressed and overwhelmed due to the pressure of upcoming exams, compounded by strained relationships at home and in the dormitory.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaohua's high level of stress and sees an opportunity to use exploring thoughts and emotions as an awareness tool for stress management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaohua is encouraged to write down thoughts and feelings during study sessions to capture immediate stress-related thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT strategies to help Xiaohua identify and understand stress-inducing thoughts, leading to reframing them in a less distressing manner.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaohua's stress and overwhelm from college exams compounded by strained relationships with family and roommates. Proposed journaling thoughts and feelings during study to identify and evaluate patterns affecting stress levels, with the aim of reframing distressing thoughts for better stress management. Emphasized the importance of gradual progress in understanding and managing stress, fostering hope and a positive approach.
