The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
L is a young adult experiencing heightened anxiety and disrupted sleep due to the recent betrayal by their boyfriend. They also feel pressured by high expectations from their family, particularly grandparents. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
L enjoys sketching as a creative outlet, though their motivation has waned recently.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
L is struggling with anxiety exacerbated by personal betrayal and familial pressure, yet there is currently no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] L feels anxious and unable to sleep, compounded by familial expectations and recent betrayal.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes L's overwhelming sense of pressure and a damaging cycle of self-blame but notes L's potential for finding solace in creative activities like sketching.
[Counseling Assignments:] L should sketch at least once this week to re-engage with activities that bring relaxation and distraction from stress.
[Session Goal:] Help L manage anxiety through CBT strategies by identifying stress triggers and promoting engagement in calming activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored L's challenges following a betrayal and pressure from family expectations, focusing on how these experiences heightened anxiety and trust issues. Encouraged L to engage in sketching and music as therapeutic activities, while recognizing thought patterns and triggers related to negative self-perceptions and assumptions. Agreed on tracking recurring thoughts to differentiate between assumptions and facts, and to incorporate grounding techniques for more positive emotions.
