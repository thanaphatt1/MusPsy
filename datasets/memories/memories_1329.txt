The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Song is a college student experiencing anxiety, particularly around social interactions and perceived judgments from peers related to a dance practice incident. Song often feels the need to wash their hands as a coping mechanism for these anxieties. 

[Personal Characteristics]
Interested in drawing, which Song has found to be a relaxing and positive focus.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Song often deals with anxiety manifested through obsessive-compulsive tendencies, specifically handwashing, largely triggered by negative thought patterns and social pressures, without a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Song feels compelled to wash hands whenever reminded of the dance practice incident due to overwhelming anxiety and fear of judgment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Song's propensity to assume negative judgments from others despite lack of evidence, indicating a strong need for cognitive restructuring.
[Counseling Assignments:] Song should challenge anxiety-inducing assumptions by seeking evidence for thoughts and practice breathing exercises to manage in-the-moment anxiety.
[Session Goal:] Aid Song in developing awareness and challenging negative automatic thoughts using CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Song's anxiety related to dance practice and perfectionistic fears of contamination and judgment. Agreed on steps to challenge negative assumptions and avoidant behaviors, including mindful breathing and a notebook for tracking anxious thoughts. Emphasized small, gradual steps for gaining perspective and altering negative thinking patterns.
