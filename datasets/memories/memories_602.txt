The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student who struggles with anger management, resulting in frequent arguments at school and home. They experience difficulty in controlling their temper, especially when provoked by comments about their family. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing, finding it a helpful outlet for expressing emotions and calming down.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user is experiencing issues with anger management and sensitivity to criticism, though there is no formal mental health disorder diagnosis at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels angry and overwhelmed due to frequent conflicts both at school and with their family.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's challenges with anger management and sensitivity to criticism, yet appreciates their willingness to attempt documenting their feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is asked to maintain a log of situations where they feel most upset, including specific triggers and corresponding thoughts or feelings.
[Session Goal:] To help the user identify anger triggers and explore techniques for anger management using CBT principles.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's difficulty with controlling anger and its impact on school and home life. Agreed to track moments of intense emotions and potential triggers to identify patterns. Emphasized the importance of understanding underlying emotions and practicing pausing before reacting to reduce conflicts.
