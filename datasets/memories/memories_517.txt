The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Chen is a student experiencing significant emotional distress, particularly from incidents at school and challenges in family communication. He finds difficulty managing his temper and often feels misunderstood by those around him.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Chen enjoys activities such as drawing and listening to music, which help him manage stress. He values supportive relationships and desires better communication with peers and family.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Chen exhibits emotional dysregulation, particularly anger triggered by criticism or conflicts at school and home, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Chen feels upset and isolated due to an incident at school where his anger led to damaging a classmate's property, resulting in tension with peers and parents.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Chen's isolation due to emotional mismanagement, noting his openness to discuss feelings and explore positive expressions through art.
[Counseling Assignments:] Chen is encouraged to schedule daily drawing sessions to help manage rising emotions and track changes in mood during these activities.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Chen manage anger, develop effective communication skills, and explore creative outlets as emotional stabilizers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Chen's feelings of anger and resulting issues at school and home, emphasizing the importance of expressing emotions to prevent misunderstandings. Agreed on incorporating drawing as a calming activity to manage emotions and planned to communicate feelings with his mother during calmer moments. Chen set a goal to prepare a conversation topic for his mother to facilitate understanding and reduce pressure in interactions.
