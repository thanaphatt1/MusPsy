The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoteng is a high school student who feels frustrated and angry due to his parents' decision to hospitalize him against his will. He is struggling with school while being heavily involved in online gaming, which his parents view negatively.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoteng enjoys programming, finding it both challenging and exciting, and uses gaming as a means to de-stress. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoteng shows signs of frustration and feelings of betrayal toward his parents due to differing views on his gaming habits, though no formal diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoteng feels frustrated and disconnected from his parents, leading to a diminished interest in school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoteng’s struggle with family tension and his emotional disconnection, but appreciates his willingness to explore his feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoteng is encouraged to observe fluctuations in his emotions in different situations, noting any patterns for further discussion.
[Session Goal:] To increase Xiaoteng's emotional awareness using CBT techniques and identify the influence of family dynamics on his emotional state.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaoteng explored feelings of frustration and disconnection due to his parents' decisions regarding his gaming and schooling, leading to emotional shutdowns. Throughout the counseling sessions, Xiaoteng was encouraged to observe and reflect on his emotional patterns, challenge his perceptions, and engage in open dialogues with his parents to express his feelings and interests. By incorporating programming into his routine and developing strategies to manage his assumptions, Xiaoteng made progress towards better self-awareness and emotional management, feeling more capable of handling future challenges.
