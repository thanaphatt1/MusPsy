The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoqi is a student experiencing significant stress related to school and interpersonal tensions with roommates. Xiaoqi feels isolated as a result of these tensions and struggles to focus on studies, exacerbating feelings of anxiety.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoqi enjoys drawing, which serves as a relaxation activity, and values maintaining peace in group settings, often taking on the role of mediator.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoqi exhibits symptoms of anxiety, particularly in response to social and academic pressures; there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoqi feels overwhelmed by the tension with roommates, leading to stress and a sense of isolation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Xiaoqi assumes responsibility for the group's dynamics, showing a tendency towards self-blame and the need to mediate conflicts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoqi is encouraged to jot down stress-related thoughts and recognize patterns in self-imposed responsibility and guilt.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoqi identify stress triggers and understand underlying beliefs causing a sense of responsibility, aiming to reduce this burden through CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoqi's feelings of stress and responsibility due to challenging roommate dynamics, which negatively impact her school experience. Discussed the underlying belief of needing to be a peacemaker and how it contributes to her anxiety and sense of burden. Agreed to identify and note down thought patterns over the week to better understand triggers and work towards healthier response strategies.
