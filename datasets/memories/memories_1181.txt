The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaowang recently moved into university dorms and is struggling with roommate interactions, feeling isolated after being locked out intentionally. Experiences anxiety and stress, which affects the ability to study and feel comfortable in their living environment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaowang enjoys reading and hopes to reconnect with past friends or find new social opportunities at university.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaowang exhibits anxiety and stress stemming from social exclusion in the current living situation, though no formal mental health disorder diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaowang feels helpless and angry due to being excluded by roommates, resulting in feeling anxious and stressed.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaowang’s sense of isolation and self-doubt triggered by roommate issues, highlighting past supportive relationships for reassurance.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaowang should reflect on positive past friendships and consider reaching out to old friends or forging new connections through university activities.
[Session Goal:] To help Xiaowang manage anxiety by using cognitive behavioral techniques to challenge self-doubt and encourage proactive social engagement as a path to improving current social interactions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaowang's feelings of isolation and stress due to conflicts with roommates, prompting feelings of self-doubt despite past supportive friendships. Discussed strategies for improving social connections, including reaching out to old friends, meeting new people, and communicating directly with roommates, along with incorporating enjoyable activities like reading to manage anxiety and uplift mood.
