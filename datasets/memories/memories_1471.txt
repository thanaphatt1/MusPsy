The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaojun is a school student dealing with negative interactions with classmates, especially concerning his height. He recently experienced a physical altercation due to bullying. He is concerned about his parents' reactions and is looking for ways to manage his emotions better.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaojun enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming and expressive outlet for him.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaojun is experiencing emotional distress and social anxiety triggered by bullying, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaojun feels upset, angry, and embarrassed after being teased for his height, leading to a physical confrontation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaojun's struggle with feelings of embarrassment and frustration, as well as his openness to exploring his emotions through journaling.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Xiaojun should keep a journal to document his feelings and thoughts whenever he feels upset, to better understand his emotions.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Xiaojun express his feelings constructively and prepare for discussing the situation with his parents.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaojun's emotional response to being teased and his subsequent involvement in a physical altercation, focusing on feelings of embarrassment and frustration. Agreed on the task of journaling feelings to facilitate emotional understanding and prepare for a calm discussion with parents. Encouraged further examination of patterns and recurring thoughts for future discussions.
