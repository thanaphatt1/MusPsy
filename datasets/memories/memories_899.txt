The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a recent graduate navigating significant stress, especially due to job searching and multiple exams, which have not been going as expected. This pressure affects Wang’s sleep and appetite.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys reading and drawing, which were previously sources of relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences heightened stress and anxiety primarily triggered by career and academic challenges, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels overwhelmed and stressed due to the challenges in job searching and exam results.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang’s difficulties in coping with the current stressors but notes an openness to exploring past relaxing activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should identify and incorporate past relaxing activities such as reading and drawing into their weekly routine.
[Session Goal:] Help Wang identify and engage in activities that promote relaxation and stress relief.
Counseling Summary:
Wang struggled with stress due to job searching and exams, impacting his daily life by reducing study efficiency, sleep, and appetite. Together with the consultant, Wang identified relaxation activities like reading and drawing, and worked on challenging negative thoughts by assessing their validity and focusing on proactive preparation strategies, which helped him feel more in control and relaxed. They established a plan to regularly engage in enjoyable activities and maintain positive strategies, empowering Wang to manage future challenges with the skills he developed during the sessions.
