The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
H is a student who recently moved to a new area and is struggling to adjust to a bigger school environment and new classmates. H is close with their mother but has not shared much about their feelings regarding the move.

[Personal Characteristics]  
H enjoys drawing and uses it as a tool to calm down during anxious periods.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
H experiences anxiety and feelings of isolation related to the recent move and the challenges of fitting into a new school setting.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] H feels lost and anxious about adapting to the new school environment and forming new social connections.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes H's hesitancy to reach out for new social interactions but recognizes a willingness to work on these issues.
[Counseling Assignments:] H is encouraged to note down situations at school where they feel out of place, helping identify anxiety triggers more clearly.
[Session Goal:] Help H adjust to the new environment by exploring social comfort zones and encouraging gradual engagement with peers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored H's feelings of anxiety and isolation resulting from a recent move and the challenges of adjusting to a new school. Suggested keeping a journal to identify specific moments of discomfort and encouraged setting aside dedicated time for relaxing activities to manage stress. Discussed the potential benefits of gradually opening up more to friendly classmates and considering how to talk to H's mom about the transition without causing worry.
