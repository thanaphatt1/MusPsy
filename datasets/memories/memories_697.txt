The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is dealing with emotional turmoil following a recent breakup, which has significantly impacted her ability to concentrate on her studies and maintain a healthy routine. She struggles with sharing her feelings about the breakup fully with her family to avoid worrying them, while her friends are aware and provide support.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang has a passion for reading and painting, activities she hasn’t pursued since the breakup but finds solace in their potential to divert her mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences stress and emotional upheaval primarily due to recent relationship challenges, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder yet.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang is feeling overwhelmed and emotionally disrupted due to a recent breakup, making it difficult to concentrate on academic responsibilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Wang as experiencing significant emotional disruption yet notes her readiness to explore coping mechanisms like confiding in her sister and engaging in past enjoyable activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to gradually resume evening walks and incorporate mindfulness practices during these activities while also journaling any pervasive thoughts and emotions.
[Session Goal:] The aim is to support Wang in managing her emotional stress using CBT strategies, reintroducing relaxing activities, and identifying patterns in her thoughts that influence her emotional well-being.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's post-breakup stress, focusing on its impact on his thoughts, emotions, and daily functioning, including difficulties with concentration and sleep. Discussed strategies to alleviate overwhelming feelings, such as reintroducing evening walks with mindfulness and journaling, while encouraging dialogue with his supportive sister. Wang expressed willingness to try these approaches, recognizing their potential for managing stress and providing insight into his emotional state.
