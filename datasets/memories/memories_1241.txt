The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ronald is a student adjusting to recent changes at home after his father's retirement. He finds the transition unsettling, often wishing for the previous normalcy of having space to himself after school. This shift has influenced his interest in attending school and his overall sense of routine.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ronald enjoys reading, finding it a useful escape when he feels stressed. This interest in reading provides a constructive outlet for relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ronald has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder, but he is experiencing anxiety linked to recent familial changes and school pressures.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ronald feels unsettled due to his father's presence at home, which disrupts his normal routine and affects his desire to attend school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Ronald's struggle with adapting to changes at home and apprehension about expressing his feelings to his father.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ronald is tasked with maintaining a journal to track daily thoughts and feelings, aiming to identify patterns and prepare for a conversation with his father.
[Session Goal:] Facilitate Ronald's adjustment to his father's retirement by exploring the impact on his mood, and encourage open communication with his father to establish new routines.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ronald's feelings of unsettlement and avoidance related to his dad's retirement disrupting his routine. Suggested keeping a journal to identify patterns in his mood and thoughts, and encouraged open communication with his dad to create a smoother transition. Discussed strategies for improving adjustment, focusing on reflection and gentle dialogue.
