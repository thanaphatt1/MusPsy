The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoli is an intern nurse dealing with feelings of frustration and inadequacy in her career. She feels out of place since attending a nursing school that wasn't her first choice and is troubled by the success of classmates who performed poorly academically but now have better opportunities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys reading, particularly mysteries and historical fiction, which serve as a form of relaxation and escape.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoli experiences a pervasive sense of inadequacy and frustration, primarily triggered by comparing herself to her classmates' achievements. There is currently no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoli feels isolated and resentful due to constant self-comparison with her peers' success, making her feel inadequate.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoli’s strong tendency towards negative self-comparison and a pattern of undervaluing her accomplishments.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoli is tasked with noting specific situations in which she feels negative emotions and reflecting on her achievements to challenge her self-critical thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoli identify and challenge negative thoughts, replace them with balanced thinking, and increase self-esteem by recognizing personal achievements.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoli's feelings of inadequacy and isolation stemming from comparisons with classmates in her nursing program, and identified the frequent negative impact on her interactions. Encouraged Xiaoli to observe and reflect on specific triggers and to recognize and value her own achievements as a way to challenge these negative thought patterns. Agreed that Xiaoli will track both challenging and positive experiences, consider sharing feelings with a supportive peer, and approach upcoming situations like a group presentation as opportunities for growth and teamwork.
