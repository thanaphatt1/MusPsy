The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yanyan is a university student experiencing significant stress due to the transition to college life, feeling homesick, and the pressure of adapting to a new environment in Shanghai after moving from a small town in Jiangxi. Family expectations also add to the stress. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yanyan enjoys writing, which serves as a creative outlet and potential stress reliever.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yanyan experiences anxiety and feelings of isolation, primarily triggered by academic challenges and social adaptation, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yanyan is feeling overwhelmed and lonely due to the cultural and social shifts encountered in university life.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Yanyan feels isolated and anxious about academic pressures and struggles with self-criticism.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Yanyan should start keeping a journal to reflect on thoughts and patterns of anxiety, focusing on contrasting perceptions and reality.
[Session Goal:] Help Yanyan manage loneliness and anxiety through CBT by reflecting on thoughts, identifying stress patterns, and encouraging positive social interactions over time.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yanyan's feelings of loneliness and anxiety due to the transition to university life and being far from family. Discussed the benefits of journaling to reflect on emotions and perceptions, and encouraged her to engage in writing as a creative outlet to manage stress. Yanyan agreed to note moments of connection and positive experiences, and to set a routine for writing, aiming to build confidence and emotional balance.
