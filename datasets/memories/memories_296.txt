The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a university student struggling with anxiety and pressure due to academic studies and multiple extracurricular commitments. He often feels overwhelmed by the demands placed on him by both academics and social activities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang enjoys reading, particularly fantasy novels, which offer him a sense of escape and relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang experiences anxiety and stress, primarily triggered by academic pressures and extracurricular overloads, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis reported.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels anxious and overwhelmed due to his engagement in numerous extracurricular activities and upcoming exams.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Yang's difficulty in managing time effectively and his sensitivity to academic and social pressures, yet sees Yang's willingness to explore time-management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is tasked with keeping a brief diary to note activities causing overwhelm to better manage time and stress moving forward.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Yang manage stress by identifying particularly demanding activities and clarifying thoughts related to anxiety.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yang's overwhelming feeling of juggling studies and extracurricular activities, leading to anxiety and irritability. Agreed to keep a diary to identify stress-inducing tasks and challenged distorted thoughts by examining them more objectively. Plan to discuss stress-reducing strategies in the next session as part of ongoing anxiety management.
