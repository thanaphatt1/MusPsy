The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaowu is a young adult struggling with feelings of failure and guilt concerning perceived familial disappointment after a recent incident. Xiaowu experiences high levels of stress and anxiety, mainly triggered by interactions with family, particularly their father, regarding future prospects.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaowu finds solace in reading novels, which serves as an escape from reality and a relaxation tool.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaowu grapples with anxiety stemming from self-critical thoughts, driven by familial pressure and recent events, though there is no formal mental health diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaowu feels engulfed by persistent beliefs of being a failure, largely impacting focus and daily functioning.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaowu’s tendency to internalize assumptions of judgment and recognizes Xiaowu’s willingness to challenge exaggerated thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaowu should make a list of stress-inducing thoughts and try engaging in reading as a form of regular relaxation, scheduling time in the evening for this activity.
[Session Goal:] Assist Xiaowu in identifying cognitive distortions and applying compassionate self-talk, using CBT strategies to manage self-critical thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaowu's recurring negative self-perceptions linked to discussions about his future, especially with his father, and recognized that these thoughts could be exaggerated. Emphasized the value of self-compassion and viewing situations from an objective standpoint, leading to Xiaowu feeling less hopeless and considering rebuilding family relationships through open dialogue. Encouraged Xiaowu to maintain stress-relieving activities, such as reading and journaling, to gain clarity and manage emotions, highlighting the importance of gradual progress.
