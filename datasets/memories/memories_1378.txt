The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]
Zhang is a university student who recently experienced stress and shame after receiving a serious warning from their school for inappropriate behavior in a women’s restroom. This behavior inadvertently began in high school and continued into university, creating anxiety about potential expulsion.

[Personal Characteristics]
Zhang enjoys playing the guitar, which they find relaxing and is working towards incorporating into their routine as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]
Zhang experiences anxiety and shame primarily triggered by fears of social judgment and potential academic consequences, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious and ashamed, primarily worried about social judgment and potential expulsion from their university.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's distress over their behavior and the potential consequences but also sees a willingness to open up about their struggles and explore supportive solutions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to reach out to supportive friends, practice guitar as a relaxation activity, and begin journaling to process their thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Zhang manage anxiety by challenging distorted thoughts, fostering supportive relationships, and integrating relaxation activities into their routine.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's anxiety and shame related to past behavior and fear of judgment. Agreed on building connections with supportive friends and incorporating guitar practice and journaling for stress and anxiety management. Encouraged gradual integration of these strategies to empower Zhang's emotional well-being.
