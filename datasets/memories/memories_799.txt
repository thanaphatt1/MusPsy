The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Q is navigating challenges related to job hunting, fearing potential failure and the possibility of disappointing their family. Xiao Q has yet to engage deeply with job interviews, creating anxiety over hypothetical scenarios.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Q has a strong interest in drawing, using it as a stress-relief method, and values familial support, particularly from a sister.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Q experiences anxiety primarily fueled by anticipatory fears concerning career prospects and family expectations. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Q is anxious about future job interviews, limited by negative assumptions despite lack of evidence for failure.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Xiao Q’s automatic negative thoughts hinder confidence but also notes Xiao Q’s willingness to adopt structured planning as a countermeasure.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Q should incorporate interview preparation into a daily journaling routine and regularly engage in drawing to balance stress.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to assist Xiao Q in managing anxiety related to job interviews by breaking tasks into smaller, manageable steps, and promoting balance through engaging in personal interests like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Q's realization of automatic negative thoughts impacting confidence in future job interviews and discussed strategies for preparation, including breaking tasks into manageable steps and integrating enjoyable activities like drawing to create balance. Xiao Q agreed to create a written plan and incorporate it into journaling, aiming to ease anxiety and make preparations more manageable. Emphasized the importance of maintaining optimism, family support, and continuing to reflect on thoughts to enhance emotional well-being.
