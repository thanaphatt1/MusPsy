The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaohau is a university student who has become increasingly anxious about her health following a past flu experience. She is feeling worried about current health, particularly following physical exertion activities, which she occasionally participates in at school events. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaohau finds solace in painting, a hobby that brings her calm, and she appreciates support from her family despite sometimes feeling misunderstood.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaohau experiences ongoing health-related anxiety triggered by her past illness, without an explicit diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaohau feels anxious and worried about her health, fearing that past illnesses might recur.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaohau’s heightened sensitivity to her health status post-flu, but also recognizes her openness to utilizing strategies for managing these anxieties.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaohau is advised to maintain a diary of symptoms and their accompanying thoughts, helping her to track and differentiate between anxiety-induced concerns and genuine health issues.
[Session Goal:] Develop Xiaohau's ability to manage health-related anxiety by identifying thought patterns and using positive affirmations to separate current health from past experiences.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Xiaohau expressed anxiety surrounding health concerns exacerbated by a recent sports event. The consultant advised Xiaohau to track symptoms in a diary to differentiate between anxiety-related feelings and actual health issues, and suggested reframing anxious thoughts about past illness instead of current health. Additionally, Xiaohau was encouraged to communicate openly with family and engage in calming hobbies like painting to balance self-care and support from loved ones.
