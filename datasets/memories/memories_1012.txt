The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Dandan is a university student who has been feeling lonely and disappointed due to feeling left out after the new semester started. Dandan has noticed changes in their friendship with L, affecting both their sleep and studies.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Dandan enjoys reading as a form of escape and relaxation, finding comfort in fiction and identifying with characters who navigate personal challenges.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Dandan experiences feelings of isolation and anxiety due to perceived social rejection and worries about being deliberately left out. No formal diagnosis is indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Dandan feels lonely and isolated due to changes in a close friendship.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Dandan's vulnerability and willingness to explore open communication to improve friendships.
[Counseling Assignments:] Dandan is advised to practice a conversation with L about their feelings and to try breathing exercises before bed.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Dandan to use CBT strategies to communicate openly about feelings and establish a bedtime routine to manage anxiety.
Counseling Summary:
Dandan expressed feelings of loneliness and disappointment due to feeling left out by a friend, L, which impacted her sleep and studies. They discussed approaching L to communicate feelings openly and positively, and Dandan planned to practice this conversation. To improve her sleep and manage anxiety, Dandan decided to try breathing exercises before bed and set small study goals to regain focus and confidence.
