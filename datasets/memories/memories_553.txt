The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Aji is a student who enjoys sharing thoughts and helping classmates during lessons. He experiences frustration when his enthusiasm is not well received by teachers, leading to feelings of embarrassment and misunderstanding.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Aji enjoys drawing as a creative outlet, which he finds calming and a form of self-expression outside the classroom setting.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Aji hasn't received a formal mental health diagnosis but demonstrates emotional distress related to communication and expression challenges in academic settings.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Aji feels upset and misunderstood due to being asked to stop talking in class, despite his intention to help.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes Aji's enthusiasm for learning and willingness to assist others, alongside feelings of misunderstanding and embarrassment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Aji is encouraged to observe and connect his classroom actions and feelings to better understand his experiences.
[Session Goal:] Help Aji use CBT strategies to understand the connection between his actions and feelings, aiming for a more balanced classroom participation.
Counseling Summary:
Aji feels frustrated and misunderstood when his enthusiasm for class participation is perceived negatively by his teacher, leading him to doubt his contributions. To address this, Aji plans to observe his actions and feelings, identify evidence challenging his belief that he's a distraction, and note any positive responses to his contributions. This approach aims to help Aji balance his enthusiasm with classroom norms, fostering self-understanding and confidence.
