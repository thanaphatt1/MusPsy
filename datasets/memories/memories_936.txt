The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoqi is a student experiencing anxiety and feelings of isolation, particularly within her living arrangements with roommates. She often perceives herself as the weakest among them and feels excluded from social activities. 

[Personal Characteristics]
She enjoys playing the piano, which occasionally serves as a creative outlet for her.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoqi shows signs of anxiety and social isolation, primarily triggered by interpersonal dynamics with her roommates. However, there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoqi feels anxious and lonely due to perceived exclusion by her roommates, impacting her sleep and daily mood.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoqi's tendency to internalize perceived exclusions as personal faults but acknowledges her reflection on these thoughts and openness to explore changes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoqi should jot down thoughts and feelings that arise around her roommates to identify patterns and recurring negative assumptions.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoqi recognize and challenge negative assumptions regarding her roommates through CBT techniques, fostering more balanced thinking patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoqi's feelings of loneliness and social anxiety due to perceived exclusion by roommates. Identified patterns of negative thinking and discussed strategies such as journaling and considering alternative explanations to improve emotional responses. Proposed practicing open communication with roommates to address feelings of being left out and increase understanding.
