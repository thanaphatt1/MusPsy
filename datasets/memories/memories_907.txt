The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user, a university student, has been feeling anxious and overwhelmed due to false rumors spread by classmates, making them feel isolated. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user has an affinity for drawing and uses it as a way to divert their mind from negativity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety and feelings of isolation due to social stressors stemming from false rumors; no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder was mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels anxious and overwhelmed because of false rumors and resultant social isolation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user is affected by perceived negative social interactions and is open to strategies that could alter their cognitive and emotional responses.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should jot down recurring thoughts or feelings as they arise and note situations triggering negative emotions.
[Session Goal:] Enable the user to connect their thoughts and emotions, recognizing patterns that contribute to stress and anxiety.
Counseling Summary:
Star discussed feeling anxious due to rumors spread by classmates, which led to isolation and overthinking. The consultant suggested recognizing the link between thoughts and emotions, identifying triggers, and using breathing exercises and walks as coping strategies, supported by reminders for practice. They agreed to tackle negative thinking patterns by focusing on these methods and reviewing progress in the next session.
