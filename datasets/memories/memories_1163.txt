The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student dealing with considerable stress, particularly regarding academics and fear of disappointing family, though the family has not communicated high expectations explicitly.

[Personal Characteristics]
Li enjoys painting and finds it to be a therapeutic outlet for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences stress and negative self-beliefs linked to academic performance, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li struggles with feelings of inadequacy, especially before exams and when assignments are returned, resulting in a reluctance to reach out to friends.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li's focus on negative assumptions and attempts to empower Li with curiosity and empathy towards their self-talk.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to explore opening up to a close friend about their experiences, note when these negative thoughts occur, and look for evidence that contradicts them while recognizing positive thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to help Li reframe negative self-beliefs, encourage social support exploration, and lay a foundation for more balanced thinking.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's negative self-assumptions related to academic challenges and their impact on social confidence. Encouraged Li to challenge these thoughts by confiding in a trusted friend, and to track both negative and positive thought patterns for greater balance. Set goal to identify supportive evidence and moments of more positive thinking to enhance self-perception.
