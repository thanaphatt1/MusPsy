The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jian is a university student who recently experienced a stressful incident during his birthday celebration, leading to conflict with school authorities. Jian values harmonious relationships and is concerned about being misunderstood by peers and teachers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing basketball, which serves as a stress-relief activity and a platform for social interactions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jian experiences stress and anxiety primarily due to social misunderstandings and fears of being misjudged, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jian feels stressed and upset over a misunderstanding stemming from a birthday celebration incident.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Jian's struggle with feeling rebellious and misunderstood but recognizes Jian's openness to resolving these misunderstandings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jian should try discussing his intentions with his director and classmates and set a regular schedule for playing basketball.
[Session Goal:] Assist Jian in managing stress by clarifying misunderstandings and integrating relaxation activities like basketball.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Jian's stress from a recent conflict over breaking school rules to celebrate his birthday; discussed feelings of rebellion and misunderstanding impacting social connections. Jian plans to clarify his intentions with the director and classmates to address misunderstandings and commit to playing basketball regularly as a stress-relief activity. Consultant reinforced the importance of gradual progress and encouraged Jian to reach out if overwhelming feelings persist.
