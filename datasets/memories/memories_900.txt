The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student experiencing significant stress from academic demands, particularly related to assignments and exams. Li struggles with maintaining self-esteem amid these pressures and has expressed concerns about self-worth when unable to meet academic expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys spending time with family, especially sharing coffee chats with their sister, and is interested in exploring creative hobbies such as pottery for relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences stress and self-doubt primarily due to academic pressures. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels overwhelmed and has low self-confidence due to academic pressures and a fear of falling behind.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Li's tendency towards self-criticism and a comparative mindset in their academic environment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is tasked with noting down stress-related thoughts and identifying patterns or triggers, particularly in group study settings.
[Session Goal:] Help Li recognize and challenge negative self-talk by identifying thought patterns and encouraging more balanced thinking.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Li discussed feeling overwhelmed and stressed by academic pressures, which impacted their self-esteem and confidence. The consultant suggested exercises for Li to jot down recurring thoughts, especially during moments of vulnerability, such as exhaustion or group study sessions, and challenged Li to reconstruct negative self-talk by considering their unique learning pace and strengths. The focus was on adopting a compassionate and balanced perspective through thought awareness and restructuring to foster self-acceptance and positive mindset shifts.
