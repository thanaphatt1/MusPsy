The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Feng is a student dealing with stress and guilt, recently having had a conflict with a classmate that led to a teacher's criticism. Feng worries about family disappointment and struggles with these events impacting their academic focus.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Feng enjoys playing basketball, an activity that serves as a mental escape and helps relieve stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Feng exhibits signs of anxiety related to interpersonal conflicts and academic stress, without a formal diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Feng feels down, guilty, and isolated, finding it hard to concentrate on studies post-conflict.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Feng's tendency to replay stressful events, which exacerbates feelings of failure and isolation, yet observes Feng's willingness to note and explore these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Feng is tasked with noting down thoughts and emotions when feeling low and considering engagement in relaxing activities like basketball as part of coping.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Feng recognize and modify thought patterns related to guilt and failure, and encourage engaging in stress-relief activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Feng's feelings of guilt and isolation following a fight with a classmate, focusing on the interplay of negative thought patterns and mood. Discussed strategies like tracking thoughts, engaging in enjoyable activities such as basketball, and considering open communication with parents to alleviate stress. Set goals to test these strategies and develop a balanced approach to managing emotions through mental and physical activities.
