The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a male in his late 30s who recently went through a divorce and is currently unemployed, relying on his parents for financial support, which makes him feel like a failure. His wife has moved to the US, leaving him feeling overwhelmed.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang used to enjoy walking in the park and visiting museums, which helped clear his mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang is experiencing feelings of being stuck and overwhelmed, with increased smoking and drinking habits as coping mechanisms. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels stuck, overwhelmed, and like a failure due to his unemployment and the divorce.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's lack of motivation and increased reliance on old habits but sees willingness to understand his emotional triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to journal specific thoughts that arise when he's alone or after family interactions, and he should revisit activities he used to enjoy once a week.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Wang identify emotional triggers, establish a routine, and engage in calming activities to break negative cycles.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's feelings of failure and lack of motivation due to divorce and unemployment; he tends to engage in unhealthy habits as coping mechanisms. Agreed on journaling thoughts to identify patterns and suggested incorporating calming activities like park visits to create a routine and provide emotional relief. Between sessions, Wang will focus on building gentle habits and continue reflecting on their impact on his mood and mindset.
