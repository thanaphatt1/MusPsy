The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student preparing for graduation and is currently experiencing stress due to unsuccessful job interviews, particularly influenced by feedback on his Cantonese accent. He values improving his interview skills and presenting his strengths effectively to potential employers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys playing basketball, using it as an activity to connect with friends and alleviate stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences stress and a lack of self-confidence primarily triggered by feedback from job interviews. There is no indication of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels stressed and insecure about his job prospects due to failed interviews, which have led to self-doubt and increased pressure from family expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang's tendency towards self-doubt and a focus on perceived weaknesses, yet identifies Zhang's willingness to explore strategies for improvement and self-assurance.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Zhang should practice articulating his unique strengths and consider seeking feedback from past interviewers to gain clarity on areas for improvement.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Zhang manage negative self-perceptions, boost confidence by identifying personal strengths, and encourage proactive steps towards skill enhancement.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's stress and self-doubt concerning job interviews, focusing on perceived language barriers and family expectations. Encouraged proactive steps, including requesting feedback from interviewers and highlighting strengths, along with engaging in stress-relieving activities like basketball. Zhang found empowerment through these strategies, leading to improved mood and readiness to face future challenges.
