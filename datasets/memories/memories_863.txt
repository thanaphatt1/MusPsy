The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a student who recently transferred to a science class, facing challenges in adjusting to new subjects like math and physics. Wang feels pressured by parental expectations and is struggling to balance academic demands with personal well-being.

[Personal Characteristics]
Wang enjoys reading and sketching, activities that provide relaxation amidst academic pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety and stress related to academic transitions and high expectations, but there is no formal mental health diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels overwhelmed and anxious due to the challenges of adjusting to a new academic setting and parental expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's anxiety stemming from self-imposed pressure and a sense of isolation from overextended studying, but notes Wang’s willingness to explore coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is encouraged to initiate self-reflection and notice recurring thoughts that exacerbate stress, particularly focusing on shifting perspective around academic challenges.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Wang manage stress, recognize and challenge negative thought patterns, and foster self-compassion.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's feelings of anxiety and isolation due to the transition to a science class and the pressure to meet parental expectations. Identified the challenges Wang faces with math and physics and the impact on emotional well-being and daily life. Discussed the importance of balancing study efforts with emotional health to reduce stress and improve adjustment.
