The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Long is a young adult dealing with anxiety and stress, particularly influenced by past acquaintances encouraging substance use. Long is committed to avoiding old habits and to establishing new, positive routines.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Long has expressed an interest in learning cooking as a potential new hobby to distract from stress and negative influences.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Long experiences anxiety and stress triggered by past relationships and substance-related pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Long is anxious and stressed due to pressure from past influences urging him toward substance use.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Long's willingness to explore cooking as a new hobby and his openness to engaging in a positive, creative activity as a form of distraction.
[Counseling Assignments:] Long is encouraged to explore cooking as a new hobby and to jot down any thoughts and feelings while cooking, aiming to redirect focus away from stressors.
[Session Goal:] Help Long establish a new routine that fosters distraction from negative influences using CBT to manage stress and build resilience.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Long's stress and anxiety due to pressure from old acquaintances urging him to use substances again. Agreed on engaging in cooking as a mindful, creative activity to focus on when overwhelmed, encouraging Long to share his cooking experiences and feelings with his mother to strengthen his support network. Highlighted importance of recognizing small victories in developing new habits and recommended journaling thoughts during the cooking process for emotional anchoring.
