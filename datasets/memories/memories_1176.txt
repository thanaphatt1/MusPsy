The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
E is a university student troubled by recent discussions among classmates regarding future job opportunities, which highlight the importance of connections and affluent backgrounds. E feels disadvantaged coming from a humble background and has expressed feeling overwhelmed and experiencing disrupted sleep due to stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
E enjoys coding and playing chess, which provide a sense of competence and relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
E is experiencing anxiety and feelings of hopelessness, primarily originating from perceived social and economic limitations, but there is no confirmed mental health diagnosis at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] E feels overwhelmed and hopeless, particularly due to discussions about future career prospects and their socioeconomic background.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes E's withdrawal from social interactions and diminished motivation towards studies, alongside significant stress from self-imposed limitations.
[Counseling Assignments:] E is tasked with noting specific situations that trigger negative thoughts and stress throughout the week.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help E identify and understand negative thought patterns and explore more adaptive perspectives on their socioeconomic background.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling sessions, E discussed feelings of anxiety and hopelessness regarding their future due to their humble background, which affected their motivation and social interactions. Through the sessions, E identified negative thought patterns and learned to challenge them by focusing on personal strengths and success stories of others in similar situations. E committed to setting goals to develop their coding skills and incorporate enjoyable activities like chess to manage stress, resulting in increased awareness and a more positive outlook on their abilities and future opportunities.
