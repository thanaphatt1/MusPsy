The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Zhang is a recent graduate navigating the challenges of job interviews and employment prospects. He has been experiencing anxiety due to repeated rejections and doubts about meeting job requirements.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys reading novels and sketching, finding solace and relaxation in these activities during stressful times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Zhang feels significant anxiety and self-doubt due to ongoing job search pressures and recent rejections but does not have a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Zhang feels anxious and lost after multiple unsuccessful job interviews, questioning his abilities and future prospects.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiao Zhang's growing insecurity following interview rejections but recognizes his willingness to explore activities that can reduce anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Zhang is encouraged to keep a journal of thoughts and feelings post-interview and to play the guitar daily for relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Xiao Zhang recognize and manage anxiety triggers, fostering resilience through hobbies and a healthy mindset.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Zhang's feelings of anxiety and insecurity due to repeated interview rejections, affecting daily life and confidence. Agreed on maintaining a journal and playing the guitar daily to manage stress and prepare for interviews positively. Discussed strategies for emphasizing personal achievements and developing a narrative for showcasing skills in future interviews.
