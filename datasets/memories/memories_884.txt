The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student faced with the dilemma of choosing between graduate school and starting a job to support family financially. Liu feels compelled by familial responsibilities while valuing further academic advancement for career opportunities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys reading mystery novels for relaxation, offering escapism from stress and making decisions. Liu expresses interest in painting, though he hasn't engaged with it recently.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu's stress and anxiety mainly stem from fear of failure and decision-making under pressure, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels stressed and confused about making decisions that balance personal aspirations and family financial needs.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liu's fear of making irreversible mistakes, influenced by a history of previous decision regrets.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should list pros and cons for each decision path and reach out to people who have navigated similar choices for broader perspectives.
[Session Goal:] Help Liu explore decision-making strategies using CBT techniques, addressing fears, and cultivating a balanced perspective.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu's stress and confusion around choosing between graduate school and starting a job, influenced by financial responsibilities and fear of past decision-making mistakes. Encouraged Liu to broaden their perspective by discussing experiences with others and journaling, while incorporating relaxation and self-care activities for stress management. Agreed on actionable tasks, such as listing pros and cons and speaking with a cousin, to gain clarity and reduce stress before the next session.
