The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Yang is a high school student dealing with substantial stress, particularly linked to adapting to online classes and meeting high parental expectations. Xiao Yang feels a strong pressure to succeed academically and fears disappointing their parents.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They enjoy reading and playing the piano, which can be used as relaxation activities to relieve stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Yang experiences stress and anxiety primarily triggered by academic pressures and parental expectations, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Yang feels stressed and anxious, struggling to concentrate during online classes and feeling the weight of parental expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Xiao Yang’s difficulty with concentration and the internalized pressure from parental expectations, but also sees openness to trying new strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Yang should keep a journal noting moments of concentration difficulties and related thoughts, and also try short invigorating activities during breaks to refresh focus.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Xiao Yang identify stressors and thought patterns, introducing activities that promote concentration and clarity.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Yang's stress and anxiety linked to online classes and parental expectations. Recommended keeping a journal to track concentration issues, noting both challenges and achievements, and suggested incorporating invigorating activities during breaks to combat fatigue. Emphasized the importance of recognizing positive moments to build confidence and provided ongoing support for gradual improvement.
