The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mui Ai is a high school student dealing with stress related to self-image issues, stemming from classmates’ comments about her appearance. She feels particularly self-conscious about her face shape and weight, impacting her social interactions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Mui Ai finds solace in drawing, using it occasionally as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mui Ai experiences notable anxiety and self-consciousness associated with negative self-image, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mui Ai is stressed and anxious, avoiding social situations for fear of judgment about her appearance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Mui Ai's vulnerability to negative comments, noting her tendency toward avoidance but also her openness to exercises focusing on self-appreciation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mui Ai is tasked with writing down three self-appreciative statements unrelated to appearance daily.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Mui Ai shift focus from external validation to intrinsic qualities and build self-esteem.
Counseling Summary:
Mui Ai discussed feelings of self-consciousness and anxiety over classmates' comments on her appearance, leading to social avoidance and stress. The consultant suggested an exercise for Mui Ai to write three non-appearance-related positive attributes about herself daily, encouraging the development of a more balanced self-view and openness with family for support. Mui Ai agreed to try these strategies, particularly speaking with her mother, to build a supportive network and gradually manage anxiety.
