The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Marilyn is a university student experiencing significant stress and feelings of inadequacy, particularly related to academic pressures and self-doubt. She has recently faced rejection from an academic project, which deeply impacted her motivation and confidence.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys painting, though hasn't engaged in it recently, and finds creative activities to be a potential source of relief and expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Marilyn is struggling with recurring negative thoughts and anxiety, likely exacerbated by academic setbacks, but there’s no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Marilyn feels down, unmotivated, and stuck, experiencing self-doubt and escaping into social drinking.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Marilyn's struggle with low self-esteem and avoidance behaviors, alongside her openness to trying small constructive steps.
[Counseling Assignments:] Marilyn should begin with simple actions like going for a walk daily, and start journaling thoughts to identify patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Marilyn manage negative thoughts, encourage small, positive daily actions, and explore constructive outlets like painting.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Marilyn's challenges with motivation and self-doubt related to academic pressures and feelings of inadequacy. Agreed on strategies to manage these emotions by setting small, achievable goals like walking, writing down thoughts, and engaging in creative activities such as painting. Emphasized the importance of patience, recognizing progress in small steps, and maintaining a balanced approach to improve emotional well-being.
