The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student currently feeling pressured about their chemistry studies, largely due to family expectations. They express a lack of passion for their major and a sense of uncertainty about their future.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys activities like sketching and cricket fighting, which serve as outlets for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences feelings of anxiety and being overwhelmed, stemming from academic and familial pressures, but no formal mental health diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels lost and pressured, struggling to find motivation in chemistry while dealing with family expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes the user's conflict between familial expectations and personal interests, noting the user's hesitance in sharing their feelings with others.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to engage in online forums or local groups related to cricket fighting and to set regular sketching times for personal relaxation.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help the user explore their passions, manage stress, and establish a routine integrating personal interests alongside academic responsibilities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's feelings of being overwhelmed by family career pressures and lack of passion for chemistry studies, alongside interests in cricket fighting. Encouraged finding community support by engaging in related activities and integrating sketching into the user's routine to offer relief and personal expression. Agreed on the small tasks of exploring online forums and setting a sketching schedule to foster connection and manage anxiety.
