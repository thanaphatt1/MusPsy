The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a student facing substantial stress related to upcoming exams and family expectations, having struggled with a recent poor performance in a test. Zhang places significant value on academic success and fears disappointing their parents.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys drawing, which serves as a creative outlet and stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety primarily linked to academic pressures, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious and pressured due to upcoming exams and the fear of not meeting parental expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's difficulty balancing parental expectations with personal stress and notes a pervasive sense of being overwhelmed by academic pressures.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is tasked with observing and noting down recurring negative thoughts and anxiety triggers to identify patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Zhang identify anxiety triggers, and unravel negative thought patterns related to academic performance and parental pressure.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Zhang discussed feeling stressed due to parental pressure to perform well in exams, leading to anxiety and difficulty in focusing. The consultant helped Zhang recognize the link between negative thoughts and anxiety and suggested observing these thoughts to identify patterns and recurring themes, aiming to challenge their logic and manage stress effectively.
