The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Doreen is a young adult who has been exhibiting some avoidance of social gatherings, leading to feelings of isolation and guilt. She enjoys spending time alone and has reflected on both the positive and potentially unhealthy aspects of this habit.

[Personal Characteristics]
Doreen has a strong interest in sketching, which she uses as a means of finding relaxation and emotional expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Doreen experiences social anxiety which contributes to her isolation and feelings of guilt about not participating in social events.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Doreen feels thoughtful yet stressed due to her absence from social events and the associated guilt of letting others down.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Doreen is insightful about her social avoidance and is open to exploring new ways to engage socially while valuing solitude.
[Counseling Assignments:] Doreen should consider arranging a small, low-pressure gathering with friends to ease into social interactions and reflect on her social experiences.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to aid Doreen in understanding her social anxiety, balancing solitude and social engagement, and gradually increasing social participation.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session focused on Doreen's struggle with social anxiety, balancing her desire for solitude with feelings of guilt about missing social events. She agreed to try attending smaller gatherings, like a coffee meetup with friends, as a step towards overcoming her avoidance and anxiety, while also planning regular sketching sessions for personal relaxation. Mindfulness and setting realistic expectations were encouraged as tools for managing anxiety and stress.
