The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoshuai is a young adult struggling with declining academic performance and frequent absenteeism. Recently, they've been spending much time with a new group of friends and indulging in online games, both of which have negatively impacted their focus.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoshuai used to prioritize studying and enjoy playing basketball, which he finds refreshing and a source of happiness.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoshuai exhibits signs of stress and confusion, driven by changes in social interactions and personal choices, although no formal mental health diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoshuai feels overwhelmed and lost due to academic underperformance and distraction from new friendships and gaming habits.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaoshuai’s awareness of their past positive routines and the impact of recent lifestyle changes on their well-being.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoshuai should reflect on positive past habits and consider what aspects might be reintroduced to improve their current situation.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoshuai recognize and reconnect with positive habits and reduce distractions, encouraging a more balanced lifestyle by identifying key influence areas.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoshuai's struggles with time management, the influence of new friendships, and increased gaming habits affecting his academic performance, alongside his stress. Encouraged Xiaoshuai to reflect on positive past routines, recognize defeatist thoughts, reach out to supportive friends for balance, and re-engage in activities like basketball for stress relief. Progressed through identifying negative thinking patterns, prioritizing connections with supportive friends, and strategizing realistic solutions to challenges.
