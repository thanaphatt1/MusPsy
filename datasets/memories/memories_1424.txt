The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Megan, a mother concerned about her son's safety, experiences anxiety triggered by her son James starting middle school and gaining more independence.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys reading and taking walks in the park as stress relief activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Megan experiences heightened anxiety, particularly related to her son’s newfound independence, with no formal diagnosis provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Megan feels anxious and preoccupied with worries about her son's safety and independence.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Megan's tendency to focus on worst-case scenarios and her overprotective instincts regarding her son's activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Megan is encouraged to reflect on past experiences where outcomes were better than feared and to identify recurring anxious thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT strategies to help Megan recognize and challenge her anxiety patterns and encourage her to engage in relaxing activities.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Megan's anxiety regarding her son's increased independence and how it impacts their relationship. Megan frequently checks on her son due to fears of potential dangers, which might be causing stress for both of them. Agreed on a task to reflect on past situations where outcomes were better than feared, to help manage these anxieties.
