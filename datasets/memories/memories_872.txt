The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a student nearing graduation, deeply stressed about socializing and fitting in due to a history of loneliness and strict parental rules, which has limited friendships especially with females.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang is primarily concerned about social success after graduation and shows interest in trying new hobbies, such as painting, as stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang exhibits symptoms of social anxiety, particularly marked by fear of judgment and an assumption that others are uninterested.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels severe anxiety about future social interactions, worrying intensely about fitting in and forming connections post-graduation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's pervasive fears concerning anticipated social integration challenges spurred by past experiences of isolation and judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] This session, Wang is encouraged to note impactful stress moments and explore reframing negative assumptions about social interactions.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help Wang identify and challenge automatic negative thoughts regarding social situations and build awareness of cognitive distortions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's social anxiety linked to future socializing post-graduation, focusing on confidence in group scenarios against fears of judgment. Discussed the potential impact of reframing negative thoughts into more positive interpretations, and set a small exercise to note and challenge those interpretations when social anxiety arises. Encouraged mindfulness in managing anxiety, with plans to revisit and build on these strategies in the next session.
