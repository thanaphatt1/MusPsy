The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Chao is a student dealing with stress from both academic criticism and lack of familial support. He reports feeling embarrassed and frustrated, particularly following incidents with his teacher and his father’s unsupportive comments. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing basketball, which occasionally serves as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Chao experiences stress and a sense of failure predominantly due to school and family pressures, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Chao feels stressed and upset, contemplating the decision to refrain from submitting homework due to fear of criticism.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiao Chao's sensitivity to criticism and his inclination to withdraw due to perceived inadequacies, yet also sees a willingness to try stress-relief strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Chao is encouraged to keep a journal reflecting on his feelings and experiences to better understand and articulate his emotions.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT techniques to help Xiao Chao explore stressors more constructively and nurture communication skills to facilitate healthier interactions with family and peers.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Chao's emotional response to criticism, focusing on feelings of embarrassment and frustration leading to avoidance of submitting homework. Discussed cognitive patterns of self-doubt and strategies for stress relief, encouraging journal keeping to identify positive aspects and opening communication with a trusted friend and his father for support. Highlighted the benefits of expressing emotions and understanding support systems as a means to navigate negative feelings.
