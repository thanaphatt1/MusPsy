The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a student who recently went through a difficult breakup with a close classmate named Li. This event has been quite challenging, impacting Zhang's emotional well-being and focus.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang used to find solace in writing poetry and has expressed interest in revisiting this hobby as a form of emotional expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences feelings of sadness and anxiety, exacerbated by self-blame and negative self-perception following the breakup.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang is feeling down and unfocused, often caught in cycles of self-blame and feelings of inadequacy.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang's tendency toward self-blame and negative assumptions about perceptions from others but also acknowledges Zhang's willingness to explore these feelings through writing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to journal thoughts and feelings as they arise to better address and process intense emotions.
[Session Goal:] Assist Zhang in recognizing and challenging self-critical thoughts through CBT strategies while fostering emotional expression through journaling.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's experience following a breakup, including feelings of self-blame and loneliness. Identified journaling and poetry as potential outlets for processing emotions and shifting negative thinking patterns. Set tasks to both note self-critical thoughts and engage in creative expression through poetry to support emotional well-being and clarity.
