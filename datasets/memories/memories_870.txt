The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student who has faced repeated failures in college entrance exams, leading to enrollment at a vocational school, which they find hard to accept. They express feelings of frustration and disappointment and are supported by their family, especially their brother.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user hasn't mentioned specific hobbies but seems open to exploring activities that could provide distraction from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user struggles with feelings of failure and anxiety related to their academic situation but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels frustrated and disappointed about their academic path and is worried about not meeting personal expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that the user is deeply affected by perceived failures but acknowledges the supportive family environment as a potential buffer.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is tasked with identifying and writing down specific instances where they feel pressure most intensely.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help the user recognize and manage stressors and to encourage awareness of supportive relational dynamics.
Counseling Summary:
Explored the user's disappointment and frustration over academic setbacks and adaptation challenges in vocational school, acknowledging declining academic performance and its impact on self-esteem and relationships. Identified anxiety around group projects and pervasive thoughts of failure defining the future, leading to withdrawal; discussed recognizing and questioning these thoughts, proposing to open up to supportive friends for perspective. Agreed on a task to list situations where negative thoughts occur and reactions to them for further exploration in managing self-perception and anxiety.
