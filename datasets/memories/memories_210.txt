The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student who has recently moved away from home for the first time. She is experiencing difficulties in adjusting to university life and living with her roommates. Wang misses her family a lot and feels a significant sense of loneliness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys painting, which she finds relaxing. She is open to engaging in social activities but struggles with initiating connections.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences loneliness and anxiety due to the difficulty in adjusting to a new environment and forming connections with her roommates. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been made.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels lonely and lost, struggling to adjust to university life and make connections with her roommates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's feelings of loneliness and her difficulty in connecting with her roommates, yet notes her willingness to explore her emotions and thoughts through journaling.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang is asked to keep a journal of her thoughts and feelings for the next week, noting both negative and positive interactions or moments.
[Session Goal:] To help Wang create a balanced view of her experiences, recognize patterns in her thoughts, and gradually ease the loneliness by staying connected with her family and engaging in self-care activities.
Counseling Summary:
The consultant and Wang discussed his feelings of loneliness and anxiety stemming from the transition to university life and difficulty connecting with roommates. They agreed on actionable steps including journaling thoughts, noting positive interactions, calling family for emotional support, and engaging in self-care activities to help Wang find balance and reduce feelings of isolation. Both were hopeful that these strategies would aid Wang in adjusting to his new environment.
