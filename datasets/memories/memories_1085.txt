The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
An is a university student experiencing significant stress and anxiety, especially heightened after a class discussion on mental health. An is concerned about balancing academic responsibilities and building social relationships in this new environment.

[Personal Characteristics]  
An used to enjoy jogging in the evenings, which was a source of stress relief in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
An exhibits signs of stress and anxiety, particularly influenced by academic pressures and social adaptation; there is no formal diagnosis of a mental disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] An feels overwhelmed and anxious about their mental health, studies, and social life due to the pressure of university life.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes An's increased irritability and withdrawal from social gatherings, but also An's openness to exploring reconnecting socially through activities like study groups.
[Counseling Assignments:] An should reach out to classmates to form a study group, combining social engagement with academic support, and start compiling stress-related thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help An manage stress and anxiety, encouraging engagement in social activities such as study groups and identifying stress triggers through thought monitoring.
Counseling Summary:
An discussed feeling anxious and overwhelmed due to pressures from university life, especially after a class discussion on mental health, leading to increased irritability and avoidance of social activities. Together with the consultant, An decided to join a study group as a manageable social activity to help alleviate stress and foster connections, while also agreeing to monitor stress levels and thought patterns for better anxiety management. The session emphasized gradual steps towards social engagement and self-awareness to enhance An's well-being.
