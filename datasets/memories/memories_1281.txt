The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wenwen is a student who experiences significant stress from upcoming exams, particularly in Chinese composition, which affects confidence and communication with friends. Wenwen has considered sharing stress with a supportive friend, Mei.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wenwen enjoys basketball, finding it an effective stress-reliever, and is open to peer support.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
While Wenwen experiences stress related to academic performance, especially in Chinese composition, there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wenwen feels stressed about Chinese composition performances, leading to self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Wenwen's self-awareness of academic stress and reliance on peers for support but acknowledges hesitation in sharing concerns due to self-doubt.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wenwen should observe stressful moments and recurring thoughts while considering discussing these experiences with Mei.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Wenwen to identify specific stress triggers, engage in supportive dialogues, and develop insight into managing stress through peer support and recognition of thought patterns.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wenwen's stress and self-doubt regarding her Chinese composition exams and its impact on her confidence and social interactions. Identified a pattern of negative thoughts triggered by criticism, and encouraged observing these thoughts without judgment. Suggested Wenwen talk to a supportive friend about her experiences to gain perspective and manage stress more effectively.
