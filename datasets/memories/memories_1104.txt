The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoqian is a young adult female who lives with her mother following her parents' divorce. She has been struggling with body image issues, which have also led to significant health concerns such as malnutrition and fainting spells. She is working toward adapting to her new living situation while managing these challenges.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaoqian enjoys painting, which previously served as a source of fulfillment but feels guilty spending time on it instead of focusing on fitness. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoqian experiences anxiety related to body image and has a tendency to engage in unhealthy behaviors like excessive dieting and exercise, but there is no formal diagnosis of an eating disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoqian feels anxious and struggles with feelings of worthlessness tied to her obsession with body weight and appearance.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoqian's internal conflict between harmful self-perceptions and unaddressed creativity, recognizing her receptiveness to positive reframing of self-view.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoqian should remind herself of positive thoughts from trusted friends and begin painting weekly to shift her focus away from body image concerns.
[Session Goal:] Support Xiaoqian in developing healthier self-perceptions and rekindle interest in painting to enhance self-worth beyond appearance.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoqian's struggle with body image issues following her parents' divorce, identifying her anxiety and negative self-perceptions as key concerns. Encouraged Xiaoqian to shift focus away from appearance and engage in painting as a form of self-expression, fostering a balanced self-view. Scheduled follow-up to assess the impact of incorporating creative activities and positive perspectives from supportive individuals into her routine.
