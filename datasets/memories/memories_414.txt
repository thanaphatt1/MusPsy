The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Abel is a university student feeling overwhelmed and anxious about his mother's worsening condition due to multiple sclerosis and the fear of being expelled from school.

[Personal Characteristics]
Abel has a passion for playing the guitar, which has been a source of stress relief in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Abel is experiencing significant anxiety and low self-esteem due to familial and academic pressures, but no formal mental health diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Abel feels anxious and overwhelmed by the pressures of managing his mother’s health and academic responsibilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Abel's struggle to balance responsibilities while maintaining self-esteem and encourages recognizing stress-triggering thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Abel should identify moments to pause and reset, focusing on stress-related thoughts that arise during challenging times.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Abel identify stress-related thoughts and establish moments of calm to manage anxiety more effectively.
Counseling Summary:
Abel is dealing with anxiety due to his mother's deteriorating health and fears about school expulsion, which has impacted his self-esteem and caused overwhelming thoughts. The consultant encouraged Abel to identify and reflect on stress-inducing thoughts and emotions, take short breaks for self-care amidst his busy schedule, and acknowledge small victories to build resilience. Abel is guided to practice mindfulness and document reflections to gain new perspectives and manage his anxiety.
