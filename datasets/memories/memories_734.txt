The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Tingxia is a university student feeling significant stress about writing her thesis and fulfilling school requirements, notably publishing a paper. She aspires to pursue a PhD, further intensifying her academic pressure. Tingxia admits to experiencing feelings of guilt over procrastination and struggles with maintaining focus, which also impacts her appetite and sleep patterns.

[Personal Characteristics]
Tingxia has a passion for painting, which allows her to unwind and serves as a creative escape from her academic pressures and stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Tingxia experiences stress and anxiety primarily triggered by academic demands and fear of disappointing herself and others. There is no formal diagnosis mentioned in the conversation.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Tingxia feels overwhelmed by the stress of writing her thesis, leading to procrastination and disruptions in sleep and appetite.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Tingxia's cycle of stress and procrastination and her probing into activities like painting to combat stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Tingxia should reflect on specific stressors related to her thesis and plan a specific time during the week for relaxation through painting.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Tingxia break down tasks, manage stress, and incorporate positive activities like painting for stress relief.
Counseling Summary:
During the session, Tingxia shared feelings of stress related to academic pressures, particularly concerning thesis writing and publishing requirements, which impacted her sleep and eating patterns. The consultant suggested strategies for managing stress, including identifying specific stressors, replacing escapist habits with enjoyable activities like painting, establishing a more structured daily routine, integrating breaks, and celebrating small achievements. Tingxia agreed to focus on these strategies, such as creating a flexible schedule and acknowledging her progress positively, to enhance her well-being before the next session.
