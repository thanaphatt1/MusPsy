The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaofan is navigating stress from parental expectations and missing paternal presence due to his busy schedule. Xiaofan desires parental approval and struggles to balance academic responsibilities.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaofan enjoys drawing, finding it a calming and enjoyable activity used as an occasional escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaofan experiences anxiety and stress predominantly stemming from familial pressures, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaofan feels overwhelmed by parental expectations and academic pressures, seeking approval from both parents but also desiring personal space.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaofan's internal conflict between academic pressure and personal relaxation, noting the potential of drawing as a helpful coping mechanism.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofan is encouraged to incorporate drawing into his weekly routine to balance relaxation with responsibilities, setting reminders to integrate this practice.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT techniques to help Xiaofan manage expectations and create a healthy balance between work and enjoyable activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaofan's feelings of stress and overwhelm due to parental expectations and school pressures. Identified drawing as a calming activity and developed a strategy to incorporate it regularly into Xiaofan's routine to reduce stress, by starting with short drawing sessions during study breaks and setting reminders. Agreed to assess the impact of this change in the next session.
