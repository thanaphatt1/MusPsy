The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background

[Personal Profile]  
L is a university student experiencing anxiety about school, stemming from the feeling of not belonging and fear of judgment by peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
L occasionally enjoys drawing, which provides relaxation and a mental break from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
L is dealing with anxiety linked to social interactions and academic pressures; no formal mental health diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] L feels anxious and nervous about returning to school due to fears of judgment and speaking in class.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes L's distress in social settings at school and the tendency to withdraw from participation due to fear of judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] L is encouraged to jot down thoughts and feelings related to school to help identify triggers and patterns affecting their anxiety.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to assist L in recognizing thought patterns contributing to anxiety and exploring ways to reframe these thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored L's anxiety about school, driven by feelings of not belonging and fear of judgment, leading to avoidance behaviors. Suggested L begin journaling thoughts and feelings related to school to gain insight into these challenges. Emphasis on understanding the impact of anxiety on daily experiences and fostering safe classroom participation.
