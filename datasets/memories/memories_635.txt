The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
B is a student passionate about the sea and marine engineering, currently grappling with pressure from parental expectations and following her own career aspirations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys hiking, particularly along coastal areas, as a form of relaxation and reflection.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
B experiences guilt and anxiety concerning career decisions, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] B feels guilty and anxious about her career choice, fearing parental disappointment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes B's struggle with wanting to follow her passion versus meeting familial expectations, but sees her readiness to open dialogues about her motivations.
[Counseling Assignments:] B should dedicate time to hiking as a stress-relief activity and prepare to communicate her career passion and reasoning to her parents.
[Session Goal:] Help B recognize cognitive distortions around guilt, encourage self-advocacy in her career discussion, and promote open communication with her parents.
Counseling Summary:
The dialogue explored B's struggle with managing parental expectations while pursuing their passion for the sea, focusing on the guilt and anxiety stemming from fears of disappointing their parents. B was encouraged to initiate an honest conversation with their parents to share their dedication and thoughtfully considered career choice, while recognizing that their doubts often stem from unrealistically dire scenarios rather than evidence. To manage stress, B was advised to integrate regular relaxing activities like hiking, and through the sessions, B has developed strategies to prioritize their happiness, maintain open communication, and advocate for their aspirations.
