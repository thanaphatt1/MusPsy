The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Miranda is a parent experiencing significant stress and anxiety, especially after a recent incident where a car almost hit her while she was pushing her daughter's stroller. She is shaken and frequently revisits what could have happened.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys painting, which she uses for relaxation, and is considering meditation to help manage her stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Miranda is experiencing anxiety primarily triggered by the recent frightening incident and a tendency to overestimate her responsibility in preventing negative events.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Miranda feels overwhelmed and anxious, replaying the recent incident and worrying about her perceived failure to prevent it.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Miranda's high self-expectation and guilt over situations out of her control, yet identifies her openness to exploring anxiety management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Miranda should maintain a journal to record recurring thoughts and identify triggers to understand her anxiety better.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Miranda manage anxiety, identify specific triggers, and challenge unrealistic expectations through structured journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Miranda's anxiety and emotional distress following a near-accident with her daughter's stroller, focusing on her sense of failure and constant replay of the event. Agreed on using a journal to track recurring thoughts and identify triggers as a strategy to manage anxiety, fostering a clearer understanding of her emotions. The session emphasized the importance of awareness and continuing discussions to aid in her healing journey.
