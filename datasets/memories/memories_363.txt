The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liming is a professional working at a company, expressing feelings of being overwhelmed by an increased workload and striving to meet expectations. Liming is concerned about balancing work commitments with personal well-being.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liming enjoys reading, an activity used to unwind and recharge, though finding time for it has been challenging due to work demands.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liming experiences stress and self-doubt driven by professional pressures and personal expectations, without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liming feels overwhelmed and stressed due to additional work responsibilities impacting energy levels and personal time.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liming's deep-seated self-doubt and worry about meeting expectations, yet notes a willingness to explore stress management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liming is encouraged to find a small window of time each day for relaxation activities, even if only for a few minutes, and to jot down stress-related thoughts for reflection.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Liming manage stress, challenge self-critical thoughts, and integrate relaxing activities into daily routines.
Counseling Summary:
In the session, Liming expressed feeling overwhelmed due to increased workload and stress at work, impacting personal time and energy levels. The consultant suggested finding small windows of time for relaxation and encouraged Liming to address stress-inducing thoughts by writing them down and reflecting on their validity. Liming was also advised to focus on positive achievements daily to balance perceptions and foster self-appreciation.
