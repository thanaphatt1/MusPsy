The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Bi is a university student navigating feelings of loss and isolation following the recent passing of their grandmother and a breakup. These events have significantly impacted Bi's emotional well-being and academic performance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Bi enjoys playing the guitar, which serves as a potential outlet for emotional expression and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Bi experiences feelings of insecurity, dependency on others for emotional support, and decreased motivation, although there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Bi feels lost, overwhelmed, and emotionally burdened due to recent personal losses and academic challenges.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Bi's vulnerability in navigating recent grief and personal setbacks but notes a willingness to explore emotions and consider personal coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Bi should document thoughts and feelings impacting mood and concentration and reintroduce guitar playing as a means to alleviate stress.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Bi understand emotional patterns and integrate enjoyable activities, like music, to improve emotional resilience.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Bi expressed feelings of being lost and overwhelmed following the recent loss of their grandmother and a breakup, which have negatively impacted their studies and self-perception. The consultant empathized with Bi’s situation and suggested writing down emotions to better understand mood patterns, while also encouraging relaxation through playing the guitar as a means to relieve stress. Bi agreed to try these approaches and will discuss their progress in the next session.
