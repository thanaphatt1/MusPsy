The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student who has reported stress and feelings of sadness, particularly after a series of conflicts with three roommates since the semester began. Zhang describes frustrations due to misunderstandings over dorm life and a denied request for a room change, leaving them feeling stuck and anxious.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys jogging, which serves as a stress-relief activity and promotes mental clarity during overwhelming periods.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences situational stress and anxiety linked to interpersonal conflicts in a shared living environment, without a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels isolated and anxious due to the ongoing tensions and conflicts with roommates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's feelings of frustration and being trapped in their current living situation, along with a tendency to avoid confrontation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to keep a journal of daily interactions and feelings to decipher patterns and dynamics of the conflicts.
[Session Goal:] Facilitate Zhang's understanding of conflict dynamics and reduce stress through CBT-based reflection and journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's stress from unresolved conflicts with roommates, which led to feelings of isolation and anxiety. He developed insights into his assumptions and began engaging in open communication with his roommates, improving his interpersonal dynamics. Emphasized the importance of regular physical activity, like jogging, to manage mood and anxiety, alongside maintaining constructive communication strategies.
