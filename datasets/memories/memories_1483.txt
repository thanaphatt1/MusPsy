The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student at an arts college, feeling significant pressure due to high personal standards and the responsibilities of being a class representative. She experiences overwhelming stress and anxiety, particularly about academic performances and family expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu enjoys drawing as a stress-relief activity and values spending time with her supportive friend, Sarah.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu struggles with anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic responsibilities and perceptions of family expectations, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels exhausted and disheartened, perceiving failures in meeting her high standards as personal defeats.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liu's tendency to internalize pressure and notice her openness to exploring stress triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should jot down moments of feeling particularly pressured to identify patterns and potential triggers.
[Session Goal:] Using CBT techniques, aim to identify stress triggers and encourage Liu to acknowledge the weight she carries in her self-imposed standards.
Counseling Summary:
The user struggles with meeting high standards and feels overwhelmed by responsibilities as a student and class representative. The consultant suggests jotting down moments of pressure to identify triggers and proposes writing thoughts down to manage anxiety and negative interpretations, encouraging the user to connect emotionally through mindfulness and checking in with themselves daily. The dialogue emphasizes small steps and emotional support systems to help the user manage stress and self-expectations.
