The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yaya is a student undergoing online classes from home, worried about falling behind in her studies. She finds it difficult to focus and motivate herself, which affects her sleep and leads to stress about potential academic decline.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys drawing and coloring which provide a calming effect, though recently she hasn't engaged much in these activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Currently, no formal mental health disorder is diagnosed, but Yaya experiences stress and concern about academic performance triggered by her study environment.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yaya feels anxious about lagging in her studies and has trouble concentrating and sleeping.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist believes that setting manageable study goals and a plan might help Yaya regain control, noting her eagerness to improve her study environment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yaya is encouraged to consider changes in her study routine or environment, such as a new study location, to help enhance focus.
[Session Goal:] Assist Yaya in managing her study environment and reducing stress by implementing cognitive strategies for better focus and motivation.
Counseling Summary:
Yaya expressed anxiety about falling behind in her studies due to a lack of focus and motivation while learning from home, leading to stress and negative self-talk. Together, she and the consultant worked on setting manageable study goals, using positive reminders, and rewarding herself to boost motivation and confidence. Additionally, integrating enjoyable activities such as art into her routine was discussed as a form of stress relief, helping her develop strategies to overcome negative thoughts and support her studies.
