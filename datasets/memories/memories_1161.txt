The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mr. Huang is a university student who is dealing with substantial stress related to his impending graduation and unmet tuition obligations, initially meant to be covered by a failed business investment. He comes from a rural background and is anxious about disappointing his family financially and academically.

[Personal Characteristics]
He is an art major and enjoys painting, a passion he has neglected due to academic pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mr. Huang experiences intense stress and anxiety prompted primarily by academic challenges and familial expectations, with no formal diagnosis mentioned in the consultations.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mr. Huang is overwhelmed and anxious about academic pressures and the fear of disappointing his family due to unpaid tuition fees.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Mr. Huang’s heightened stress levels due to academic failure and financial concerns, appreciating his willingness to explore stress management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mr. Huang is tasked with keeping track of moments when he feels most stressed and considering small breaks, like walking outdoors or carrying a sketchbook for drawing.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Mr. Huang to identify stress triggers and engage in small stress-relief activities, laying the groundwork for using CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
In the session, Mr. Huang shared his overwhelming stress due to academic pressures, financial concerns, and fear of disappointing his family. The counselor suggested practical steps, including tracking moments of stress and introducing activities like walking and doodling, to disrupt negative thought patterns and alleviate anxiety, with plans to review their effectiveness in a future meeting.
