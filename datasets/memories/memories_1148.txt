The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xu is a university student experiencing significant stress related to physical symptoms such as frequent urination and insomnia, which impact academic performance. Xu comes from a background where certain middle school habits have persisted, contributing to current anxiety levels.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xu finds solace in painting, using it as a temporary escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xu deals with anxiety symptoms centered around physical discomforts and academic pressures but hasn't received any formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xu feels stressed and overwhelmed due to physical symptoms like frequent urination and insomnia, which affect concentration and exacerbate exam-related anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xu's openness in discussing the impact of these symptoms and the willingness to explore journaling as a tool for identifying stress triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xu is encouraged to keep a simple journal documenting daily thoughts and feelings to help identify triggers and understand the relationship between thoughts and physical symptoms.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xu identify and challenge negative thought patterns, providing improved stress management techniques through activities like painting and journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xu's stress related to physical symptoms impacting academic focus, and discussed anxiety linked to past habits. Xu agreed to a journaling exercise to identify thought patterns and triggers, alongside painting for relief, aiming to gradually break the cycle of overwhelming fear and stress.
