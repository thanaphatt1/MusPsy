The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
C is a university student facing significant stress, especially around graduation and uncertainties regarding career direction. C is the only child and feels pressure from familial expectations, struggling with a lack of passion for their current field of study.

[Personal Characteristics]  
C enjoys photography, which they find to be a source of relaxation and joy.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
C experiences anxiety and stress primarily related to uncertain career prospects and familial expectations, yet there is no specific mention of a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] C feels overwhelmed by the imminent prospect of graduation, unsure of their future career path, and unenthusiastic about their field of study.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes C’s internal conflict between familial expectations and personal aspirations, noting C’s willingness to explore their thoughts and feelings further.
[Counseling Assignments:] C should jot down recurring thoughts and emotions that arise during the week to identify specific stressors and gain clarity on their mindset.
[Session Goal:] Assist C in uncovering underlying thoughts contributing to stress and exploring the potential of alternative career paths that align with personal interests.
Counseling Summary:
The dialogue explored C's overwhelming feelings about graduating and dissatisfaction with their current path, highlighting the pressure from familial expectations and reluctance towards their chosen field. C agreed to journaling as a method to identify recurring thoughts impacting their mindset, and considered photography as a potential alternative, proposing to dedicate time for skill development and networking in areas of interest. The session concluded with encouragement for C to embrace self-compassion and explore new possibilities, maintaining hope for future paths.
