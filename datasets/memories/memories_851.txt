The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Chen is a college student navigating a challenging transition into university life, feeling directionless and unmotivated. Gaming has become a predominant activity, affecting his relationships, despite having supportive parents.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys video games and has shown interest in possibly returning to reading science fiction and fantasy as a leisure activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Chen is experiencing feelings of anxiety and being overwhelmed, though there is no indication of a clinical diagnosis at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Chen feels lost, overwhelmed, and anxious, primarily due to a lack of motivation and clear goals.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Chen's struggle with adjusting to the college environment and self-imposed inactivity, yet recognizes a willingness to explore solutions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Chen should list his challenges and consider small actionable steps to address them, and attempt to balance gaming with studying by setting specific times.
[Session Goal:] Help Chen identify and address the root of his feelings of aimlessness through structured goal setting and time management strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Chen expressed feeling lost and unsure about his future, with the transition to college affecting his motivation and relationships due to excessive gaming. Together with the consultant, they identified the need for a structured routine to balance gaming with studies, including setting specific gaming times and checking in weekly to review progress, ensuring accountability and motivation through rewards for small achievements. The session concluded with a plan for Chen to write down his challenges, steps towards change, and celebrate successes, while being kind to himself during the process.
