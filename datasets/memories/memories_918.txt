The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yuan is a university student dealing with stress and feelings of isolation after a conflict with dorm mates became public, creating tension in their living environment. Yuan did not intend to partake in the situation and struggles with the aftermath and relationships with roommates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yuan enjoys singing, finding it a helpful stress-relief activity, and is considering joining a campus choir.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yuan experiences anxiety and isolation due to recent interpersonal conflicts but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yuan feels stressed, anxious, and isolated due to ongoing tension with dorm mates following the revelation of a dorm incident.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yuan's internalization of blame and a tendency to feel guilty, yet notes Yuan's willingness to engage in strategies for emotional understanding.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yuan is assigned to jot down thoughts and feelings throughout the day to identify emotional patterns and gain clarity.
[Session Goal:] Help Yuan use CBT techniques to recognize emotional patterns, reduce self-blame, and consider healthier viewpoints.
Counseling Summary:
Yuan expressed feelings of stress and isolation due to a tense situation with dorm mates following an unauthorized activity incident. Through counseling sessions, Yuan worked on identifying and challenging negative thought patterns, planned a conversation to clarify misunderstandings with dorm mates, and explored singing as a stress-relief activity. Ultimately, Yuan gained insights into emotional patterns, learned the importance of open communication, and developed strategies for managing anxiety and enhancing well-being.
