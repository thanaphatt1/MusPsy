The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university freshman experiencing anxiety and embarrassment following a public speaking incident at a school ceremony. He values peer perception and fears judgment from classmates since the event. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
He finds solace in writing poetry, a preferred activity when needing emotional expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang exhibits social anxiety stemming from perceived judgment and embarrassment, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels haunted by the memory of failing to answer a question during his speech, fearing it has damaged his reputation with classmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's tendency to replay negative scenarios and fears of judgment without evidence but notes his openness to identifying and addressing these anxious thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should write down recurring thoughts and feelings related to the incident for further exploration in therapy sessions.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT strategies to identify and challenge negative thoughts, promoting a more balanced perspective on social interactions.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang experienced anxiety after feeling embarrassed during a school ceremony, which led to assumptions that peers were judging him. Through counseling, he learned to challenge his negative thoughts, considered discussing the incident with the classmate involved to clarify misunderstandings, and embraced writing poetry as a therapeutic outlet. By the end of the sessions, Zhang felt more aware of his thought patterns and equipped with strategies to handle future challenges.
