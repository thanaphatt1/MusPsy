The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Feng is a student experiencing significant stress due to their father's recent diagnosis of cirrhosis, which has compounded existing financial strains. These events have led to a disrupted focus on studies and a heavy emotional burden.
  
[Personal Characteristics]  
Feng enjoys reading and walking, but currently, the stress often overshadows these activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Feng is experiencing anxiety and stress, exacerbated by family health and financial challenges, without mention of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Feng feels overwhelmed by the responsibility of managing family stressors and financial implications, alongside personal academic pressures.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Feng's sense of sole responsibility for family burdens and acknowledges the difficulty in finding space for personal relaxation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Feng is encouraged to take short daily breaks for reading or walking and to identify recurring stress-related thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Feng manage stress by incorporating relaxing activities and exploring thoughts that contribute to their heightened sense of responsibility.
Counseling Summary:
The dialogue explored Feng's overwhelming stress due to his father's cirrhosis and financial strain, affecting his studies and emotional health. The consultant suggested setting aside brief daily relaxation activities, like reading or walking, to alleviate stress, and recommended acknowledging and challenging recurring burdensome thoughts. Additionally, connecting with friends or siblings for support was encouraged, aiming to gradually ease Feng's anxiety and promote self-care during difficult times.
