The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student in their final year, preparing for graduate school exams. Recently, they initiated a breakup with their girlfriend, which has left them emotionally conflicted, though there is also a sense of relief.

[Personal Characteristics]  
They have a history of using writing, such as story creation during high school, as a method to process emotions and events.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences emotional difficulty post-breakup, alongside stress related to academic pressures, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels conflicted and emotionally strained following a breakup, impacting their focus on academic responsibilities.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's willingness to engage with feelings and reflect on past helpful strategies, such as writing.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to start journaling to capture thoughts and emotions, possibly integrating storytelling to process these experiences.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT techniques to help the user recognize and manage their recurring thoughts and emotions regarding the breakup while maintaining focus on academic duties.
Counseling Summary:
The user is experiencing emotional conflict and a lack of focus due to a recent breakup, impacting their studies. The consultant suggested journaling to explore thoughts and emotions, introduced story writing as an expressive tool, and encouraged establishing a comforting evening routine and gradually re-engaging with friends for support. Both agreed upon exploring these strategies and looked forward to discussing the user's progress in the next session.
