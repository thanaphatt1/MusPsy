The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Tong is a teenager facing significant stress due to bullying at school and conflict at home regarding their academic performance. Tong feels caught between these pressures and is looking for ways to escape from the stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Tong enjoys drawing, which provides relaxation and a momentary escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Tong experiences anxiety and self-blame, triggered by peer interactions and parental expectations. There is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Tong feels upset and anxious due to negative interactions with peers and parental arguments about grades.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Tong's tendency to internalize blame for external events and identifies openness to exploring these distressing thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Tong is encouraged to write down recurring thoughts and feelings throughout the day to better understand their experiences.
[Session Goal:] Explore CBT techniques to help Tong recognize and manage self-blaming thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session delved into Tong's experiences of distress at school and home, focusing on the impact of peer bullying and parental conflict on her emotions (sadness and anxiety). The consultant suggested journaling thoughts to uncover patterns of unrealistic self-blame, aiming to guide Tong toward recognizing and challenging these negative thought patterns. Tong agreed to continue this self-reflection exercise to aid in her emotional understanding and management.
