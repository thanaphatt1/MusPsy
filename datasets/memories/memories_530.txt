The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user, a young student, feels anxious due to perceived social rejection, experiencing loneliness and a desire to engage more positively with peers at school.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys drawing, which has proven to be an effective way of connecting with classmates when they show interest in the user's sketches.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
While not diagnosed with a mental health disorder, the user experiences social anxiety and low self-esteem, rooted in worries about others' perceptions and acceptance.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels sad and nervous due to negative perceptions about peer interactions, compounded by worrying about not being fun enough.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's heightened sensitivity to social scenarios and an openness to exploring positive interaction strategies through art.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is tasked to identify exceptions to negative thoughts by noting positive interactions and considering why people might enjoy their company.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help the user challenge negative beliefs and recognize positive social encounters, emphasizing art as a conversation starter.
Counseling Summary:
The consultant and Star discussed Star's tendency to worry about others' perceptions, which contributes to social anxiety. They identified positive interactions, like classmates showing interest in Star's drawings, as potential conversation starters. Star was encouraged to focus on positive moments and challenge negative assumptions, using art to facilitate social engagement.
