The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao G is a young individual experiencing emotional turmoil due to their parents' recent divorce, leading to feelings of loneliness and neglect as they navigate their family dynamics.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao G enjoys playing soccer, using it as a means to destress and find joy.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao G displays signs of emotional distress, primarily linked to family issues and a lack of perceived support from parents, yet no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao G feels upset and bored, struggling with feelings of loneliness and boredom due to parental divorce.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiao G's search for distraction through gaming as a coping mechanism and their potential openness to alternative coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao G is encouraged to maintain a diary to track emotions and note signs of parental care, aiding reflection and understanding of emotional triggers.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Xiao G to CBT strategies to manage emotional response, enhance family relationship insight, and initiate involvement in enjoyable activities like soccer.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao G's feelings of loneliness and being unnoticed after his parents' divorce, highlighting his coping mechanism of gaming as a distraction. Agreed on maintaining a diary to track both positive family interactions and challenging emotions, and suggested joining a soccer club as a small step towards building social connections and alleviating stress. Xiao G was receptive to trying these strategies, aiming for a more balanced view on his family dynamics and personal life.
