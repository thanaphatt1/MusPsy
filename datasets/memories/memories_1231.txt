The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
B is a university student experiencing difficulty concentrating in class and during study sessions, and often feels that their classmates might be plotting against them. They also have a strained relationship with their parents, particularly their father.

[Personal Characteristics]  
B enjoys reading and writing poetry, which provides them a sense of peace and a means to express emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
B experiences anxiety and feels unsettled due to perceived social dynamics and family tensions; however, no formal diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] B is feeling distracted, anxious, and conflicted, unable to enjoy activities that were previously valued.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist sees B struggling with anxiety tied to social perceptions, yet notes B's awareness and openness to exploring these issues.
[Counseling Assignments:] B should engage in poetry writing once a week and jot down any recurring negative thoughts for further reflection.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help B identify thought patterns contributing to anxiety and support the development of a constructive outlet through poetry.
Counseling Summary:
Explored B's feelings of anxiety and perceived social threat, recognizing lack of evidence and discussing the impact on focus and enjoyment. Emphasized the importance of reality-checking thoughts, considering open dialogue with peers for reassurance, and incorporating poetry as a therapeutic outlet for emotional expression. B plans to implement these strategies, including regular poetry time, to enhance confidence and well-being.
