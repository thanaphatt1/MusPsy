The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is experiencing significant emotional difficulties, feeling low energy and a lack of motivation, particularly struggling with loneliness after distancing from a close friend. They find it hard to concentrate at work and are disinterested in socializing. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user displays symptoms of depression and loneliness, affecting their emotional and social well-being but does not mention a formal diagnosis.

[Personal Characteristics]
The user has enjoyed reading and walking but has not engaged in these activities recently.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed and isolated, struggling with low energy and a lack of motivation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices the user's difficulty in coping with emotional distress and a tendency to withdraw from social interactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user should track their emotions and thoughts, especially moments of intensified loneliness, and try to engage in a relaxing activity like reading before bed.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to manage emotional distress by identifying and understanding the source of negative feelings and encouraging engagement in comforting activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored User's emotional struggles related to distancing from a close friend, impacting work and social interactions, particularly loneliness in the evenings. Encouraged tracking emotions, incorporating reading as a comforting evening routine, and journaling thoughts for clarity. Established a step-by-step approach to gradually engage in activities that offer comfort and distraction, while maintaining a gentle mindset toward healing.
