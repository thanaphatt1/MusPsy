The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
K is navigating the emotional upheaval of their parents' divorce and an argument with a close friend. K values friendships deeply and feels troubled by the distance that has developed in one of their significant relationships.

[Personal Characteristics]  
K finds solace in drawing, which has been a passion yet neglected due to recent stressors, but wishes to pick it back up as a form of expression and exploration of feelings.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
K struggles primarily with self-critical thoughts and feelings of loneliness, but there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] K feels overwhelmed by self-blame, loneliness, and the negative assumptions surrounding their friendships.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices K's tendency towards negative self-talk and unrealistic assumptions about their relationships yet appreciates their openness towards addressing these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] K should observe the thoughts that arise in socially triggering situations, identifying patterns or triggers, and consider rekindling their interest in drawing to help process emotions.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help K recognize and evaluate negative thought patterns, work on interpersonal communication, and encourage engaging in creative activities to explore feelings.
Counseling Summary:
In the dialogue, K recognized a pattern of negative self-blaming thoughts and explored triggers related to feeling unimportant in friendships. As part of their counseling strategy, the consultant encouraged K to reflect on these thoughts' realism, initiate a conversation with their friend for clarity, and re-engage with therapeutic hobbies like drawing. K expressed hopefulness in addressing their challenges and felt more confident in applying strategies discussed in their sessions.
