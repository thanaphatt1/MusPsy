The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaofu is a young student dealing with significant stress following their parents' recent divorce, requiring frequent moves between homes. Xiaofu has been feeling lonely and struggling with concentration on academics due to the lack of emotional support.

[Personal Characteristics]
Xiaofu enjoys drawing, which serves as a therapeutic activity, and has expressed interest in playing piano as well.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaofu experiences anxiety largely arising from familial changes, contributing to feelings of inadequacy and concern over academic performance. No formal mental health diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaofu is feeling overwhelmed and lost due to the changes in their family life and the emotional challenges associated with it.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaofu's challenges with adjusting to major life changes and notes their openness to trying new coping strategies, like journaling.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofu is encouraged to start journaling to track thoughts and feelings daily, helping to identify anxiety patterns.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Xiaofu gain awareness of negative thought patterns and develop coping strategies to handle stress more effectively.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaofu’s difficulties in balancing academics and emotional well-being following parental divorce, focusing on feelings of inadequacy and loneliness. Implemented journaling, goal setting, and engagement in hobbies to manage anxiety and negativity while cultivating a positive focus on personal progress. Encouraged ongoing use of these strategies to empower and support Xiaofu through challenging times.
