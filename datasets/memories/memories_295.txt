The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lu is a university student who feels overwhelmed by academic pressures and expectations, particularly about maintaining good performance in studies. Lu has a strong drive to excel academically, contributing to their feelings of stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lu enjoys playing video games with friends, which has been a stress-relief activity in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lu experiences feelings of being overwhelmed and stressed primarily due to academic pressures, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lu feels overwhelmed by academic pressures and harbors guilt over taking personal breaks from studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Lu's tendency toward self-criticism and guilt about taking breaks, but also observes a willingness to explore more balanced habits.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lu is advised to allocate guilt-free time for activities they enjoy, such as scheduling video game time, to facilitate relaxation and recharging.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Lu to CBT strategies to manage academic stress and encourage the practice of separating and scheduling enjoyable activities without guilt.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed Lu's overwhelming pressure from studies and the impact on their social life. Agreed on incorporating guilt-free breaks and reconnecting with hobbies, like gaming with friends, to manage stress and improve focus during study sessions. Will evaluate the effects of these changes on Lu's stress levels in the next session.
