The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaofei is a university student feeling overwhelmed and increasingly anxious due to mounting academic pressure and looming deadlines. Xiaofei frequently experiences a loop of negative thoughts, especially when assignments are incomplete.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Enjoys drawing as a means of relaxation and escape. Also finds relief in walking their dog.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaofei displays symptoms of anxiety exacerbated by schoolwork stress but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaofei feels anxious and trapped in negative thought cycles triggered by academic challenges.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies a tendency in Xiaofei to become overwhelmed by self-imposed pressures, yet notices a willingness to explore methods for stress reduction.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofei should track moments when they feel overwhelmed, aiming to identify patterns in their thinking, particularly around deadlines.
[Session Goal:] Introduce and apply CBT strategies to help Xiaofei recognize and manage anxiety-inducing thought patterns while engaging in stress-relief activities.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaofei's overwhelming anxiety stemming from school pressures and identified patterns of negative thinking that contribute to this stress. Agreed on strategies such as noting moments of overwhelm, considering alternative perspectives in stressful situations, and incorporating stress-relieving activities like drawing into their routine. Xiaofei found value in self-awareness and the importance of breaks, planning to continue realistic assessments and seeking support in the future.
