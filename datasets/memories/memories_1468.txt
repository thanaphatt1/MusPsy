The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Little Ke is a school-age child who has recently faced disciplinary issues for hitting a classmate, stemming from feelings of being teased and misunderstood. Little Ke experiences tension from parental comparison to an older sibling who excels academically.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Little Ke enjoys drawing, which serves as a potential outlet for emotional expression and relaxation amidst internal turmoil.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Little Ke seems to struggle with anger management and feelings of isolation, heightened by negative social interactions and family comparisons, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Little Ke feels upset and angry due to being teased at school and is struggling with feelings of loneliness and parental disappointment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Little Ke’s difficulty in managing anger and the negative impact of teasing and family comparisons, while noting a willingness to explore emotional triggers and communication strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Little Ke should write about incidents leading to anger for self-awareness and try expressing emotions through drawing as a calming activity.
[Session Goal:] Identify triggers of anger and encourage healthier emotional expression techniques to manage negative feelings and promote self-understanding.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Little Ke's experience with anger and feelings of isolation stemming from a school incident and family comparisons. Agreed on strategies like writing and drawing to express emotions and identify triggers, with a focus on healthier expression and reflection. Emphasized consistency and small steps for gradual improvement in emotional management.
