The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
H is a student who has recently been feeling irritable and upset, especially after being criticized by a teacher. H feels like their teachers and classmates don't like them and fears failing.

[Personal Characteristics]  
H is interested in reading and finds solace in getting lost in fictional worlds.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
H exhibits signs of anxiety and low self-esteem triggered by perceived criticism and social interactions, though no formal diagnosis is present.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] H is feeling irritable and anxious, struggling with feelings of failure and belief that others dislike them.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes H's awareness of emotional changes and openness to exploring their triggers and thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] H should begin a journaling exercise to track thoughts and feelings, aiming to identify patterns and triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help H observe thoughts objectively, reduce assumptions about others' perceptions, and explore feelings through writing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored H's feelings of irritability and perceived rejection after a teacher's criticism, which has led to strained interactions with peers and family. Suggested that H keep a journal to track thoughts and emotions to identify patterns, and encouraged open communication with parents to seek support. Emphasized the importance of gradual progress and self-compassion in navigating these emotional challenges.
