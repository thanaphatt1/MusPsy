The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaojun is a secondary school student experiencing feelings of loneliness and anxiety. Recently, Xiaojun was targeted by classmates in a prank involving dead cockroaches, which has negatively impacted Xiaojun's relations at school.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaojun enjoys playing the piano and drawing, both of which serve as potential outlets for emotional expression and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaojun exhibits signs of social anxiety and distress primarily due to negative peer interactions at school but has no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaojun feels isolated and anxious, believing that classmates are avoiding and possibly talking negatively about her.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaojun's tendency to interpret social cues negatively and withdraw from interactions, yet recognizes Xiaojun’s willingness to explore positive activities and connections.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaojun is encouraged to integrate music and drawing into daily routines and record thoughts that contribute to feelings of anxiety.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiaojun recognize and challenge negative thoughts, promote engagement in positive activities, and consider alternative explanations for classmates' behavior.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaojun expressed feelings of loneliness and anxiety following a prank by classmates, which led to isolation and negative assumptions about peers. Together, Xiaojun and the consultant explored these feelings and developed a plan to challenge automatic negative thoughts by engaging in enjoyable activities like music and maintaining a diary to track and balance perspectives. Additionally, Xiaojun was encouraged to reach out to a trusted teacher for support to alleviate feelings of isolation and enhance personal connections.
