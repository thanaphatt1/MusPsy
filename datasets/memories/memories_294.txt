The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Shi is a young aspiring fashion designer who recently experienced a setback by being rejected in a school fashion design competition. This has led Shi to question their passion and skills, amidst balancing personal aspirations with parental expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Shi is passionate about art and fashion design, with painting being an activity that brings relaxation and joy.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Shi experiences anxiety and self-doubt which are amplified by perceived expectations from family and past setbacks, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Shi feels disappointed and doubtful after the competition rejection, leading to questioning their passion for art.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Shi’s struggle with self-doubt and parental expectations but sees an openness to explore these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Shi is encouraged to keep a journal capturing thoughts and feelings about art and aspirations over the week.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT principles to identify and challenge negative thoughts, supporting Shi's self-belief and passion for art.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Shi's journey from feeling discouraged after a recent rejection in a fashion design competition to gradually rebuilding confidence through self-reflection, constructive feedback, and engaging with supportive peers. Agreed on implementing strategies like maintaining a journal and scheduling regular painting sessions to support creative expression and personal growth. Shi learned to value feedback, embrace imperfection, and maintain positivity, utilizing communication as a tool for overcoming challenges in pursuit of their artistic passion.
