The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a student who feels stressed and worried about being noticed negatively at school, especially after a reprimand from their teacher. This concern heightens their anxiety in classroom settings. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
They enjoy spending time with their dog and drawing, which are activities that help them calm down and relax.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety, particularly in educational settings, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels sad, confused, and embarrassed due to an incident in class, leading to increased anxiety about school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices the user's heightened self-awareness and the inclination to assume judgment from peers, indicating a need for strategies to build self-confidence.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, the user is to note positive moments during class and incorporate drawing and time with their dog into their routine for stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to address the user's anxiety by focusing on positive class experiences and integrating soothing activities into their daily routine.
Counseling Summary:
The session explored the user's social anxiety, highlighting the tension between their desire for connection and fear of judgment, especially following a classroom incident. The consultant suggested focusing on positive moments during class and engaging in calming activities like drawing and spending time with their dog to manage stress. The user agreed to try these strategies and practice self-kindness while tackling self-criticism.
