The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jin is a first-year university student experiencing challenges in transitioning from high school, where he was more outgoing. He feels disconnected and lonely, missing a sense of belonging in his new environment.

[Personal Characteristics]
He has interests in sports and the arts, which were significant parts of his life during high school but have not yet been pursued through university clubs.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jin feels disconnected and experiences loneliness due to struggles with making new connections at university, with no official diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jin feels disappointed and lonely, struggling to fit into university life and missing his high school social connections.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Jin's sense of missing out and self-pressure in making new friendships but notes his willingness to explore new social activities.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jin should observe moments of disconnection and note associated thoughts and emotions, along with researching and considering joining university clubs.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Jin manage feelings of disconnection, encourage exploration of interests in a new environment, and identify supportive social opportunities.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Jin discussed feelings of isolation and disappointment after starting university, missing the familiarity of high school social life. The consultant encouraged Jin to observe emotions of disconnection, consider joining university clubs to find a community, and reminded them to challenge negative thoughts. Jin agreed to explore club opportunities to ease the transition and committed to continuing this progress.
