The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is an adult who experiences anxiety triggered by unexpected events like the news of glass breakage, causing discomfort at home. He values safety and finds reassurance in logical thinking and support from loved ones.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhao enjoys jogging and drawing, activities previously used as relaxation outlets.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao struggles with anxiety and tends to catastrophize, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels anxious and unsettled, worrying constantly about unexpected breakages.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhao’s heightened state of anxiety linked to news events and sees potential in using cognitive strategies for managing fears.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao should keep a daily journal noting anxious thoughts and feelings to identify patterns.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Zhao understand and manage anxiety by identifying thought patterns and introducing relaxation techniques.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling sessions, Zhao explored his anxiety related to catastrophic thinking, particularly his fear of unpredictable glass breakages, and began to identify and challenge these thought patterns by evaluating their likelihood and discussing them with his wife. This process of self-reflection and open communication helped reduce his anxiety, and he also planned to re-engage in enjoyable activities like jogging for additional relaxation and positivity. Zhao progressed in building strategies to manage his anxiety, with intentions to continue questioning negative thoughts and maintaining supportive communication.
