The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xu is a university student struggling with overthinking and compulsive thoughts, primarily triggered by conflicts with dormmates. Xu recently moved to a new dormitory, and the situation there appears to exacerbate these thoughts. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xu finds comfort in writing poetry and short stories, using them as a distraction from overwhelming thoughts.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xu presents symptoms of anxiety heightened by interpersonal stress without a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xu is anxious and overwhelmed by persistent overthinking concerning their relationships with dormmates.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xu’s sensitivity to perceived criticism and isolation but recognizes Xu's willingness to explore thought management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xu should document the thoughts occurring throughout the week to identify patterns and potential triggers.
[Session Goal:] To help Xu recognize and manage overthinking patterns using CBT strategies and to introduce thought recording as a method for identifying triggers.
Counseling Summary:
Xu faced overthinking and anxiety due to roommate conflicts, which impacted his daily life and studies. He was encouraged to challenge his negative thoughts and communicate feelings openly with his roommates, leading to a positive conversation that relieved his anxiety. Xu found value in expressive writing as a stress management tool and plans to continue using these strategies for emotional self-regulation and clear communication.
