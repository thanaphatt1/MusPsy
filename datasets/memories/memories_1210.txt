The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student experiencing substantial stress from academic demands and ongoing disagreements in her relationship with her boyfriend. She recently expressed feeling overwhelmed and uncertain about her future.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys jogging and finds it mentally refreshing, serving as a potential stress-management activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li is experiencing anxiety and stress related to academic pressures and relationship challenges, though there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels overwhelmed and uncertain due to frequent disagreements with her boyfriend and academic stress.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li’s struggle with self-imposed pressure and conflict-management but recognizes her willingness to explore stress relief through activities like jogging.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should jot down recurring thoughts and feelings during stressful moments to uncover patterns in her emotional responses.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Li manage stress, recognize patterns in stressful thoughts, and explore stress-relief activities like jogging.
Counseling Summary:
Li discussed feeling overwhelmed due to disagreements with her boyfriend about future plans and stress from increasing school demands. The counselor encouraged Li to become more aware of self-critical thoughts and assumptions, suggesting she challenge these to improve her mood and stress levels. They also focused on setting realistic goals, like completing a chapter of readings, and applying past successful strategies in communication and task management to navigate current challenges.
