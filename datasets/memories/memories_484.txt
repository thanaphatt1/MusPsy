The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a professional dealing with significant anxiety related to his job, particularly regarding a complex case that challenges his professional abilities. He questions his career path and capabilities, which exacerbate self-doubt and stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei used to find running a helpful stress-relief activity, though he has not engaged in it recently due to his busy schedule.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei experiences anxiety and self-doubt, particularly triggered by work-related pressures, but no formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels anxious and overwhelmed by the complexity of his current work case, questioning his skills and career path.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang Wei's doubts, though he is open to observing and recording thought patterns for self-understanding and stress management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is encouraged to journal his thoughts related to his work case and identify moments of confidence, and incorporate short runs into his weekly routine.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to increase Zhang Wei's self-awareness of stress triggers, encourage engagement in physical activities like running, and develop strategies to manage self-doubt.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang Wei's professional anxiety and self-doubt linked to a complex work case, encouraging journaling to identify thoughts and triggers. Suggested reintroducing running as a stress-relief activity, aiming for achievable short runs twice a week, and continued journaling for confidence insights. Emphasized patience and self-reflection in managing self-doubt, preparing to review patterns and insights in the next session.
