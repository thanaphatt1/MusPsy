The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wen is a student experiencing stress due to academic underperformance and criticism from a teacher. Wen has expressed a fear of not meeting both personal and external expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wen enjoys reading, particularly fantasy novels, which serves as an escape and stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wen exhibits signs of stress and low self-esteem related to academic pressures and self-criticism, though there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wen feels stressed and overwhelmed, with a belief of not being good enough.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Wen's internalization of criticism and a cycle of negative self-talk but notes the willingness to explore and understand these patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wen is tasked to write down recurring negative thoughts and associated emotions to better understand them.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Wen identify negative thoughts and initiate self-awareness activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wen's self-esteem issues stemming from academic criticism and fears of judgment. Implemented strategies including cognitive restructuring of negative thoughts, engaging in trusted conversations, and scheduling enjoyable activities like reading to boost mood. Progress noted in Wen's increased awareness of thought patterns and readiness to use learned coping strategies for future challenges.
