The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He also enjoys reading novels and painting, which are sometimes used as stress-relief activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels stressed and anxious due to the transition to college and unclear academic goals coupled with parental expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Liu's heightened anxiety surrounding parental expectations and the struggle with managing overwhelming feelings but also sees willingness to explore stress management methods.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu should keep a simple journal tracking anxious and pressured moments and designate time each weekend for reading as a relaxation activity.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to identify specific stress-related triggers, encourage discussion about parental expectations, and promote engagement in relaxing activities.
Counseling Summary:
The session discussed the user's stress and anxiety due to academic pressures and parental expectations during college. They agreed on maintaining an emotion journal to identify stress triggers and challenging assumptions about parental disappointment, and decided on setting dedicated time for a relaxing activity, like reading, to manage stress better. The counselor encouraged the user to be mindful of their emotions and prioritize self-care on their journey to a more peaceful college experience.
