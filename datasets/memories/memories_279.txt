The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
J is a high school student struggling with peer acceptance and feeling embarrassed due to classmates' comments about socioeconomic differences. J finds solace in helping their mom at a dumpling stand and enjoys drawing during free time.

[Personal Characteristics]  
J is a reserved individual with a strong familial bond and values kindness, often displaying helpfulness and creativity through drawing and assisting at the dumpling stand.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
J experiences feelings of social anxiety and self-consciousness primarily triggered by peers' judgments but has not received a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] J feels down and embarrassed after classmates made fun of their lunch, leading to social avoidance and isolation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes J’s internalization of negative peer comments and recognizes J’s potential to focus on positive experiences for self-esteem enhancement.
[Counseling Assignments:] J is tasked with recalling positive moments, such as helping at the dumpling stand, to counter negative thoughts and nurture confidence.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help J challenge negative assumptions and build a more positive self-view through recalling affirmative experiences.
Counseling Summary:
During the session, J discussed feelings of embarrassment and isolation after being mocked by classmates about his meals, leading to avoidance behaviors. The consultant encouraged J to reflect on and draw confidence from positive experiences, like working at his mother's dumpling stand, to challenge negative assumptions about being judged at school. J was advised to gradually counter negative thoughts with positive evidence and continue reflecting on uplifting moments to improve self-esteem and social perception.
