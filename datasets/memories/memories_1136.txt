The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Bai is a student who tends to feel overwhelmed in social situations, often worried about peer perceptions and exhibiting avoidance behaviors.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Bai enjoys painting and reading, which serve as comforting and stress-relieving hobbies.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Bai experiences anxiety in social settings, characterized by feelings of isolation and self-doubt, though no formal mental health diagnosis has been reported.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Bai feels anxious and isolated due to perceptions of needing to change to fit in, particularly in social or unfamiliar group settings.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Bai's tendency to assume negative perceptions from others and recognizes Bai's willingness to challenge these automatic thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Bai should write down instances of automatic negative thoughts and document evidence supporting or contradicting them.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Bai identify and challenge negative thoughts, reduce social anxiety, and promote self-efficacy in peer interactions.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Bai expressed anxiety and feelings of alienation related to social situations and the fear of judgment, often assuming negative perceptions from others. The consultant suggested countering these automatic thoughts by documenting them, alongside evidence supporting or contradicting them, and reinforcing positive affirmations based on moments of feeling valued, like the praise from Bai's art professor. The session concluded with Bai agreeing to focus on positive experiences and emotions, aiming to foster a balanced self-view for better integration into social settings.
