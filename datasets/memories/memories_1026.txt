The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyang is a young adult, concerned about professional certifications and past academic challenges. He describes a recent failure in a cost evaluator certification exam which brought back memories of not performing well in college entrance exams.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyang is experiencing anxiety and low self-esteem, primarily triggered by academic and professional setbacks, but there is no formal diagnosis provided.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys playing the guitar, jogging, and reading novels, which serve as stress relief activities.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyang feels down and trapped in a cycle of negative thoughts due to recent exam failure, reminiscent of past academic disappointments.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoyang's tendency to fixate on past failures and self-imposed pressure, but also identifies his willingness to understand and manage these emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyang is tasked with journaling his low moments, noting triggers, emotional, and behavioral reactions for better insight.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Xiaoyang recognize patterns of negative thinking and emotional responses, starting with journaling as a tool for exploration and understanding.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling sessions with Xiaoyang focused on addressing his negative thought patterns stemming from past academic failures, improving his self-perception, and boosting his motivation. Together, they worked on cognitive restructuring techniques, setting realistic goals, and incorporating a balanced routine with enjoyable activities such as jogging, to support his mental health and study habits. Xiaoyang gained insights into challenging negative beliefs, organizing his study plans, and maintaining a healthy balance between work and leisure, which helped him build confidence and resilience for future challenges.
