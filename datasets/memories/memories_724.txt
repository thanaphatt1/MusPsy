The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu is a university student facing significant stress, particularly about upcoming exams and meeting family expectations, having grown up in a rural setting. Liu values academic achievement deeply and is concerned about disappointing their family. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu enjoys painting, which is sometimes used as a stress-relief activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu experiences anxiety and stress primarily triggered by academic pressures, yet there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu feels down and tired, facing increased anxiety due to difficulties in college, particularly with peer interactions and self-consciousness about appearance and accent.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Liu's struggle with self-esteem issues and adapting to new environments, while also noting Liu's readiness to explore their emotions and stressors.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu is encouraged to take note of situations or thoughts that elevate anxiety for identifying patterns and to keep track of these moments for further discussion.
[Session Goal:] Begin incorporating CBT to help Liu identify patterns of stressors and manage feelings of anxiety related to new social interactions and self-perception.
Counseling Summary:
The consultant worked with Liu to address feelings of anxiety and isolation triggered by social interactions in college, encouraging Liu to challenge negative assumptions and engage in open conversations with classmates. Liu learned to express feelings openly, practiced mindfulness, and planned to incorporate enjoyable activities like reading into the routine, significantly reducing stress and improving self-confidence. The sessions concluded with Liu gaining strategies for navigating challenges and enhancing well-being, leaving equipped to face future situations with increased resilience.
