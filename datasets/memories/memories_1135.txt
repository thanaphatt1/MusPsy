The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a new university student navigating feelings of anxiety and displacement due to the transition into university life and diverse social dynamics. Yang feels homesick and struggles with a perceived cultural and economic gap with peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yang used to practice calisthenics back home, which provided relaxation and a sense of discipline, and is considering incorporating it into their current routine to manage stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang is experiencing anxiety and homesickness, primarily triggered by the transition to a new environment and social adjustments at university, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder indicated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels down, anxious, and out of place among roommates due to socioeconomic and cultural differences.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Yang's homesickness and feelings of isolation, coupled with a reluctance to engage socially due to fear of judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is tasked with trying to initiate casual conversations with roommates to discover common interests, aiming for small steps towards social engagement.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Yang to use CBT strategies to manage anxiety and develop self-awareness, facilitating social integration and reducing feelings of isolation.
Counseling Summary:
Yang discussed feelings of cultural and economic isolation after starting university, which contributed to his anxiety about interacting with his more affluent roommates. He initiated a conversation with them, which went well, and now plans to continue challenging his assumptions by observing and questioning anxious thoughts to foster more balanced perspectives. The focus is on gradual self-awareness and engagement to ease his transition.
