The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yueyue is a kindergarten student experiencing significant separation anxiety, particularly when parting from her mother each morning. She is afraid and feels alone but finds some comfort during playtime.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yueyue enjoys drawing and finds comfort in her toy bunny, often using it for self-soothing and imaginative play.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yueyue shows signs of separation anxiety typical for her age but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yueyue feels upset and anxious due to the challenges of starting kindergarten, primarily due to separation from her mother.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yueyue's strong attachment to her mother and her fear of the new environment but notes her potential openness to art as a calming activity.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yueyue and her mother should create a goodbye routine to help ease her morning anxiety at kindergarten.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Yueyue manage separation anxiety by creating routines and engaging in comforting activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yueyue's separation anxiety related to starting kindergarten (fear of being alone vs. comfort during playtime). Suggested creating a goodbye routine with her mom to ease anxiety about separation. Aimed to improve Yueyue's comfort at school through this new strategy.
