The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yunyun is a recent university graduate who returned home to work at a family-run ad company, feeling isolated from friends and uncertain about career prospects. She values independence and seeks personal fulfillment beyond familial expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yunyun has a strong interest in creative outlets like sketching, which she finds calming but hasn’t engaged in recently.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yunyun is experiencing symptoms of loneliness and dissatisfaction, largely stemming from the transition back home and the imposed career direction, though there is no formal diagnosis stated.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yunyun feels lonely and disconnected after relocating home for a family job, causing confusion about her future.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yunyun's internal conflict over imposed decisions versus personal desires and her openness to exploring these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yunyun is encouraged to identify specific moments that trigger her feelings of loneliness and pay attention to thoughts that accompany these moments.
[Session Goal:] Help Yunyun recognize and articulate her feelings of isolation, and begin using CBT techniques to explore the thoughts causing emotional distress.

## Brief Summary
Yunyun discussed her loneliness and uncertainty about the future after returning home for a job and losing touch with friends, and she is tasked with observing triggers of these feelings.
Counseling Summary:
Yunyun has been struggling with feelings of isolation and career uncertainty after moving home due to her mom's insistence to work in the family business. Throughout the sessions, she explored her conflicting thoughts about this decision and learned to express her feelings to her mom, as well as to re-engage with hobbies like sketching to help manage stress. She has also identified strategies such as open communication and participating in enjoyable activities to better navigate future challenges.
