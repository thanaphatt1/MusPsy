The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lili is a university student feeling distressed about social interactions, particularly dealing with rumors and misunderstandings with peers. Lili values peace and understanding in social circles and experiences anxiety related to conflict.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys drawing, which serves as a stress-relief and distraction from daily pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lili experiences anxiety stemming from social conflict and misunderstandings, though there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lili feels anxious and overwhelmed due to conflicts and rumors involving classmates, leading to feelings of isolation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Lili's difficulty in coping with social scrutiny and her potential openness to using creative outlets like drawing for stress relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lili should write down her feelings regarding the situation to process emotions and consider reducing social media usage to lessen anxiety.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Lili manage anxiety linked to social interactions, encourage relaxation activities like drawing, and consider positive coping strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Lili's anxiety stemming from a conflict and subsequent rumors, highlighting her feeling of being scrutinized. Encouraged her to take breaks from social media, engage in calming activities like reading and drawing, and record her emotions to help manage stress. Agreed to discuss her experiences with these strategies in the next session to gain further insights.
