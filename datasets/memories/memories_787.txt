The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Chen is a college student going through stress and anxiety, primarily due to roommate issues with Zhang and academic pressures. His living situation has been tense, affecting his sleep and study routines, notably impacting his performance on exams necessary for graduation. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Chen enjoys drawing and sketching, which he uses as a creative outlet amidst stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Chen experiences anxiety exacerbated by environmental stressors and academic worries but does not have a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Chen feels anxious and frustrated, particularly due to roommate disturbances affecting his sleep and studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Chen's struggle with self-blame over the situation and anxiety regarding academic outcomes, yet identifies his insight into facing challenges positively.
[Counseling Assignments:] Chen is encouraged to journal his stressful thoughts and feelings, identifying triggers, and contemplate effective communication strategies with Zhang.
[Session Goal:] Help Chen explore CBT strategies to manage anxiety, establish boundaries, and engage in relaxing activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Chen's stress and anxiety related to roommate issues and academic pressures, which negatively impacted his sleep and academic performance. Chen agreed to journal his thoughts and emotions to identify stress triggers and explore effective communication strategies with his roommate. Additionally, Chen committed to regularly engaging in drawing as a relaxation activity to improve his mood and stress management.
