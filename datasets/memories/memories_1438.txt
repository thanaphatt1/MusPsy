The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Brian is a professional dealing with significant stress concerning an upcoming presentation at a sales meeting, having expressed anxiety around public speaking and performance pressure. He is determined to overcome this challenge and improve his skills.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Brian values preparation and seeks constructive feedback to enhance his presentation abilities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Brian exhibits anxiety primarily related to public speaking and possibly generalized performance anxiety, though a formal mental health diagnosis has not been made.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Brian is highly stressed and anxious due to the upcoming sales presentation and worries about making mistakes.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Brian's difficulties with negative anticipation related to public speaking, yet notes his willingness to explore strategies for anxiety management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Brian is tasked with maintaining a journal of thoughts and emotions about the presentation to identify anxiety triggers and thought patterns.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Brian manage public speaking anxiety, challenge negative assumptions, and break down the presentation into manageable steps.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Brian's stress about public speaking, focusing on fears of making mistakes and forgetting his content. Agreed to use journaling to identify anxiety triggers, while emphasizing breaking the presentation into manageable steps, drawing on past experiences with similar stress management. Encouraged clarity in communication to reduce anxiety, with plans to review insights in the next session.
