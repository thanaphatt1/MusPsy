The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a new university student dealing with separation from their parents and the transition to university life. Zhao feels a profound sense of homesickness and the challenge of adjusting to a new environment. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhao enjoys playing the piano, which has been a source of comfort in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao experiences significant homesickness and adjustment anxiety due to being away from home for the first time, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels overwhelmed and struggling with homesickness after starting university.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhao's reliance on a new relationship as a temporary comfort and acknowledges Zhao's willingness to explore new routines for better adjustment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao is encouraged to plan engaging post-class activities, like studying with friends or joining campus activities, to counteract solitude and enhance belonging.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhao identify routine changes to manage homesickness and establish new social connections at university.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhao's feelings of homesickness and separation anxiety after starting university, including using a new relationship for comfort. Discussed engaging in campus activities and regular communication with family to mitigate feelings of isolation and encouraged small social interactions to gradually improve social connections and manage loneliness. Agreed to develop coping strategies for moments of intense homesickness in upcoming sessions while Zhao works on implementing suggested activities and interaction goals.
