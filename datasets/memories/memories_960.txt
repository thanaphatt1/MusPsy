The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student facing challenges adapting to dorm life, including issues with roommates, which have affected their sleep and feelings of inclusion. Wang values harmonious relationships and seeks to connect better within their living environment.

[Personal Characteristics]
Wang enjoys reading fiction and taking walks in nature, which are employed as stress-relief activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences feelings of isolation and anxiety triggered by roommate interactions but has not been diagnosed with any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels isolated and anxious due to roommate conflicts, struggling with dorm life adaptation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Wang's feelings of frustration and isolation but notes Wang's willingness to explore avenues for connection and engagement.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should attempt to initiate casual interactions with roommates, such as inviting them for a meal or suggesting a game night.
[Session Goal:] Encourage Wang to engage in social activities with roommates and adopt a calming pre-sleep routine to improve sleep quality.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's challenges with dorm life adjustments, specifically feelings of isolation and sleep disturbances due to roommates' activities. Agreed on initiating casual social interactions, such as a meal or game night, to address isolation, along with reinstating a calming herbal tea ritual for better sleep. Encouraged tracking these changes for further evaluation in upcoming sessions.
