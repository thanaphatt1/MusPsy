The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Cheng is a student transitioning from a college course to a bachelor's degree, which involves a heavy focus on upcoming exams. Cheng frequently experiences significant pressure due to high parental expectations and personal academic goals.

[Personal Characteristics]
Cheng enjoys playing guitar and drawing, both of which serve as potential stress-relief activities.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Cheng exhibits signs of anxiety and stress primarily due to academic pressures and familial expectations, but there has been no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder described.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Cheng feels stressed and overwhelmed by the impending exams and the pressure to not disappoint their family.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Cheng's anxiety, self-imposed high standards, and the pressure from parental expectations, while recognizing Cheng's resilience and openness to exploring effective stress management.
[Counseling Assignments:] Cheng should note moments of stress and the thoughts that accompany them, along with listing past achievements that they are proud of to build self-confidence.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Cheng manage stress, explore the relationship between thoughts, emotions, and behaviors, and encourage a shift towards self-compassion and recognizing personal resilience.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Cheng's academic stress and familial expectations, impacting focus and sleep. Cheng agreed to note stress triggers and list past achievements, harnessing resilience and self-compassion for pressure management. Encouraged reflection on strengths and gradual progress for confidence building.
