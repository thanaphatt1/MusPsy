The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
K is an individual experiencing difficulties with interpersonal relationships, specifically with roommates. These conflicts primarily arise from misunderstandings and differing expectations of shared responsibilities. K uses video games as a temporary relief from stress.

[Personal Characteristics]
K enjoys immersive experiences such as role-playing games which serve as both a relaxation tool and a form of escapism.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
K has not had a formal mental health diagnosis but experiences significant stress and frustration linked to interpersonal conflicts.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] K is frustrated and upset due to conflicts with roommates about shared responsibilities and accusations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes K's sense of being misunderstood and the stress stemming from unresolved conflicts with their roommates.
[Counseling Assignments:] K should note down specific moments when emotions are heightened and track recurring thoughts that could contribute to these feelings.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help K identify stress-inducing thoughts, examine their validity, and explore alternative perspectives.
Counseling Summary:
K struggled with stress from conflicts with roommates, leading to feelings of anger and isolation. Through counseling, K learned to identify and challenge negative thoughts, ultimately deciding to have open conversations with roommates to address misunderstandings, and incorporated regular gaming as a stress-relief activity. The sessions concluded with K feeling better equipped to manage conflict and stress through communication and balanced perspectives.
