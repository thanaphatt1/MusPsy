The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jiang recently transferred to a new school following a breakup, feels misplaced and disconnected, and is unsure about fitting into the new environment. These feelings have affected his emotions and daily focus.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He used to enjoy playing badminton, a hobby he finds joyful and fulfilling.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jiang experiences anxiety triggered by social transitions and new environments but shows no indication of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jiang feels down, lost, and anxious in his new school setting.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Jiang's struggle with feeling out of place and his desire to regain a sense of belonging.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jiang should observe and note situations that provoke anxiety for further exploration in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Address Jiang's anxiety using CBT strategies by identifying situations causing discomfort, promoting gradual exposure to social settings, and encouraging engagement in enjoyable activities.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Jiang's feelings of disconnection and anxiety following a school transfer, focusing on challenging negative assumptions about fitting in and encouraging him to reach out socially. Agreed on actionable steps like engaging with classmates and reconnecting with past interests, such as playing badminton, to improve his mood and social connections. Jiang developed skills to manage similar future challenges by seeking evidence against negative thoughts and actively forming connections, concluding the sessions feeling better equipped to manage his feelings independently.
