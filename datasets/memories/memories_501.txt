The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoc is a university student dealing with intense academic pressure and a lack of emotional support at home, particularly from a strict father. Xiaoc has assumed responsibilities as the eldest child but feels isolated.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaoc finds enjoyment in activities like drawing, which serves as a momentary escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoc experiences anxiety and feelings of inadequacy reinforced by family and academic expectations, but no formal mental health disorder has been diagnosed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoc feels anxious and isolated due to the lack of emotional support and school pressure.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaoc's eagerness to find small activities that promote self-care and provide a brief relief from stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoc should dedicate time to a small enjoyable activity like drawing to foster self-care and reflection on self-worth.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT to help Xiaoc establish a balance between academic responsibilities and relaxation activities to mitigate stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoc's challenges with school pressure and lack of emotional support at home, focusing on identifying negative thought patterns and reinforcing positive self-talk. Encouraged Xiaoc to express needs to family and engage regularly in stress-relieving activities like painting. Highlighted progress in self-awareness and practical strategies to manage stress and anxiety.
