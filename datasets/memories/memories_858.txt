The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student who recently experienced emotional distress after seeing her ex-boyfriend with another classmate, impacting her view of relationships. Zhang is currently feeling isolated and struggling to focus on her studies.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang frequently experiences feelings of inadequacy and betrayal, leading to negative assumptions about her self-worth and future relationships. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys reading, which serves as an escape and relaxation technique.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels down and isolated due to perceived betrayal by her ex-boyfriend, negatively impacting her view on relationships.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's struggle with self-worth and isolation but also notes her willingness to explore her feelings and thought patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang is encouraged to observe specific situations that intensify her feelings and note recurring thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to help Zhang identify and challenge negative thoughts, fostering a more balanced perspective on her self-worth and relationships.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's feelings of betrayal and negative assumptions about self-worth in relationships after seeing her ex with another classmate. Facilitated Zhang's recognition and challenge of negative thoughts, promoting a balanced perspective and empowerment, and identified reading as a positive activity to enhance well-being. Encouraged ongoing use of strategies to maintain self-worth and pursue activities that bring joy, with an open invitation for future support if needed.
