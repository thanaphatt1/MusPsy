The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaozhuang is a university student experiencing significant stress related to parental expectations and recent academic performance, amid a strict upbringing that prioritizes high grades. Xiaozhuang values their parents' approval deeply and feels anxiety over not meeting familial expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaozhuang enjoys playing guitar and basketball, both of which provide relaxation and enjoyment, although academic pressures have recently sidelined these hobbies.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaozhuang exhibits symptoms of anxiety, primarily triggered by academic performance pressures, but there is no formal diagnosis for a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaozhuang feels overwhelmed and stressed due to recent academic underperformance and resultant parental displeasure.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes Xiaozhuang's significant worry over disappointing parents and a tendency to focus on negative outcomes while showing willingness to explore stress-related thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaozhuang is encouraged to jot down recurring stress-related thoughts to facilitate further exploration in future sessions.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to assist Xiaozhuang in identifying specific stress-related thoughts and begin the process of managing overwhelming stress by exploring thoughts and perceptions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaozhuang's stress and anxiety related to academic performance and parental expectations. Encouraged recognition of negative thought patterns and reframing them to focus on efforts and positive qualities; facilitated communication with parents about personal achievements. Established strategies for managing stress, such as open communication and engaging in positive activities, with an emphasis on using these skills for future challenges.
