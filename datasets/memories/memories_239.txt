The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaomo is a university student feeling overwhelmed by schoolwork. Recently, they have been experiencing distressing nightmares about failing exams and having difficulty concentrating during classes. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaomo used to enjoy drawing, which serves as a relaxing activity, and has a supportive family that values effort as much as results.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaomo experiences stress and anxiety due to academic pressures, with self-doubt and fears of failing prevalent in their thoughts, but a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is not present.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaomo feels overwhelmed by schoolwork and experiences nightmares about failing and getting lost, which affects their concentration.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaomo’s anxiety about academic performance and identifies a potential link with self-imposed pressure and family expectations.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaomo is asked to jot down when they feel most stressed and what they were doing prior, to help identify stressors.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiaomo identify specific stress-related triggers and start recognizing and managing negative thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaomo has been struggling with stress and nightmares related to schoolwork and exams. Through counseling, Xiaomo learned to identify stress triggers, challenge negative thoughts, and implement practical strategies like breaking tasks into smaller parts and using drawing as a reward during study breaks. These approaches helped Xiaomo gain better control over stress and feel more prepared for future academic challenges.
