The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Jun is a student experiencing significant stress related to school and academic expectations. These pressures lead to physical symptoms such as nausea and stomach pain, especially noticeable at the beginning of a new school term.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Jun enjoys playing basketball, which has previously served as an enjoyable and stress-relieving activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Jun exhibits anxiety linked to academic stress, though there is no formal diagnosis of an anxiety disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Jun feels overwhelmed by physical symptoms of stress, such as stomach pain and nausea, particularly relating to school and academic pressures.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Jun's tendency to associate academic discussions with anxiety and physical discomfort, observing a pattern where stress affects Jun's focus and sleep.
[Counseling Assignments:] Jun is advised to keep a diary documenting feelings and symptoms, noting any accompanying activities or thoughts, and to consider engaging in basketball as a stress-relieving activity.
[Session Goal:] Identify stress-related triggers and explore healthier coping mechanisms, using CBT strategies to manage stress and introduce stress-reducing activities like basketball.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Jun's school-related anxiety and its physical manifestations, linking stress to his symptoms of nausea and stomach pain. Set a task to keep a diary of feelings, symptoms, and thoughts to identify triggers and patterns, and discussed reintroducing enjoyable activities like basketball for stress relief. Emphasized gradual, supportive progress towards healthier coping mechanisms.
