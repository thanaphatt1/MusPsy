The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
D is a professional navigating job instability due to the pandemic, facing recurring job losses and feelings of self-blame. This cycle of losing jobs and starting over exacerbates stress and feelings of uncertainty about the future.

[Personal Characteristics]  
D finds solace in photography, a hobby that provides creative expression and distraction from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
D experiences stress and self-critical thoughts due to career instability but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] D feels overwhelmed and uncertain, experiencing significant stress due to ongoing job instability and fear of future prospects.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes D's struggle with persistent self-blame and the potential to explore external factors influencing their job situation to relieve some mental pressure.
[Counseling Assignments:] D is assigned to maintain a journal of thoughts and emotions to identify patterns and triggers, and to re-engage in photography as a form of self-care and stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help D manage stress by identifying self-critical thoughts and reinforcing engagements in positive activities like photography.
Counseling Summary:
Explored D's distress over job instability due to the pandemic and its impact on mental well-being. Agreed to use journaling to identify thought patterns and triggers, and suggested incorporating photography as a form of creative escape and self-care. D will record thoughts and emotions, and the next session will focus on insights from journaling and photography.
