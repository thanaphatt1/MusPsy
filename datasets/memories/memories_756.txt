The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is an individual dealing with substantial stress due to impending exams, as well as emotional exhaustion from a recent divorce. The user is coping with a lack of close support from their father and tension between parents, contributing to the stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user enjoys sketching and watching series, which serve as means of relaxation and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety primarily associated with academic and personal life pressures but no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed and anxious due to upcoming exams and family dynamics following their divorce.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices the user's isolation due to limited family support but acknowledges their willingness to explore stress management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is encouraged to engage in self-care activities and relaxation, particularly through sketching and series watching, while noting negative thoughts when they arise.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help the user manage anxiety, recognize and address negative thought patterns, and engage in relaxing activities like sketching.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session focused on addressing the user's stress and anxiety stemming from upcoming exams and the aftermath of their divorce, further exacerbated by family tensions. To manage these stressors, the counselor encouraged the user to engage in self-care activities and proposed journaling to identify and challenge recurring negative thoughts. The dialogue emphasized the importance of gaining awareness of these thought patterns and offered continued support to facilitate emotional insight and coping strategies.
