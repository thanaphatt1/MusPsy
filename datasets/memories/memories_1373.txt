The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Han is a professional experiencing conflict and discomfort at work due to feelings he developed for a new colleague who did not reciprocate his interest. This situation has stirred emotions of embarrassment and uncertainty in Han.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Han enjoys cycling, which he used to partake in frequently as a form of relaxation and mental clarity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Han has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder but experiences stress and emotional turmoil linked to his work environment and interactions.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Han feels conflicted and downcast, struggling with embarrassment and self-doubt following a romantic rejection at work.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Han's inclination to withdraw socially at work, likely as a mechanism to manage potential awkwardness and maintain professionalism.
[Counseling Assignments:] Han is encouraged to jot down his thoughts and emotions related to the situation and try engaging in small social interactions where he feels comfortable.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Han manage emotional distress, regain self-confidence, and reconstruct perceptions regarding workplace interactions.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling session, Han expressed feelings of distress and embarrassment following the rejection from a colleague, which impacted his professional interactions. The consultant encouraged Han to track his thoughts and emotions related to this situation and proposed engaging in small social interactions to ease tension, coupled with rekindling his interest in cycling as a means to bolster his mood and mental well-being. Han agreed to these actionable steps, feeling motivated to gradually improve his social and emotional experiences.
