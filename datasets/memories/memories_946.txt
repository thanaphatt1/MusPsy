The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
S is a university student experiencing stress due to changes in campus life and increasing study loads. S struggles to balance academic responsibilities with extracurricular activities while feeling pressured about upcoming exams.

[Personal Characteristics]  
S enjoys drawing, which used to be a relaxing hobby, and values spending time with supportive friends.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
S reports experiencing stress and anxiety primarily related to academic pressures and unmet personal expectations. No formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] S feels stressed and overwhelmed by the study load and extracurricular commitments, resulting in a lack of personal time.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices S's struggle with balancing responsibilities and a willingness to explore stress management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] S should try setting aside time for enjoyable activities like taking short walks, and identify thoughts leading to stress for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help S manage stress, identify stress-related thoughts, and encourage re-engagement in relaxing activities like drawing.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored S's stress from increased campus responsibilities, focusing on how it affects daily life and emotional well-being. Suggested setting aside time for enjoyable activities to manage stress and identifying thought patterns that exacerbate feelings of inadequacy. Agreed on observing thoughts and triggers to gain insights and promote emotional balance.
