The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaomi is a student who recently argued with their mother about schooling choices, leading to familial tension. They wish to stay in their current school location for its social and academic benefits.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaomi enjoys activities like jogging, which they plan to incorporate as a stress-relief mechanism. They value open communication but express difficulty when emotions run high.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaomi experiences stress primarily due to familial disagreements, but there's no mention of a diagnosed mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaomi feels stressed and sensitive due to an unresolved conflict with their mother over school decisions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Xiaomi shows awareness of both their own and their mother's perspectives but struggles with initiating calm conversations.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaomi should note triggers related to school and familial discussions and explore how these affect their emotions.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Xiaomi manage stress and improve communication with their mother through structured discussion and reflection activities.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaomi expressed stress due to an argument with her mom over schooling, feeling misunderstood and frustrated about her lack of control. The counselor suggested identifying emotional triggers, drafting conversations, and engaging in calming activities like jogging to improve communication. Xiaomi agreed to these strategies to foster a better understanding and balance between asserting her needs and acknowledging her mom's concerns.
