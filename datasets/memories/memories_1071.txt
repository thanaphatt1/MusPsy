The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Little Kitten is a student experiencing embarrassment and stress due to a recent bedwetting incident that has led to teasing from classmates. They feel nervous about attending school as a result.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Little Kitten has a supportive friend at school who provides comfort and enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Little Kitten experiences anxiety and stress but hasn't been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Little Kitten feels embarrassed and stressed due to a bedwetting incident, leading to teasing at school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices the stress caused by peer teasing and Little Kitten's avoidance behavior regarding school attendance.
[Counseling Assignments:] Little Kitten is encouraged to journal their feelings, noting any positive moments to recognize patterns in their emotional experiences.
[Session Goal:] Assist Little Kitten in managing anxiety by using CBT strategies to navigate social stressors and foster resilience through journaling.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Little Kitten's feelings of embarrassment and anxiety related to a bedwetting incident discovered by classmates, leading to teasing at school and nervousness about attending. Discussed the impact of negative self-talk on emotions and the importance of challenging these thoughts and seeking out supportive classmates. Assigned tasks to keep a feelings journal, question negative thoughts, and identify potentially supportive peers to foster a safer school environment.
