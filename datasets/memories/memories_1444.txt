The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaofan is a young student experiencing heightened stress and anxiety due to their academic performance and tension at home, primarily stemming from parental arguments. Xiaofan feels blamed for these arguments, leading to feelings of guilt and helplessness but is willing to seek improvement.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaofan finds solace in drawing, which serves as a relaxing and creative outlet amid stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaofan displays symptoms of anxiety linked to familial conflicts and school pressures, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis documented.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaofan feels stressed and anxious, trapped by guilt over family arguments perceived as self-caused.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaofan's struggle with family dynamics and self-blame yet identifies potential for cognitive perspective shifts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofan should jot down specific thoughts and scenarios that induce distress to foster awareness and future examination.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Xiaofan to basic CBT techniques, encouraging identification and analysis of distressing thoughts and recognizing their impacts.
Counseling Summary:
During the counseling sessions, Xiaofan explored the impact of her parents' arguments on her stress and self-blame, and began to recognize that not all issues were her fault. Through guided self-reflection, she started viewing situations from different perspectives and acknowledged other possible factors contributing to her parents' disputes. Xiaofan found solace in drawing and decided to allocate time to this activity weekly, while also realizing the value of discussing her feelings openly with her parents and others she trusts, ultimately leading to increased hope and preparedness to face future challenges.
