The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaodan is a college student who recently transitioned to a polytechnic program following an unsatisfactory performance on entrance exams, feeling burdened by academic self-doubt and a recent breakup.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys reading mystery novels, which serve as an escape and relaxation activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaodan experiences anxiety linked to academic performance and personal relationships, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaodan feels down and lost, struggling with academic motivation and sleep issues, exacerbated by a recent breakup.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaodan's feelings of inadequacy and persistent self-questioning but sees her openness to exploring solutions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaodan is encouraged to start noticing her thoughts during stressful times and record them for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Introduce Xiaodan to CBT strategies to identify and manage distressing thoughts, improving mood and motivation through thought recording.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaodan's struggles with a lack of motivation following college entrance exam results and feelings of inadequacy after a breakup, affecting mood and sleep. Agreed to focus on understanding the impact of thoughts and emotions on her well-being. Xiaodan will track her thoughts during stressful moments as a starting point for addressing these issues.
