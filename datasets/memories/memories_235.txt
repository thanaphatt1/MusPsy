The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a student dealing with considerable stress due to school workload and exam preparations, coupled with a lack of familial support as both parents work long hours. Wang is deeply committed to not disappointing their parents despite infrequent contact.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wang enjoys reading historical fiction, using it as a form of distraction and relief from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences anxiety and stress triggered by academic pressures and family expectations but has not received a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels overwhelmed by school pressures and family responsibilities, experiencing exhaustion and loneliness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wang's struggle with guilt over perceived lack of achievement despite hard work and notes Wang’s openness to adjusting stress-related thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should establish a routine reading time for relaxation and record recurring stress-related thoughts to evaluate their accuracy.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Wang manage stress by acknowledging personal efforts and engaging in restful activities, while challenging self-critical thoughts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wang's stress related to school assignments and exams, feeling unsupported, and fear of not meeting parental expectations. Agreed on incorporating structured reading as a stress-relief activity and writing down recurring negative thoughts to assess their accuracy, aimed at managing stress and cultivating a healthier mindset. Encouraged Wang to recognize their efforts and practice self-kindness while maintaining small, consistent changes.
