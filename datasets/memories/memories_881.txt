The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a university student who identifies as shy and introverted, expressing significant stress and anxiety, particularly when required to present or speak in class. Recently, Zhang had a troubling experience during a presentation, leading to increased self-criticism after receiving negative feedback from a teacher.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang experiences a deep-seated anxiety about being judged by others, which resonates through academic and social aspects of life.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang is experiencing performance-related anxiety and self-doubt, particularly in public speaking situations, although there's no indication of a formal diagnosis at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels overwhelmed by anxiety, particularly in public speaking situations, which is now affecting several areas of life.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang's awareness of their anxiety and willingness to explore its triggers, alongside hesitance due to self-imposed high expectations and perceived judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should begin a thought diary to log instances of negative thoughts and explore recurring patterns to better understand their anxiety triggers.
[Session Goal:] Utilize cognitive behavioral strategies to help Zhang identify negative thinking patterns, explore their origins, and work towards modifying these perceptions to alleviate anxiety.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed Zhang's anxiety around public speaking and its negative impact on his life, particularly in academic and social settings. Introduced the idea of documenting his negative thoughts and triggers to gain clarity and identify patterns. Planned to use these insights to apply cognitive behavioral strategies in future sessions to help Zhang challenge and transform his anxious thoughts.
