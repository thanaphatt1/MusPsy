The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a professional who is experiencing a lot of stress at work, questioning her role and competence in her career. This distress is affecting her sleep and amplifying negative thoughts. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li finds joy in reading novels, which serves as a way to escape from worries.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li deals with stress and anxiety induced by high workplace expectations, with no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels low and overwhelmed due to increasing responsibilities at work, leading to self-doubt about her career fit.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Li's tendency to question her self-worth amidst high expectations but sees potential in her willingness to explore positivity and challenge negative thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to take note of a recent positive moment and reflect on recurring stress-related thoughts for broader understanding.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to explore Li's thoughts and emotions, identifying one positive moment to shift perspectives on self-worth.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ms. Li's work-related stress impacting her self-esteem and engagement, with a focus on negative thoughts of inadequacy. Agreed to track positive feedback and past successes to strengthen her self-worth and confidence, alongside managing stress. Encouraged a reflective practice to enhance balanced self-view for gradual positive change.
