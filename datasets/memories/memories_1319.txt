The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a student dealing with anxiety and nervousness, particularly stemming from a classroom incident where they felt embarrassed. Li is self-conscious about speaking in class and worries about being judged by peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys playing strategy video games as a way to relax and distract from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li experiences anxiety and stress, notably from academic and social interactions, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels anxious and down, primarily due to an incident where classmates laughed at them during a class for giving a wrong answer.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li's heightened self-consciousness and anxiety about peer judgment, along with a tendency to focus on mistakes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li should note down feelings after positive classroom experiences and try to identify and acknowledge extreme or unrealistic thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Help Li utilize CBT to explore thoughts related to anxiety, reduce self-criticism, and improve classroom participation confidence.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's academic-induced anxiety and its extension into social settings, focusing on fear of judgment versus past supportive experiences. Established tasks for cognitive observation and social risk-taking, proposing small engagements with friends while tracking outcomes for confidence building. Agreed on balancing stress management through frequent mindfulness and relaxing activities, committing to progress review in future sessions.
