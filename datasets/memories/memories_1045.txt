The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
B is a student who has been navigating emotional turmoil arising from recent family changes, notably their parents' divorce. This situation has caused disruptions at both home and school, leading B to feel overwhelmed. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
B enjoys drawing and has found it to be a potential outlet for stress relief, although they haven't consistently engaged in this activity recently.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
B experiences stress, anger, and feelings of losing control, particularly triggered by familial conflict and school pressures. There is no formal diagnostic label specified at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] B feels frustrated and overwhelmed, grappling with anger and a loss of control due to family tensions and school challenges.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes B's struggle with managing intense emotions and the negative impacts of their home environment yet sees potential in leveraging drawing for stress relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] B is encouraged to try setting aside time for drawing on weekends as a means of emotional expression and stress relief and explore the possibility of opening up to someone they trust.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help B manage overwhelming emotions, recognize stress triggers, and support engagement in calming activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored B's struggles with stress and anger stemming from their parents' divorce, impacting both home and school life. Agreed on incorporating calming activities into their routine, like drawing and walking, and suggested gradual opening up to trusted individuals for support. Encouraged B to maintain a reflective journal to enhance emotional understanding and resilience.
