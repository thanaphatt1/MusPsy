The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhi is a university student dealing with anxiety primarily triggered by academic and social situations. Recently, Zhi faced an embarrassing moment in class, which heightened their concerns about peer judgment. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhi finds comfort in reading, particularly fantasy novels, as a means of relaxation. He struggles with self-esteem issues and is sensitive to others' opinions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhi exhibits signs of anxiety related to social and academic stress, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhi feels down and anxious after an embarrassing experience in class, worrying about others judging them negatively.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhi's sensitivity to peer perceptions and their tendency to internalize social mishaps deeply.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhi should observe feelings in various school situations and note down specific thoughts for discussion in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Zhi identify and reduce negative thought patterns and encourage engagement in joyful activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhi's anxiety stemming from a classroom incident, emphasizing the impact of negative self-perceptions and assumptions about others' judgments. Encouraged Zhi to challenge these thoughts and engage in enjoyable activities like reading to alleviate stress and enhance well-being. Agreed on using self-kindness when reflecting on mistakes and tracking thoughts to discuss in future sessions.
