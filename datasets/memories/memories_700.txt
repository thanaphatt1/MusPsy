The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is an adult male who experienced a disciplinary action at work last year due to a mistake made during an exam inspection. This incident has left him feeling ashamed and anxious, leading him to take a long leave of absence from work. The user avoids social interactions out of fear of judgment.

[Personal Characteristics]
He has a passion for cooking, which he uses as a joyful distraction and a means to manage stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences anxiety and shame related to past mistakes and the perceived judgment of coworkers. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels extremely ashamed and anxious about past mistakes, leading to social withdrawal.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes the user's courage in sharing his experience and identifies a pattern of avoidance due to fear of judgment.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is tasked with noting down situations or thoughts that trigger anxiety for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Begin identifying thought patterns contributing to anxiety using CBT, introducing methods to ease the emotional burden and promote understanding.
Counseling Summary:
Explored User's experience of shame and anxiety following a workplace mistake, leading to social withdrawal and fear of judgment. Encouraged User to identify and challenge negative thinking patterns, consider open communication with colleagues to resolve misunderstandings, and engage in enjoyable activities like cooking to improve their wellbeing. Throughout the sessions, User gained awareness of their thoughts and emotions and developed strategies to manage anxiety and seek connection.
