The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Roger is transitioning to a new job, feeling anxious and overwhelmed by the unfamiliar environment and expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys creating a peaceful home ambience but struggles with social interactions and sometimes resorts to drinking to cope with stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Roger experiences anxiety related to work performance and social situations, and there is an avoidance tendency coupled with increased alcohol use as a coping mechanism.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Roger feels anxious and overwhelmed, particularly in his new job setting where expectations are unclear to him.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist identifies Roger's avoidance behaviors and reliance on alcohol as coping mechanisms, while also noting his willingness to explore healthier coping strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Roger is encouraged to practice deep breathing exercises and begin focusing on actual feedback rather than assumed expectations at work.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Roger manage workplace anxiety, challenge assumptions, and foster healthier coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed Roger's workplace anxiety, stemming from fear of making mistakes and uncertainty about meeting expectations, impacting his social life and leading to unhealthy coping strategies like drinking. Proposed deep breathing exercises and direct communication with his manager for feedback to mitigate work stress, and encouraged regular walks and self-compassion to foster relaxation and a balanced routine. Provided supportive guidance to build healthier habits and reinforced the importance of small steps toward managing anxiety and stress effectively.
