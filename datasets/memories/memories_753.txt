The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Song is a university student dealing with stress from a recent breakup and the lingering impact on her self-esteem and daily life. She feels overwhelmed and isolated, struggling to engage with friends and academic responsibilities. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Song enjoys reading and walking, activities which she finds somewhat soothing after feeling overwhelmed.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Song exhibits signs of anxiety and stress stemming primarily from relationship issues, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Song feels down and stressed, particularly over her recent breakup, impacting her sleep and concentration.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Song's pervasive self-doubt and feelings of inadequacy, yet also sees openness to exploring supportive connections and self-validation.
[Counseling Assignments:] Song should note times when self-critical thoughts arise and observe moments of support and affirmation from others.
[Session Goal:] Begin using CBT techniques to help Song identify and shift negative self-beliefs and enhance awareness of external support.
Counseling Summary:
Song discussed feelings of stress and self-doubt following a breakup, impacting her sleep, studies, and social interactions. The counselor helped Song identify and challenge the belief that she needs to be perfect to be valued, highlighting examples where she is appreciated for who she is. Song agreed to focus on recognizing moments of self-criticism and support, aiming to shift her self-view positively.
