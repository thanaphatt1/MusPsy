The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ms. Zhang, a woman in her late 30s, recently discontinued lithium medication, feeling initially stable but now grappling with increased anxiety and a sense of losing emotional control. She experiences self-doubt about her efficiency in housework and her role in her relationship.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ms. Zhang has shown interest in yoga as a potential stress-relief activity and occasionally reflects on her interactions with her partner.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ms. Zhang experiences heightened anxiety and stress following the medication change but lacks a formal diagnosis of a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ms. Zhang feels anxious and overwhelmed, struggling with everyday tasks and emotions since stopping lithium.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes Ms. Zhang's critical self-perceptions and her openness to recognizing trigger situations and exploring self-supportive strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ms. Zhang is encouraged to identify situations that exacerbate these feelings and replace negative self-talk with more supportive thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Assist Ms. Zhang with identifying anxiety triggers and employing self-compassionate thoughts using CBT techniques to improve emotional stability.
Counseling Summary:
Ms. Zhang shared feelings of overwhelm and anxiety since discontinuing lithium, impacting daily activities and interactions with her partner. The consultant suggested identifying situations that trigger anxiety and noting critical self-talk, encouraging positive self-dialogue and communication with her partner to alleviate tensions. Ms. Zhang expressed optimism in practicing these strategies to enhance emotional control and stability.
