The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Miss Zhang is a single mother in her late 20s, struggling with heroin use and facing scrutiny from social services over concerns about her two-year-old daughter’s safety. She feels overwhelmed by the pressures associated with this situation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Miss Zhang enjoys drawing, which provides her with moments of peace amidst her challenges.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Miss Zhang is dealing with stress and negative self-perception stemming from her circumstances, although no formal diagnosis is specified.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Miss Zhang feels overwhelmed and hopeless, particularly due to ongoing stress involving social services and her daughter.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes her openness to change and willingness to explore her thoughts and emotions as pathways to stress relief.
[Counseling Assignments:] Miss Zhang is encouraged to note specific thoughts and feelings that occur during stressful moments for pattern recognition.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Miss Zhang identify interconnected thoughts and feelings, paving the way for healthier coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed Miss Zhang's feelings of overwhelm and hopelessness due to stress from social services concerning her daughter's safety amidst her heroin use. Focused on identifying the connection between her thoughts and feelings during stressful times to discover relief strategies; assigned the task of noting specific thoughts and feelings to recognize patterns for intervention. Miss Zhang expressed openness to change and committed to the assignment.
