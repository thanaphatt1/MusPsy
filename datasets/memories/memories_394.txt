The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Eve is a mother recovering from treatment and adjusting to life with her son who has returned from foster care. She faces the challenge of balancing her recovery with parental responsibilities and staying resilient amidst these changes. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Eve enjoys gardening and painting, which serve as therapeutic activities for her.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Eve experiences anxiety and stress, particularly due to the pressures of meeting her responsibilities, but there's no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Eve feels overwhelmed as she adjusts to life post-treatment and aims to balance her recovery with being a good mother to her son.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Eve’s anxiety about her dual roles and recognizes her motivation to create a balanced life despite these pressures.
[Counseling Assignments:] Eve is encouraged to set small, manageable goals to help balance her recovery with her responsibilities and to practice capturing negative thoughts.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to assist Eve in managing anxiety by identifying and challenging negative thought patterns while establishing a balanced daily routine.
Counseling Summary:
Eve is navigating the challenging transition of resuming life post-treatment, including settling into a new home and managing the return of her son from foster care. Throughout the sessions, she focuses on setting small, manageable goals to create daily routines and addresses anxiety by practicing mindfulness techniques, such as challenging negative thoughts and engaging in deep breathing. By integrating these practices with her interests, like gardening, Eve aims to cultivate a balanced approach to managing her responsibilities and personal recovery.
