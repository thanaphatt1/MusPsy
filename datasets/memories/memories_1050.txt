The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Y is a student experiencing feelings of loneliness and stress due to changes in friendships and the pressure to excel academically. This stress has been exacerbated by a recent period of feeling ignored by classmates, including a best friend, which has led Y to feel invisible and anxious.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Y enjoys drawing and reading but has not engaged in these activities recently due to academic pressures and worry about parental expectations.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Y struggles with anxiety related to social interactions and academic performance. There is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Y feels stressed and lonely, overwhelmed by the sense of being ignored by peers and uncertain about the reasons behind it.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Y's heightened sensitivity to social dynamics and isolation but also identifies Y's willingness to explore self-expression as a means to cope with stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Y should start a journal to document thoughts and feelings when feeling overwhelmed and incorporate self-care moments with activities like music.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Y recognize thought patterns contributing to stress and explore constructive self-care activities as coping mechanisms.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Y's feelings of stress and loneliness due to perceived social rejection, and suggested journaling to process emotions and identify thought patterns. Recommended incorporating a small daily self-care activity, like listening to music, to improve mood and manage anxiety. Emphasized consistency in these practices to foster positive change over time.
