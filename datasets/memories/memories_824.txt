The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Chenchen is a young adult experiencing stress primarily from academic pressures and family interactions. She feels disappointed with her recent exam performance and faces teasing from her younger sister, which adds to her stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Chenchen enjoys reading, particularly fantasy novels, but has sidelined it due to exam stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Chenchen struggles with self-criticism linked to academic outcomes, leading to feelings of disappointment and isolation, without a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Chenchen feels downcast and isolated, burdened by academic disappointment and sibling challenges.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes a pattern of self-criticism related to academic achievement and notes Chenchen's sensitivity to familial teasing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Chenchen should engage in reading as a form of stress relief and identify recurring negative thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Apply CBT to help Chenchen manage stress by questioning negative self-evaluations and re-engage in leisure activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
The session with Chenchen explored her feelings of disappointment and isolation following poor exam results and her sister's teasing, linking these to a pattern of self-criticism tied to academic performance. Through discussing the impact of negative assumptions and exploring strategies such as reframing these thoughts and communicating more calmly with her sister, Chenchen considered how engaging in hobbies like reading could help manage stress and contribute to a more balanced self-view. Chenchen committed to setting aside time for reading to help lift her mood, while also planning to approach her sister about how her teasing affects her.
