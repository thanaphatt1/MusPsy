The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Zhang is a student experiencing overwhelm and lack of interest, leading to conflicts in school due to frequent forgetfulness in homework submission. Zhang reports difficulty in concentrating and often feels tired, even during school breaks.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Zhang struggles with inattentiveness and anxiety related to performance at school, although there is no mention of a formal diagnosis.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Zhang enjoys reading comic books, which are sometimes used as a means to relax and escape stress.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Zhang feels anxious and left out, facing struggles in concentration and managing schoolwork.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiao Zhang's feelings of disconnection and heightened anxiety when criticized by teachers, sensing Zhang's openness to identifying stress patterns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Zhang is encouraged to keep a simple diary of thoughts and feelings to better understand recurring stressors.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiao Zhang manage anxiety through CBT strategies, focusing on identifying stressors and exploring ways to improve school engagement.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Zhang's feelings of overwhelm and academic struggles, identifying patterns of self-doubt and procrastination linked to anxiety about school performance. Suggested strategies such as breaking tasks into manageable steps, forming a study group, incorporating short breaks, and maintaining a diary to tracking thoughts, with a goal of fostering better focus and emotional resilience. Xiao Zhang agreed to try these methods and keep tracking their thoughts for continued exploration in future sessions.
