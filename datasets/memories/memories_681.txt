The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Dandan is a university student feeling isolated due to conflicts in friendships, including a fallout over a bill during a group dinner. Dandan often feels anxious and insecure in social situations, particularly with classmates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Dandan enjoys art, specifically drawing and painting, and finds these activities soothing.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Dandan experiences social anxiety and loneliness stemming from perceived exclusion and insecurities, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Dandan feels guilt and anxiety due to a recent disagreement with a friend, amplifying feelings of isolation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Dandan's tendency to focus on negative interactions and anxiety arising from perceived social exclusion.
[Counseling Assignments:] Dandan should reflect on and note situations that intensify feelings of loneliness, aiming to identify recurring thoughts or patterns.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help Dandan identify and challenge negative assumptions about social interactions, focusing on positive experiences.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Dandan's feelings of isolation and anxiety stemming from a disagreement with a friend, leading to insecurity in social settings. Acknowledged patterns of feeling like an outsider from high school and encouraged Dandan to identify and reflect on moments of connection and acceptance with peers. Agreed on noting instances of positive social interactions to shift focus from negative thoughts to feelings of inclusion.
