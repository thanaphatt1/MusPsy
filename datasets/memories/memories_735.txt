The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao A is a university student struggling with conflict during military training, including an incident involving police intervention. Xiao A tends to lose their temper quickly, leading to disagreements with instructors and peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao A enjoys playing basketball, which serves as an effective outlet for stress and mental clarity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao A exhibits signs of frustration, defensiveness, and a tendency to anticipate negative interactions, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao A feels frustrated and isolated, perceiving a need to consistently defend themselves in interactions with peers.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiao A's quickness in making negative assumptions about others' intentions and recognizes Xiao A’s insight into their own rash reactions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao A is encouraged to practice deep breathing consistently and document instances of negative assumptions to better assess their validity.
[Session Goal:] Begin identifying thought patterns that exacerbate conflicts and explore accessible stress-reduction techniques like mindfulness and physical activity.
Counseling Summary:
Xiao A expressed frustration and anger stemming from conflicts during military training, linked to a tendency to assume others are undermining him without evidence. The counselor helped Xiao A recognize and challenge these assumptions by questioning their accuracy and practicing deep breathing techniques to manage emotional responses. Xiao A committed to applying these strategies to create space between impulse and reaction, aiming to reduce defensiveness and improve interactions.
