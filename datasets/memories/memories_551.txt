The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaohao is a boarding school student who experiences anxiety and loneliness due to being away from home and his mother. He frequently calls his mother, which affects his routine and schoolwork.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaohao has shown interest in writing and enjoys sharing meaningful stories and experiences with his mother.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaohao struggles with feelings of loneliness and has developed coping strategies to manage being away from family, though no formal diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaohao feels anxious and lonely due to being separated from his mother.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Xiaohao's sincere longing for connection with his mother and his willingness to express emotions through writing.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaohao should write a letter to his mother to organize his thoughts and express his feelings more clearly.
[Session Goal:] To help Xiaohao express and manage his emotions through writing and feel more connected to his mother despite the distance.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling session, Xiaohao expressed feelings of anxiety and loneliness due to being away from his mother at boarding school. The consultant suggested writing a letter to his mom as a way to articulate his feelings, express the emotional impact of her absence, and enhance self-awareness. Xiaohao acknowledged the therapeutic potential of writing to gain clarity and agreed to explore this task, aiming to manage his emotions and improve connections despite the distance.
