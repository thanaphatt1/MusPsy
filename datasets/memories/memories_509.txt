The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaowen is a high school student experiencing significant emotional stress due to interpersonal conflicts in a school setting. They mention feeling upset and angry during situations where teamwork and communication with classmates break down.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaowen enjoys drawing, which they find helpful for relaxation and emotional expression.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaowen shows signs of emotional distress linked to feeling ignored or disrespected in social interactions but does not have a formal diagnosis of any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaowen feels upset and angry due to conflict with classmates and feelings of being disregarded.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaowen's tendency to feel overlooked but also recognizes Xiaowen's willingness to identify triggers of anger.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaowen should maintain a diary to note triggers of anger and incorporate drawing into their routine to assist with emotional regulation.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to aid Xiaowen in recognizing and addressing anger triggers while encouraging insight into emotional responses during conflicts.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaowen's challenges with managing anger in conflict situations, particularly during group activities at school. Agreed on actions to identify and understand triggers through journaling, and to consider separating feelings from facts when conflicts arise. Encouraged exploring art as a calming activity and adopting a perspective of advising a friend to facilitate clearer communication with classmates.
