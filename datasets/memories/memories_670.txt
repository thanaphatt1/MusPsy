The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao H is a college student currently facing anxiety and stress, mainly due to ongoing health issues, which include undertaking medical tests like an MRI and trying traditional Chinese medicine, all without significant improvements. Being unable to participate in college activities due to these health challenges adds to the stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao H previously enjoyed activities such as reading novels and gardening, which served as pastimes.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao H experiences anxiety and stress, significantly impacted by health-related challenges, with no formal mental health disorder diagnosis reported.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao H feels anxious and overwhelmed because of health issues and their detrimental impact on daily life and academics.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Xiao H's struggle with their perceived responsibility over their health situation but appreciates Xiao H’s willingness to explore these concerns.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao H is encouraged to document specific anxious thoughts or feelings experienced throughout the week to gain more insights.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiao H identify stress-related thoughts and begin working toward balanced thinking using CBT strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao H's anxiety stemming from ongoing health issues and its impact on their emotional and daily life. Encouraged Xiao H to identify and document negative thought patterns, particularly those around self-blame and pessimism about recovery, to help develop more balanced perspectives. Set a task for Xiao H to continue observing their thoughts, laying groundwork for future sessions.
