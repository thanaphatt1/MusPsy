The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Mr. Qin is recovering from a stroke and experiencing frustration due to the slow recovery process, impacting his ability to move independently. He relies on his son for assistance, leading to feelings of helplessness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Mr. Qin appreciates gardening and enjoys spending time with his family, finding comfort in their support.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Mr. Qin is dealing with frustration and irritability due to a loss of independence post-stroke, although no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Mr. Qin feels irritated and low due to his dependence on his son and a slow recovery.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Mr. Qin’s resilience amidst his new limitations and recognizes his openness to identifying stressors and managing emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Mr. Qin is encouraged to keep a journal detailing his thoughts and emotions, specifically what triggers his frustrations.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Mr. Qin recognize stressors, focus on accomplishments, and shift attention from limitations to progress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Mr. Qin's feelings of frustration and helplessness following his stroke, particularly due to loss of independence, and discussed the emotional impact on his daily life. Agreed on tasks such as journaling to identify stressors and cultivate gratitude to shift focus from limitations to small victories, involving support from his son. Mindfulness and celebrating progress were highlighted for managing emotions and enhancing well-being.
