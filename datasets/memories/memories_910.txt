The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
P is a master’s student struggling with anxiety upon entering their second year. The academic pressure has led to increased isolation and reliance on meal delivery services. P identifies as an introvert, finding it difficult to reach out for help.

[Personal Characteristics]  
P enjoys playing video games, which serve as a form of escape and relaxation during overwhelming times.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
P experiences anxiety triggered by academic stress, though no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Anxiety stemming from academic pressures and feelings of isolation due to introverted tendencies.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes P's introversion complicating social connections but identifies openness to engaging more with peers.
[Counseling Assignments:] P is encouraged to step outside daily for a short walk to change their environment and consider initiating small social interactions with classmates.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to manage anxiety by breaking isolation, recognizing the interconnectedness of thoughts and feelings, and fostering relaxation through environmental changes.
Counseling Summary:
Explored P's academic and social anxiety, emphasizing the pressure from studies and isolation due to introversion. Agreed on starting a daily walking routine to challenge avoidance and encourage brief social interactions, like meeting a classmate for coffee, to build a supportive network. Encouraged incorporating leisure activities into P's schedule to promote balance and stress management.
