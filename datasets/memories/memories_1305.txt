The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ms. Wang is a nurse preparing for a qualification exam, frequently experiencing anxiety, particularly related to fears of contamination stemming from reading about occupational health risks. She finds it challenging to manage her time for previously enjoyed activities such as gardening due to overwhelming stress.

[Personal Characteristics]
Ms. Wang enjoys gardening, which provides her with a sense of calm and achievement.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ms. Wang exhibits signs of anxiety and compulsive behaviors, such as frequent handwashing, without any formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ms. Wang feels anxious and overwhelmed, particularly due to compulsive handwashing which interferes with her focus on studying for the nursing exam.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Ms. Wang's anxiety stemming from an exaggerated fear of contamination and notes her potential for improvement through structured behavioral changes.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ms. Wang should write down thoughts before and after handwashing to identify triggers and patterns, and reintroduce gardening for stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Help Ms. Wang manage anxiety through CBT by identifying thought patterns and encouraging relaxation techniques like gardening.
Counseling Summary:
Ms. Wang is experiencing heightened anxiety related to obsessive hand washing, triggered by fears of contamination after reading about occupational health risks. In her counseling sessions, she was encouraged to track her thoughts to identify patterns, practice rational evaluation of these fears, and gradually expose herself to smaller contamination challenges to reduce anxiety over time. Additionally, stress-relief activities such as gardening were recommended to create a balance between exam preparation and self-care, with gradual implementation of these strategies towards improving her well-being.
