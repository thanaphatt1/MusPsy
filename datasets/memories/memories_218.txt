The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaozhang is a student facing difficulties in adjusting to a new semester and increased academic pressure. He often feels stressed about keeping up with his studies, especially math, which has been a persistent challenge.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaozhang enjoys drawing, which provides him an escape from academic stress and has been identified as an interest to explore further for relaxation.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaozhang experiences stress primarily related to academic pressures; however, there is no indication of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaozhang feels overwhelmed by his new schedule and the challenging math subject, leading to stress and anxiety.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaozhang's openness to exploring strategies to manage his academic stress and sees potential for improvement through structured study sessions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaozhang should implement a routine of studying math for 25 minutes with 5-minute breaks and employ deep breathing exercises before studying.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaozhang manage stress by creating achievable study goals and utilizing relaxation techniques to ease the anxiety associated with challenging subjects.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaozhang's stress and difficulty in adapting to a new academic schedule, particularly with math, and worked on strategies to manage time and anxiety. Established realistic study goals with incorporated breaks, positive affirmations, and deep breathing exercises for effective concentration and stress relief. Planned follow-up to assess the impact of these strategies and support Xiaozhang's ongoing progress.
