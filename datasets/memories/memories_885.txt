The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Cheng is a university student encountering high levels of stress from adjusting to a new semester, managing social anxiety, and balancing coursework. The stress has a noticeable impact on Cheng’s eating habits and health.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Cheng enjoys reading fiction, especially fantasy novels, and finds solace in conversations with a supportive step-sister.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Cheng experiences anxiety, particularly in social and academic settings, but there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Cheng feels overwhelmed by the semester's demands, with heightened social anxiety and difficulties adapting to new environments.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist recognizes Cheng’s struggles with anxiety and perceives openness to techniques that could foster positivity and address stress.
[Counseling Assignments:] Cheng is encouraged to journal one positive interaction or experience each day to gradually shift focus towards positive aspects of life.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Cheng recognize and challenge negative thoughts, reinforcing daily awareness of positive experiences amidst stress.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Cheng's difficulty adapting to a new semester, with social anxiety and coursework causing significant stress. Suggested daily journaling of positive interactions to help shift focus and manage anxiety. Agreed to review the impact of this practice on Cheng's emotional well-being in the next session.
