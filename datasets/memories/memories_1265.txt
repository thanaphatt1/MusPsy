The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Aqiy has been feeling pressured by parental comparisons with their younger sister's academic performance. The pressure has led to feelings of not being good enough and guilt over their emotions towards their parents.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Aqiy finds solace and pride in art, using creativity as a means to escape from stress and academic pressures. 

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Aqiy is experiencing stress and a mix of guilt and frustration, rooted in family dynamics, but there is no mentioned formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Aqiy feels overwhelmed by parental comparisons and harbors guilt for feeling upset with them.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Aqiy as emotionally weighed down by family-related performance expectations but recognizes the potential empowerment in Aqiy's self-awareness and interest in art.
[Counseling Assignments:] Aqiy is encouraged to document instances of feeling pressure and their emotional responses to use as a basis for exploring coping strategies.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Aqiy identify and manage stress from parental expectations, promoting self-acceptance and constructive communication with their parents.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Aqiy's feelings of pressure and inadequacy stemming from parental comparisons to a sibling's academic performance, which led to feelings of sadness and disconnection. Agreed on tracking moments of pressure and considering sharing personal achievements (such as an art project) to foster more positive family interactions and shift focus away from grades. Encouraged Aqiy to discuss the art project with parents as a starting point for open communication and building self-confidence.
