The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yang is a student experiencing high levels of stress due to impending exams and family tensions at home, where parents argue frequently. His academic pressures are compounded by feelings of responsibility toward supporting his mother, which intensifies his stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He has a previous interest in drawing, which he finds calming and expressive, suggesting a creative side that could aid in stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yang is currently facing elevated stress and anxiety related to academic and family demands, but there has been no formal diagnosis of any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yang feels overwhelmed and anxious due to the pressure from exams and family issues, resulting in difficulty focusing on his studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yang's tendency to seek temporary escape through gaming, which offers momentary comfort but adds to his stress afterwards due to feelings of guilt.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yang is encouraged to write down stress-inducing thoughts to assess their realism, while also making space for relaxing activities like drawing.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to help Yang challenge unrealistic expectations, engage in self-kind and mindful practices, and integrate creative outlets to alleviate stress.
Counseling Summary:
Yang expressed stress due to upcoming school exams and familial conflict at home, leading to feelings of being overwhelmed and using online games as an escape. In the counseling session, it was recognized that Yang could benefit from acknowledging and softening unrealistic expectations, focusing on effort over perfection, and engaging in calming activities like drawing. Yang agreed to track burdensome thoughts and incorporate drawing as a means to manage stress and promote self-kindness and mindfulness.
