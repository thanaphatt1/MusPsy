The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaoyan is a student dealing with significant academic pressure, particularly regarding mock exams, and the stress of not meeting personal expectations. Xiaoyan is concerned about comparing their performance with classmates and fears disappointing themselves and their family.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys drawing, using it as a method to relax and escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaoyan experiences stress and anxiety primarily triggered by academic and performance pressures. There is no indication of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaoyan feels stressed and overwhelmed due to disappointing exam results and perceives a lack of progress despite efforts.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiaoyan's pressure from self-comparison with classmates and sees potential in exploring how Xiaoyan can focus on personal growth to alleviate anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaoyan should document feelings about each study session and note thought patterns that emerge, focusing on maintaining personal growth rather than comparison.
[Session Goal:] Help Xiaoyan manage academic stress by identifying and charting thought patterns and encouraging engagement in relaxing activities like drawing.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaoyan's stress and discouragement stemming from academic pressure and comparison with classmates, suggesting the practice of journaling to identify thought patterns. Encouraged focusing on personal growth, setting realistic goals, and celebrating small victories to boost confidence, and proposed incorporating regular drawing sessions as a means to relax and provide balance.
