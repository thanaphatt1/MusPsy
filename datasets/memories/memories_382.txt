The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ms. Zhang is a mother who recently stopped taking lithium and is experiencing increased anxiety and disarray in daily life. She struggles with fulfilling responsibilities at home, especially concerning her daughter’s needs. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Ms. Zhang enjoys spending time in nature and sketching, although she hasn’t engaged much in these activities recently. She values being a good role model for her daughter.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ms. Zhang exhibits symptoms of anxiety, feeling overwhelmed by daily tasks and pressures she imposes on herself.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ms. Zhang feels low and anxious, especially since ceasing lithium, and perceives daily tasks as overwhelming.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Ms. Zhang's high expectations for herself, as well as her openness to exploring thought patterns affecting anxiety.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ms. Zhang is encouraged to end each day on a positive note by reflecting on what went well and focus on efforts rather than outcomes.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to shift Ms. Zhang’s perspective from perfectionism to self-compassion and recognize life's normal setbacks.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ms. Zhang's heightened anxiety and pressure to maintain perfection after stopping lithium, impacting daily life and motherhood. Identified morning routines and self-imposed standards as key stressors, agreed to shift focus from outcomes to efforts, and introduce daily positive reflection to enhance coping strategies. Encouraged self-compassion and role modeling resilience to daughter, paving way for gradual adjustment.
