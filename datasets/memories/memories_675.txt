The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
S is a first-year university student struggling with adapting to their new environment and feeling increased social anxiety, particularly exacerbated by a stutter. S often feels socially isolated and finds university life overwhelming.

[Personal Characteristics]  
S is empathetic and a good listener, qualities that allow them to connect well with others despite communication challenges.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
S experiences significant social anxiety linked to their speech difficulties, alongside feelings of depression and inadequacy, but there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] S feels anxious, frustrated, and isolated due to difficulties fitting into their new environment at university and is troubled by their worsening stutter.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives S's struggle with social adaptation and fear of judgment due to stuttering but notices S's potential for positive interactions through their listening skills.
[Counseling Assignments:] S should note down specific situations that trigger anxiety or frustration throughout the week to explore further in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help S identify anxiety triggers, understand self-critical thoughts, and strengthen social ties through their listening strength.
Counseling Summary:
Throughout several counseling sessions, Star explored their social anxiety related to starting university, influenced by a fear of judgment due to their stutter, and engaged in identifying strengths such as being empathetic and a good listener, to facilitate connection over avoidance. They applied these strengths in social settings, noticing reduced anxiety and improved interactions, and committed to mindfulness practices and setting aside time for personal activities like reading. Star expressed increased confidence and self-awareness, developing strategies to manage future challenges independently.
