The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wu is a professional dealing with significant work stress after losing a major project and is struggling with self-esteem and practical lifestyle balance. Wu reports feelings of inadequacy stemming from recent job challenges.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wu previously enjoyed jogging but has not found time for it amid work stress, indicating a need to reconnect with interests for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wu exhibits symptoms of work-related stress and anxiety, manifesting in disrupted eating and sleeping patterns, yet no formal mental health disorder diagnosis is mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wu feels overwhelmed and insufficient following the loss of a major project, leading to severe doubts about his professional competence.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wu's strong self-critical tendencies and commitment to improving, highlighting the significance of restoring confidence through cognitive restructuring.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wu should document recurring negative thoughts about work and explore their emotional impact, aiming for clearer insight and pattern recognition.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Wu recognize and restructure negative thought patterns, improve self-esteem, and promote healthier work-life balance.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wu's low mood and self-doubt following a project loss at work. Wu noticed the unrealistic nature of his negative thoughts and began shifting his perspective to view challenges as opportunities for improvement, applying this mindset to time management and meeting preparation. Continued focus on mindfulness and practical strategies, like task breakdown and engaging in enjoyable activities such as jogging, to manage stress effectively.
