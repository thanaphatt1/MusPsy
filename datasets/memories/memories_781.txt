The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a nursing student dealing with anxiety related to an upcoming nursing qualification exam. Zhang struggles with compulsive cleaning behaviors that disrupt their study schedule.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys reading mystery novels, which they find absorbing and a way to shift focus away from anxiety.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety largely triggered by exam preparation pressures and compulsive behaviors, with no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels anxious and overwhelmed due to exam pressure and compulsive cleaning interfering with study time.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Zhang's difficulty managing time due to compulsive behaviors but sees their willingness to identify and address these issues.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should jot down specific instances of compulsive behavior and note associated thoughts to understand triggers better.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Zhang manage time effectively and reduce the impact of compulsive behaviors on exam preparation.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's compulsive cleaning behavior interfering with exam preparation and identified anxiety as a trigger, correlated with irrational thoughts about cleanliness impacting success. Recommended strategies included setting cleaning time limits and incorporating enjoyable activities like reading to balance stress management with study focus, fostering a shift towards healthier thought patterns and active coping mechanisms.
