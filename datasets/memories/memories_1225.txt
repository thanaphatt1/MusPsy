The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student experiencing overwhelming stress due to the transition to university life, difficulty keeping up with coursework, and feeling isolated from classmates.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys sketching, which offers a sense of grounding and an escape from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li is dealing with significant stress and anxiety related to social interactions and academic pressures without a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels overwhelmed by the transition to university, struggles with social interactions, and experiences a sense of isolation and constant unease.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li's acknowledgment of feeling isolated and stressed but also notes Li's willingness to explore relaxation through sketching and social connection strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is encouraged to note down situations triggering negative feelings and incorporate sketching into the weekly routine as a means of stress relief.
[Session Goal:] Help Li identify triggers of stress related to social and academic settings, and explore stress-reduction activities like sketching to facilitate coping strategies.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's struggles with university transition, including feelings of isolation and stress impacting coursework and concentration. Agreed on strategies like identifying emotional triggers, integrating sketching into the routine, and engaging in small social actions for connection, with an emphasis on manageable steps to improve academic and social comfort. Encouraged combining calming environments with sketching for relaxation and potential conversation starters to foster positive interactions.
