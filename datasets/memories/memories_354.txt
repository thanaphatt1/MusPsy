The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is experiencing significant difficulties after losing his place of residence, leading to living on the streets, and is feeling low and hopeless due to these circumstances. There is ongoing tension in the relationship with his psychiatric social worker.  

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei enjoys poetry writing as a form of self-expression and has shown interest in reading, finding it a relaxing and enjoyable activity.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei struggles with feelings of hopelessness, anxiety, and the perception of being a burden, though there is no formal mental health diagnosis noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels trapped and worried about his future due to current living conditions and strained interaction with his social worker.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang Wei's genuine struggle with hopelessness and acknowledges his courage to express his emotions despite challenging circumstances.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is encouraged to try engaging the social worker in a clear and constructive conversation to discuss his needs and past supportive efforts.
[Session Goal:] Explore CBT strategies to foster effective communication with the social worker and identify small, actionable steps for addressing current life challenges.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang Wei's challenges with homelessness and strained communication with a psychiatric social worker. Agreed on rekindling dialogue with the social worker and introduced poetry writing as a coping strategy to manage nighttime anxiety. Encouraged preparation and positive framing for the conversation to facilitate better understanding and support from the social worker.
