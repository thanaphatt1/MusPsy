The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
H is a university student burdened with responsibilities both academically and as part of the student council, feeling overwhelmed by workload and personal issues.

[Personal Characteristics]  
H enjoys reading fiction, using it as a stress relief and escape from daily pressures.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
H experiences significant stress and anxiety, particularly from high demands and expectations, without indication of a formal mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] H expresses feeling overwhelmed and stressed due to increased workload and personal embarrassment.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes H's pressure from internal and external expectations, but also notes H's willingness to explore stress management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] H should write down stressful moments and accompanying thoughts while integrating relaxing activities like reading into their routine.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to identify and challenge stress-inducing thoughts, encouraging relaxation through reading.

---
Counseling Summary:
H is experiencing overwhelm due to an increased workload and personal issues, leading to stress and feelings of inadequacy. The counselor suggested noting stressful moments and the accompanying thoughts, challenging their accuracy, and incorporating enjoyable activities like reading fiction to manage stress. Regular reflection and dedicated relaxation time were proposed to help H gradually navigate these challenges.
