The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Dihao is a student who has been feeling frustrated and overwhelmed with school, especially due to a new, fast-paced course which has been hard to adapt to. Dihao often feels misunderstood in terms of the stress they're facing.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Dihao enjoys drawing, which is a calming activity for them, but they haven't had much time to engage with it lately.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Dihao experiences stress and anxiety related to academic challenges, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Dihao feels frustrated and overwhelmed due to difficulty adapting to a new school course.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Dihao's struggle with feeling lost in class dynamics yet notes their willingness to explore collaborative learning approaches, like studying with a peer.
[Counseling Assignments:] Dihao is encouraged to identify specific overwhelming moments at school and consider reaching out to a classmate, like Jake, for study sessions.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Dihao manage academic stress and identify coping mechanisms like collaborative learning.

---
Counseling Summary:
Explored Dihao's feelings of frustration and overwhelm with adapting to a fast-paced new course, focusing on specific challenges with math. Agreed on recording overwhelming moments to identify triggers and strategizing by reaching out to a classmate for study support. Encouraged reflection on experiences and continued exploration of underlying thought patterns.
