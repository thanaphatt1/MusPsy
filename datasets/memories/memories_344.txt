The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lihua is a teacher experiencing overwhelming stress due to workload and the fear of not meeting students' expectations. Recently, the start of a new semester has significantly increased her stress levels.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She enjoys reading and writing, which previously served as a creative outlet and a way to express emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lihua experiences stress and anxiety primarily linked to her professional responsibilities, although there is no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lihua feels overwhelmed and anxious due to increased workload and fear of not meeting students' expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Lihua's high self-standards and the pressure she places on herself, noting her openness to exploring anxiety management techniques.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lihua should note situations or thoughts that increase her anxiety to help identify patterns and address them effectively.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Lihua manage her anxiety and stress by identifying specific stress-related thoughts and engaging in relaxing activities like reading and writing.
Counseling Summary:
Lihua has been experiencing stress and anxiety related to increased workload and fear of not meeting student expectations. Through the sessions, Lihua recognized a pattern of self-critical thoughts and began to challenge these beliefs by reflecting on past successes, leading to a more balanced perspective. With the implementation of breaking tasks into manageable parts and incorporating leisure activities like reading, Lihua reports improvements in stress management and overall mood.
