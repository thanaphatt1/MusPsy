The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaofang is a new high school student feeling overwhelmed by the academic pressure of a gifted class and experiencing homesickness from living away from home. Adjusting to the rigorous environment is challenging for Xiaofang, who used to excel academically but now struggles with self-doubt.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaofang has a passion for drawing, which has been a source of relaxation and enjoyment in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaofang experiences anxiety primarily triggered by academic pressures and social isolation, with no formal diagnosis reported.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaofang feels overwhelmed and isolated, struggling with self-doubt in the new school environment.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Xiaofang's anxiety related to academic performance and social isolation but sees a willingness to explore stress-relief activities and new routines.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaofang is encouraged to keep a diary of thoughts and feelings and to allocate time for drawing as a relaxation activity.
[Session Goal:] Introduce strategies to manage academic stress and social anxiety by using cognitive-behavioral techniques, such as creating a balanced study and relaxation routine.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiaofang's feelings of anxiety due to academic pressure and homesickness in a new school environment. Suggested keeping a diary to track thoughts and drawing to balance stress, alongside setting manageable study goals. Encouraged joining the art club to connect with peers and enhance social interactions, while leveraging her brother's supportive words for added perspective.
