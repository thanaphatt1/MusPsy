The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liu Li, a reformed inmate, recently faced disappointment in an award competition designed for reformed inmates, which deeply impacted their self-esteem. Liu Li manages a consistent struggle with self-worth and external validation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liu Li enjoys reading and values the idea of learning, using these activities as a means to improve personal skills and self-worth.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liu Li experiences feelings of inadequacy and shame, largely influenced by past events and perceived judgments, and currently lacks a formal mental health diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liu Li feels deeply inadequate following not winning a reformed inmate award, heightening feelings of shame and self-consciousness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Liu Li's vulnerability concerning self-esteem and the influence of external validation, yet sees a readiness to redirect focus onto personal growth.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liu Li is encouraged to jot down recurring thoughts and try engaging more in educational reading for personal enrichment.
[Session Goal:] Assist Liu Li in redirecting focus away from external validation towards self-growth activities by employing CBT principles like thought recording.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liu Li's feelings of inadequacy and self-consciousness following a recent disappointment in not winning a reformed inmate award. Encouraged Liu Li to reflect on and write down recurring thoughts to assess their fairness, and to engage in activities like reading and educational programs to improve self-worth and focus on personal growth. Agreed on setting a routine to balance reflection with growth-focused activities to shift mindset from seeking external validation to fostering personal development.
