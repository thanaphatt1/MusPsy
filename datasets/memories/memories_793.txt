The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Liang is a university student experiencing significant stress related to academic performance, particularly recent exam results, and the pressure to meet family expectations. Liang is worried about letting their parents down and uncertain about their future prospects.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Liang enjoys drawing, which serves as a distraction and a means to calm their nerves during stressful periods.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Liang is experiencing anxiety and stress, primarily triggered by academic pressures, with no mention of a formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Liang feels stressed, overwhelmed by recent academic results, and anxious about potential judgment from others.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Liang's tendency to internalize stress and highlights the importance of acknowledging and regulating emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Liang is encouraged to recognize current emotions during overwhelming instances, take note of recurring thoughts, and ensure to integrate drawing into their routine as a stress-relief activity.
[Session Goal:] Use CBT strategies to help Liang manage stress, identify and challenge specific stress-related thoughts, and incorporate regular relaxation activities like drawing.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Liang's stress and anxiety related to academic performance and fear of judgment, identifying a pattern of negative thought triggers in social contexts. Introduced strategies for managing emotions, including recognizing and pausing negative thoughts, and suggested incorporating drawing for relaxation. Agreed on a goal to record emotions and thoughts and to set aside time for drawing as part of stress management, with plans to discuss progress in the next session.
