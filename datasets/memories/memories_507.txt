The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiao Z is a student experiencing overwhelming academic pressure, particularly around subjects like math and science, which they find challenging and complex. Xiao Z is determined to improve their grades despite struggling to find effective study methods.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiao Z tends to fall into harsh self-criticism and negative thinking patterns when facing academic challenges.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiao Z experiences significant academic stress, accompanied by anxiety and a tendency towards negative self-beliefs, but no formal mental health diagnosis has been made.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiao Z feels worried and overwhelmed due to academic pressures and doubts about their abilities in math and science.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Xiao Z's pattern of harsh self-criticism, notably assuming failure before attempting tasks, but also recognizes Xiao Z’s potential willingness to identify specific stress triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiao Z should identify one or two areas in their studies where they feel they need the most help and track moments of negative thinking.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to help Xiao Z manage academic stress by identifying thought patterns and developing self-compassion.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Xiao Z's academic anxiety, focusing on concerns about foundational knowledge and study techniques. Agreed to identify specific challenging subjects (math and science) to target in future sessions, and assigned a task to track negative self-thoughts to recognize patterns. Encouraged shifting self-talk to be more positive and supportive as a strategy for coping and improvement.
