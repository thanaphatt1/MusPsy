The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
C is a young adult student experiencing conflicting emotions due to a budding interest in a classmate, overshadowed by concerns about perceived superficial traits such as height. C values personal connections but finds themselves often caught in self-doubt, questioning their self-worth based on others' perceptions.

[Personal Characteristics]  
C enjoys reading and finds it a soothing activity that helps alleviate stress before sleep.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
C’s main challenge involves anxiety triggered by self-imposed judgments and perceived expectations of others, with no formal mental health diagnosis conveyed.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] C feels conflicted and anxious about developing feelings for a classmate, troubled by superficial concerns and struggling with self-doubt.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives C's internal conflict rooted in self-worth questioning but notes C's willingness to explore cognitive patterns affecting their feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] C is encouraged to write down recurring anxious thoughts, particularly concerning judgment, and incorporate daily reading sessions to relax.
[Session Goal:] Facilitate C's self-reflection on distorted beliefs impacting emotional responses and emphasize relaxation through enjoyable activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
Discussed C's struggle with anxiety related to a new classmate and self-worth issues due to societal judgments. Agreed on recording feelings when anxiety arises and identifying truths versus assumptions, while incorporating relaxing activities like reading to manage overthinking. Scheduled 30 minutes of reading each day to support relaxation and reflection, encouraging gradual progress.
