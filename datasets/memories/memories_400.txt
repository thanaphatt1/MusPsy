The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a male professional recently promoted to lead a sales team, which has brought about significant stress and anxiety, notably with public speaking and client interactions. He expresses concern about his work affecting his family life, particularly reduced quality time with his child.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Wei enjoys playing basketball, which has served as a stress relief activity in the past.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei predominantly experiences anxiety related to work pressures, with no formal mention of a diagnosed mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels anxious and stressed due to increased responsibilities and fears of underperformance in his new role.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist perceives Wei as being overwhelmed by the demands of his role but also notes his willingness to recognize and challenge his anxiety triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei should track situations or thoughts that trigger his anxiety for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT strategies to assist Wei in identifying anxiety triggers and managing stress effectively.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wei's work-related anxiety linked to his new leadership role and fear of public speaking, acknowledging its impact on personal life. Agreed on strategies including challenging negative assumptions, focusing on strengths, recording practice sessions, and reintroducing basketball for relaxation. Wei reported feeling more confident and planned to continue using these strategies to manage stress.
