The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yu is a student experiencing emotional challenges despite medication, feeling overwhelmed by academics and a sense of inadequacy compared to peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yu previously enjoyed drawing, which could be utilized as a creative outlet for stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yu exhibits symptoms of anxiety and decreased motivation related to academic performance, but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental disorder shared.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yu feels stuck and frustrated due to academic pressures and a lack of motivation.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yu's pattern of self-comparison and avoidance in assignments, yet notes a willingness to explore self-awareness tools.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yu is encouraged to note mood and thought changes throughout the day and maintain a journal of small personal achievements.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT to help Yu recognize and articulate thoughts contributing to feelings of inadequacy and increase motivation through self-awareness activities.
Counseling Summary:
Yu struggles with feelings of inadequacy related to academic performance, leading to avoidance of assignments and motivation challenges. During the session, Yu agreed to start journaling accomplishments, focusing on small, personal goals to boost self-confidence and counter negative self-dialogue. The consultant encouraged Yu to practice self-compassion and challenge negative thoughts, aiming for gradual change and self-awareness.
