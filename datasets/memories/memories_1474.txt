The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Hai is a high school student who is experiencing stress due to the upcoming college entrance exam. He feels additional pressure due to his desk mate's improving grades, which amplifies his anxiety about academic performance.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Hai enjoys playing the guitar, though he sometimes struggles to find the time for it.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Hai experiences stress and anxiety centered around academic pressures, with a tendency to compare his performance unfavorably to that of his peers, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Hai is stressed and overwhelmed by academic pressures, particularly by the comparisons with his desk mate's improving grades.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Hai's focus on peer comparison as a source of stress, noting both his willingness to address this issue and his use of self-defeating thoughts.
[Counseling Assignments:] Hai should list situations where he feels most pressured and note his thoughts at those moments for further discussion.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT strategies to help Hai recognize and modify automatic negative thoughts, focusing on self-comparison issues.

---
Counseling Summary:
Hai expressed stress about his upcoming college entrance exams and the pressure from comparing himself to his improving desk mate. Through sessions, he learned to recognize and challenge his negative thoughts, focusing instead on personal growth by practicing positive self-talk and incorporating relaxing activities like playing the guitar. As a result, Hai felt more at ease with his progress and developed coping strategies to manage stress and avoid unhealthy comparisons.
