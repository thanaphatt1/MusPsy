The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
The user is a university student struggling with the transition to university life, facing significant stress due to academic pressures and uncertainty about choosing a major, while dealing with high parental expectations.

[Personal Characteristics]  
The user finds joy and relaxation in playing the guitar, which they had set aside due to their hectic university schedule.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
The user experiences stress and anxiety, primarily triggered by academic pressures and comparison to peers, but there is no formal diagnosis provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] The user feels overwhelmed and stressed by academic pressures and parental expectations.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant notes the user's tendency to self-compare and high sensitivity to perceived academic shortcomings, indicating a need for self-compassion and focus on personal strengths.
[Counseling Assignments:] The user is asked to write down specific stressful situations and the thoughts associated with them to help identify triggers.
[Session Goal:] To use CBT strategies to help the user identify stress triggers, understand the impact of thoughts on emotions, and introduce self-compassion practices.
Counseling Summary:
The sessions explored the user's stress and anxiety stemming from academic pressure and self-comparison at university. Through reflective exercises and focusing on personal achievements and hobbies like playing the guitar, the user gained confidence and identified strategies to manage anxiety, such as focusing on self-progress rather than comparing to others. The user plans to continue setting aside time for self-care and reflection to maintain balance and manage stress effectively.
