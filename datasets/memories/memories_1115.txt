The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Yali is a young individual experiencing stress and self-blame, especially related to past relationships and family expectations. They have grown up in a context where external validation was significant. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
Yali enjoys drawing and taking nature walks as a form of relaxation and finding personal peace.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Yali experiences stress and negative self-talk but has not been formally diagnosed with any mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Yali feels overwhelmed in the evenings, particularly when alone, with thoughts focused on past relationship missteps and self-blame.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Yali's tendency towards negative self-talk and critical thinking patterns but notes Yali’s potential openness to adopting more balanced perspectives.
[Counseling Assignments:] Yali is encouraged to write down counter-thoughts to negative self-talk, creating one alternative thought for each negative perception.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT methods to help Yali replace negative self-talk with balanced perspectives and cultivate self-compassion.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Yali's overwhelming feelings and negative self-talk patterns, particularly focusing on self-blame and insecurity. Agreed on practicing counter-thoughts and sharing small parts of her story with trusted individuals to alter perceptions and foster healing, while incorporating drawing as a creative outlet alongside nature walks for emotional balance. Planned to gradually build new thought patterns and monitor progress in the next session.
