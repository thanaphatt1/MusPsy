The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Tang is a middle-aged individual who recently went through a divorce and is struggling with loneliness and self-blame. She finds herself isolated in her new living situation and is trying to adjust to this significant life change.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Tang enjoys reading and playing music, specifically the guitar. She is also considering exploring activities like running for mental clarity and stress relief.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Tang experiences feelings of anxiety and sadness, often triggered by self-critical thoughts. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder, but her emotional distress is noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Tang feels overwhelmed and lost following her divorce, with a heightened sense of isolation, particularly in the evenings.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Tang's tendency towards self-blame and recognizes her openness to finding new routines to manage these feelings.
[Counseling Assignments:] Tang should note times of elevated anxiety or sadness to identify triggers and write down supportive words she would offer to a friend in similar distress.
[Session Goal:] Assist Tang in identifying thought patterns that contribute to self-blame and explore strategies like establishing new routines for support and emotional balance.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Tang’s feelings of isolation and self-blame following his divorce, and discussed how these contribute to his anxiety and sadness, particularly in the evenings. Agreed on tracking moments of heightened emotions and suggested viewing challenges from a friend’s perspective to reduce self-blame, as well as implementing reading as a soothing activity before bed to help manage loneliness and improve sleep. Planned for Tang to share his experiences and any changes in upcoming sessions, encouraging patience and self-compassion during this adjustment period.
