The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Ivy is a student who feels isolated and rejected by classmates, triggering memories of past experiences of feeling worthless. Ivy struggles with fitting in and often feels excluded in group settings.

[Personal Characteristics]  
She values meaningful connections and is open to exploring personal thoughts and emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Ivy experiences social anxiety and feelings of rejection but has not been formally diagnosed with a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Ivy feels isolated and struggles with feelings of being inherently flawed and not good enough.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Ivy's openness to exploring feelings and her reflection on past experiences influencing present perceptions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Ivy is encouraged to jot down positive interactions to gain a balanced perspective on social experiences.
[Session Goal:] Assist Ivy in identifying negative self-perceptions and promote a shift toward recognizing positive social interactions.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Ivy's feelings of isolation and self-doubt stemming from past experiences and recent social interactions, which contribute to her negative self-perception. Agreed on a cognitive-behavioral approach to challenge and reframe negative beliefs by focusing on positive social interactions and maintaining a record of these moments. Encouraged Ivy to notice and challenge negative thoughts with a focus on cultivating hope and progress, with ongoing support in future sessions.
