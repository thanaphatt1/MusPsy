The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
A is a young individual who feels neglected due to the absence of their father, who is frequently away for work. This situation has led A to feel lonely and unsupported at home. 

[Personal Characteristics]  
A enjoys drawing and reading, which serve as peaceful activities and potential coping mechanisms.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
A experiences feelings of loneliness and low self-esteem due to emotional neglect, but there is no formal diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] A feels upset and ignored, particularly due to their father's absence, leading to loneliness and challenges in focusing at school.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes A’s isolation from peers alongside a noticeable yearning for connection and understanding.
[Counseling Assignments:] A is encouraged to maintain a daily journal to track thoughts and emotions, aiming for better self-awareness.
[Session Goal:] Utilize CBT techniques to help A express emotions through journaling and introduce activities like drawing as a relaxation method to improve emotional resilience.
Counseling Summary:
The session addressed A's feelings of loneliness and being ignored due to their father's absence, impacting their focus and social interactions. They agreed to practice daily journaling to explore emotions and patterns, and set a goal to incorporate enjoyable activities like reading to help improve their mood. A expressed that the counseling sessions feel supportive and plans to continue exploring their feelings and experiences.
