The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang Wei is a parent experiencing tension at home due to his son's conflicts at school. His interactions with his son's teachers and subsequent family dynamics have led to stress and guilt from losing patience with his son.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang Wei values open communication and is driven to support his son through understanding and problem-solving.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang Wei is experiencing stress and guilt related to parenting challenges, with particular pressure regarding his son’s school issues. There is no formal mental health disorder diagnosis noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang Wei feels overwhelmed and under pressure both from resolving his son’s conflict and household tensions.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notes Zhang Wei's high levels of stress and self-imposed pressure regarding his parenting role, but also his willingness to seek solutions and improve family dynamics.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang Wei is encouraged to note stress triggers related to his interactions with his son's school to identify patterns for future sessions.
[Session Goal:] To use CBT techniques to help Zhang Wei manage stress and gain insight into his parenting pressures.
Counseling Summary:
Zhang Wei addressed the stress stemming from his son's school conflict and its impact on family dynamics, identifying meetings with teachers and arguments with his wife as key stress triggers. Through professional guidance, he challenged negative self-assessments as a parent, explored constructive communication strategies with his son, and prioritized basketball for stress relief. Over the sessions, he gained perspective by practicing mindfulness and considering alternative viewpoints, enhancing his coping mechanisms for future challenges.
