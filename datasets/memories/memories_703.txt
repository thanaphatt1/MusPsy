The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student experiencing familial stress, particularly due to conflicts between his parents, which impact his concentration on studies. He feels obligated to visit home over weekends but struggles with the emotional burden of ongoing arguments and his father's temper.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li finds relief in running, which helps him clear his mind and manage stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li feels anxious and uneasy, largely due to family dynamics, yet there is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder at this time.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels stressed and overwhelmed due to family conflict and the pressure of maintaining his focus on studies.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Li's apprehension about family visits and his struggle to balance his own space with family expectations.
[Counseling Assignments:] This week, Li should try running as a stress-relief activity and jot down recurring overwhelming thoughts for further exploration.
[Session Goal:] Help Li identify stressors related to family dynamics and encourage engaging in relaxing activities like running for emotional relief.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's stress and anxiety related to family conflict and its impact on academic focus. Together, they agreed on stress management strategies, including journaling thoughts, incorporating running into Li's routine, and practicing the 4-7-8 breathing technique for anxiety relief. Li expressed optimism and commitment to these self-care practices and will bring notes to the next session for further discussion.
