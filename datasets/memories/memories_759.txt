The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhao is a school student who recently moved and transferred schools. He struggles to keep up with subjects like English and math, leading to stress and discouragement. Zhao once enjoyed reading and playing soccer but now diverts his time to online gaming as an escape from stress.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys activities like reading and soccer, but has recently been spending more time gaming, which provides a temporary sense of achievement.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhao experiences stress linked to academic performance and a belief that he is not good enough, particularly in subjects he finds challenging. There is no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder provided.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhao feels stressed and overwhelmed, struggling with low motivation for schoolwork and hobbies due to a perceived lack of success.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhao's tendency to escape into gaming to avoid stress and the challenge of adapting to new academic pressures.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhao should monitor gaming urges and triggers, aiming to understand the underlying patterns driving this behavior.
[Session Goal:] Help Zhao recognize and manage stress triggers and explore healthier coping mechanisms through cognitive behavior therapy (CBT).
Counseling Summary:
Explored how Zhao's gaming habit serves as an escape from the stress of adjusting to a new school and struggling with English and math, recognizing feelings of inadequacy as a trigger. Agreed on goals to reduce gaming by re-engaging with past hobbies like reading and soccer, and on using journaling as a tool to track progress and reflect on thoughts. Highlighted the importance of setting realistic goals and sharing progress with friends to build motivation and regain a sense of control.
