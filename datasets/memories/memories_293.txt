The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wang is a university student adjusting to campus life, encountering challenges with roommates with differing habits, living away from family for the first time. Wang is focused on succeeding in math studies but feels initial stress in forming new social connections.

[Personal Characteristics]  
He enjoys chess and solving puzzles, previously used as leisure activities that fostered problem-solving skills during high school.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wang experiences stress and anxiety due to a noisy living environment and perceived lack of control over his surroundings. No formal diagnosis has been noted.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wang feels stressed and overwhelmed due to roommate dynamics, impacting his ability to study and relax effectively.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Wang's awareness of the negative impact his surroundings have on his stress levels and his openness to exploring stress management strategies.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wang should note stressful moments and thoughts to identify patterns and consider peaceful strategies for coping in a chaotic setting.
[Session Goal:] Implement CBT strategies to assist Wang in recognizing stress patterns and promoting small peaceful actions in daily life to enhance study and relaxation potential.
Counseling Summary:
The counseling session focused on Wang's struggles with adapting to university life, particularly due to the disruptive habits of his roommates, which have affected his studies and social interactions. Together, Wang and the consultant decided to work on identifying moments of stress and related thoughts to uncover patterns in Wang's feelings, and explore strategies for finding peace despite the noise. The consultant encouraged Wang to recognize these stress points as a step towards effective management, and Wang expressed commitment to these tasks before their next session.
