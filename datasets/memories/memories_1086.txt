The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Momo is a teacher who struggles with eye contact at work, which impacts interactions with students and colleagues, sparking anxiety and self-doubt.

[Personal Characteristics]
Momo tends to be shy and has enjoyed gardening, which serves as a peaceful, rewarding hobby.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Momo experiences anxiety linked to eye contact but there's no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Momo feels anxious and embarrassed about avoiding eye contact, fearing it affects professionalism and teaching effectiveness.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes that Momo's anxiety stems from longstanding habits and self-assumed judgments by others.
[Counseling Assignments:] Momo should note instances and triggers causing anxiety when making eye contact and engage in gentle self-challenging exercises.
[Session Goal:] Help Momo identify and gradually confront situations provoking eye contact anxiety using CBT techniques, focusing on reducing fear-driven avoidance.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Momo's anxiety regarding eye contact at work, likely rooted in childhood shyness and fear of judgment; identified the avoidance impacting teaching interactions. Encouraged Momo to track anxiety triggers and perceptions, with plans for a small eye contact exercise to challenge avoidance, alongside reflective journaling to gain insights. Emphasized mindful growth versus judgment in addressing fears, aiming to boost confidence in professional interactions.
