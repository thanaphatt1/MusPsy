The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Zhang is a student in their early 20s dealing with body image concerns related to asymmetry, particularly anxious about upcoming swimming classes exacerbating these worries. Zhang has not shared these feelings widely and experiences heightened self-consciousness.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Zhang enjoys reading fiction, which provides a mental escape from stressors and allows them to clear their mind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Zhang experiences anxiety primarily triggered by body image concerns, although no formal diagnosis of a mental health disorder has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Zhang feels worried and stressed due to impending swimming classes and is concerned about others noticing their body asymmetry.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Zhang's heightened self-consciousness and anxiety over perceived flaws but notes Zhang's willingness to explore thoughts that trigger discomfort.
[Counseling Assignments:] Zhang should write down instances that trigger anxiety about body image and observe feelings at those moments to better understand their experiences.
[Session Goal:] Employ CBT strategies to help Zhang manage anxiety by identifying specific moments of distress and encouraging self-reflection.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Zhang's anxiety about body asymmetry and upcoming swimming classes, reflecting on their fear of judgment and tendency to avoid situations. Suggested writing down triggering instances and associated feelings to gain insight and facilitate coping. Initiated discussion on long-term strategies beyond avoidance.
