The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Li is a university student experiencing stress related to living in a dormitory with another student who also stutters, worsening his own stuttering experience. He expresses concerns about being judged by peers.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Li enjoys reading and painting, which provide him relief from stress and serve as a means to relax and unwind.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Li is struggling with stress and anxiety exacerbated by stuttering. There is no formal mention of a mental health disorder diagnosis.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Li feels stressed and anxious, particularly during social situations where speaking is involved, such as class presentations and meal times.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Li's susceptibility to assuming negative judgments from others and a quick mental leap to worst-case scenarios.
[Counseling Assignments:] Li is tasked with noting specific situations where he feels pressure related to stuttering and reflecting on whether there's evidence supporting his fears of judgment.
[Session Goal:] The goal is to help Li identify and challenge assumptions contributing to his anxiety and encourage a focus on positives in communication.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Li's increased stress and anxiety due to stuttering exacerbated by shared dormitory living, focusing on the impact of negative thoughts during class presentations and cafeteria interactions. Agreed on tasks to challenge negative thinking and prioritize enjoyable activities, particularly painting, to manage stress. Li showed progress in reframing thoughts towards focusing on positive communication and plans to incorporate joyful activities regularly for stress relief.
