The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Wei is a school teacher grappling with past regrets while trying to maintain a new relationship with a colleague. He feels guilty about previous boundary-crossing interactions with a student and anxious about how it might impact his current relationship. 

[Personal Characteristics]
Wei enjoys reading literary novels and finds this hobby a welcome escape that provides perspective.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Wei experiences guilt and anxiety related to past actions affecting his present sense of self-worth and relationship trust. No formal mental health disorder diagnosis has been mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Wei feels conflicted and guilty due to past actions, impacting his self-esteem in his current relationship.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Wei's struggle with self-worth and his fear of past actions affecting his present, yet notes his potential for constructive self-reflection.
[Counseling Assignments:] Wei should focus on recalling positive moments in his current relationship to counter negative thoughts and prepare for an honest conversation with his partner.
[Session Goal:] Help Wei address guilt by fostering positive self-dialogue, encouraging open communication with his partner, and introducing stress-relief activities like reading.
Counseling Summary:
Explored Wei's feelings of guilt and anxiety stemming from past boundary issues with a student, which are impacting his current relationship. Agreed on steps to enhance his well-being by focusing on positive aspects of his relationship, preparing for an open conversation with his partner, and integrating relaxing activities such as reading. Wei committed to maintaining a positive self-dialogue and establishing a balanced perspective.
