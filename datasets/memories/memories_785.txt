The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Xiaotian is a student facing stress primarily due to parental arguments about academic performance and their impact on familial relationships. He is worried about being the cause of his parents' conflicts and has fears of family separation.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Xiaotian enjoys playing video games with his younger brothers and drawing, using these activities as a form of relaxation and distraction from stress.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Xiaotian experiences anxiety and stress related to his family dynamics and academic expectations, though not diagnosed with a specific mental health disorder.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Xiaotian is stressed and feels guilty, fearing parental separation as a result of their arguments about his academic performance.
[Counselor Observations:] The consultant observes Xiaotian's heightened anxiety related to his parents' relationship and notes his capacity for open communication about emotional challenges.
[Counseling Assignments:] Xiaotian is encouraged to write down his feelings and note when they are most intense; schedule relaxing activities like drawing or gaming more regularly.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Xiaotian manage stress and separate his identity from parental conflicts, reinforcing the importance of relaxation activities.
Counseling Summary:
Xiaotian is experiencing stress and anxiety due to parental conflicts over exam results, making it difficult to focus on schoolwork and causing feelings of guilt. The consultant suggests Xiaotian write down emotions and triggers to gain insight and encourages engaging in calming activities like drawing or gaming with siblings. They agree to implement a schedule for these activities and reconvene next week to discuss progress and insights.
