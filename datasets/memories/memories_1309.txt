The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Honghong is a young student experiencing anger issues, particularly during interactions with peers at school. Recently, Honghong had an incident involving a fight over a playground ball, highlighting difficulties in managing impulses and emotions.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Honghong has not received a formal diagnosis, but displays tendencies towards impulsive reactions and difficulty in emotional regulation, primarily linked to frustration and unmet desires.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Honghong enjoys drawing, which serves as a calming activity, and displays a willingness to learn effective ways of handling conflicts and emotions.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Honghong feels angry and upset due to a playground conflict over shared toys, struggling with the impulse to react physically when frustrated.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist observes Honghong's challenge with controlling immediate reactions during anger but notes Honghong’s openness to noticing and understanding emotional triggers.
[Counseling Assignments:] Honghong should begin observing situations that provoke anger, noting thoughts and events that contribute to these feelings, to discuss in the next session.
[Session Goal:] Help Honghong identify anger triggers and develop preliminary cognitive strategies to manage emotional responses better, focusing on awareness and reflection.
Counseling Summary:
In the counseling dialogue, Honghong explored the triggers for his anger and realized his desire for immediate wants often led to feelings of unfairness. Through the sessions, he identified the importance of considering others' perspectives and learned constructive strategies like discussing wants with classmates and engaging in calming activities like drawing. Honghong developed skills to manage his emotions and navigate social situations more positively, feeling hopeful for future interactions.
