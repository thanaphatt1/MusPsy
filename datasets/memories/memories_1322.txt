The 1th Counseling
Client Profile:
Static Traits: ## User Background
[Personal Profile]  
Lily is a high school student experiencing discomfort due to frequent arguments between her father and stepmother at home. This tension impacts her ability to focus on her studies and she often finds herself anxious and worried.

[Personal Characteristics]  
Lily enjoys drawing and finds solace in artistic expression, using it as a means to relax and focus.

[Assessment and Diagnosis]  
Lily displays signs of anxiety primarily related to domestic stress, although there is no formal mental health diagnosis mentioned.
Counseling Memory:
[Emotion and Cognitive State:] Lily is feeling uneasy due to the domestic environment, affecting her concentration on schoolwork.
[Counselor Observations:] The therapist notices Lily's awareness of her need for coping strategies and her willingness to explore activities like drawing and journaling to process her emotions.
[Counseling Assignments:] Lily should start journaling her thoughts and feelings, and set aside dedicated time for drawing as a calming activity.
[Session Goal:] Introduce CBT techniques to help Lily identify stress-related thoughts and incorporate coping strategies, such as drawing and journaling, into her routine.
Counseling Summary:
Lily discussed feeling anxious due to family conflicts affecting her concentration and emotional well-being. The consultant recommended journaling to better understand and manage her thoughts, along with establishing a routine incorporating drawing for relaxation alongside her studies. Lily agreed to try these strategies and planned to review their impact in the next session.
